The conventions namespace

https://nanoql.org/schemas/conventions is the XML namespace of nanoql's conventions files: calendars, day counts, currencies, indexes and instrument conventions, as records a program reads, overrides and writes.

  • The schema: nanoql-conventions.xsd.
  • The format: docs/conventions.md in nanoql's repository.
