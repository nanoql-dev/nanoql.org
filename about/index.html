About nanoql

nanoql is an open-source library for quantitative finance on .NET, under the Apache License 2.0. Its models, curves and pricers are plain functions of immutable inputs, and its market conventions are data.

Where it comes from

nanoql builds on open-source work, and every file it ports records where from:

  • QuantLib. nanoql's core is derived from QuantLib 1.44, and its results are checked against QuantLib's own. Its design differs: it has no observers, lazy objects or global evaluation date. Where porting finds a defect, nanoql usually fixes it, and each place its results depart from QuantLib's is documented in Divergences.
  • QLNet. nanoql's extensions port QLNet's mortgage-backed securities, with their prepayment models, and its amortised-cost bonds, checked against QLNet 1.13.2.
  • FpML. nanoql's conventions files accept the codes of FpML's coding schemes, such as its day-count and business-centre codes, beside nanoql's own names.

Their copyright notices and licences are on Licences and notices. nanoql is an independent project: the QuantLib and QLNet projects, and ISDA, which publishes FpML, are not affiliated with it and don't endorse it.
