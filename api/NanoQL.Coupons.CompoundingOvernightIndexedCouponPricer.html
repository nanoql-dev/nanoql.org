Remarks

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where the Black compounding pricer derives from it. Past fixings come from the context's store; the forward part is forecast on the index's curve, telescoped through one discount ratio wherever the coupon's CanApplyTelescopicFormula holds. A nonzero spread compounded daily is compounded day by day over that range, on the forecast fixings, as QuantLib compounds it outside the range and once the fixings are published; QuantLib multiplies both compound factors by the discount ratio, which drops the spread (a documented divergence).
