Namespace
NanoQL.Experimental

The two-dimensional finite-difference engine of a storage option on the spot e^(f(t) + X), X an extended Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process: the storage level in the second direction, on QuantLib's elevator mesher (the levels reached from empty and from full at the change rate) or a uniform one, and the injections and withdrawals by the simple storage condition at the exercise dates.

Remarks

The maturity and the exercise times are read on the discount curve's day counter from its reference date, as QuantLib's. A time past the seasonal shape's last is refused (FdmExpExtOUInnerValueCalculator).
