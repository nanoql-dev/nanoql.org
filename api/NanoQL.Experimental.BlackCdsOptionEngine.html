The Black-formula engine for options on credit default swaps: the underlying's fair spread against its running spread, discounted by its risky annuity, plus the front-end protection of a payer option that doesn't knock out.

Remarks

The engine assumes, as QuantLib's warns, that the exercise date equals the start date of the swap. The swap is priced by its engine, a delegate, where QuantLib's reads the swap's attached engine. The time to the exercise is floored at 0: an exercise between the evaluation date and the discount curve's reference date gives QuantLib a negative time and a NaN standard deviation, here the intrinsic value (Divergences/BlackCdsOptionEngineTests.cs); there a payer that doesn't knock out reads its front-end protection with a default probability of 0 and a discount of 1 before each curve's date, as the other credit engines read them.
