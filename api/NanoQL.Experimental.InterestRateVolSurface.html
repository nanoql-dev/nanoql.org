Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib. QuantLib's acyclic visitor isn't ported.

Documented divergence (Divergences/InterestRateVolSurfaceOptionDateTests.cs): every option tenor's date on the surface is the index's, the at-the-money volatility and variance and the smile section at a tenor included, where QuantLib's optionDateFromTenor hides its base's non-virtual member, so those members, defined on the bases, date the option on the calendar instead. OptionDateFromTenor(Period) hides the base's member as QuantLib's does; called through a VolatilityTermStructure reference, the base's date it is.
