Class GJRGARCHProcess

Namespace
NanoQL.Processes
Assembly

The GJR-GARCH(1,1) process in its continuous-time limit: dS = μS dt + √v S dW₁ and dv = (ω + (β + αq₂ + γq₃ − 1)v) dt + (ασ₁₂ + γσ₁₃)v dW₁ + √(α²(σ₂² − σ₁₂²) + γ²(σ₃² − σ₁₃²) + 2αγ(σ₂₃ − σ₁₂σ₁₃)) v dW₂, with the moments of the news shocks at the market price of risk λ, the daily parameters scaled by the days per year. Its state is the underlying and the annualized variance.

Remarks

Immutable: the curves, the spot and the parameters are fixed at construction, where QuantLib's process holds handles and a quote; the shocks' moments, which depend on λ, α, γ and the days per year only, are computed once there by QuantLib's arithmetic, where QuantLib's process recomputes them in each call. The in-place Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) steps by QuantLib's arithmetic without allocating, its rates read without building QuantLib's rate objects, so its bits are the allocating form's.

The model's time runs from the earliest reference date of the two curves on the risk-free curve's day counter, as HestonProcess's does, where QuantLib's runs from the risk-free curve's date and its analytic engine's day count turns negative before a later-dated curve.

Reference: Glosten, L., Jagannathan, R., Runkle, D., 1993. Relationship between the expected value and the volatility of the nominal excess return on stocks. Journal of Finance 48, 1779-1801.
