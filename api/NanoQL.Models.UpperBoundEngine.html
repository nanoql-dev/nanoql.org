Remarks

A working type: it holds the caller's outer and inner evolvers by reference, one engine at a time, and clones of the products, exercise values and strategy. An outer path allocates, as QuantLib's does: a curve-state copy a step and a callable clone an inner simulation.

The exercise values (documented divergences): the two exercise-value parts are an internal adapter paying the exercise value at every exercise date, done at its last step, where QuantLib reads them from an ExerciseAdapter done at its first and relies on the composite stepping a finished part again; and no date after the step at which the hedge ends is an exercise date, no inner simulation and no exercise value, a hedge with no possible cash-flow times exempt, that date's inner evolver still taken in QuantLib's order. The end's own date stays one (Divergences/UpperBoundEngineTests.cs).

Refused, QuantLib reading past them: fewer inner evolvers than the exercise dates before the last step.
