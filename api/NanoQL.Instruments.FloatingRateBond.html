Remarks

Unsealed, an extension point, as in QuantLib, where the Italian CCTEU derives from it. Its coupons are built by IborLeg when the bond is, so the bond takes the context: a leg with no caps, floors or in-arrears fixing gets QuantLib's default pricer, which reads the context's at-par switch then, and the bond keeps that choice. A capped, floored or in-arrears bond gets no pricer, and prices only once one is set with SetCouponPricer<T>(T, FloatingRateCouponPricer?).

It is built through Bond's construction contract, so its issue date isn't checked against the first payment, as in QuantLib. Its schedule's calendar is its settlement and payment calendar.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

context, schedule or iborIndex is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays or fixingDays is negative, or a convention isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date), or there are more gearings, spreads, caps or floors than coupons.

NanoQLException

The schedule is empty, or a date is computed on QuantLib's empty calendar where it can't give one.
