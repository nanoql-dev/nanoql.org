A cash flow paying an index ratio: the notional times I(T)/I(0), or times I(T)/I(0) - 1 when only the growth is paid. It isn't a coupon: it has no accruals.

Remarks

Meant for use inside an instrument that does the date adjustments: it takes its dates and doesn't change them. Paying the whole ratio is a bond's setting, the growth only a swap's.

The amount is computed on each call, where QuantLib caches it. An extension point: the CPI and zero-inflation cash flows derive from it, as will an equity cash flow.
