Remarks

It can be also used for T/N and S/N indexes, even if such indexes do not have ICE fixing. See https://www.theice.com/marketdata/reports/170. This is the rate fixed in London by ICE; use Eonia for the fixing by the ECB.

An extension point: QuantLib's EURLiborON derives from it.
