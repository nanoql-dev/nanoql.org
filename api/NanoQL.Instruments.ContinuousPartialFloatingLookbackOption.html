Class ContinuousPartialFloatingLookbackOption

Namespace
NanoQL.Instruments

NanoQL.dll

A continuous partial-time floating-strike lookback option (Heynen and Kat, 1994): the extremum is observed from the start up to a date before expiry, and a call pays the terminal spot less λ times the minimum, a put λ times the maximum less the terminal spot.

Remarks

Immutable. Its terms are checked before they are read, where QuantLib's validation dereferences a European exercise and a floating payoff unchecked.
