Remarks

Convolve(Distribution, Distribution) is the distribution of the sum: its bucket k holds the products of the buckets i and k − i, in buckets of the inputs' width. QuantLib's assigns each product where it should add it (distribution.cpp:309) and spreads its n₁ + n₂ − 1 buckets over [0, xmax₁ + xmax₂], one bucket wider, so its result's mass isn't 1 (an unused class; Divergences/DistributionMembersTests.cs).
