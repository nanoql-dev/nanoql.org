The interface of one-factor interest-rate models whose state is Gaussian: a derived model implements the numeraire and the zero bond for a value of the state variable y, the standardized (zero mean, unit variance) form of the model's state x.

Remarks

The variance of the state process given x(t) = x must not depend on x.

Immutable, unsealed: GSR and the Markov-functional model derive from it, as a model of one's own may (an extension point). QuantLib's model is a LazyObject that copies the evaluation date and the historic-fixings flag at its calculation and keeps every swap it builds; here the members that read the date or the flag take the EvaluationContext (ForwardRate, SwapRate, and SwapAnnuity, whose swap is built under it), and what one call computes twice it computes once, through a memo that lives for the call: each engine call builds one and passes it through the internal members, and each public member builds its own, so nothing outlives the call (the playbook's Gaussian1d row).

A derived model of another assembly implements NumeraireImpl(double, double, YieldTermStructure?) and ZerobondImpl(double, double, double, YieldTermStructure?), QuantLib's hooks, and sets its state process with SetStateProcess(StochasticProcess1D) while its constructor runs.

A curve given on another clock than the model's own (another day counter or reference date) is read at the instant QuantLib's argument denotes: by the date QuantLib derives a time from, where it has one, and the numeraire time, which has no date, at the instant it denotes on the model's clock; where the curves share a clock, QuantLib's reads and bits stand. QuantLib reads such a curve at the model curve's times: a documented divergence (Divergences/Gaussian1dMismatchedTimesTests.cs).

The price of an option on a zero bond, at a date and a value of the standardized state, by the integration of the payoff's cubic spline over a grid of the state at the expiry.

public double ZerobondOption(OptionType type, Date expiry, Date valueDate, Date maturity, double strike, Date? referenceDate = null, double y = 0, YieldTermStructure? yts = null, double yStdDevs = 7, int yGridPoints = 64, bool extrapolatePayoff = true, bool flatPayoffExtrapolation = false)

Parameters

yts YieldTermStructure

The curve to read instead of the model's; null, QuantLib's empty handle, by default.

The grid's width, in standard deviations; 7 by default.

The grid's points on each side; 64 by default.

Whether past the grid the payoff is flat, at its boundary node's value; false by default.

The option's price.

Remarks

With flatPayoffExtrapolation, the right tail integrates the payoff's last node, where QuantLib's integrates its second-last: a documented divergence (Divergences/FlatPayoffExtrapolationTests.cs). The numeraire at the expiry reads a curve on another clock at the expiry's date.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

type isn't a member of its type, or yGridPoints is negative.
