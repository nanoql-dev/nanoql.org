Remarks

Derivative-free global optimizer. Standard (μ/μ_w, λ)-CMA-ES with positive recombination weights. Reference: N. Hansen, "The CMA Evolution Strategy: A Tutorial", 2016, arXiv:1604.00772.

Immutable: each minimization draws from a new Mersenne Twister built from the seed, so a second minimization repeats the first's draws, where QuantLib's optimizer keeps its generator and continues its stream (Divergences/CmaesTests.cs). The samples are ranked by a stable sort of their costs, so equal costs keep QuantLib's order up to a population of 32 (MSVC's insertion sort) and their own beyond, where MSVC's introsort reorders them. A NaN cost is refused before the ranking, where QuantLib's sort reads it and breaks its ordering.
