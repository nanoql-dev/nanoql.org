Remarks

A Libor's fixing calendar is London's, and its financial-centre calendar the currency's, which its class joins to London's for its value and maturity dates, as QuantLib's does; a EUR Libor's is TARGET. An overnight index and the BMA index fix their convention and end of month, so a preset refuses conventions that change them.
