Remarks

The engine reports the value, delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho and dividend rho. The correctness of the returned value is tested by reproducing results available in literature, and of the Greeks by reproducing numerical derivatives.

QuantLib builds each term's flat curve and constant volatility on the risk-free curve's reference date with the volatility's time from its own (jumpdiffusionengine.cpp:62-67, :113-114); here both are built on the volatility's reference date, so the time and the structures agree: QuantLib's where the two dates coincide (Divergences/JumpDiffusionReferenceDateTests.cs). The terms' curve runs over the volatility's time, so their rho is per unit of its rate; the engine's rho is per unit of the risk-free curve's own rate over its own time, and theta's risk-free term runs over that time, as the analytic European engine's: QuantLib's where the two times agree. With no diffusive volatility the no-jump term's vega weight √(variance/t)/v and its theta's jump term are 0/0 (:110, :123-127); here they are their limits, 1 and 0, the values they take at any positive volatility (Divergences/JumpDiffusionZeroVolatilityTests.cs).
