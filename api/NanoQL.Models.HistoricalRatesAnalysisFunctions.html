HistoricalRatesAnalysis(SequenceStatistics, Date, Date, Period, ReadOnlySpan<InterestRateIndex>, EvaluationContext)

The relative changes of the indexes' fixings between successive dates, from the first business day after the start date to the end date at the given step, added to the statistics, a sample a date after the first; a date whose fixing is missing or refused is skipped.

public static HistoricalRatesAnalysisResults HistoricalRatesAnalysis(SequenceStatistics statistics, Date startDate, Date endDate, Period step, ReadOnlySpan<InterestRateIndex> indexes, EvaluationContext context)

Parameters

statistics SequenceStatistics

The statistics filled.

startDate Date

The start date.

endDate Date

The end date.

step Period

The step between dates, positive.

indexes ReadOnlySpan<InterestRateIndex>

The indexes, at least one; the first's fixing calendar gives the dates.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context, holding the fixings.

Returns

HistoricalRatesAnalysisResults

The skipped dates and their messages.

Remarks

The statistics are reset to the number of indexes and filled in place, as QuantLib's reference argument is; the skipped dates and their messages are returned. The fixings are read in the given context, as QuantLib's read the global settings. QuantLib's catch (std::exception&) catches the NanoQLException and ArgumentException QuantLib's errors map to. A step of no length, on which QuantLib's loop never ends, is refused.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

No index is given, or the step isn't positive.
