Remarks

A working type: it holds its own clone of the pathwise product.

An already-deflated product (a documented divergence): its amounts are in units of the money-market numeraire, so the wrapper divides each by its payment time's money-market deflator on the current state (MarketModelDiscounter in bond 0, the factor the pathwise engine skips for it), giving the ordinary engines an amount to discount. QuantLib's wrapper hands them the deflated amount (multisteppathwisewrapper.cpp:69), which the accounting engine discounts again: wrapped deflated caplets 11.8% below MultiStepOptionlets on the same paths (Divergences/MultiProductPathwiseWrapperDeflatedTests.cs). A product not deflated gives QuantLib's amounts.
