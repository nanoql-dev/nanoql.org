Remarks

The reset schedule's first and last dates are the coupon's accrual start and end. Its rate is its pricer's: the compounding pricer's or the averaging pricer's. The coupon spread is added to the final rate, the rate spread to each fixing.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

The schedule is empty, with QuantLib's text.
