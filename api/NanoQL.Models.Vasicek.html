Remarks

Its arguments are a (positive), b, σ (positive) and λ, in that order, each a constant parameter. QuantLib's a() and b() share their names with the affine A(t, T) and B(t, T), so all four are methods.

A(t, T), B(t, T) and the bond option's variance are QuantLib's master's forms after the pin (#2882), free of cancellation at small a and exact in the limit a → 0, where QuantLib 1.44 cuts to the a = 0 forms below a = √ε and cancels above it. They read std::expm1 (nanoql's own), so their last bits differ from 1.44's at every a.

An extension point, as in QuantLib: Hull-White derives from it.
