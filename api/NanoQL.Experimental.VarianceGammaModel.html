Remarks

Its arguments are σ, ν and θ, in that order, each a constant parameter: σ and ν positive, θ free. Immutable: WithParams(Vector) returns a new model, whose process GenerateArguments() builds from the given process's spot and curves and the new parameters. QuantLib doesn't implement its calibration (no helper prices on it).
