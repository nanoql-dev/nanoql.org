Remarks

Immutable: its value is solved once, at construction, from the guess given (§2.2's quote row), at a discount of 1 and no displacement. QuantLib's quote starts each solve after the first from the last one's result; here a new forward or price is a new quote, solved from its caller's guess, which may be the last quote's Value. A documented divergence: a second solve differs from QuantLib's within the accuracy.

A solve that fails gives 0, by QuantLib's design (its catch (Error&), impliedstddevquote.cpp:54-63): a price out of the formula's range, a negative price, or a solver that finds no root within its evaluations. QuantLib's isValid isn't ported: a value is always valid.
