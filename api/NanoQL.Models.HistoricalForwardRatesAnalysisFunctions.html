HistoricalForwardRatesAnalysis<TTraits, TInterpolator>(SequenceStatistics, Date, Date, Period, InterestRateIndex, Period, Period, ReadOnlySpan<IborIndex>, ReadOnlySpan<SwapIndex>, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, double, TInterpolator)

The relative changes, between successive dates, of the forward rates of the given index at fixed periods ahead, read off a curve bootstrapped on each date from the deposit and swap indexes' fixings, from the first business day after the start date to the end date at the given step, added to the statistics, a sample a date after the first. A date whose fixing is missing or refused is skipped; a date whose curve or forward rate fails is failed.

Remarks

The statistics are reset to the number of forwards and filled in place; the dates skipped and failed, their messages and the fixing periods are returned. Each date is evaluated in a copy of the given context on that date with EnforcesTodaysHistoricFixings true, QuantLib's SavedSettings and global settings.

The curve is bootstrapped afresh on each date (a documented divergence): QuantLib's single curve restarts each date's bootstrap from the last date's solution, so its forwards differ within the curve's accuracy. QuantLib's catch (std::exception&) catches the NanoQLException and ArgumentException QuantLib's errors map to. A step of no length, on which QuantLib's loop over the dates never ends, is refused, as is a forward index of no tenor where the first forward is within the horizon, on which its loop over the fixing periods never ends.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The step isn't positive, or the index's tenor isn't where the initial gap is within the horizon.
