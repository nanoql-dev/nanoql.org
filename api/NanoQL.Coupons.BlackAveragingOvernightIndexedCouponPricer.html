Remarks

The methods that are implemented here to price capped / floored average ON coupons are highly experimental and ad-hoc. As soon as a market best practice has evolved, the pricer should be revised.

It prices averaged coupons only, refusing a compounded one, and averages exactly (with no approximation and no convexity adjustment). Each member computes, inside the call, what QuantLib's initialize keeps: the coupon's swaplet rate and its forward rate.
