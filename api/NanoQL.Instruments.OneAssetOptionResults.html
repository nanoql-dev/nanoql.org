Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib's results class: the results of options derived from OneAssetOption derive from it. QuantLib's Greeks and MoreGreeks are replaced by its members.

Where QuantLib's option throws "<greek> not provided" on reading a Greek its engine left null, the member is null and the caller checks it, as InstrumentResults documents for every result.
