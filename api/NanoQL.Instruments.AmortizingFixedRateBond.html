Remarks

It reads its schedule's tenor first, so a schedule built from dates alone is refused, where FixedRateBond takes no frequency for one, as in QuantLib. It has no single redemption: each notional step but the last is an amortizing payment, the last a redemption, so Redemption() refuses it. AmortizingFixedRateBondFunctions builds a French amortization's schedule and notionals.

The coupons' schedule, whose calendar is the bond's.

The issue date, or null for none.

The ex-coupon period; a zero-length one, the default, means no ex-coupon dates.

exCouponCalendar Calendar

The calendar the ex-coupon period is counted on, or null, QuantLib's empty calendar.

exCouponConvention BusinessDayConvention

The business-day convention of the ex-coupon dates.

exCouponEndOfMonth bool

Whether the ex-coupon dates stay at month end.

redemptions IEnumerable<double>

The payments at the notional steps, in base 100: step i from 1 takes element i, or the last one past the list's end, so element 0 is read only for a one-element list; null for QuantLib's default, 100.

paymentLag int

The business days from each accrual end to its payment date.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

notionals, schedule, coupons or accrualDayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays is negative, or a convention isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date).

NanoQLException

The schedule has no tenor, or is empty, or has one date; no notional or coupon rate is given; or a date is computed on QuantLib's empty calendar where it can't give one.
