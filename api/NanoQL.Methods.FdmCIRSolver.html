The finite-difference solver of a Black-Scholes equity under a Cox-Ingersoll-Ross short rate: FdmCIROp on the problem's grid in the logarithm of the underlying by the short rate, rolled back by the two-dimensional solver, read at a spot and a rate.

Remarks

A working type built per call: computed at construction, where QuantLib's lazy object computes at the first read, and immutable after. With a quanto helper the operator applies its adjustment, where QuantLib's solver takes none (FdmCIROp's remarks).
