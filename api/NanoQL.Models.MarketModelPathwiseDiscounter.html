The number of units of the discretely compounding money-market account a unit paid at a time buys on a step's rates, and its derivative with respect to each forward rate, for the pathwise Greeks: the discount factors of the rate times bracketing the payment, log-linearly interpolated between them.

Remarks

Discounting reads the simulated rates only: to discount back to time 0, multiply by the discount factor of the first rate time.

Immutable: a pure function of a step's discount factors.

The bracket (a documented divergence): the period's start is the last rate time at or before the payment, clamped to the periods, as MarketModelDiscounter's. QuantLib's lower_bound takes the first rate time at or after the payment (pathwisediscounter.cpp:32), so a payment strictly inside a period but the last is extrapolated from the next period (Divergences/MarketModelDiscounterBracketTests.cs). A payment on a rate time, before the first or after the last gives QuantLib's bits.
