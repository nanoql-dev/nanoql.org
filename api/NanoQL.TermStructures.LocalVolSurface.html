Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the local-volatility surface that doesn't refuse derives from it. QuantLib marks the class as untested and probably unreliable.

QuantLib's surface answers its reference date, day counter and maximum date from its Black surface's, which can move; here the reference date and day counter are the Black surface's, passed at construction, since that surface's are fixed. Its calendar is QuantLib's empty one, as QuantLib's surface leaves it. The underlying's level is a number, so QuantLib's constructors taking a quote and a number coincide.

QuantLib reads the risk-free and dividend curves at the Black surface's times (localvolsurface.cpp:82-118), each measuring time from its own reference date in its own day counter. Here a curve on another day counter or reference date is read at the instant the surface's time denotes, on its own clock (between Actual/N day counters exactly, on others through the date, linear within the day); a curve that shares the surface's is read as QuantLib reads it. A documented divergence.
