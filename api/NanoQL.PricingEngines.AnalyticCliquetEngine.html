The analytic engine for a European cliquet option: each period a forward-start option struck at the moneyness times the spot, by Black's formula on the period's forward variance, weighted by the dividend discount to its start.

Remarks

Stateless. Each period's theta is its value times the dividend rate to its start, over which its weight decays, where QuantLib's reads the rate over the period itself, after the start (Divergences/CliquetThetaTests.cs): QuantLib's forward engine's theta, period by period. Each period's vega is under a parallel shift of the input volatilities, its vega in the forward volatility times dσ_f/dε, exactly QuantLib's where the volatilities at the period's ends are equal (Divergences/ForwardVegaTermStructureTests.cs).

Every structure is read by date, accruing nothing before its own date, the rates on the process's clock (Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?)), where QuantLib's reads throw for a reset before a later-dated structure's date (Divergences/ExoticMismatchedTimesTests.cs); a reset before the clock's own date keeps QuantLib's "negative time" refusal; on structures dated alike, QuantLib's reads.
