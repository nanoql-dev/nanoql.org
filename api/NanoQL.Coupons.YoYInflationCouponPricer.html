The base pricer of year-on-year inflation coupons, capped and floored ones included: the swaplet is the gearing times the coupon's fixing plus the spread; a volatility-dependent optionlet needs a derived pricer.

Remarks

An empty handle is null. A price discounts on the nominal curve, at the payment date when it is after the curve's reference date, at 1 otherwise; with no nominal curve, a price refuses "no nominal term structure provided", while a rate needs none.

An optionlet whose fixing date is at or before the volatility's base date pays its intrinsic value on the fixing. QuantLib reads the volatility's base date before checking that there is a volatility, so its "missing optionlet volatility" is unreachable and a missing one throws the empty handle's text; here the check comes first, a documented divergence. An extension point: the Black, unit-displaced Black and Bachelier pricers derive from it.
