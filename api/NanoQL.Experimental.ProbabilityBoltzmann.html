A probability from a Boltzmann distribution: a point is accepted where 1/(1 + e^(−(current − new)/T)) is above a uniform draw, T the largest temperature: QuantLib's ProbabilityBoltzmann.

Remarks

Its draws are std::uniform_real_distribution<Real> on a std::mt19937, as MSVC draws them, on the seed, which is required: QuantLib's default seeds from the clock. A working type.
