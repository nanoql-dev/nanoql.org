Remarks

The exchange receives the notional of the period that ends and pays that of the period that starts, each the constant leg's notional converted at its observation's FX rate: the first exchange has no ending period and the last no starting one. Immutable: SetFxResetPricer(FxResetPricer?) returns a copy holding the pricer.
