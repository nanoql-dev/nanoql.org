Remarks

A working type: its scratch is QuantLib's members, so an instance serves one caller at a time. It copies the pseudo-root.

The derivative (a documented divergence): RatePseudoRootJacobian's, where QuantLib's sums the drift's terms without their accrual periods and carries f_j, not f_j + d_j (ratepseudorootjacobian.cpp:280, :294, :298).
