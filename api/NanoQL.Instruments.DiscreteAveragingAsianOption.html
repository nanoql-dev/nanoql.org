Remarks

Immutable. Two constructors, as in QuantLib: one taking the running sum or product of the past fixings and their number beside the future fixing dates; one taking every fixing date with the past fixings' values, which an engine splits by the evaluation context's date, the dates before it past (QuantLib's setupArguments on its global evaluation date). Both sort their fixing dates, a stable sort, where QuantLib's second constructor keeps the caller's order and its engines then read unsorted dates (Divergences/AsianUnsortedDatesTests.cs); the past values are taken by count, as QuantLib takes them.

A geometric option's past fixings are carried as QuantLib's running product and, beside it, their log-sum, which the geometric engines read where the product leaves double's range: 120 past fixings at 1,000 multiply to ∞, where QuantLib's analytic engine prices ∞ (Divergences/GeometricAsianProductRangeTests.cs).
