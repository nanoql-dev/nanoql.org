Remarks

Immutable, its mapping function computed once, on the first call of G(), as QuantLib's lazy object computes it. The collocation points are the Gauss nodes of the kernel's non-central χ² distribution (GaussNonCentralChiSquaredPolynomial, exact), their range clamped by the pMin and pMax quantiles and rescaled by QuantLib's map a·x + (xMin − x.front()), a = (xMax − xMin)/(x.back() − x.front()); where only the top bound clamps, as at every date of QuantLib's two cases, the points scale toward zero by a and the top one stops short of the pMax quantile, QuantLib's design, kept. Where that map's lowest point falls below the pMin quantile, breaking the lower bound the method states, it is raised by the shortfall: QuantLib's map with its bits wherever its lowest point meets the bound, the affine map onto [xMin, xMax] where the low bound clamps (Divergences/SquareRootCLVRescaleTests.cs).

The non-centrality 4κe^(−κt)x₀/(σ²(1 − e^(−κt))) takes 1 − e^(−κt) as −expm1(−κt) below κt 1e-2, where QuantLib's cancels, and QuantLib's form from it (Divergences/SquareRootCLVNonCentralityTests.cs). The mapping interpolates linearly in time between the maturities; past the last one QuantLib's reads its map's end before its check, and here refuses with QuantLib's text (Conventions/CLVModelTests.cs).
