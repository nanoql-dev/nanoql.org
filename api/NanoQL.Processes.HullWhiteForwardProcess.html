Remarks

The drift's and α's terms are written as HullWhiteProcess's; B(t, T) = (1 − e^(−a(T − t)))/a as (T − t)·(1 − e^(−a(T − t)))/(a(T − t)); and M_T(s, t, T), QuantLib's σ²/a²[(1 − e^(−u)) − ½(e^(−v) − e^(−v−2u))], u = a(t − s), v = a(T − t), as σ²(t − s)f(u)[(T − t)f(v) + ½e^(−v)(t − s)f(u)], f(x) = (1 − e^(−x))/x, every term positive: a divergence, QuantLib's cutting at QL_EPSILON and cancelling to O(σ²) from terms of order σ²(t − s)/a above it, its half-year expectation −5.55e9 at a = 1e-15 (Divergences/HullWhiteForwardProcessSmallReversionTests.cs).

QuantLib's constructor leaves the forward measure time unset; WithForwardMeasureTime(double) gives a copy at one, and the drift and the expectation refuse to read an unset one.
