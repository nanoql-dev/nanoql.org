The simulation of an EventSet: its paths are the set's events in successive periods as long as the simulated one, from the first such period starting in the set's first year, shifted to the simulated period, until the set runs out.

Remarks

A period starting or ending on 29 February in a year without one takes 28 February, QuantLib's own clamp for a date plus years, where QuantLib's constructor throws ("day outside month") (Divergences/EventSetTests.cs).
