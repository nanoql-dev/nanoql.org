Remarks

Immutable: Compute() returns the fitted calibration, where QuantLib's compute() changes the object; before it the parameters are the guesses, and the weights 1/n.

One of QuantLib's refusals is narrowed: QuantLib's transformation leaves b free, so its optimizer can step to a trial point the abcd function's validation refuses, and the fit throws. Here such a trial point's residuals are 1.0 each, a guard that keeps every fit QuantLib completes bit for bit, since none of them reaches such a point. A fit on which the guard acted is returned where it ends inside the valid region and refused with QuantLib's text, its first invalid trial point's, where it ends against the zero floor: its lowest instantaneous volatility below 1% of the smallest Black volatility given, as for data whose total variance shrinks, which no valid curve fits.

One of QuantLib's defects is fixed: its transformation gives a as a positive a + d less d, so with a fixed and d free it fixes a + d, and the fit moves a by what it moves d. Here a fixed keeps its guess, and d stays above max(0, −a), the same region; every other choice of fixed parameters is QuantLib's.
