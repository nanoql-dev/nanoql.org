Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's. The bivariate normal is QuantLib's default, BivariateCumulativeNormalDistributionWe04DP, where QuantLib's engine reads Drezner's 1978 algorithm, to six decimal places (Divergences/TwoAssetCorrelationBivariateTests.cs). The second asset's volatility is read at its own strike X₂, where QuantLib reads it at X₁ (Divergences/TwoAssetCorrelationSmileTests.cs). At X₂ < 0 the call is linear in the second asset where the first is in the money and the put worth nothing, where QuantLib takes the log of a negative ratio (Divergences/TwoAssetCorrelationNegativeStrikeTests.cs).

Each asset's carry reads its own process's curves and the payoff is discounted on the first process's risk-free curve, where QuantLib takes the rate from the first process alone (Divergences/TwoAssetTwoRiskFreeCurvesTests.cs). Every rate and volatility is read over the time to expiry on the clock across both processes (NanoQL.Methods.FdmBlackScholesOp.ClockAcross(System.Collections.Generic.IReadOnlyList{NanoQL.Processes.GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess})), each structure at the instants that time denotes, accruing nothing before its date, where QuantLib reads each volatility at its own process's time and everything else at the second process's; a structure sharing the clock's times is read as QuantLib reads it (Divergences/TwoAssetMismatchedTimesTests.cs).
