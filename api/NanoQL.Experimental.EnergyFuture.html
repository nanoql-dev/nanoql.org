Remarks

The value is (quote − trade price) × quantity × lots × buy-sell, in the base currency and unit, less the secondary costs. The quote is the index's stored fixing when its last quote date is the evaluation date or the day before, its forward price at the evaluation date otherwise (with QuantLib's warning). The future never expires, as QuantLib's.

Three of QuantLib's lines are fixed (Divergences/EnergyFutureTests.cs):

  • The lot multiplies the quantity once: QuantLib's conversion factor already carries it and the value multiplies by it again, so lot 1,000 prices 5,000,000 there for 5,000 here;
  • The secondary costs are charged on the quantity the value uses, in the base unit with its lots, where QuantLib's reads the raw amount (a fee of 0.1 a unit on 10 gallons reads 42 there, 1 here);
  • Within QuantLib's window the quote is the stored history's entry for the evaluation date where there is one (QuantLib's), else the day before's, read from the history itself: QuantLib reads the evaluation date's fixing alone, so with the last fixing yesterday it refuses ("missing quote"), and through the index's fixing, which refuses a Saturday evaluation on a weekday index. Neither entry: QuantLib's "missing quote".
