Remarks

Method, by |x|:

  1. [0, 0.84375): erf(x) = x + x·R(x²), R = P/Q, P an odd polynomial of degree 8 and Q of degree 10; |R − (erf(x) − x)/x| ≤ 2^−57.90.
  2. [0.84375, 1.25): with s = |x| − 1 and c = 0.84506291151 rounded to single precision, erf(x) = sign(x)·(c + P1(s)/Q1(s)), P1 and Q1 of degree 6; |P1/Q1 − (erf(|x|) − c)| ≤ 2^−59.06.
  3. [1.25, 1/0.35): erf(x) = 1 − (1/x)·exp(−x² − 0.5625 + R1/S1), R1 of degree 7 and S1 of degree 8 in z = 1/x²; |R1/S1 − f(x)| < 2^−62.57.
  4. [1/0.35, 6): erf(x) = sign(x)·(1 − (1/x)·exp(−x² − 0.5625 + R2/S2)), R2 of degree 6 and S2 of degree 7 in z = 1/x²; |R2/S2 − f(x)| < 2^−61.52.
  5. [6, ∞): erf(x) = sign(x)·(1 − tiny), which is exactly ±1.

erf(0) = 0, erf(±∞) = ±1, and erf(NaN) is NaN.
