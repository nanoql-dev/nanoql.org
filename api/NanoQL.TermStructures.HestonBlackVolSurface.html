Remarks

Immutable; it builds an analytic engine per query, as QuantLib's does. Its reference date is the model's origin, the earlier of its curves' reference dates, and its times are on the risk-free curve's day counter from there, each curve read at the instant a time denotes, where QuantLib's runs from the risk-free curve's reference date and reads the dividend curve at the risk-free curve's times; a market whose dividend curve shares the risk-free curve's reference date and day counter keeps QuantLib's values. Where the model's price isn't positive the volatility is √θ, QuantLib's guard.
