Remarks

QuantLib's swap stores these from its own results class, or derives them from the swap's results; here they are members of the results FetchResults(InstrumentResults) returns. The fixed leg is the first leg and the year-on-year leg the second.

A result QuantLib refuses to read ("result not available") is null: the fair rate when the fixed leg's BPS is zero or unknown, the fair spread likewise for the year-on-year leg, both for an expired swap, and a leg's NPV when it is unknown. Equality compares the per-leg arrays element by element, and the fair rate and spread by value.
