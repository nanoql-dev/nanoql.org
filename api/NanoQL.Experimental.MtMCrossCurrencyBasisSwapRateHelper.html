Remarks

A helper for a cross-currency swap with a resetting notional: at each accrual-period boundary the notional on the mark-to-market leg is reset to reflect changes in the FX rate, reducing the counterparty and FX risk of the structure. For more details see N. Moreni, A. Pallavicini (2015), FX Modelling in Collateralized Markets: foreign measures, basis curves and pricing formulae.

The coupon payment lag applies to both legs; notional exchanges remain on the effective and maturity dates. In both frequency parameters, NoFrequency is read as none.
