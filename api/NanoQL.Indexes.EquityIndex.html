Remarks

The equity index object allows to retrieve past fixings, as well as project future fixings using either both the risk free interest rate term structure and the dividend term structure, or just the interest rate term structure in which case one can provide a term structure of equity forwards implied from, e.g. option prices.

In case of the first method, the forward is calculated as I(t, T) = I(t, t) P_D(t, T) / P_R(t, T), where I(t, t) is today's value of the index, P_D(t, T) is a discount factor of the dividend curve at future time T, and P_R(t, T) is a discount factor of the risk free curve at future time T. In case of the latter method, the forward is calculated as I(t, T) = I(t, t) / P_F(t, T), where P_F(t, T) is a discount factor of the equity forward term structure.

To forecast future fixings, the user can provide the current index spot. If the spot is null, today's fixing will be used instead.

Each discount factor is read from the evaluation date t: P(t, T) is the curve's discount at T over its discount at t, nothing accruing before the curve's own reference date. QuantLib reads each curve's discount at T from its own reference date (equityindex.cpp:84-88), which is the formula's only where the curve is dated on or after the evaluation date; on such a curve the forecast is QuantLib's bit for bit, and on a curve dated before it a documented divergence.
