Remarks

The underlying may be a VanillaSwap or an OvernightIndexedSwap; most engines would treat an overnight-indexed swap as a vanilla one, which is at best a decent proxy, and BlackSwaptionEngine fully supports it. A swaption has no payoff, and its results are an engine's InstrumentResults.

The settlement type and method are checked against each other when the swaption is priced, as QuantLib's arguments check them, not when it is built.

Remarks

QuantLib's helper runs the engine's body alone, without the swaption's checks: a physical swaption with a cash method prices as a physical one, and a cash swaption with a physical method fails in the engine. Each evaluation prices afresh, where QuantLib's helper reuses its last result while the volatility is unchanged.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

context or discountCurve is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

maxEvaluations is negative, or type isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The accuracy isn't positive, the bracket is empty, or the guess is outside it.

NanoQLException

The swaption has expired or isn't European, the price can't be bracketed, the solver exceeds its evaluations, or the engine fails.
