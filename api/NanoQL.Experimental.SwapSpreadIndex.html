Remarks

The two indexes must share their fixing days and calendar, currency, day counter, and fixed leg's tenor and convention; the index takes the first one's tenor, fixing days, currency, calendar and day counter. Its name is QuantLib's, "<name₁>(<gearing₁>) + <name₂>(<gearing₂>)", each gearing to four decimals. It stores no fixings of its own: a past fixing is the two indexes' past fixings combined. Immutable. Experimental, as in QuantLib.
