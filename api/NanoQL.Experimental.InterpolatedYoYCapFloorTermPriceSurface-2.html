Remarks

At each maturity the at-the-money swap rate is the strike where the interpolated cap and floor prices meet, searched in steps of 0.5% from the last floor strike and solved by Brent to 1e-7; a maturity with no meeting point within 2%, or one below the arbitrage-free bound, takes the bound plus 0.1% (or the bounds' midpoint), and past five years either refuses. The year-on-year curve is bootstrapped linearly on year-on-year swap helpers every year from the nominal curve's reference date to the last maturity, each quoting the swap rate at its maturity, then checked to reprice them to 1e-5. Everything is computed at construction (Create(int, Period, YoYInflationIndex, CPIInterpolationType, YieldTermStructure, DayCounter, Calendar, BusinessDayConvention, IReadOnlyList<double>, IReadOnlyList<double>, IReadOnlyList<Period>, Matrix, Matrix, EvaluationContext, TInterpolator2D, TInterpolator1D)), the curve's helpers dated and priced in the context given, as QuantLib computes at construction and again on a change.

Two fixes (Divergences/YoYCapFloorPriceSurfaceTests.cs): each swap rate's date is its own maturity's option date, where QuantLib's takes the date of the maturity counted among the valid ones, another maturity's once one fails the intersection (yoycapfloortermpricesurface.hpp:506-508); and the repricing check reads each helper's own maturity, where QuantLib's reads the surface's reference date plus whole years while the helpers sit on the nominal curve's. With every maturity valid and the two reference dates equal, both are QuantLib's.
