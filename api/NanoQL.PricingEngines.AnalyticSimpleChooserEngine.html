Remarks

Stateless. QuantLib evaluates Rubinstein's formula on the zero rates and the volatility at maturity, its times from the evaluation date, so it is exact only on flat markets; here the option is C(K, T) + D_q(t, T)·P(K·D_r(t, T) / D_q(t, T), t), put-call parity at the choosing date t, each priced by AnalyticEuropeanEngine on the process: exact under deterministic rates, dividends and volatility (Divergences/SimpleChooserTermStructureTests.cs), QuantLib's formula's value on a flat market within its last bits. Each structure is read on its own times, so QuantLib's refusals of day counters that differ, which its formula's single time needed, don't apply.

QuantLib's refusals of a spot, a strike or a volatility at maturity that isn't positive, and of a choosing date on or before the evaluation date, are kept.
