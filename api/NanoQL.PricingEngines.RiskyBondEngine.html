Remarks

The engine prices a bond on a default-probability curve and a recovery rate: each cash flow's amount is weighted by the probability of survival to its date and discounted; in each coupon period, from the later of the curve's reference date and the leg's start, the coupon's nominal times the recovery rate is weighted by the probability of default in the period and discounted from its midpoint. The settlement value counts the cash flows after the settlement date, discounted to it.

A static class: Calculate(Bond, EvaluationContext, DefaultProbabilityTermStructure, double, YieldTermStructure) takes the engine's inputs and returns the results, in place of QuantLib's engine object, as DiscountingBondEngine does. An expired bond's results are SetupExpired()'s.
