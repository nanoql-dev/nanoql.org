Remarks

The helper takes the spot days, calendar, convention, end of month, collateral side and trading calendar, but no currencies, its quotes those of whatever pair the curves are, and no payment calendar; the forward takes the currencies, the spot days as its settlement days and the payment calendar, and none of the others: a record giving a field its route can't take is refused there.
