Remarks

Near β = 1 the formula fails: δ = (1 − 2β)/(1 − β) divides by zero at β = 1, and around it Boost's non-central chi-squared refuses or runs for seconds. For |β − 1| < 1e-3 the value is Black's formula at Hagan's CEV-equivalent volatility, the SABR volatility at ν = 0 (UnsafeSabrLogNormalVolatility(double, double, double, double, double, double, double)), which is α at β = 1 and within 4.6e-7 of the CEV formula at the band's edge, where QuantLib throws or hangs. A documented divergence (Divergences/CevNearOneTests.cs).

Where a tail QuantLib takes as 1 − cdf (or Γ-ratio − cdf, analyticcevengine.cpp:50-80) is below 1e-3, the difference loses most of its digits: a 36-day call at a strike of 150 comes out about 20% low. There the tail is Boost's complement cdf (and gamma_q); QuantLib's arithmetic and bits elsewhere. A documented divergence (Divergences/CevTailTests.cs).

At a time of 0 the formula's non-centrality x₀/t is infinite, which Boost refuses; the value there is the payoff at f0, the limit QuantLib's own values approach as the time shrinks. A documented divergence (a run find, Divergences/CevAtExpiryTests.cs).
