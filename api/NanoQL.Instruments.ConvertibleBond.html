Remarks

Its engine (BinomialConvertibleEngine) reads what QuantLib's setupArguments computes for the settlement date of the context it prices under: the callabilities not yet occurred there, their clean prices made dirty with the bond's accrued amount at the call date, and each one's trigger, a soft call's own or none. A plain bond's engine (DiscountingBondEngine) refuses a convertible bond, as QuantLib's refuses it when it sets up its arguments, with "wrong argument type".

Most members inherited from Bond, such as the yield and the yield-based prices, refer to the underlying plain-vanilla bond and don't take convertibility and callability into account, as QuantLib warns of its own.

Unsealed, an extension point, as QuantLib's base is: the zero-coupon, fixed-coupon and floating-rate convertibles derive from it. Its protected constructor follows Bond's construction contract: a derived constructor writes the cash flows and builds the redemption, QuantLib's notional of 100.

Remarks

QuantLib checks the last callability's date only, not the latest's, as here. Its exercise and its conversion ratio are checked when the bond is priced, as QuantLib's are, but the exercise's null, which is refused here.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The exercise, the callabilities or the schedule is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays is negative.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date); a callability is null; or the last callability is after the maturity date.

NanoQLException

The schedule is empty.
