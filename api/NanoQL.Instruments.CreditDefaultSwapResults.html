Remarks

A result QuantLib leaves at Null<Real>(), whose accessor refuses it ("… not available"), is null: the fair spread where the coupon leg is worth nothing, the fair upfront where the upfront has occurred, the coupon-leg BPS for a zero running spread, the upfront BPS without an upfront. Equality is by value.
