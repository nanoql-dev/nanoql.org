Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's. Strikes outside the corridor integrate only over terminal spots inside it, upstream's fix after v1.44 (#2881; Divergences/BarrierUpstreamFixesTests.cs).

Documented divergences. Each rate is read on the volatility's clock and each time is the volatility's, as AnalyticBarrierEngine reads them, where QuantLib reads every structure at the risk-free curve's times. The series runs from −series to series, QuantLib's terms, and on while the next pair of terms exceeds machine epsilon of the sum, where QuantLib stops, unconverged on narrow corridors and long terms (Divergences/DoubleBarrierSeriesTests.cs). Where a term's power overflows, as at low volatility, it is taken in logs with its normal difference, QuantLib's own technique in the binary barrier engine, where QuantLib's product is NaN and its max(0, ·) prices 0; each difference of two normal probabilities whose arguments share a sign is taken from the small tail, ½erfc, where the plain difference cancels (Divergences/DoubleBarrierLowVolatilityTests.cs). QuantLib's value and bits stand wherever its five terms agree with this converged series within 1e-12 on the value's scale, max(|value|, 1), which QuantLib's own rounding of the plain sum's cancelling terms stays inside, QuantLib's cases among them. Below machine-epsilon variance the underlying follows its forward, a knock-out paying its payoff where the forward stays inside the corridor. And the rebate is priced, QuantLib's single-barrier convention, where QuantLib's engine reads none: a knock-out's paid at the first touch of either barrier, through the double-barrier binary engine's KIKO and KOKI at unit cash; a knock-in's paid at expiry where neither is touched, through its knock-out at unit cash (Divergences/DoubleBarrierRebateTests.cs).

The formula is a first-passage closed form in equivalent-constant rates and a volatility, the variance and discounts to expiry: no exact closed form exists for a first passage under general deterministic term structures, and on shaped markets an independent Monte Carlo finds a knock-out within two of its standard errors, +0.04%. It is exact where (r − q)/σ² is constant over the option's life and the volatility depends on time alone, the price then a time change of a constant market's; a knock-out's rebate, paid at the first touch, where r/σ² and q/σ² are each constant (Conventions/ClosedFormExactnessTests.cs). The term-structure-exact route is a Monte Carlo on the process, which monitors the barriers at its grid's dates.
