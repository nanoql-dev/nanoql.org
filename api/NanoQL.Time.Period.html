Remarks

Equality follows QuantLib's and never throws: every zero-length period equals every other; other periods compare normalised (years as months, weeks as days); a pair whose order QuantLib can't decide (1M against 30D) compares unequal, where QuantLib's == throws. Ordering follows QuantLib's and throws NanoQLException on such a pair.
