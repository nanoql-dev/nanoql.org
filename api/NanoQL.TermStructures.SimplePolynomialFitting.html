Remarks

Constrained at zero, c_0 is 1 and the parameters are the other coefficients. The powers are the Bernstein polynomials B_{i,i}(t) = t^i (BernsteinPolynomial), Pow(double, double)'s as QuantLib's are std::pow's. QuantLib's constructor without the optimization method is the first constructor with l2 named.
