An Italian CCTEU (Certificato di Credito del Tesoro): a floating-rate bond on six-month Euribor plus a spread, with two settlement days, a face of 100 and a semiannual schedule ending on its maturity date.

Remarks

Its accrued amount is rounded to five decimals, as QuantLib's is; the bond's clean price and yield read it.

Parameters

maturityDate Date

The maturity date.

spread double

The spread over six-month Euribor.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context: the schedule starts from its evaluation date when no start date is given, and the coupons read its at-par switch.

fwdCurve YieldTermStructure

The curve six-month Euribor is forecast on, or null, QuantLib's empty handle.

startDate Date?

The schedule's start date, or null: whole years back from the maturity date, past the evaluation date.

issueDate Date?

The issue date, or null for none.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

context is null.

ArgumentException

A date is default(Date), or no start date is given and the maturity date isn't after the evaluation date (QuantLib's "null effective date").
