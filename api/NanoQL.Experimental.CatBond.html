Remarks

On the bond contract's protected constructor: a derived bond writes the leg, as FloatingCatBond does. An engine for plain bonds refuses it, as QuantLib's does with "wrong arguments type"; its engine is MonteCarloCatBondEngine, whose results carry the loss statistics QuantLib's bond keeps. An expired bond's statistics are null, which QuantLib's leaves at their last values. An extension point, as in QuantLib.
