The Bachelier calculator: the value and sensitivities of a striked payoff on a forward with a normal distribution of a given standard deviation (in price units), discounted. It supports plain vanilla, cash-or-nothing, asset-or-nothing and gap payoffs.

Remarks

Three of QuantLib's results are fixed, documented divergences:

  • QuantLib tells a call from a put by alpha >= 0 (bacheliercalculator.cpp:102-114, :183-188), which a put's N(d) − 1 meets where N(d) rounds to 1, so a put far out of the money prices as the call; here the payoff's type decides (Divergences/BachelierPutBranchTests.cs).
  • QuantLib's visitor sets alpha, beta and x for cash-or-nothing, asset-or-nothing and gap payoffs, which its value and Greeks never read, so each prices as the plain vanilla; here the value is D·(F·α + x·β + σ·∂α/∂d) and the Greeks its derivatives, Bachelier's closed forms for each payoff (Divergences/BachelierPayoffTests.cs). The plain vanilla's are QuantLib's expressions.
  • QuantLib's theta reads the spot gamma where the time decay reads the forward gamma, missing (S/F)²; its vanna, the sensitivity of the vega to the spot by its documentation, misses D·F/S; its volga divides by σ√T where the derivative in σ divides by σ, missing √T. Each is here the derivative, the vanna taking the spot as Vanna(double, double) does (Divergences/BachelierGreeksTests.cs).

Immutable. At a standard deviation within QL_EPSILON the density terms are 0, as QuantLib's: the value is the discounted payoff at the forward, the gammas, vega, vanna and volga 0.
