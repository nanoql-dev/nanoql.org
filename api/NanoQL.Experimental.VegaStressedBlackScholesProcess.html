Remarks

The diffusion inside the box [lower time, upper time] × [lower asset, upper asset], ends included, is the process's plus the stress level. QuantLib's setters, which change the process in place, return a copy here.

With a nonzero stress the standard deviation, the variance and the step go through the discretization, QuantLib's own route for a strike-dependent volatility, so the stressed diffusion reaches them; QuantLib's take the exact forms from the term structures on a strike-independent volatility, which read no diffusion, so the stress never reaches a step there (Divergences/VegaStressedProcessTests.cs). The expectation keeps QuantLib's exact value from the curves, the mean of the asset reading no volatility, where the discretized route refuses ("not implemented"). With no stress the process is QuantLib's.
