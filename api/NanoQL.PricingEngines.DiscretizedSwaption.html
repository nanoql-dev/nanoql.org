Remarks

A working type (DiscretizedAsset). Date adjustments can get the swap's times out of step with the exercise times, which could misprice the swaption, so the swap's schedule dates in the week before an exercise date (but the last) are snapped forward to it, for each exercise date in turn, a later one comparing against the dates earlier ones snapped; a coupon whose date is snapped is adjusted in the swap's post-adjustment. The underlying is a VanillaSwap on the snapped schedules, a NullCalendar's, and on the swap's index (an overnight-indexed swap's index too), so its Ibor coupons read the context's at-par switch.

The snapping, fixed: QuantLib also snaps a date in the week after an exercise date back to it (discretizedswaption.cpp:93-111), so a swap starting on its usual two-day lag after the exercise is valued from the exercise date, its first coupon accruing days it doesn't; and its snapped swap pays every coupon on its NullCalendar schedule's dates (:113-119), without the swap's payment calendar, convention or lag. Here no date after an exercise date is snapped, and each coupon pays on the swap's own payment date, a snapped one on the swap's payment rule (its payment calendar, lag and convention) applied to its snapped date; with QuantLib's checks, Jamshidian's and the finite-difference engines and QuantLib 1.25's cached tree values (Divergences/TreeSwaptionSnappingTests.cs). A swap without a lag on business-day schedules keeps QuantLib's values wherever nothing snaps.

Its Reset(int) initializes the underlying at the last payment time on the swaption's lattice, then resets the option.
