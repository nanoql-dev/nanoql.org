Remarks

The integration's cut-off is set by LinearTsrPricerSettings: explicit lower and upper bounds; the strike where a vanilla swaption has a given ratio (1%) of the at-the-money swaption's vega; the strike where the undeflated payer or receiver price falls below a threshold; or a number of standard deviations of a Black-Scholes process at the at-the-money volatility. The bounds are applied in every case: on a shifted lognormal section to the strike plus the shift, so a zero lower bound is the shifted model's lowest rate; on a normal one the lower bound is min(−upper, lower), unless the bounds are given explicitly.

Immutable: each public member computes, inside the call, what QuantLib's initialize(coupon) stores in the pricer, and a capped or collared coupon's rate reads its three rates from one such state. WithMeanReversion(double) and WithSwaptionVolatility(SwaptionVolatilityStructure?) return copies, where QuantLib's setters replace its quote and handle in place; the day counter of the model's slope is the volatility's the pricer holds, where QuantLib's keeps its constructor's volatility's after setSwaptionVolatility.

QuantLib's fixes here: the price-threshold strategy searches with its threshold, from the bracket's midpoint, where QuantLib's passes the vega ratio and a guess at the bracket's end, which Brent refuses, so every threshold gave the default bounds' price; the standard-deviations strategy sets each option's own bound, its lognormal width k·σ·√T and the shift subtracted once; below a reversion of 1e-4 the model's shape is −expm1(−a·τ)/a, τ at 0, where QuantLib's reads every such reversion as 0; on a lognormal section and a forward above −shift a caplet struck at or below −shift is the coupon's expected rate less the strike and a floorlet there 0, where QuantLib's refuses, a forward at or below −shift keeping QuantLib's refusal; every section is wrapped at the coupon's forward, where QuantLib's keeps a section's own at-the-money level, which on the SABR and ZABR cubes is an interpolated forward; and the vega-ratio search reads a normal section's vega from Bachelier's derivative, where QuantLib's section refuses it.
