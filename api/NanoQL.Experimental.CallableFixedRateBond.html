Remarks

It is built through Bond's construction contract, on the schedule's calendar, so its issue date isn't checked against the first payment, as in QuantLib.

Parameters

settlementDays int

The settlement days.

faceAmount double

The notional.

schedule Schedule

The coupons' schedule, whose last date is the maturity date and whose calendar settles.

coupons IEnumerable<double>

The coupon rates, simple and annual; the last one repeats for any further coupons.

accrualDayCounter DayCounter

The coupons' day counter, and the payments'.

paymentConvention BusinessDayConvention

The business-day convention of the payment dates.

redemption double

The redemption price, in base 100.

issueDate Date?

The issue date, or null for none.

putCallSchedule IEnumerable<Callability>

The calls and puts, copied; none by default.

exCouponPeriod Period

The ex-coupon period; a zero-length one, the default, means no ex-coupon dates.

exCouponCalendar Calendar

The calendar the ex-coupon period is counted on, or null, QuantLib's empty calendar.

exCouponConvention BusinessDayConvention

The business-day convention of the ex-coupon dates.

exCouponEndOfMonth bool

Whether the ex-coupon dates stay at month end.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

schedule, coupons or accrualDayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays is negative, or a convention isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date); a callability is null, or after the maturity date.

NanoQLException

The schedule is empty (QuantLib reads its last date unchecked), or has one date; no coupon rate is given; or a date is computed on QuantLib's empty calendar where it can't give one.
