The American engine based on the QD+ approximation to the exercise boundary (M. Li, "Analytical Approximations for the Critical Stock Prices of American Options: A Performance Comparison", 2009): the boundary at Chebyshev nodes in √τ, the early-exercise premium integrated over it by tanh-sinh quadrature.

Remarks

Its main purpose is to provide a good initial guess to the exercise boundary for the superior fixed-point engine, QdFpAmericanEngine. The engine reports the value.

Immutable: each τ's boundary is solved by a working evaluator built for that solve, and each price builds its own boundary, as QuantLib's does. The static Calculate builds an engine with the given settings and prices through it. An extension point, as in QuantLib.
