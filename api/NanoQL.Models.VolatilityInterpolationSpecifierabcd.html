The volatility interpolation of synthetic short rates on abcd curves: each long rate's a, b and d scaled, each short rate between two long ones on the mean of their a, b, c and d, those before the first and after the last on the nearest long rate's, the last rescaled to match the last caplet's volatility.

Remarks

Immutable: WithScalingFactors(ReadOnlySpan<double>) and WithLastCapletVol(double) return a new specifier, where QuantLib's setters change it in place.

One departure from QuantLib (Divergences/VolatilityInterpolationSpecifierOffsetTests.cs): a short rate between two long rates resets at its own index, i + j·period + offset, where QuantLib's resets at i + j·period, an earlier rate's, at an offset other than 0 (volatilityinterpolationspecifierabcd.cpp:169-170). A period of 0, which QuantLib divides by, no long rates, whose last QuantLib reads, no short rate times, and long rates' times past the short ones, which QuantLib reads past, are refused.
