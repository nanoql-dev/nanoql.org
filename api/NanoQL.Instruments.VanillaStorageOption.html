A storage option: the right, at each of its Bermudan exercise dates, to inject or withdraw up to the change rate into a storage of the given capacity, starting from the given load.

Remarks

Immutable; its payoff a NullPayoff, as QuantLib's. Its engines are experimental and not yet ported; the instrument carries QuantLib's terms and checks.
