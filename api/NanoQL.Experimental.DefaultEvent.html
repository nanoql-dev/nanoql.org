A credit event on the bonds of a seniority and currency, with its optional settlement: a realized event, of an atomic type, which the bonds of that seniority and currency suffer together.

Remarks

Immutable, and an extension point (FailureToPayEvent, BankruptcyEvent). An event that affects every seniority is a set of events of the same type, or one settled for NoSeniority.

Equality is QuantLib's: two events are the same, whatever their settlements, when their currencies, types, dates and seniorities are. It is sealed. An issuer keeps every event it is given, equal ones included (Issuer).
