public FixedRateBondHelper(double price, int settlementDays, double faceAmount, Schedule schedule, IEnumerable<double> coupons, DayCounter dayCounter, EvaluationContext context, BusinessDayConvention paymentConv = BusinessDayConvention.Following, double redemption = 100, Date? issueDate = null, Calendar? paymentCalendar = null, Period exCouponPeriod = default, Calendar? exCouponCalendar = null, BusinessDayConvention exCouponConvention = BusinessDayConvention.Unadjusted, bool exCouponEndOfMonth = false, BondPriceType priceType = BondPriceType.Clean)

Parameters

price double

The bond's quoted price, per 100 of notional.

settlementDays int

The bond's settlement days.

faceAmount double

The bond's notional.

schedule Schedule

The coupons' schedule.

coupons IEnumerable<double>

The coupon rates, simple and annual.

dayCounter DayCounter

The coupons' day counter.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context, which gives the earliest date.

paymentConv BusinessDayConvention

The business-day convention of the payment dates.

redemption double

The redemption price, in base 100.

issueDate Date?

The issue date, or null for none.

paymentCalendar Calendar

The calendar of the payment dates and of the settlement, or null for the schedule's.

exCouponPeriod Period

The ex-coupon period; a zero-length one, the default, means no ex-coupon dates.

exCouponCalendar Calendar

The calendar the ex-coupon period is counted on, or null, QuantLib's empty calendar.

exCouponConvention BusinessDayConvention

The business-day convention of the ex-coupon dates.

exCouponEndOfMonth bool

Whether the ex-coupon dates stay at month end.

priceType BondPriceType

Whether the price is clean or dirty.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

context, schedule, coupons or dayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays is negative, or an enum argument isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date).

NanoQLException

The bond can't be built.
