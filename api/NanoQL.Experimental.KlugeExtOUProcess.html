Kluge's power model beside a correlated extended Ornstein-Uhlenbeck gas model: the power state (X, Y) of an ExtOUWithJumpsProcess, and the gas log-price U, dU = κ(μ(t) − U)dt + σdW_U, its Brownian motion correlated with X's by ρ.

Remarks

Immutable; three state variables (X, Y, U) and four factors (the power process's three, then U's own draw, mixed with X's by ρ). The in-place Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) allocates nothing.

Documented divergence (Divergences/KlugeExtOUDiffusionTests.cs): QuantLib's diffusion writes the gas row at row size() and column factors(), one past each end of its size() × factors() matrix, so its gas row is 0 and the writes corrupt the heap; here the gas row is the last, ρσ in the first column and √(1 − ρ²)σ in the last, so the diffusion's square is the covariance.
