The integral engine for synthetic CDOs: each premium period integrated in steps of a given size from the later of its start and the evaluation date, the premium paid on the tranche's notional less the expected loss at each step.

Remarks

A static class: Calculate(SyntheticCDO, EvaluationContext, YieldTermStructure, Period?) takes the engine's inputs and returns the results. It reads the statistics through the CDO's basket, which must be of the engine's evaluation date. Premium accrual on default isn't paid, as in QuantLib's.

The legs and the upfront are levered by the CDO's leverage factor, which QuantLib's engine ignores, so a tranche at twice its notional reads an unchanged value; the fair premium and fair upfront are the unlevered rates, the additional "fairPremium" the instrument's, the fair upfront side-invariant (Divergences/CdoLeverageTests.cs). A basket with realized defaults is priced on the tranche's outstanding notional, and a discount before the curve's reference date is 1, as MidPointCDOEngine's.
