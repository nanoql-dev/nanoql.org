The factory of FlatVol models on a yield curve: the initial rates the curve's simple forwards, the volatilities interpolated linearly in the rates' fixing times and displaced, and the exponential correlation made time-homogeneous.

Remarks

Immutable: it holds its curve, and Create(EvolutionDescription, int) returns a new model. QuantLib's factory observes its curve's handle; here a new curve is a new factory.
