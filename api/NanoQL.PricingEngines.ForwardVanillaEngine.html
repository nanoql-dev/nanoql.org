Remarks

An extension point, as QuantLib's template: ForwardPerformanceVanillaEngine derives it and overrides OriginalResults(ForwardVanillaOption, OneAssetOptionResults). Immutable; it holds the process and the inner engine, a delegate in the engines' form taking the forward process; the forward process is built per call.

The payoff must be a plain vanilla one: QuantLib builds the inner option's plain vanilla payoff from the payoff's type alone, so a cash-or-nothing forward start prices as the vanilla (Divergences/ForwardNonPlainPayoffTests.cs); it is refused with the text of QuantLib's Monte Carlo forward engines. The vega is the derivative under a parallel shift of the input volatilities, the inner vega in the period's forward volatility times dσ_f/dε, where QuantLib's is the inner vega alone (Divergences/ForwardVegaTermStructureTests.cs): exactly QuantLib's where the volatilities at the reset and at expiry are equal. The dividend rate read for the theta is on the process's clock (Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?)), each curve read by date, and the dividend rho's reset term on the dividend curve's own time, the derivative of its zero rates (Divergences/ExoticMismatchedTimesTests.cs); on structures dated alike, QuantLib's reads.
