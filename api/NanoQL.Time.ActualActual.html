Remarks

The day count can be calculated according to:

  • the ISDA convention, also known as "Actual/Actual (Historical)", "Actual/Actual", "Act/Act", and according to ISDA also "Actual/365", "Act/365", and "A/365";
  • the ISMA and US Treasury convention, also known as "Actual/Actual (Bond)";
  • the AFB convention, also known as "Actual/Actual (Euro)".

For more details, refer to https://www.isda.org/a/pIJEE/The-Actual-Actual-Day-Count-Fraction-1999.pdf

With the ISMA or Bond convention and a schedule, the counts read the schedule's period dates, quasi-coupon dates included, which the day counter derives once, at construction; two such day counters are equal when they derive the same dates, and one with a schedule never equals one without, where QuantLib compares their names alone. A schedule built from dates alone can't be derived from (its tenor and end-of-month flag aren't available): the counts then refuse it, as QuantLib's do, and two such day counters are equal when their schedules' dates are.
