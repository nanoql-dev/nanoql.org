Remarks

Immutable. The curves and the volatility are read on the escrowed dividend adjustment's clock (Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?) in the shout engine), each on its own, QuantLib's reads where they share its times.

The residual option is struck at the spot, the escrowed level s_t less the dividend adjustment, and its volatility read there, where QuantLib strikes it and reads its volatility at s_t (fdmshoutloginnervaluecalculator.cpp:47-73): a shout locks in S_τ − K and leaves (S_T − S_τ)⁺, so struck at the escrowed level QuantLib's residual hands the dividends' present value back (24.90999 where the shout is worth 20.116 on QuantLib's testLargeDividendShoutNPV option). Without dividends the two levels are one, and QuantLib's bits stand.
