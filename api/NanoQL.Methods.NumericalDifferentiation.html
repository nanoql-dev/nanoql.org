Remarks

Immutable: the weights are computed at construction. Fornberg's algorithm divides by the offsets' differences, which QuantLib doesn't check: repeated offsets give NaN weights, which its evaluation then skips, so x³'s first derivative at 1 on offsets (−0.1, 0, 0, 0.1) comes out 0 for 3. Repeated offsets are refused here. Terms whose weight is at most ε² in magnitude are skipped, as QuantLib's.
