Remarks

Immutable and safe to share across threads: the vector is copied.

Where a·e is negative, the reflection vector is (a − |a|e)/|a − |a|e|, QuantLib's general form, so a vector opposite e maps to |a|e; QuantLib's near-parallel branches assume a·e positive, so its zero vector for a ∥ −e maps a to −|a|e and its series branch near −e to neither (Divergences/HouseholderOppositeTests.cs).
