Remarks

Like a swap-spread index, it has no fixings and no forwarding curve of its own: it delegates both to its components, so the store refuses fixings for it (AllowsNativeFixings is false), and it can't be cloned onto a single curve. The components must share their conventions (fixing days, fixing calendar, day counter, currency); only their tenors differ, and the maturity of the combination is that of the longest component.

Its name lists each component's name with its weight, written with four decimals as QuantLib's std::fixed and std::setprecision(4) write it: the exact binary value, ties rounded to even (0.03125 gives "0.0312").
