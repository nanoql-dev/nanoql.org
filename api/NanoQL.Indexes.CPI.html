Remarks

An inflation coupon observes its index with a lag, interpolating between fixings or not; QuantLib's indexes no longer interpolate, the coupons do, through these functions.

interpolationType CPIInterpolationType

The interpolation type.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context.

Returns

double

The rate of the lagged date's period; or, interpolated, the line between it and the next period's. For a ratio index whose fixings are past, the interpolated underlying fixing over the one a year earlier, less one: the convention is to interpolate the underlying first, which differs from interpolating the ratios, and it needs past fixings, since forecasts go through the year-on-year curve.

Remarks

QuantLib chooses between the two interpolated forms by whether the unlagged date needs a forecast, though the fixings it reads are those of the lagged date's period and the next one, so a fixing it never reads can change the rate it returns; and where the lag is shorter than the index's period, it can take the ratio form on a fixing not yet published and forecast the underlying index, which its own comment rules out. Here the choice reads the latest fixing the ratio form uses: the next period's first day, or the lagged period's own when the date starts its period. The first case gets QuantLib's value with that fixing added, the second the ratios forecast through the year-on-year curve: a documented divergence that changes no QuantLib case.
