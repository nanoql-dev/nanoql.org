The one-dimensional mesher of a Black-Scholes process in the logarithm of the underlying: from below the lowest to above the highest forward the dividends and the curves give up to maturity, by a multiple of the standard deviation, concentrating around a critical point when one is given inside.

Remarks

Immutable. QuantLib reads the dividend curve and the volatility at the risk-free curve's times (fdmblackscholesmesher.cpp:63-103); here each is read at the instants those times denote, on its own clock, as FdmBlackScholesOp reads them, on the engine's times from the earliest reference date of the structures, each accruing nothing before its own: the curves' discounts there, and the variance accrued to maturity. A structure that shares the risk-free curve's day counter and reference date is read as QuantLib reads it; a documented divergence.
