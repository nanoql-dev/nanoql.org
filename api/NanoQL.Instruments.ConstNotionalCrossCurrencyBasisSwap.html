Remarks

The first leg holds the pay currency cash flows and the second leg holds the receive currency cash flows. Each leg is an overnight leg on an overnight index and an Ibor leg otherwise.

Payment lags apply only to coupon payments. Notional exchanges remain on the effective and maturity dates, unless paymentLagOnNotionalExchanges is set, in which case each leg's exchanges are lagged by that leg's coupon payment lag: the final exchange settles together with the final coupon and the initial exchange falls on the lagged settlement date.

The constructor builds the legs, as QuantLib's does: an Ibor leg reads the context's at-par switch, so it takes the context. An engine's results are a ConstNotionalCrossCurrencyBasisSwapResults, whose fair spreads FetchResults(InstrumentResults) derives from the legs' BPS when the engine gives none.

Remarks

The fair spreads are the engine's when it gives them; otherwise each is derived from the NPV and its leg's BPS, and is null when that BPS is unknown or zero, where QuantLib divides by it and gives an infinite spread (QuantLib's float-float and mark-to-market swaps test the zero).

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The results aren't a swap's, or a per-leg result has the wrong number of legs.

ArgumentException

The results aren't a cross-currency swap's, or a per-leg result has the wrong number of legs.
