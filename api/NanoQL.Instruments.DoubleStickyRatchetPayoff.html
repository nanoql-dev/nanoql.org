The payoff of a single or double sticky or ratchet option: on a forward F, the accrual factor times the swaplet a₃F + b₃ less, by the first type t₁, t₁ max(t₁(swaplet − K₁), t₁t₂ max(t₂(swaplet − K₂), 0)), with the effective strikes K₁ = a₁I₁ + b₁ and K₂ = a₂I₂ + b₂ on the initial values I₁ and I₂, a forward rate or a coupon over its accrual factor.

Remarks

An immutable function object. Each type is +1 (sticky), −1 (ratchet) or 0 (no second option), checked when the payoff is evaluated, as QuantLib checks them. An extension point: the six named payoffs derive from it, as in QuantLib. QuantLib's single StickyRatchetPayoff and its two payoffs, in a block of comments the double payoff superseded, aren't ported.
