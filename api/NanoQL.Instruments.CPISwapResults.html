Remarks

QuantLib's swap stores these from its own results class, or derives them from the swap's results; here they are members of the results FetchResults(InstrumentResults) returns. The CPI leg is the first leg and the floating leg the second.

The fair rate is the engine's: DiscountingSwapEngine's CPISwap overload gives it, where QuantLib derives it from the CPI leg's BPS, which leaves out the index ratio that a CPI coupon's sensitivity to its rate carries, so the swap repriced at QuantLib's fair rate isn't worth zero (a documented divergence). The fair spread is derived from the floating leg's BPS, as QuantLib derives it. A result QuantLib refuses to read ("result not available") is null: the fair rate when the engine gives none or the CPI leg has no live coupon, the fair spread when the floating leg's BPS is zero or unknown, both for an expired swap, and a leg's NPV when it is unknown. Equality compares the per-leg arrays element by element, and the fair rate and spread by value.
