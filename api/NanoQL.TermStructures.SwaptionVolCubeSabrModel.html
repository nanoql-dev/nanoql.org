Remarks

A stateless trait: its members are static, and every instance, its zero value included, is the same model. QuantLib's primary traits read the first four guesses and fixed flags unchecked; fewer than four of either are refused here, in the form of the checks QuantLib's ZABR traits make. More are ignored, as QuantLib ignores them.
