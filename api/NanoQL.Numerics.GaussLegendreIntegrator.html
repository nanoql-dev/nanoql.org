The integral over [a, b] by the Gauss-Legendre quadrature of a given order, mapped from [−1, 1]: QuantLib's detail::GaussianQuadratureIntegrator<GaussLegendreIntegration>. It counts no evaluation, as QuantLib's doesn't.
