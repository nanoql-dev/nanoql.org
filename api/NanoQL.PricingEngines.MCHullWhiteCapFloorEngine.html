Remarks

A static class: Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1) on the model, an argument where QuantLib's engine holds it, through McSimulation<TPath, TStats>: a HullWhiteForwardProcess on the model's curve and parameters at the last payment's time, on a grid of the fixings after the reference date and the last payment; the NPV is the samples' mean, and the error estimate is theirs where the random-number traits allow one. The type parameters, the traits and the accumulator, are QuantLib's template parameters, whose defaults are PseudoRandom and GeneralStatistics.

The seed is explicit: QuantLib's 0, a seed from the clock, is refused.

The number of samples, or null, QuantLib's null.

requiredTolerance double?

The absolute tolerance, or null; it takes precedence over the number of samples.

maxSamples int?

The most samples for a tolerance, or null for no limit.

seed long

The seed, in [1, 4294967295].

The instrument's results, the NPV and, where the traits allow one, the error estimate: the expired results when it has expired.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The cap, the context or the model is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

requiredSamples or maxSamples is negative, or seed is outside its range.

NanoQLException

The cap's terms are inconsistent, a cash flow isn't a floating-rate coupon, a forward can't be computed, neither a tolerance nor a number of samples is given, or the samples can't reach the tolerance.
