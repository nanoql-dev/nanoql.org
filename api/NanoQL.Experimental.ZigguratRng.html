Remarks

The algorithm is Marsaglia and Tsang's (2000), "The Ziggurat Method for Generating Random Variables", Journal of Statistical Software 5 (8), with their right-most step; each draw uses the leftmost 24 bits of the twister's output for its acceptance, which avoids the correlation between the paper's steps 1 and 2, and its tail is sampled through the inverse cumulative normal.

A working type, as the generators are: each draw changes it, and it is not thread-safe. The seed is required: QuantLib's default, 0, seeds the twister from the clock.
