Remarks

An empty handle is null. A price discounts on the nominal curve, at the payment date when it is after the curve's reference date, at 1 otherwise; with no nominal curve, a price refuses "no nominal term structure provided", while a rate needs none.

QuantLib casts the coupon without a check and then reads through the null pointer, which is undefined; here a coupon that isn't a CPI coupon is refused, "CPI coupon needed". An optionlet whose fixing date is at or before the evaluation date pays its intrinsic value on the index ratio, the quantity the live optionlet's forward reads, where QuantLib's reads the index's level against a strike on the ratio; both are documented divergences. An extension point.
