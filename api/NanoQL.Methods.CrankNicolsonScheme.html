The Crank-Nicolson scheme: an explicit step scaled by 1 − θ, then an implicit one scaled by θ. In one dimension it is the Douglas scheme; in more, operator-splitting schemes such as Craig-Sneyd or Hundsdorfer-Verwer usually beat it.
