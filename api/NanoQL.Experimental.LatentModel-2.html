Remarks

An unsealed, immutable class: its derived models give the latent variables a meaning (a default time, a recovery), and an integrable one its integration. The correlation of Yᵢ and Yⱼ is Σₖ aᵢₖaⱼₖ. Its factor samplers are working types of their own (LatentModelFactorSampler<TCopula, TInitTraits, TUsng>, BoxMullerFactorSampler<TUrng>, PolarStudentTFactorSampler<TUrng>).

QuantLib's four constructors are three and a factory: its quote constructor takes a correlation and uses its square root as the factor (latentmodel.hpp:696-710), where its (correlSqr, nVariables) constructor takes the factor itself, so the two can't merge once a quote is a double; the correlation form is FromCorrelation(double, int, TInitTraits), and a new correlation is a new model, where QuantLib's observes its quote. A model of no variables or no factors is refused, where QuantLib's reads the first row of an empty matrix (latentmodel.hpp:653). The constructor on one factor's weights builds the policy from the weights, where QuantLib's default-constructs it first, which the Gaussian policy can't do: QuantLib crashes (latentmodel.hpp:667-680; Divergences/LatentModelVectorConstructorTests.cs).
