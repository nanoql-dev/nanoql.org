Remarks

Immutable; its smile is built at each query, as QuantLib builds it.

Its curves are read at the instants its times denote, each on its own clock, where QuantLib reads them at the surface's own times (blackvolsurfacedelta.cpp:96-97, :210): curves on Actual/360 under a surface on Actual/365 (Fixed) gave the strikes of curves on Actual/365 (Fixed). Where the curves share the surface's day counter and reference date, QuantLib's bits stand.

Its smile section's at-the-money level is the surface's forward, AtmLevel(double), where QuantLib passes the at-the-money volatility, or 1 without an at-the-money quote (blackvolsurfacedelta.cpp:118, :135), which the section's option prices, vega, digital prices and density read as the forward. Its volatilities are QuantLib's. A documented divergence.
