Remarks

QuantLib 1.44's series returns values past its range (chisquaredistribution.cpp:33-91): where its first term or its Poisson weight exp(−ncp/2) falls below the normal range of doubles, it runs on subnormal numbers, then zeros (at df 10 and x 1,547 it reads 1.81 where the answer is 1; from ncp 1,490.3 every term is 0 and the sum 0); and where its loop ends at 10,000 iterations with its bound's denominator never positive, the negative bound passes as converged. Here the series is QuantLib's master's, upstream's #2908 (b76016ff5): it skips the underflowing weights and terms by their logarithms, returns 1 where a Chernoff bound puts the upper tail below 1e-12, runs to 1,000,000 iterations, and refuses a loop that ends before its bound can be computed ("didn't converge"). A documented divergence from 1.44 (Divergences/ChiSquaredSeriesRefusalTests.cs), checked by upstream's case against Boost's distribution (DistributionsTests.TestNonCentralChiSquareLargeArguments); every value inside 1.44's range is QuantLib's bit for bit.

QuantLib's large-df branch tests |x/2 − df/2| without assigning it and starts from t = 0 (:48-52), as #2908 keeps it; above df 1.1e15 and within 1.5e-8 of df its bound then never falls to 1e-12 within the iterations, so it is refused, and its missing assignment needs no fix of its own.
