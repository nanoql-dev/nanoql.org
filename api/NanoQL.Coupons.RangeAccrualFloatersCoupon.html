Remarks

The observation dates are the observation schedule's dates but its first and last, the coupon's start and end. The start, end and observation times are measured on the coupon's day counter from the index's forwarding curve's reference date, when the coupon is built, as QuantLib's. QuantLib's deprecated constructor taking a shared schedule, and its observationsSchedule inspector, aren't ported.

index or observationSchedule is null.

NanoQLException

The index has no forwarding curve, or no day counter was given, each with QuantLib's text.
