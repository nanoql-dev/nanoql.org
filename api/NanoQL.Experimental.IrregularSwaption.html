Remarks

It has no payoff, and its results are an engine's InstrumentResults: Hagan's engine prices it (HaganIrregularSwaptionEngine). QuantLib's impliedVolatility isn't ported: on QuantLib 1.44 every call throws, "null pricing engine" on an unpriced swaption and "wrong argument type" on a priced one, its Black helper's arguments being a vanilla swaption's.
