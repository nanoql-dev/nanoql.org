Remarks

Immutable. Each curve's discount is read by date, nothing accrued before its own date, and the time on the risk-free clock from its reference date, as QuantLib's. The state reports the strip of exponents u with E[S^u] finite, the roots of 1 − θνu − σ²νu²/2, which the base's damping reads; where 1 − θν − σ²ν/2 isn't positive the spot's mean is infinite and the engine refuses, where QuantLib returns NaN.
