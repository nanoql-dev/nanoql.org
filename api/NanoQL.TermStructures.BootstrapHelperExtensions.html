Remarks

One method per family of term structures, since a family's helpers take that family's base: the yield family's is here, and each later family adds its own beside its helpers.

Methods

SetTermStructure<T>(T, YieldTermStructure)

A copy of the helper bound to a yield term structure, of the receiver's static type: QuantLib's setTermStructure, which links the curve into the helper in place. The copy prices against the curve; the helper it is called on stays unbound.
