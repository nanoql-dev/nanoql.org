Remarks

The rate is the embedded option's: a long floorlet, a long caplet, or for a collar the floorlet less the caplet, at the underlying's effective strikes, priced by the underlying's own pricer; QuantLib caches it, and here it is computed per call. A pricer set on the stripped coupon is set on its underlying too, as QuantLib's. The coupon's terms are the underlying's, its ex-coupon date and fixing convention QuantLib's constructor's defaults.
