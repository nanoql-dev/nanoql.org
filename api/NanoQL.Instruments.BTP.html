An Italian BTP (Buono del Tesoro Poliennale): a fixed-rate bond with two settlement days, a face of 100, a semiannual schedule ending on its maturity date, coupons on Actual/Actual (ISMA) paid on TARGET business days.

Remarks

Its accrued amount is rounded to five decimals, as QuantLib's is; the bond's clean price and yield read it. Its conventions are read through Current when it is built (ResolveBond(string, Func<BondConventions>)): QuantLib's with nothing configured.
