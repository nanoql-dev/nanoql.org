Remarks

On CdsHelper's form: the helper's dates follow the evaluation date, its settlement the settlement days after it on the calendar and its latest date the maturity adjusted to the fixed convention; a bound copy (SetTermStructure<T>(T, DefaultProbabilityTermStructure)) builds its swap on the bound curve. The integration step is stored and unread, as QuantLib's is.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
