Remarks

Immutable, computed per call of ModelOutputs(EvaluationContext). QuantLib's model keeps its outputs and a dirty flag; its at-the-money levels and annuities are appended to at every computation, so a second one after a recalculation holds stale entries before the fresh ones (markovfunctional.cpp:618-648). Here each call gives the model's own outputs once, and there is no dirty flag.
