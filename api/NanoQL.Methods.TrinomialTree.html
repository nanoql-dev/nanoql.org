Remarks

The diffusion term of the process must be independent of the underlying variable. Each node has three descendants, the middle one the node closest to the expectation of the variable. On a step shorter than 1% of the grid's longest, the spacing is floored at the largest natural spacing of the grid (Clewlow and Strickland 1998), which keeps a short mandatory step from widening the tree; the floored step's probabilities are the general moment-matching ones, which can be slightly negative.

Immutable once built. A NaN or an out-of-range node offset converts to the smallest integer, as QuantLib's conversion does on MSVC, so the classical regime's probability check refuses as QuantLib's does.
