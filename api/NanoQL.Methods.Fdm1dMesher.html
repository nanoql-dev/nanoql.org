Remarks

Immutable once built. An extension point, as in QuantLib: a derived mesher fills the protected locations, dplus and dminus in its constructor, and never after.

The distance past an edge is QuantLib's Null<Real>(), 3.4028234663852886e38: the derivative operators read it there, as QuantLib's do, and their edge rows don't use it.
