Remarks

Immutable; exact on constant parameters (ExtendedBinomialTree<T>). An even number of steps is raised by one. The constructor checks the strike and the up probability at time 0 only, with the standard tree's texts (Joshi4); its up_, down_, pu_ and pd_ members, which nothing reads after that check, aren't ported.
