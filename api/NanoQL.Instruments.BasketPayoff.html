Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the minimum, maximum, average and spread payoffs derive from it, and later multi-asset options define their own. The assets' prices come as a ReadOnlySpan<T>, which takes an array, a Vector's storage or a stack buffer alike, so the path pricers and the finite-difference inner values pass their buffers without a copy, where QuantLib's members take an Array.

The payoff is immutable and safe to share across threads.
