Remarks

Its arguments are a, σ, b and η (positive) and ρ (in [−1, 1]), in that order, each a constant parameter; φ is the analytical fitting parameter, φ(t) = f(t) + ½(σ(1 − e^(−at))/a)² + ½(η(1 − e^(−bt))/b)² + ρ(σ(1 − e^(−at))/a)(η(1 − e^(−bt))/b), f the curve's instantaneous forward. As in Vasicek, QuantLib's a() and b() share their names with B(x, t), so they are methods. QuantLib's header warns that the class "was not tested enough to guarantee its functionality".

V(t) and B(x, t) are QuantLib's master's forms after the pin (#2885), free of cancellation at small mean reversion; the members QuantLib leaves, the bond option's σ_P, the fitting parameter and the swaption function's means, standard deviations and correlation, are written in the same forms, exact in the limit a, b → 0, so their last bits differ from 1.44's at every reversion.
