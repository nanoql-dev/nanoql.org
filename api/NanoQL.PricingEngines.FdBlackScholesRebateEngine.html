The finite-difference engine for a barrier option's rebate: the value of the rebate paid at the hit, on a grid bounded by the barrier, with the rebate as its Dirichlet boundary value and a zero payoff at expiry.

Remarks

QuantLib's engine, its times read on the engine's clock (Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?)), the earliest reference date of the structures it reads, as the finite-difference vanilla engine reads them: the maturity, and the dividends' and exercise's step conditions' reference date and day counter, where QuantLib takes the maturity from the process and the step conditions' from the risk-free curve. Where every structure shares the risk-free curve's date and day counter, QuantLib's times stand.
