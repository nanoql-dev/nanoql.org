A smile section with an at-the-money level of its own, the source section's smile optionally recentred on it: the volatility at a strike is the source's at that strike moved by the source's level less the given one.

Remarks

Every inspector but AtmLevel() answers the source section's. Without recentering, or where either level is missing, the adjustment is 0. The base is given the source's exercise time, day counter, type and shift, as AtmSmileSection's.

The option prices, digitals, vega and density are the base's formulas on the section's own level and volatility, as AtmSmileSection prices. QuantLib's return the source's at the adjusted strike (atmadjustedsmilesection.hpp:49-69), priced on the source's forward, so without recentering every price ignores the level the section reports, and with it they agree with the section's own level and volatility only under Bachelier's formula, whose price reads the strike and the forward only through their difference.
