The one-factor model swaption engine: a European or Bermudan swaption rolled back over a grid of a Gaussian1d model's state, each step integrating the next exercise's values' cubic spline against the state's Gaussian density.

Remarks

A static class (the playbook's engine row): Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1), the model and QuantLib's settings arguments. Every fixed coupon whose start is on or after an exercise date is part of the swap exercised into there. An overnight-indexed underlying is supported: a compounded coupon's daily compounding telescopes to a zero bonds' ratio over its rate period, over the shifted value dates under an observation shift, normalised by the coupon's own rate accrual, which is exact for plain compounding and a lookback with the shift; a lookback without the shift and a lockout are priced as if they weren't there. A payment delay is handled statically. Cash-settled swaptions and arithmetically averaged overnight coupons aren't supported.

One call shares one memo of the model's terms, built inside it. QuantLib's OpenMP branch, which warms the model's caches before a parallel loop, isn't ported: the loop runs in sequence, as QuantLib's default build does.

Two documented divergences. A discount or forwarding curve on another clock than the model's is read at the instants QuantLib's arguments denote: the exercise's date in the numeraire's read and the Digital probabilities' discount, the coupons' dates where QuantLib passes them; QuantLib's reads where the curves share a clock (Divergences/Gaussian1dMismatchedTimesTests.cs). With flatPayoffExtrapolation, the right tail integrates the payoff's last node, where QuantLib's integrates its second-last (Divergences/FlatPayoffExtrapolationTests.cs).

Parameters

swaption Swaption

The swaption.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context.

model Gaussian1dModel

The model.

integrationPoints int

The grid's points on each side of the state's mean; 64 by default.

stddevs double

The grid's width, in standard deviations; 7 by default.

extrapolatePayoff bool

Whether to integrate the payoff past the grid; true by default.

flatPayoffExtrapolation bool

Whether past the grid the payoff is flat; false by default.

discountCurve YieldTermStructure

The discount curve; null, QuantLib's empty handle, for the model's.

probabilities Gaussian1dSwaptionEngineProbabilities

The exercise probabilities to compute, an additional result; none by default.

Returns

InstrumentResults

The swaption's results: the expired results when it has expired; with probabilities, the additional result probabilities, one per exercise alive and the last the probability of no exercise.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The swaption, the context or the model is null.

NanoQLException

The swaption's terms are inconsistent, it is cash settled at the par yield curve, or its swap's nominals aren't constant.
