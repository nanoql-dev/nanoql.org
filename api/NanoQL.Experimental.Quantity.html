Remarks

A value: its zero value, default(Quantity), is QuantLib's Quantity(), 0.0 of no commodity type in no unit, both null.

The operators combine quantities in one unit as QuantLib's do, and never convert: QuantLib's operators read the static Quantity::conversionType, and these see no context, so they behave as under its NoConversion. On a unit mismatch every operator but == and != throws QuantLib's "unitOfMeasure mismatch and no conversion specified"; == returns false and != true, where QuantLib's throw, as Money's do. The conversion modes are methods that take the context, which holds the settings (QuantitySettings) and the conversions (UnitOfMeasureConversions): Add(Quantity, EvaluationContext), Subtract(Quantity, EvaluationContext), Divide(Quantity, EvaluationContext), Equals(Quantity, EvaluationContext), LessThan(Quantity, EvaluationContext), LessThanOrEqual(Quantity, EvaluationContext), GreaterThan(Quantity, EvaluationContext), GreaterThanOrEqual(Quantity, EvaluationContext), and QuantityFunctions' Close and CloseEnough. A converted quantity is rounded by its unit's rounding, which leaves it as it is (Rounding).

Equals(Quantity) and GetHashCode() compare the commodity type, the unit and the amount, the amount by Equals(double), so that equality is reflexive.
