Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's setPrices and setBasisOfCurve change the curve, and here return a copy. Its constructor from dates has a fixed reference date, the first date; its constructor without dates follows the evaluation date (TermStructure(0, …)) and is the factory Create(string, CommodityType, Currency, UnitOfMeasure, Calendar, EvaluationContext, DayCounter?), whose curve gets its prices from SetPrices(IReadOnlyDictionary<Date, double>).

The node times are measured from the reference date. QuantLib's setPrices measures them from the first price's date, so on the moving-date curve with the first price ten days after today every price in the ten days before each node is the next node's (Divergences/CommodityCurveTests.cs); on the curve from dates the two are one.

A basis curve is read at the date, on its own reference date and day counter, and its price converted by the factor from this curve's unit to its unit, a price's conversion. QuantLib reads it at this curve's time, on this curve's clock, and converts by the factor from the basis curve's unit to this one's, which inverts it; where the two curves share their reference date, day counter and unit both give QuantLib's prices.

QuantLib's operator== compares the curves' names; compare Name, as for an index (FinancialIndex).
