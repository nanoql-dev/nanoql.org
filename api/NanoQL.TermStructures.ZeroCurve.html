Remarks

QuantLib's typedef and QuantLib-Python's %template name for InterpolatedZeroCurve<TInterpolator> on Linear: it adds constructors only.

Parameters

dates IEnumerable<Date>

The nodes' dates, sorted.

yields IEnumerable<double>

The zero rates at the nodes, with the given compounding and frequency.

dayCounter DayCounter

The day counter.

calendar Calendar

The calendar, or null, QuantLib's empty calendar.

jumps IEnumerable<double>

The discount-factor jumps, or null for none.

jumpDates IEnumerable<Date>

The jumps' dates, or null for 31 December of successive years.

interpolator Linear

The interpolator.

compounding Compounding

The rates' compounding.

frequency Frequency

The rates' compounding frequency, read only when the compounding isn't continuous, as QuantLib reads it.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

dates, yields or dayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

compounding isn't a member of its enum, or the compounding isn't continuous and frequency isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

The nodes or the jumps are refused, as InterpolatedZeroCurve<TInterpolator> refuses them.

NanoQLException

The interpolation refuses the nodes.
