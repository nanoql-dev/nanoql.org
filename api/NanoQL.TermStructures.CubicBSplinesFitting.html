Remarks

See McCulloch, J. 1971, "Measuring the Term Structure of Interest Rates." Journal of Business, 44: 19-31, and McCulloch, J. 1975, "The tax adjusted yield curve." Journal of Finance, XXX811-30. "The results are extremely sensitive to the number and location of the knot points, and there is no optimal way of selecting them." James, J. and N. Webber, "Interest Rate Modelling", John Wiley, 2000, pp. 440.

Constrained at zero, the coefficient of the second basis function, N_{1,3}, is solved for so that d(0) = 1; a small but nonzero N_{1,3}(0) may make the problem ill-conditioned. QuantLib's constructor without the optimization method is the first constructor with l2 named. The B-splines read 0 over a zero knot span (BSpline), so a repeated knot fits where QuantLib's divides by zero and throws.
