Namespace NanoQL.Extensions.Conventions

Classes

AliasRecord

An alias (Alias): another name for a value, its base, in the base's value kind: FpML's and ISDA's codes (ACT/360, EUTA), QuantLib's own aliases (Thirty360.ISMA), or a firm's own codes.

BespokeCalendarRecord

A bespoke calendar (BespokeCalendar): weekend days and holidays given as data, its name the calendar's own.

BondConvention

A government bond market's convention resolved in a set (Bond(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the fixed-rate bond and bond helper it feeds; the trade's terms are arguments.

BondConventionRecord

A government bond market's conventions (BondConvention), which feed FixedRateBond and FixedRateBondHelper; or, named FixedRateBond, the defaults a set's bonds take; or, named BTP, the preset's conventions.

CalendarPresetRecord

A calendar preset (CalendarPreset): one of QuantLib's calendars by its type and market, its rules code, with QuantLib's name and the holiday edits the preset's constructor applies through the set.

CalendarRecord

A named calendar on a base calendar with holidays added or removed (Calendar), as QuantLib's addHoliday and removeHoliday edit one: under the base's own name, the base's edits.

CapFloorConvention

A cap and floor convention resolved in a set (CapFloor(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder it feeds, on its index built in the set.

CapFloorConventionRecord

A cap's or floor's conventions (CapFloorConvention), which feed MakeCapFloor; or, named MakeCapFloor, the builder's defaults.

CdsConvention

A credit default swap convention resolved in a set (Cds(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder and the two default probability helpers it feeds.

CdsConventionRecord

A credit default swap's conventions (CdsConvention), which feed MakeCreditDefaultSwap, SpreadCdsHelper and UpfrontCdsHelper; or, named MakeCreditDefaultSwap, the builder's defaults.

CmsConvention

A CMS swap convention resolved in a set (Cms(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder it feeds, on its swap index and floating index built in the set.

CmsConventionRecord

A CMS swap's conventions (CmsConvention), which feed MakeCms; or, named MakeCms, the builder's defaults.

CommodityIndexRecord

A commodity index's conventions (CommodityIndex), which CommodityIndex takes; its forward curve and exchange contracts are market and contract data, which no record holds.

CommoditySettingsRecord

The commodity settings (CommoditySettings), which CommoditySettings takes; named CommoditySettings, QuantLib's defaults.

CommodityTypeRecord

A commodity type (CommodityType), which CommodityType takes: its name and code.

ConventionOrigin

Where a name in a set comes from: the record that defines it, and its file or QuantLib's presets.

ConventionProvenance

A set's provenance report (Provenance()): where each name comes from, and a warning for each record that overrides no name and that nothing references, as a likely misspelling.

ConventionRecord

A record of a conventions file: one value's definition, an element named by its kind with Name and an optional Base as attributes and every business field a child element.

ConventionRecords

Built objects back to records: Describe(string, Calendar, ConventionSet?) and its overloads give the records that resolve, in a set, to an object equal to the one given, an index field by field.

ConventionSet

An immutable set of convention records, layered snapshot on snapshot, and the ConventionProvider that answers from them: QuantLib's shipped presets (QuantLib) or none (Empty), with a user's files on top.

ConventionSnapshot

One conventions document's records, in order: what Read(string) returns and With(ConventionSnapshot) layers onto a set. A name is unique within its value kind (calendars, day counters, currencies), an alias's among all of them.

ConventionsXml

Reads and writes conventions documents: one Conventions element in the namespace Namespace, validated by its XML Schema on every read.

CrossCurrencyBasisSwapConvention

A cross-currency basis swap convention resolved in a set (CrossCurrencyBasisSwap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the rate helper it feeds, on its indexes built in the set.

CrossCurrencyBasisSwapConventionRecord

A cross-currency basis swap's conventions (CrossCurrencyBasisSwapConvention), which feed the constant-notional or, marked to market, the mark-to-market basis swap helper.

CrossCurrencySwapConvention

A cross-currency fixed-against-floating swap convention resolved in a set (CrossCurrencySwap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the rate helper it feeds, on its floating index built in the set.

CrossCurrencySwapConventionRecord

A cross-currency fixed-against-floating swap's conventions (CrossCurrencySwapConvention), which feed the constant-notional cross-currency swap helper.

CurrencyPresetRecord

A currency preset (CurrencyPreset): one of QuantLib's currencies by its ISO 4217 code, with the data its constructor reads through the set.

CurrencyRounding

A currency's rounding convention: QuantLib's Rounding, its type, and for every type but None its precision and rounding digit.

DayCounterPresetRecord

A day counter preset (DayCounterPreset): one of QuantLib's day counters by its type and convention, its formula code, with QuantLib's name and, for Business252, the calendar whose business days it counts.

DayCounterRecord

A named day counter on a base (DayCounter): the base itself, or for Business252 the base counting another calendar's business days, the only day counter with convention data.

ForeignAttribute

An attribute another tool put on a record or a root, in its own namespace, kept as read.

ForeignContent

Another tool's content kept on a record or a document's root: the attributes and elements in a namespace other than the conventions', each as the XML text it was read as, compared by content.

FxSwapConvention

An FX swap and forward convention resolved in a set (FxSwap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the rate helper and forward it feeds.

FxSwapConventionRecord

An FX swap's and forward's conventions (FxSwapConvention), which feed FxSwapRateHelper and FxForward.

IborFamilyRecord

An Ibor index family (IborFamily): one of QuantLib's families by its class (Euribor, USDLibor), QuantLib building it at any tenor, its convention and end of month by the tenor's unit.

IborFamilyUnit

A tenor unit an Ibor family takes, with its business-day convention and end-of-month rule.

IborIndexRecord

An Ibor index (IborIndex): one of QuantLib's presets by its class (Euribor6M, DailyTenorUSDLibor, BMAIndex), usually its family at a tenor, or a user's own: an IborIndex, a Libor or a CustomIborIndex by its Style.

JointCalendarRecord

A joint calendar (JointCalendar): two or more calendars joined by a rule.

MultipleResetsSwapConvention

A multiple-resets swap convention resolved in a set (MultipleResetsSwap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder it feeds, on its index built in the set.

MultipleResetsSwapConventionRecord

A multiple-resets swap's conventions (MultipleResetsSwapConvention), which feed MakeMultipleResetsSwap; or, named MakeMultipleResetsSwap, the builder's defaults.

OisConvention

An overnight-indexed swap convention resolved in a set (Ois(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder and rate helper it feeds, on its overnight index built in the set.

OisConventionRecord

An overnight-indexed swap's conventions (OisConvention), which feed MakeOIS and OISRateHelper; or, named MakeOIS, or MakeOIS. and an index's class name, the builder's defaults.

OvernightIndexRecord

An overnight index (OvernightIndex): one of QuantLib's presets by its class (Sofr), or a user's own; its convention is Following, without end of month.

OvernightIndexedSwapIndexRecord

An overnight-indexed swap index (OvernightIndexedSwapIndex), a user's own, built at a tenor on an overnight index's name.

PaymentTermRecord

A commodity payment term (PaymentTerm), which PaymentTerm takes.

QuantitySettingsRecord

The quantity settings (QuantitySettings), which QuantitySettings takes; named QuantitySettings, QuantLib's defaults.

RegionRecord

A region (Region): one of QuantLib's regions by its class (EURegion), keeping its name, or a user's own, built as a CustomRegion.

ScheduleConvention

A schedule convention resolved in a set (Schedule(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder it feeds, on the set's calendars.

ScheduleConventionRecord

A schedule's conventions (ScheduleConvention), which feed MakeSchedule; the dates are the trade's.

SwapConvention

A swap convention resolved in a set (Swap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder and rate helper it feeds, on its index built in the set.

SwapConventionRecord

A fixed-against-Ibor swap's conventions (SwapConvention), which feed MakeVanillaSwap and SwapRateHelper; or, named MakeVanillaSwap. and a currency's code, the builder's defaults for that currency.

SwapIndexRecord

A swap index family (SwapIndex): one of QuantLib's families by its class (EuriborSwapIsdaFixA), or a user's own, built at a tenor, its floating leg's index an Ibor index's name.

SwaptionConvention

A swaption convention resolved in a set (Swaption(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder it feeds, on its swap index built in the set.

SwaptionConventionRecord

A swaption's conventions (SwaptionConvention), which feed MakeSwaption; or, named MakeSwaption, the builder's defaults.

UnitOfMeasureConversionRecord

A conversion factor between two units of measure (UnitOfMeasureConversion), which a set's UnitOfMeasureConversions adds to QuantLib's known factors.

UnitOfMeasureRecord

A unit of measure (UnitOfMeasure): one of QuantLib's eight presets, by its class's name, or a unit of a user's own, which UnitOfMeasure takes.

YearOnYearInflationSwapConvention

A year-on-year inflation swap convention resolved in a set (YearOnYearInflationSwap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the rate helper it feeds, on its index built in the set.

YearOnYearInflationSwapConventionRecord

A year-on-year inflation swap's conventions (YearOnYearInflationSwapConvention), which feed YearOnYearInflationSwapHelper.

YoYInflationCapFloorConvention

A year-on-year inflation cap and floor convention resolved in a set (YoYInflationCapFloor(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the builder it feeds, on its index built in the set.

YoYInflationCapFloorConventionRecord

A year-on-year inflation cap's or floor's conventions (YoYInflationCapFloorConvention), which feed MakeYoYInflationCapFloor; or, named MakeYoYInflationCapFloor, the builder's defaults.

YoYInflationIndexRecord

A year-on-year inflation index (YoYInflationIndex): one of QuantLib's quoted presets by its class (YYEUHICP), or a user's own, quoted from its fields or the ratio of an underlying zero index's.

ZeroCouponInflationSwapConvention

A zero-coupon inflation swap convention resolved in a set (ZeroCouponInflationSwap(string)): its record, its base chain merged, and the swap and rate helper it feeds, on its index built in the set.

ZeroCouponInflationSwapConventionRecord

A zero-coupon inflation swap's conventions (ZeroCouponInflationSwapConvention), which feed ZeroCouponInflationSwap and ZeroCouponInflationSwapHelper.

ZeroInflationIndexRecord

A zero inflation index (ZeroInflationIndex): one of QuantLib's presets by its class (EUHICP), or a user's own, on a region's name.
