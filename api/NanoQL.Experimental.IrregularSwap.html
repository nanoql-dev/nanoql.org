Remarks

The fixed leg is paid on a payer swap and received on a receiver swap. Any swap engine prices it, and its results are an IrregularSwapResults, whose leg accessors read the fixed leg first. Hagan's engine prices a swaption on it (HaganIrregularSwaptionEngine), reading its fixed leg's cash flows as FixedRateCoupons and its floating leg's as IborCoupons.

The fair rate and the fair spread are an engine's own results only, null on a swap engine's: QuantLib's fetchResults sets both to a debug 0 there (// Debug: legs_[0]->fixedRate_ - …), 0 where the vanilla swap's fair rate is 0.0305, and an irregular swap has no single fixed rate or spread to solve for; null is QuantLib's own "result not available" on reading it (Conventions/IrregularSwapTests.cs).
