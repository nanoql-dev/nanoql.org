A Levy flight inertia: while a particle keeps improving its personal best the inertia is a random one, and after more than threshold iterations without improvement its velocity is a Levy flight, u^(−1/α): QuantLib's LevyFlightInertia.

Remarks

The random factor's draws are a Mersenne Twister's and the flight an isotropic walk's on a std::mt19937, its sphere an ellipsoid over the problem's bounds, all on the seed, which is required: QuantLib's default seeds from the clock. Each minimization starts them from the seed.
