The base of the Gauss-Laguerre cosine and sine polynomials: the moments of e^(−x) times cos(ux) or sin(ux), by their recurrence mₙ = (2n·mₙ₋₁ − n(n − 1)·mₙ₋₂)/(1 + u²), and the factorials.

Remarks

QuantLib keeps the moments and factorials in NaN-filled memos; here each call computes moment n and n! by a loop, the same products in QuantLib's order, so QuantLib's values, in O(n) with nothing kept.
