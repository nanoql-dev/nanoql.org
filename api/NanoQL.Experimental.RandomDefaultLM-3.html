Remarks

Bound to a basket, the model simulates the basket's live names on the latent model restricted to their rows, their recoveries kept, and tranches against the restated amounts: QuantLib's simulates the defaulted names too against the inception amounts (randomdefaultlatentmodel.hpp:352-353, :927-936). The simulation's rules are RandomLM<TCopula, TInitTraits, TUsng>'s.
