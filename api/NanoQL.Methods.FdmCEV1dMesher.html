A one-dimensional mesher for the constant elasticity of variance (CEV) process df = α·f^β·dW, absorbed at 0: from the density's ε-quantile, divided by a scale factor, to its (1 − ε)-quantile, multiplied by it, concentrating around a point where one is given within them, equally spaced otherwise. Where the density's mass at zero exceeds ε, the lower end is 0, or NanoQL.NumericLimits.QL_EPSILON for β < 0.

Remarks

Immutable. Within |β − 1| < 1e-3 the bounds are the lognormal limit's quantiles, the CEV density at β = 1 (CEVRNDCalculator), where QuantLib's density throws or hangs in Boost's non-central chi-squared; elsewhere QuantLib's bounds and bits.
