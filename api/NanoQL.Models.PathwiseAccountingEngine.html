The engine that values pathwise market-model products and their deltas along a log-normal Euler evolver's paths, by Giles and Glasserman's smoking adjoints: on each path it gathers the products' cash flows and their derivatives, then carries the derivatives back to the initial forward rates through the Euler step's Jacobian.

Remarks

Each path adds the products' values, then each product's deltas, to the caller's statistics: product i's delta to rate j at P + i·R + j, P products and R rates, in units of the initial numeraire, the path's weight in the values. It reads the model's pseudo-roots and displacements, and works in the discretely compounding money-market account.

A working type: it holds the caller's evolver, as QuantLib's shared_ptr, and steps it, so an evolver serves one engine at a time; it holds its own clone of the product and a copy of each step's pseudo-root. Its buffers are sized at construction, so a path allocates nothing after a warm-up path.

Three departures from QuantLib (documented divergences; Divergences/PathwiseDeltaTests.cs): the Euler drift's derivative reads the step's start forwards, where QuantLib's reads the step's end state (pathwiseaccountingengine.cpp:167); the rates' ratios and the drift term carry the displacement, f + d, and the drift's derivative its factor 1 − τd, where QuantLib's read f (:169-170, :285); and product i's deltas sit at P + i·R + j, where QuantLib's (i + 1)·P + j overwrites another product's deltas when P ≠ R and writes past the values when P > R (:306). The prices are QuantLib's.

Refused: an evolver not in the money-market measure, which QuantLib requires and doesn't check (:99): the adjoint recursion is the spot measure's. Refused too, where QuantLib reads past its buffers: a product whose rates or evolution aren't the model's, and a product not finished at the evolver's last step.
