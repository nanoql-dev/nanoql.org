Remarks

Immutable. A conversion given by the user is Direct; Chain(UnitOfMeasureConversion, UnitOfMeasureConversion) derives one from two others. A derived conversion converts through the conversions it was chained from, and, as QuantLib's, has no commodity type (a null CommodityType) and an empty Code.

Two conversions are equal when their commodity types, units, factors, types and codes are, and those they were chained from; QuantLib defines no equality, and UnitOfMeasureConversions' needs one.
