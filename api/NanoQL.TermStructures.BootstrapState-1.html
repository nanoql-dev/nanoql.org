Remarks

A working type, as ProblemState is: it lives only while its curve's constructor runs, belongs to that one computation and isn't thread-safe. The curve under construction answers from it, as QuantLib's curve answers from its members while its bootstrap fills them, and freezes it into its immutable nodes when its bootstrap returns. A bootstrap of one's own reads and writes its members and sets Interpolation itself.

The instruments are the curve's helpers, each re-dated for the curve's evaluation date (see BootstrapHelper<TTermStructure>). A bootstrap binds the ones it prices to the curve with Bind(BootstrapHelper<TTermStructure>) and keeps the bound copies in Instruments, as QuantLib's binds its curve's helpers in place.
