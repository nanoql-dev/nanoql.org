Remarks

It copies its inputs, as every Interpolation does. Outside its range the value is the nearest end's and the derivative 0, as QuantLib's. The primitive there is the flat value's integral, where QuantLib's extends the end intervals' lines past the ends, so that its slope outside the range isn't the value (Divergences/LinearFlatPrimitiveTests.cs); on a single point, where QuantLib's primitive and derivative read before their arrays, they are the flat value's.
