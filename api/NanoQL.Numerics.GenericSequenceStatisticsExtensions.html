Class GenericSequenceStatisticsExtensions

Namespace
NanoQL.Numerics
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The members of GenericSequenceStatistics<TStats> that need more of its type parameter than its class does, lifted over the components: the empirical risk measures, where each component's statistics keeps its samples (IEmpiricalStatistics, with the members of RiskStatisticsExtensions), and the downside variance and deviation of IncrementalStatistics, its own. QuantLib's template compiles a lifted member only where its type parameter has it; C# 14 extension members with these constraints keep that.
