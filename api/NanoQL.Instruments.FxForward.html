A foreign-exchange forward: an agreement to exchange an amount of one currency for an amount of another at a future date, at a rate fixed today.

Remarks

Immutable. The settlement conventions are QuantLib's: overnight (0 settlement days), tom-next (1) and spot (2, the default); the settlement date is the evaluation date advanced by the settlement days on the payment calendar. DiscountingFxForwardEngine prices it, its results adding the fair forward rate and the NPV in each currency (FxForwardResults).
