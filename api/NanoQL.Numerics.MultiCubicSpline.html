Remarks

QuantLib's recursion: at construction a natural cubic spline's second derivatives along the last dimension, for every line of it; at each evaluation the splines of the lower dimensions through the values the higher ones interpolate, the first dimension last, each step's arithmetic and order QuantLib's, so the values are QuantLib's.

Immutable and safe to share across threads: the grid and the values are copied, and each evaluation computes QuantLib's coefficients in scratch of its own, where QuantLib's spline holds references to its inputs and evaluates through mutable members. The dimension is the grid's, read at construction, where QuantLib's is a template parameter.

The last grid point of a dimension is interpolated on its last interval, where QuantLib refuses it as extrapolation (its documented bug, "cannot interpolate at the grid points on the boundary surface"), and with extrapolation allowed a point beyond it reads the last value, where QuantLib's flat value reads one value past the data; an argument that isn't a number is refused, where QuantLib's search reads past the grid (Divergences/MultiCubicSplineBoundaryTests.cs).

Whether each dimension extrapolates flat beyond the grid; empty, QuantLib's default, for none.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The grid's dimension isn't 1 to 15, a dimension is empty, holds fewer than four points or isn't increasing, the values don't fill the grid, or the extrapolation flags aren't one per dimension.
