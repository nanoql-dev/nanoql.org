Remarks

A static class: Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1) on the model, an argument where QuantLib's engine holds it, on a lattice built through the swap's mandatory times with the given number of steps, or on a time grid, or on a lattice already built. The swap is initialized at its last mandatory time and rolled back to 0; only its NPV is computed. A floating coupon fixed on or before the reference date is its amount (DiscretizedSwap's public constructor).

The term structure is needed only when the model can't provide one: a model that is an ITermStructureConsistentModel reads its own curve's reference date and day counter.
