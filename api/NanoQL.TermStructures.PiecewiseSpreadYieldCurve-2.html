Remarks

QuantLib's curve is PiecewiseYieldCurve<SpreadTraits<Discount>, I, B>, deriving from the spread discount curve its traits name; SpreadTraits<Discount> is Discount with that curve type, its guesses, bounds and iteration counts Discount's. So this curve is the piecewise discount curve on a base: its discount is the base's times the interpolated spread discount, and its maximum date the earlier of its own and the base's (see PiecewiseYieldCurve<TTraits, TInterpolator, TBootstrap>); its reference date, calendar, settlement days, day counter and extrapolation flag are the base's, read when it is built. It adds QuantLib's constructor and BaseCurve only.

QuantLib's spread discount curve reads the base without extrapolation (spreaddiscountcurve.hpp:157), so a bootstrap or a read past a base that doesn't extrapolate throws where the zero- and forward-spreaded curves extrapolate; here the base is read with extrapolation on, as theirs is. A documented divergence.
