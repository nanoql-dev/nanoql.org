The no-arbitrage SABR smile fitted to discrete volatilities: α, β, ν and ρ fitted to the lognormal volatilities at the strikes, interpolated by the volatility of the no-arbitrage SABR section at the fitted parameters.

Remarks

The fit is SABRInterpolation's, XABR's, over the no-arbitrage SABR's parameters mapped onto the model's domain: it runs when the interpolation is built, from the guesses and then, while the error stays above errorAccept, from up to maxGuesses − 1 Halton guesses seeded with QuantLib's 42, keeping the best. Immutable and thread-safe once built.

vegaWeighted bool

Whether the errors are weighted by the strikes' Black vegas.

The end criteria, or null for the fit's default.

optMethod OptimizationMethod

The optimizer, or null for the fit's default.

errorAccept double

The error at or below which a fit is accepted and the restarts stop.

useMaxError bool

Whether a fit is scored by its maximum error, not its root-mean-square error.

maxGuesses int

The most minimizations; 0 runs one, as 1 does.

shift double

The shift, which must be 0.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The shift isn't 0 ("NoArbSabrInterpolation for non zero shift not implemented"), or the points or the parameters are refused.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

maxGuesses is negative.

NanoQLException

The optimizer or the model fails.
