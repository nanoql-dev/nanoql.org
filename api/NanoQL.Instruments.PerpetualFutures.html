Class PerpetualFutures

Namespace
NanoQL.Instruments
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

A perpetual futures contract on an exchange rate FOR/DOM: no expiry, its price kept near the spot by funding payments at the funding frequency (continuous where the frequency's length is 0). See Ackerer, Hugonnier and Jermann, "Perpetual Futures Pricing" (2024).
