Kernel interpolation between discrete points (Hakala and Wystup, Foreign Exchange Risk, p. 256): the value at x is Σᵢ αᵢ·K(|x − xᵢ|) / Σᵢ K(|x − xᵢ|), the weights α solving y = M·α with Mᵢⱼ = K(|xᵢ − xⱼ|)/Σₖ K(|xᵢ − xₖ|).

Remarks

Immutable: the weights are solved at construction, by QRSolve(Matrix, Vector, bool, Vector?). The kernel is general, though the cited text considers a Gaussian (GaussianKernel). The primitive and the derivatives are refused, as QuantLib's.
