Remarks

An option is given by its time, its date or its tenor from the reference date, the bond by its length in years or its tenor. A tenor's option date is the reference date advanced on the structure's calendar by its business-day convention; QuantLib's empty calendar, the default, can't advance by days and refuses that with "no calendar implementation provided".

A structure whose reference date moves with the evaluation date is built by a derived class's factory taking the context, which gives this base the reference date and the settlement days. An extension point, as QuantLib's abstract class is: the constant volatility derives from it.
