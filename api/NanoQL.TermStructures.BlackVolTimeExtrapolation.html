Remarks

QuantLib reads times.back() of an empty list in the flat strategy, and times[N-2] of a list of one time in the linear one, both undefined (blackvoltimeextrapolation.cpp:39, :49-50); nanoql refuses them, the second with the text QuantLib's curve overload refuses it with (:87).

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

varianceSurface is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

type isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The strategy needs more times than given (one for FlatVolatility, two for LinearVariance), or the time or the times don't suit the linear extrapolation.

NanoQLException

The linear extrapolation's variances decrease.
