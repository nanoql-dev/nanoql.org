Remarks

The fit (SviInterpolation) runs when the section is built: QuantLib's lazy calculation (as SabrInterpolatedSmileSection's). QuantLib's quote forms are the value forms here, a quote being its value, and a volatility quote that isn't valid is a null value, its strike left out of the fit. With floating strikes, the strikes are offsets from the forward and the volatilities spreads over the at-the-money volatility. QuantLib's sections float with the evaluation date, having no reference date; here the Create factories take the evaluation context and fix the date.

QuantLib reads one strike per volatility, past the strikes' end where there are fewer strikes; they are refused here.
