Remarks

QuantLib's optionlet price sets the pricer's integration limits, which upperLimit(), lowerLimit() and stdDeviations() read afterwards; here IntegrationLimits(FloatingRateCoupon, OptionType, EvaluationContext) computes them from the arguments of the optionlet price that sets them.

IntegrationLimits(FloatingRateCoupon, OptionType, EvaluationContext)

The integration limits QuantLib's members hold after the coupon's optionlet of the given type is priced on a fresh pricer: lowerLimit(), upperLimit() and stdDeviations().

public NumericHaganPricerIntegrationLimits IntegrationLimits(FloatingRateCoupon coupon, OptionType optionType, EvaluationContext context)

Parameters

coupon FloatingRateCoupon

The coupon.

optionType OptionType

The optionlet's type: a call sets the upper limit, a put the lower one.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context.

Returns

NumericHaganPricerIntegrationLimits

The limits: a call's upper limit the forward at 8 standard deviations, a put's lower one the constructor's on a lognormal volatility and the forward less 8 normal standard deviations otherwise, the other limit the constructor's; a coupon whose fixing is known integrates nothing, so the constructor's limits and no number of standard deviations, where QuantLib's member is still unset.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The coupon isn't a CMS coupon.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

optionType isn't a member of its type.

NanoQLException

The coupon has no accrual period, or a curve or the volatility is missing.
