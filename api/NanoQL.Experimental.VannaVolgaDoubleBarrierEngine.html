The Vanna-Volga engine for FX double barrier options (Castagna and Mercurio, 2007): a double-barrier engine's price at the money, corrected by the vanna and volga of the at-the-money, 25-delta call and put options, weighted by the probability of touching neither barrier, and bounded by the vanilla price the Vanna-Volga smile gives.

Remarks

QuantLib's template on its double-barrier engine as a delegate of the engines' Calculate form: the engine given, such as AnalyticDoubleBarrierEngine.Calculate or SuoWangDoubleBarrierEngine.Calculate, is called on the option's knock-out at each of QuantLib's quote shifts, built as QuantLib builds it, on a Black-Scholes process at the at-the-money volatility dated by the evaluation date on Actual/365F, with QuantLib's number of series terms. Its shifts for the barrier's vega, volga and vanna are replayed on local values in QuantLib's order (each shift and set-back made on the value the quote holds, since a + s − s need not be a). Its additional results carry QuantLib's tags: "VanillaPrice", "BarrierInPrice", "BarrierOutPrice" and, inside the barriers, "lambda".

Documented divergences, each leaving QuantLib's bits where it doesn't apply (Divergences/VannaVolgaDoubleBarrierTests.cs). The Vanna-Volga smile's interpolation takes the domestic discount first, as its constructor's order and QuantLib's single-barrier engine take it, where QuantLib passes the foreign discount twice: the vanilla on the smile moves by up to 1.4e-4 at QuantLib's rates. The engine reads each curve at the instant the quotes' time denotes on the volatility's clock, nothing accrued before the curve's own date, where QuantLib reads each at that time on its own clock: its discount factors and the survival probability's zero rates. The already-out and already-in tests include a spot on a barrier, as the option's own Triggered(double), where QuantLib's strict tests send it to the inner engine's "barrier touched". And a rebate is priced as each option pays it: an out option's price with its rebate is QuantLib's, but where QuantLib's bounds bind its rebate-free price, the bound plus the rebate's value at the hit; an in option is QuantLib's rebate-free in price plus the inner engine's value of the rebate paid at expiry; on or beyond a barrier an out option is its rebate, due now. At a zero rebate every added term is 0. The inner engine's own fixes stand: the analytic engine and Suo-Wang price a rebate and converge their series.

The method's equivalent-constant reads stand, QuantLib's: one volatility and the zero rates to the quotes' time. Its volatilities are quotes, flat in time, so its Black-Scholes legs are exact where r_d − r_f is constant, and an out option's rebate, paid at the hit, where r_d and r_f are each flat; the smile's correction is the method's approximation.
