Remarks

The rate is the underlying's rate plus the floorlet less the caplet, which the coupon's own pricer, an OvernightIndexedCouponPricer, computes. SetPricer<T>(T, FloatingRateCouponPricer?) sets the coupon's own pricer only, as QuantLib's setPricer does; SetCouponPricer(ImmutableArray<CashFlow>, FloatingRateCouponPricer?) sets it on the underlying too. Without daily caps and floors, a coupon of non-positive gearing stores its cap as its floor and its floor as its cap, as QuantLib's does; Cap and Floor read them back.
