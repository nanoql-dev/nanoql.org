Remarks

The fixing is adjusted first, then by the base pricer's timing adjustment: for a fixing after the optionlet volatility's reference date, with σ the optionlet volatility and σ_FX the exchange rate's Black volatility at the fixing date and the fixing, ρ their correlation and t the fixing time, the shifted fixing times e^(σσ_FXρt) on a shifted lognormal volatility, and the fixing plus σσ_FXρt on a normal one (Hull, 6th edition, p. 642, as QuantLib's). QuantLib's correlation quote is a double.
