The Heston Fokker-Planck forward operator on a mesher of the log spot in direction 0 and the variance (or a transformation of it, FdmSquareRootFwdOpTransformationType) in direction 1: the density's transport under the log spot's drift r − q − ½vL² and diffusion ½vL², the variance's square-root dynamics and their correlation, L a leverage function's value at each point (1 without one), with zero-flux boundaries in the variance.

Remarks

A working type: built at construction; SetTime(double, double) rewrites the equity part in place, the leverage composition written straight into its bands by QuantLib's arithmetic in its order, and the apply, splitting solves and preconditioner write into the caller's spans through buffers it holds, so a step allocates nothing.

The curves and the leverage function are read on a clock, each at the instants its times denote: QuantLib reads the dividend curve and the leverage at the risk-free curve's times. The public constructor's clock is Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)'s, the risk-free curve itself where no structure is dated before it, QuantLib's reads.
