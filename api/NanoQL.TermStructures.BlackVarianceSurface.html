Remarks

SetInterpolation<TInterpolator>(TInterpolator) returns a copy re-interpolated with another interpolator; the surface it is called on is unchanged, where QuantLib's re-interpolates the surface itself and notifies its observers.

The variance is floored at zero. QuantLib extrapolates the bilinear variance linearly in strike with no floor, so beyond the strikes it can read a negative variance and a NaN volatility, where its variance curve and its time extrapolation floor theirs at zero (blackvariancesurface.cpp:79-83, blackvariancecurve.cpp:68); a documented divergence. Inside the grid the interpolated variances are QuantLib's.

Remarks

QuantLib reads dates.back() of an empty list before its checks (blackvariancesurface.cpp:36), which is undefined; nanoql refuses no dates first.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

dates, strikes, blackVolMatrix or dayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

An extrapolation isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

No dates are given; the matrix's size doesn't match the dates and strikes; the first date is before the reference date; the dates aren't sorted and unique; or fewer than two strikes are given.
