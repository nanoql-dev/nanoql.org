The Svensson fitting method: a discount function d(t) = e^(−r t), the zero rate r = c_0 + (c_1 + c_2)(1 − e^(−κt))/(κt) − c_2 e^(−κt) + c_3((1 − e^(−κ_1 t))/(κ_1 t) − e^(−κ_1 t)).

Remarks

See Svensson, L. (1994): "Estimating and interpreting forward interest rates: Sweden 1992-4." Discussion paper, Centre for Economic Policy Research (1051). The parameters are c_0 to c_3, κ and κ_1; the method is constrained at zero. QuantLib's constructor without the optimization method is the first constructor with l2 named.
