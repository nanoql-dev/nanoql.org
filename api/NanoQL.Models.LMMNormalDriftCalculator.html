Remarks

A working type: QuantLib's mutable scratch is the calculator's own, so it is owned by one computation and isn't thread-safe; one evolver builds one per step. The forwards' and the drifts' sizes are checked at every computation, QuantLib's extra-safety checks, the plain and reduced forms included.
