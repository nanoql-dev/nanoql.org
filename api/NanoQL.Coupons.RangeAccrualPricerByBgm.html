The range-accrual pricer in the BGM model: each observation's range is a pair of digitals on the index's forward rate, the forwards from the coupon's start S and its end T lognormal with the expiry and payment smiles' volatilities and a correlation, priced without the smile, with the smile through a call spread, or with a smile correction.

Remarks

Each observation's digitals are deflated by the discount at the payment date times the fixing at the coupon's start. A trigger at or below ε/2, ε = 1e-8, prices as the deflator, as QuantLib's.

A coupon in progress: QuantLib's call-spread route and its smile correction take the variance and the drift over the before-fixing phase clamped, max(S, 0)·λS² + min(U − S, U)·λT², and its drift and volatility functions carry the after-fixing branches for S ≤ 0; its no-smile route alone takes S·λS² + (U − S)·λT² unclamped, high on a started coupon when λS < λT and a NaN refusal when λS > λT, so here it is clamped as the other two. And an observation whose fixing is known is counted by its fixing f, deflator × (1{f > l} − 1{f > u}), the two digitals QuantLib's range reads, where no QuantLib route prices a past one: known as QuantLib's index decides it, its fixing date (the observation date less the fixing days) before the evaluation date, or on it once its fixing is published or the context enforces it. Before the coupon's start every route is QuantLib's.
