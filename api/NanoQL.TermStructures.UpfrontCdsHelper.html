Remarks

The swap buys protection on a notional of 100 with an upfront of 1% and the given running spread, its upfront paid on the upfront date: the evaluation date advanced by the upfront settlement days on the calendar, which a re-dated copy recomputes. The implied quote prices the swap with today's cash flows included, under context with { IncludeTodaysCashFlows = true }, QuantLib's SavedSettings block; the rest is CdsHelper's.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
