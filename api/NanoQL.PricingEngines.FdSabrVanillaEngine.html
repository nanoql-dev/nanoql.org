The finite-difference pricing engine for vanilla options, European, American or Bermudan, on the SABR model dF = α·F^β·dW₁, dα = ν·α·dW₂, d⟨W₁, W₂⟩ = ρ·dt: on FdmCEV1dMesher's grid of the forward, at the volatility's upper quartile, by a grid of the log volatility over ε-quantiles, with the payoff at both ends of the forward discounted from maturity.

Remarks

The engine reports the value. Its finite-difference working types are built inside each calculation and dropped after it. Its times are the discount curve's, from its reference date in its day counter, as QuantLib's; an exercise on or before that date is refused, as FdCEVVanillaEngine refuses it, where QuantLib's engine throws.

At ν = 0, which QuantLib's parameter check accepts, the log volatility's grid collapses and QuantLib's engine throws ("end must be larger than start"); so it does at a tiny positive ν, where the grid's span is a few ulps of ln α or none, and a little above it returns NaN (ν about 1e-15 over a year). SABR at ν = 0 is the CEV process with α its volatility, and wherever ν√T is at most 1e-10·max(1, |ln α|) the engine prices on FdCEVVanillaEngine with its own grids (the forward's for the CEV's), damping steps, scaling, ε and scheme: from there up QuantLib's grid holds, and its values are the CEV's up to the two grids' difference.
