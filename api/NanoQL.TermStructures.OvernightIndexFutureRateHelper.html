Remarks

The future is priced on a copy of the index forwarding on the curve the helper is bound to (the rate helpers' contract), QuantLib's index cloned on the helper's relinkable handle; its dates are fixed, so it has none to re-date. Its earliest date is the reference period's first day, its latest the delivery date.
