The one-dimensional mesher of the Heston model's variance: points averaged over the variance's non-central chi-squared quantiles at several times up to maturity, v₀ among them, with an estimate of the average volatility that sizes the equity's mesher.

Remarks

Immutable. Where the quantile grid fails, its default mesh: evenly spaced over θ and v₀ ± 4σ√(θ/2κ).

QuantLib's master since 1.44 (#2908's mesher part and #2940, taken as fixes) falls back to the default mesh also where a normal approximation of the quantiles shows the distribution too narrow for the grid's minimum steps, where the quantiles can't hold v₀ (checked before and after building the grid), and where the grid is degenerate, its probabilities repeating; and it skips the quantiles beyond 1 − ε, which give the same grid. 1.44's grid holds no v₀ where the variance falls fast (v₀ 0.25, θ 0.0025, κ 10), so its engines throw, and at small σ it builds degenerate grids; elsewhere the two are the same, bit for bit (Divergences/FdmHestonVarianceMesherFallbackTests.cs).

The volatility estimate scales by a skew factor, max(1, σ_m/κ)^1.5 in QuantLib, σ_m the mixing factor times σ, which at small κ widens the equity's mesher until its prices leave the analytic engine's: +30% at σ 1.0 and κ 0.1, +638% at κ 0.02, refining the grid not bringing them back. Here the factor is bounded at σ_m/κ = 2, min(2, max(1, σ_m/κ))^1.5, so QuantLib's bits stand wherever σ_m/κ ≤ 2, every QuantLib case among them (Divergences/FdHestonSkewFactorTests.cs).
