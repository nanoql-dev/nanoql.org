The ZABR model (J. Andreasen and B. Huge, "ZABR - Expansions for the masses", 2011): SABR with the volatility's own volatility scaled by a power γ of the volatility. Its short-maturity lognormal and normal volatilities, and its local volatility, and call prices by finite differences: on the Dupire equation in the strike under that local volatility, and on the model's full two-dimensional equation in the forward and the volatility.

Remarks

Nu is the transformed ν, ν α1−γ, as QuantLib's is. The volatilities rest on the expansion's x(K): for γ close to 1 its closed form, SABR's; otherwise the solution of an ordinary differential equation in y, integrated outward from y = 0 by AdaptiveRungeKutta<T> to each strike's y in turn, in QuantLib's order.

The vector members take strictly ascending strikes, positive where β is 1. An empty vector is refused, where QuantLib reads its first element.

Immutable and thread-safe.
