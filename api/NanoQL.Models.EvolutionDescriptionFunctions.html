CheckCompatibility(EvolutionDescription, ReadOnlySpan<int>)

Checks that there is one numeraire for each evolution time, and that each step's numeraire bond hasn't expired at the step's end, as QuantLib checks: at every step but the last.

public static void CheckCompatibility(EvolutionDescription evolution, ReadOnlySpan<int> numeraires)

Parameters

evolution EvolutionDescription

The evolution.

numeraires ReadOnlySpan<int>

The numeraires, one per step, each the index of a rate time.

Remarks

QuantLib's loop stops before the last step, so a last step's numeraire may mature inside that step, which its documentation's contract ("each numeraire must not have expired before the end of the step") excludes. It is kept: the models cut each forward's covariance at its own fixing, so the drifts under such a numeraire are those under the bond rolled into the next at its maturity, and the curve state's discount ratio to the matured bond reads its frozen forward, the rolled position's value, which prices correctly (bonds stay martingales and caplets within their Monte Carlo errors of Black's).

A numeraire past the rate times at a step QuantLib checks is read past its vector, and refused (Divergences/MarketModelUndefinedReadsTests.cs); the last step's numeraire isn't read here, as in QuantLib.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The numeraires aren't one per step, or a step's numeraire before the last has expired before the step's end.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A numeraire QuantLib reads isn't the index of a rate time.
