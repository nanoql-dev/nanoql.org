Remarks

Immutable. The stationary density is QuantLib's product β^α·v^{α−1}·e^{−βv − lnΓ(α)} wherever that product is finite, its bits; past α of about 170 the product overflows into NaN, and there it is the same density in logarithms, exp(α ln β + (α − 1) ln v − βv − lnΓ(α)).
