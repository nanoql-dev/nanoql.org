Remarks

Immutable, a LocalVolTermStructure implementing ICalibratedModel over its arguments: WithParams(Vector) returns a copy on its own fixed surface, which a calibration reads, where QuantLib's model regenerates its surface in place. QuantLib's update(), which forwards a notification to both its bases, has no counterpart: nothing here changes.
