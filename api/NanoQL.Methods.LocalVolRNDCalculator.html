The risk-neutral density of the logarithm of the underlying under a local volatility: the forward (Fokker-Planck) equation solved by Douglas's scheme from a Gaussian first step, on a grid that widens where probability mass leaks past its ends.

Remarks

Immutable: the forward equation is solved at construction (the playbook's LazyObject row), where QuantLib solves it at the first query, and its meshers and density interpolations are held read-only. Its argument x is ln(S) and its times are the local volatility's.

The risk-free and dividend curves are read at the instants the local volatility's times denote, each on its own clock, where QuantLib reads them at those times (the mismatched-dates class); curves that share the local volatility's times keep QuantLib's bits. A documented divergence.
