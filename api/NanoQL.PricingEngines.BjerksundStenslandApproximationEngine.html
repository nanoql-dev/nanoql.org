Remarks

The correctness of the returned value is tested by reproducing results available in literature. The engine reports the value, the delta, gamma, theta (and per day), vega, rho, dividend rho and strike sensitivity, and the additional results "strikeGamma" and "exerciseType" (European, American or Immediate); no forward delta, elasticity or in-the-money probability, which QuantLib's engine leaves uninitialized (its results are built in a local it never resets; Divergences/BjerksundStenslandUnsetGreeksTests.cs). The Greeks call the C runtime's erfc, which nanoql reads through Boost's, so their last bits can differ from QuantLib's.

Documented divergences, each leaving QuantLib's arithmetic where it doesn't apply:

  • Below a variance of ε the value is the European one floored at the exercise value, as QuantLib's master does since the pin (#2854).
  • Where the trigger price falls below its lower bound B₀, or isn't finite (bjerksundstenslandengine.cpp:196-206, the trigger's exponent positive), the trigger is the perpetual boundary B∞, in the same closed form and Greeks, its derivatives B∞'s: a flat boundary at or above B₀ is a feasible exercise rule, whose value bounds the option's from below (Bjerksund and Stensland 2002, §3). QuantLib prices there with an infeasible trigger and returns its European floor, up to 3.4 below its own finite-difference engine. Where the perpetual boundary is infinite the European value stands (Divergences/BjerksundStenslandTriggerTests.cs). The value jumps at the region's edge, between QuantLib's trigger and B∞, two of the method's lower bounds. QuantLib's shortcut to the European value where the trigger lies 12.5 standard deviations above the forward (:212-216) isn't taken at B∞: in the region the forward falls far below the spot while B∞ stays just above it, so the path reaches B∞ early or not at all.
  • Where a power (I/S)^κ overflows against an underflowing normal tail, QuantLib's product is NaN or 0 (:37-112); there the product is taken through logarithms, log N's asymptotic tail beyond −30, as one exponent with the Gaussian densities it divides; QuantLib's arithmetic elsewhere (Divergences/BjerksundStenslandOverflowTests.cs).
  • The American Greeks' times run from each structure's own reference date, where QuantLib's run from the risk-free curve's (Divergences/BjerksundStenslandMismatchedTimesTests.cs); each structure is read at the exercise date by date, nothing accrued before its own reference date; and an exercise window opening after the reference date is priced by NanoQL.PricingEngines.ApproximationWindow.
