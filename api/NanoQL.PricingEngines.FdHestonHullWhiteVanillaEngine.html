Remarks

The engine reports the value, delta, gamma and theta, the theta null when the problem's first stopping time is time 0; the delta and gamma are central differences at a shift of 1% of the spot. Of the Hull-White process it reads the reversion and the volatility only, its short rate's curve the Heston process's risk-free curve. Its finite-difference working types are built inside each calculation and dropped after it.

Its times run on Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?), from the earliest reference date of the Heston process's structures, each accruing nothing before its own date, where QuantLib's run from the risk-free curve's; where none is dated before the risk-free curve, they are QuantLib's (Divergences/HestonHullWhiteMismatchedTimesTests.cs).

QuantLib sizes the equity mesher on a helper process whose curves its call binds in each other's places, as the finite-difference Heston engine's does (FdHestonVanillaEngine), so where the carry is large against the volatility its grid falls short of the distribution: a five-year call at 1.25 times the forward, volatility 0.1, priced −6.32 at carry −0.30 where the analytic Heston-Hull-White engine gives 0.392 and 0 at carry 0.30 for 1.760, and a year's put at 0.7 of the forward, carry −0.2, priced 0 for 0.0068. Here the mesher is QuantLib's wherever its range holds the true forwards' band and the strike's, at half this engine's scale factor of 1.5, and widened to the bands where it doesn't, and the multi-strike solve solves each strike alone where its grid doesn't hold the forwards' band at the points it is read at (Divergences/FdHestonHullWhiteHighCarryTests.cs).

QuantLib's enableMultipleStrikesCaching serves later options at its strikes from one solve, each V(S·d)/d with d = K/Kᵢ, the strike a later call is served depending on which option solved first, which a cache may not do here: a value doesn't depend on what was computed before it. Here CalculateStrikes(VanillaOption, IEnumerable<double>, EvaluationContext, HestonModel, HullWhiteProcess, double, int, int, int, int, int, bool, FdmSchemeDesc?) returns every strike's results from one solve on the given option, each QuantLib's cached value bit for bit; after a reference whose payoff isn't a plain vanilla, whose scaled values aren't the strikes' prices, each strike's plain vanilla is solved alone, QuantLib's engine priced for that strike (Divergences/FdHestonHullWhiteStrikesReferenceTests.cs).
