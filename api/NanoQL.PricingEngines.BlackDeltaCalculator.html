Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's setDeltaType and setOptionType return copies. QuantLib's cumD1, cumD2, nD1 and nD2, deprecated as internal, are private; the header's two solver classes, BlackDeltaPremiumAdjustedSolverClass and BlackDeltaPremiumAdjustedMaxStrikeClass, deprecated as "Obsolete: do not use", aren't ported. Where a premium-adjusted put's delta lies beyond the range of the non-adjusted delta QuantLib starts its solve from (the foreign discount factor for spot deltas, 1 for forward ones), QuantLib refuses it; nanoql starts the solve from the forward on QuantLib's own bracket, a documented divergence.
