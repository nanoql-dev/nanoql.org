Remarks

QuantLib's swap derives the fair payment from its results on each call (fairFixedPayment); here it is a member of the results, which DiscountingSwapEngine's ZeroCouponSwap overload gives, null, QuantLib's "result not available", from another engine. QuantLib's fairFixedRate, which takes a day counter, is FairFixedRate(ZeroCouponSwapResults, DayCounter). Equality compares the swap's results and the fair payment.
