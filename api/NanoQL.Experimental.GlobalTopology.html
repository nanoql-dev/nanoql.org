Remarks

The best is the smallest personal best, and every particle's social best is set to it, the best particle's own included: QuantLib's keeps the largest (if (bestF < pBF_[i])), steering every particle toward the worst, and skips the best particle, leaving its social best at the zero vector it started with; on a shifted sphere it stops at 6.4e-5 where the k-neighbours topology reaches 2.7e-18 (its standard QuantLib's own definition above the class; Divergences/ParticleSwarmTopologyTests.cs).
