Remarks

Each product's results are its value, its R deltas and its vegas, in units of the initial numeraire; MultiplePathValuesElementary(Span<double>, Span<double>, int) gives the vegas with respect to every pseudo-root element, step by step, rate by rate, factor by factor. The bumps' vegas are combinations of the elements' means, so they carry no standard error: theirs are written as 0, as in QuantLib.

A working type: it holds the caller's evolver and steps it, so an evolver serves one engine at a time; it holds its own clone of the product and copies of the pseudo-roots and bumps. Its buffers are sized at construction, so its valuation allocates nothing after a warm-up, and it writes its means and errors into the caller's spans, QuantLib's caller vectors.

Departures from QuantLib: PathwiseVegasAccountingEngine's (pathwiseaccountingengine.cpp:910-911, :913-914, :1043), with RatePseudoRootJacobianAllElements's derivative.
