The discounting engine for perpetual futures: the spot times the funding payments' discounted sum (a linear payoff), or over it (an inverse payoff), the funding rates and interest-rate differences interpolated in time and flat past the last, the sum to a horizon on a grid and its tail in closed form.

Remarks

Stateless. The grid's times are the instrument's day counter's from the evaluation date, and each curve is read at the instants they denote, on its own clock, nothing accruing before its date (Divergences/PerpetualFuturesMismatchedTimesTests.cs), where QuantLib reads the curves at the grid's times themselves; on curves counted and dated as the grid, QuantLib's reads.

The tail past the horizon converges only where the last funding rate k and the curves' last forward rates r_f and r_d give k + r_f − r_d > 0 (continuous funding) or (1 + k)·e^{Δt(r_f − r_d)} > 1 with 1 + k > 0 (discrete funding); elsewhere it is refused, where QuantLib refuses only k ≤ 0 and returns the divergent series' analytic continuation below (Divergences/PerpetualFuturesDivergentTailTests.cs). A non-positive last funding rate on a convergent tail is priced.
