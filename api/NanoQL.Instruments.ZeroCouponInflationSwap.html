A zero-coupon inflation-indexed swap, quoted as a fixed rate K: at maturity it exchanges the notional's growth at K, N[(1 + K)^T - 1], against its inflation growth, N[I(T)/I(0) - 1], where T is the time from the start to the maturity.

Remarks

The type, payer or receiver, refers to the inflation leg. The fixed leg is the first leg, one simple cash flow; the inflation leg is the second, one ZeroInflationCashFlow paying the growth only. Inflation is available on every day, holidays and weekends included, so the observation dates are the start and the maturity less the lag, unadjusted. The standard DiscountingSwapEngine prices it; QuantLib's own arguments and engine classes aren't ported, since no engine uses them.

The constructor reads no evaluation date and forecasts nothing. It refuses a type that isn't a member of SwapType on entry, where QuantLib refuses it after building the legs. The inflation leg pays on the inflation calendar and convention, the fixed leg's when they aren't given. QuantLib's default convention is BusinessDayConvention(), Following, which it replaces with the fixed leg's, so an inflation leg asked to pay Following pays on the fixed convention; here a null convention takes the fixed leg's and an explicit one is kept, a documented divergence. AdjustObservationDates is stored and exposed, as QuantLib's is, and read by nothing.

The results-dependent members take the engine's SwapResults. QuantLib's fairRate is the rate that pays the inflation growth, growth^(1/T) - 1, which zeroes the swap only when both legs pay on the same day; where they pay on different days, FairRate(EvaluationContext, SwapResults?) scales the growth's excess by the ratio of the legs' end discounts from the results, so the swap rebuilt at it is worth zero, a documented divergence.

FixedLegBPS(SwapResults)

The fixed leg's sensitivity to a basis point on the fixed rate, from the fixed leg's end discount in a swap engine's results: the engine's own BPS is zero, a simple cash flow being insensitive to a rate, and its annual compounding isn't linear in the rate.

public double FixedLegBPS(SwapResults results)

The sensitivity, signed by whether the leg is paid.

Remarks

Given at the results' NPV date, as their leg NPVs are: the end discount over the NPV date's discount, where QuantLib's reads the end discount alone, from the curve's reference date, so with a later NPV date its BPS isn't the fixed leg's NPV move (zerocouponinflationswap.cpp:147-154). At the default NPV date the two agree.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

results is null.

NanoQLException

The results hold no end discount for the fixed leg.
