A bond's asset swap: the bond's coupons and final payment against a floating leg, at par (the bond bought at par, an upfront paying its premium) or at market (the floating notional scaled by the bond's dirty price); D. O'Kane, "Introduction to Asset Swaps", Lehman Brothers European Fixed Income Research, January 2000.

Remarks

The bond's clean price is its (forward) price at the floating schedule's start, the upfront date. On an overnight index the floating schedule must be given, and the floating coupons are overnight-indexed. QuantLib warns that the fair prices are wrong with indexed coupons.

A non-par repayment is per 100 of the bond's notional at the upfront date, as the fair repayment reads it, and paid as that share of the notional, where QuantLib's pays the number given as an amount (assetswap.cpp:125-134); with none given the swap repays the bond's redemption, and its repayment is that redemption per 100 of notional, where QuantLib's stores 100 whatever the redemption (:130). On a bond of 100 redeemed at 100 the two agree.

The swap is priced by DiscountingSwapEngine's asset swap overload, its fair values derived from the swap's results; QuantLib's AssetSwap::arguments, which no QuantLib engine reads, aren't ported.
