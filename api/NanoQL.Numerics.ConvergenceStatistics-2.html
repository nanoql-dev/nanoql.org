Remarks

QuantLib's ConvergenceStatistics<T, U> derives from its type parameter, which C# can't; this holds its statistics and forwards IStatistics to it. A working type: Add(double, double) and Reset() change it, and it isn't thread-safe.

Statistics is the held statistics itself: a sample added through it reaches the statistics but not the table.
