Remarks

Immutable: an interpolation copies its inputs and computes its coefficients when it is built. QuantLib's interpolations keep iterators into their caller's data and recompute on update(); here a change of data is a new interpolation, so neither update() nor empty() is ported. Extrapolation follows Extrapolator: EnableExtrapolation<T>(T, bool) returns a copy.

An extension point, as in QuantLib: a derived interpolation passes its inputs and the points it requires to the base constructor, computes what it needs from X and Y, and implements the four …Core members, which the public members call after the range check.
