Remarks

A float-vs-float cross-currency basis swap in which the notional of one leg (the resettable leg) is reset for each coupon period to track the prevailing FX rate, while the other leg keeps a constant notional that is exchanged at inception and maturity. Each reset has a fixing date, on which the FX rate is observed, and a value date at the start of the accrual period.

Following the convention of MtMCrossCurrencyBasisSwapRateHelper, the two legs are identified by their role in the FX pair: the base-currency leg (the first) and the quote-currency leg (the second), and isFxBaseCurrencyLegResettable selects which of them carries the resetting notional. The trade's type controls whether the base-currency leg is paid or received.

Resetting the notional removes most of the FX exposure of the principal and is the market-standard convention for many currency pairs. The resettable leg is built from FX-linked cash flows: FxResetCoupon instances, whose notional is the constant-leg notional converted at the period's FX-reset observation, and FxResetNotionalExchange instances paying the netted notional differences (the first notional at inception, the reset difference at each period boundary, and the last notional at maturity).

During pricing, DiscountingMtMCrossCurrencyBasisSwapEngine gives copies of the FX-linked cash flows an FxResetPricer built from its market data, leaving the swap's own cash flows unchanged; querying them independently needs a pricer set through SetFxResetPricer<T>(T, int, FxResetPricer?). A future reset is projected as the forward FX implied by its pricer's discount curves. If a reset fixing date precedes the evaluation date, the realized exchange rate must be available from the context's ExchangeRates on that fixing date; direct, inverse and derived exchange rates are supported.

The constructor builds the legs, as QuantLib's does: an Ibor leg reads the context's at-par switch, so it takes the context.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

A schedule, an index or the context is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

The type, a convention or an averaging method isn't a member of its enum, or fixing, lookback or lockout days are negative.

ArgumentException

The constant leg's notional is zero, or a leg can't be built from the arguments.

NanoQLException

An FX fixing date can't be derived (a fixing lag with no calendar), or a notional exchange's date can't be adjusted (no schedule calendar).
