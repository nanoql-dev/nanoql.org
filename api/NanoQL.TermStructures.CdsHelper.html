Remarks

The helper's dates follow the evaluation date: the protection starts the settlement days after it, and the schedule runs from the protection start, or the given start date, to the maturity the tenor gives, under a CDS rule CdsMaturity(Date, Period, DateGenerationRule)'s; the start is adjusted to the payment convention under the other rules only. The earliest date is the schedule's first, the latest its last adjusted, a day later under the ISDA model. The constructor computes them through the helper's own body, as QuantLib calls CdsHelper::initializeDates() explicitly; a re-dated copy computes them through InitializeDates(EvaluationContext), which a derived helper extends.

A bound copy (SetTermStructure<T>(T, DefaultProbabilityTermStructure)) builds its swap, which Swap returns, and prices it with the model's engine on the bound curve: QuantLib's resetEngine(), whose engine half is the same for both helpers and lives here. The mid-point engine takes the context's settlement-date flag; the ISDA engine leaves out the flows on the protection start, with QuantLib's options. A model outside the enum is refused at construction, with QuantLib's "unknown CDS pricing model".

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
