CalculateUnitCost(CommodityType, CommodityUnitCost, Currency, UnitOfMeasure, Date, EvaluationContext)

A unit cost as money per base unit in a base currency: QuantLib's CommodityPricingHelper::calculateUnitCost, the cost converted by the factor from the base unit to its unit (a price) and from its currency to the base currency, through a direct rate.

Remarks

QuantLib converts the cost by its lookup's stored factor between the cost's unit and the base unit, which inverts a price where that factor is stored from the cost's unit: a cost of 0.1 a thousand-barrels reads 100 a barrel there and 1e-4 here; a gallon's is stored from the barrel, so 0.1 a gallon reads 4.2 a barrel in both.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

An argument is null.

NanoQLException

The cost's currency or unit is QuantLib's empty one, or a conversion or rate can't be found.

CreatePricingPeriods(Date, Date, Quantity, EnergyCommodityDeliverySchedule, EnergyCommodityQuantityPeriodicity, PaymentTerm)

An energy swap's pricing periods between two dates: QuantLib's createPricingPeriods, a monthly period from each start, [start, start + 1M − 1], paid on the payment term's date after its end.

public static ImmutableArray<PricingPeriod> CreatePricingPeriods(Date startDate, Date endDate, Quantity quantity, EnergyCommodityDeliverySchedule deliverySchedule, EnergyCommodityQuantityPeriodicity qtyPeriodicity, PaymentTerm paymentTerm)

Parameters

startDate Date

The first period's start.

endDate Date

The date the last period starts before.

quantity Quantity

The quantity per period of the periodicity.

deliverySchedule EnergyCommodityDeliverySchedule

The delivery schedule: Monthly, each period the monthly quantity, or Daily, each the daily quantity times its days.

qtyPeriodicity EnergyCommodityQuantityPeriodicity

The quantity's periodicity: per month for a monthly schedule, per day for a daily one.

paymentTerm PaymentTerm

The payment term.

Returns

ImmutableArray<PricingPeriod>

The periods, which QuantLib appends to a list it is given; none when the start isn't before the end.

Remarks

A daily period's quantity is the daily quantity times its days, both ends counted: QuantLib's counts end − start, one day short, 300 barrels for January at 10 a day (Divergences/PricingPeriodTests.cs; FpML's PerCalendarDay, "deemed to apply once per Calendar Day").

The other schedules are refused, where QuantLib returns no period and its swaps then refuse "no pricing periods" or price as expired: its two branches read a schedule two ways, monthly as periods of its own tenor and daily as monthly periods summing its quantity, and no QuantLib case or caller says which a weekly, quarterly or yearly schedule means, while a constant, window or hourly one has no tenor. A caller builds such periods directly. A period starting on a month's last day ends the day before the same day next month (31 January to 27 February), and the last period may run past the end date, as QuantLib's.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

paymentTerm is null.

ArgumentException

A date is default(Date).

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

An enum argument isn't a member of its enum.

NanoQLException

The periodicity doesn't fit the schedule, or the schedule is neither monthly nor daily ("Invalid period quantity/pricing period combination.").
