Remarks

A mutable working type (§2.7), not for a multithreaded simulation: the polar method is a rejection method. Its seed constructor is Create<TUrng>(TCopulaPolicy, long), a seed in [1, 4294967295] with no default, where QuantLib's defaults to 0, the clock; a generator given instead is copied.

One uniform stream feeds the t generators in turn, and each draw is scaled by its factor's variance factor √((ν − 2)/ν), so that the factors are independent and of unit variance, as the policy's are. QuantLib's gives each t generator its own copy of one uniform generator, so the systemic draw k equals the idiosyncratic stream's draw k, and leaves its draws raw, of variance ν/(ν − 2) (latentmodel.hpp:779-794, polarstudenttrng.hpp:86): at ν 5 and a weight √0.3 a 1% default threshold simulates 2.65%. A documented divergence (Divergences/PolarStudentTSamplerTests.cs).
