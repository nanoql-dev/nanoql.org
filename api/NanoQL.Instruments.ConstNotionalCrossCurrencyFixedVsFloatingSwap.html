Remarks

Payment lags apply only to coupon payments. Notional exchanges remain on the effective and maturity dates, adjusted by the corresponding leg's payment convention. A payer swap pays the fixed leg, which is then the first leg; a receiver swap pays the floating leg. The floating leg is an overnight leg on an overnight index and an Ibor leg otherwise.

The constructor builds the legs, as QuantLib's does: an Ibor leg reads the context's at-par switch, so it takes the context. An engine's results are a ConstNotionalCrossCurrencyFixedVsFloatingSwapResults, whose fair rate and spread FetchResults(InstrumentResults) derives from the legs' BPS when the engine gives none.

Remarks

The fair rate and spread are the engine's when it gives them; otherwise each is derived from the NPV and its leg's BPS, and is null when that BPS is unknown or zero, where QuantLib divides by it and gives an infinite rate (QuantLib's fixed-vs-floating swap tests the zero).

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The results aren't a swap's, or a per-leg result has the wrong number of legs.

ArgumentException

The results aren't a cross-currency swap's, or a per-leg result has the wrong number of legs.
