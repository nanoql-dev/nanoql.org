Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's. The averaging's start date is the option's (QuantLib's deprecated constructor taking it isn't ported); a seasoned option takes the current average, a value where QuantLib takes a quote.

Documented divergences. The two moments are integrated under deterministic term structures, each at 1e-12 relative: E[A] from the forwards on NanoQL's adaptive Kronrod rule, as spot·t₂ plus the integral of F/spot − 1, exact at zero carry (QuantLib's master's #2926 cases), and the average's covariance, on a first 15-point estimate, (2/T²)∫∫F(s)F(t)(e^{V(min(s, t))} − 1) on QuantLib's adaptive Gauss-Lobatto rule, the log-variance ln(E[A²]/E[A]²) its log1p over E[A]², so nothing cancels, where QuantLib reads one volatility and the zero rates at maturity, +38.5% and −17.2% off a Monte Carlo on shaped markets, and its closed form cancels near the carries b = 0, −σ²/2 and −σ², up to 75% off and NaN, and at small volatility, NaN (Divergences/LevyTermStructureTests.cs). The covariance's integrands have kinks where the structures have nodes, where the Kronrod rule accepts panels whose two estimates agree while both are off: 9e-9 off the exact value on shaped markets, in tens of millions of evaluations, where the Gauss-Lobatto rule is within 7e-13 of Levy's formula on the moments in 30 digits, in under 100,000. At zero variance the average is its forward. An effective strike at or below zero is the certain exercise, the call Se − X·e^{−rT₂} and the put 0, the formula's limit, where QuantLib takes log(X) and prices NaN (Divergences/LevyEffectiveStrikeTests.cs).

An averaging started from the evaluation date to the clock's origin, where the structures settle after the evaluation date, reads the span before the origin at the spot (Divergences/AsianLateSettlementTests.cs), where QuantLib takes it as seasoned and asks for a current average.
