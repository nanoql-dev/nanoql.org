Remarks

A static class: Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1) and returns the instrument's results, with BlackCapFloorEngine's additional results under the same keys, from the Bachelier formulas, which take no displacement and accept negative strikes and forwards. A collar's results hold no optionletsStdDev, as QuantLib's don't, and a caplet the engine prices whose coupon is paid before the evaluation date has no forward, refused where QuantLib prices on its Null<Rate>() sentinel, a documented divergence.

QuantLib's constructors on a volatility value and on a quote coincide once a quote is a double: the overload on a double builds, at each call, the constant volatility QuantLib's builds, on no calendar, with its reference date the context's evaluation date and QuantLib's default type, shifted lognormal, which the engine reads as a normal volatility. Its day counter defaults to QuantLib's, Actual365Fixed, for null.
