Remarks

See Li, B., E. DeWetering, G. Lucas, R. Brenner and A. Shapiro (2001): "Merrill Lynch Exponential Spline Model." Merrill Lynch Working Paper. κ can be given a fixed value, which takes it out of the optimization. Convergence may be slow. Constrained at zero, the first coefficient is 1 less the others' sum, so d(0) = 1. QuantLib's constructor without the optimization method is the first constructor with l2 named.

With κ fixed, QuantLib's discount function sums one term fewer than the coefficients it fits (nonlinearfittingmethods.cpp:84, :91), right when the last parameter is κ: the last coefficient is dead. Here every coefficient is summed when κ is fixed, the discount function of the method with κ free at the same coefficients and κ; with κ free it is QuantLib's. A documented divergence.
