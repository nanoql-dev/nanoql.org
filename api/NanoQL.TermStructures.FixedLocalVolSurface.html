A local volatility surface on fixed values: a matrix of local volatilities, one column per time and one row per strike, each time's strikes its own; interpolated in strike at each time, linearly by default, and linearly in time between them, clamped to the first and last times.

Remarks

Immutable: its matrix and strikes are copied when it is built, where QuantLib's surface shares the caller's matrix. WithInterpolation<TInterpolator>(TInterpolator) returns a copy re-interpolated in strike, QuantLib's setInterpolation.

A strike outside a time's strikes is held at the nearest one wherever the constant extrapolation is asked for, at a node time as between nodes, where QuantLib's surface clamps between nodes alone and at a node, and at every time before the first, which clamps onto it, extrapolates by the interpolator (fixedlocalvolsurface.cpp:135-137): at the first node, a strike below the strikes read the interpolator's line extended, and a nanosecond later the clamped volatility. nanoql's value at a node is QuantLib's own limit as the time tends to the node. A documented divergence.
