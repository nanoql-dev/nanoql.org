Remarks

A per-period vector gives each period its element, the last element past its end, and the default when empty: a nominal and a gearing of 1, the index's fixing days, a spread of 0, and no call or put strike or payoff. A zero-gearing period's fixed coupon pays the spread, 0 by default, where QuantLib's defaults it to 1.0 there, so that such a period of a leg without spreads pays 100% (QuantLib's FloatingLeg on the same period pays 0). The floating coupons take no ex-coupon date and fix on the preceding business day, as QuantLib's template builds them.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

No nominal is given; a nominal, gearing, spread or strike vector has more elements than the schedule has periods; the schedule is empty; or a period has a zero gearing and no payment day counter is given.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A fixing-days element is negative, or a convention isn't a member of its type.

Remarks

A per-period vector (nominals, fixing days, gearings, spreads, caps, floors) gives each period its element, the last element past its end, and the default when empty: a nominal and a gearing of 1, the index's fixing days, a spread of 0, and no cap or floor (null, QuantLib's Null<Rate>()). Each factory receives QuantLib's constructor arguments: the payment date, nominal, accrual start and end, fixing days, index, gearing, spread (then the cap and the floor), reference period start and end, payment day counter, in-arrears flag, ex-coupon date and fixing convention.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

No nominal is given; a vector has more elements than the schedule has periods; the leg is both in arrears and zero; the schedule is empty; or a period has a zero gearing and no payment day counter is given.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A fixing-days element is negative, or a convention isn't a member of its type.
