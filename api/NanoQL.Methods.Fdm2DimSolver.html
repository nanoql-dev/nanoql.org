The two-dimensional finite-difference solver: rolls the cell-averaged inner values back from maturity to time 0, and reads the value, its derivatives and the theta at a point through a bicubic spline.

Remarks

Computed at construction, where QuantLib's lazy object computes at the first read, as the one-dimensional solver is, its spline built there; immutable after, its reads changing nothing. The theta comes from a snapshot of the values a short time before time 0.
