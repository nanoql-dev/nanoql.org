Remarks

Immutable. QuantLib's Pimpl becomes the virtual members: a constraint implements Test(Vector), and overrides UpperBoundCore(Vector) and LowerBoundCore(Vector), whose defaults give no bound, where its bounds are known. QuantLib's empty constraint, Constraint(), is null.

An extension point, as in QuantLib: each constraint derives from it.
