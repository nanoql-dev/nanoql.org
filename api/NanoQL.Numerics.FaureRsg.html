Remarks

A mutable working type: each draw changes it, and it is not thread-safe. A draw returns a view of its buffer, valid until its next draw. The correctness of the returned values is tested by reproducing known good values.

Its integers are QuantLib's long under MSVC, 32 bits wide, which also sets the number of base-b digits the sequence carries, ⌊ln(2^31 − 1)/ln b⌋: 30 in two dimensions (base 2), 4 in a hundred (base 101). Once its counter carries past them the generator refuses ("Error processing Faure sequence."), after 2^30 − 1 draws in base 2 and 101^4 − 1 in base 101, where QuantLib built with a 64-bit long (Linux) draws other numbers and refuses after about 10^18. These are the Python wheel's numbers.
