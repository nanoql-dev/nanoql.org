Remarks

Immutable: computed at construction, as QuantLib's constructor computes; its matrix reads return copies.

Refused, as SwaptionImpliedVolatility(MarketModel, int, int) refuses, with its texts: steps straddling the swaption's expiry, whose variance QuantLib sums in whole steps (swaptionpseudojacobian.cpp:53), and unequal displacements, of which it reads the first (:44). On every grid holding the expiry, QuantLib's value.
