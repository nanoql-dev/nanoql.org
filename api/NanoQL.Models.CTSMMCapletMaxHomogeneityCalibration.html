The maximally homogeneous coterminal swap market model caplet calibration: each caplet fitted by moving the next swap rate's volatilities to the point closest to its given ones on the sphere of its swaption variance and the cylinder of the caplet's, the caplet or the swaption favoured where they don't meet.

Remarks

Immutable. CapletMaxHomogeneityCalibration(EvolutionDescription, PiecewiseConstantCorrelation, ReadOnlySpan<PiecewiseConstantVariance>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, CurveState, double, double, int, int, double) returns its pass's pseudo-roots, failures, deformation size and swaption error in place of QuantLib's out-parameters.

One departure from QuantLib (Divergences/CTSMMCapletMaxHomogeneityDeformationTests.cs): the deformation size sums each moved volatility's squared change, where QuantLib's loop adds the step index's own change once per volatility (capletcoterminalmaxhomogeneity.cpp:370-371).
