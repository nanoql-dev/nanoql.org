Class CTSMMCapletOriginalCalibration

Namespace
NanoQL.Models
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The original coterminal swap market model caplet calibration: the swap variances made time-inhomogeneous by 1/(1 + α s)² and rescaled to their totals, then each swap rate's variance before the previous rate's reset scaled by a² and after it by b², a and b solving the caplet approximation's quadratic.
