Enum DifferentialEvolutionStrategy

Namespace
NanoQL.Numerics
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The strategies for creating a mutant population, QuantLib's DifferentialEvolution::Strategy.

public enum DifferentialEvolutionStrategy

Fields

BestMemberWithJitter = 1

The best member plus a jittered weighted difference.

CurrentToBest2Diffs = 2

The current member moved toward the best one, plus a weighted difference.

EitherOrWithOptimalRecombination = 5

Either the standard mutation or an optimal recombination, at random.

Rand1DiffWithDither = 4

A random member plus a difference weighted with dither.

Rand1DiffWithPerVectorDither = 3

A random member plus a difference weighted per coordinate with dither.

Rand1SelfadaptiveWithRotation = 6

Self-adapting weights, with a rotation of the best member.

Rand1Standard = 0

A random member plus the weighted difference of two others.

Remarks

The names are taken from Price, K., Storn, R., 1997. Differential Evolution - A Simple and Efficient Heuristic for Global Optimization over Continuous Spaces. Journal of Global Optimization, Kluwer Academic Publishers, 1997, Vol. 11, pp. 341 - 359.
