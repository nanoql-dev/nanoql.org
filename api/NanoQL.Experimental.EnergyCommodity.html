Remarks

An extension point. Each instrument values in the context's base currency and unit, CommoditySettings, QuantLib's CommoditySettings, converting its prices and quantities with the context's UnitOfMeasureConversions and ExchangeRates.

A unit conversion factor here is the value of one unit of the first unit in the second, the conversion's own Convert(Quantity) of a unit quantity, whichever way the conversion is stored; a quantity converts to the base unit by the factor from its unit to the base, and a price, money per unit, by the factor from the base to its unit. QuantLib's factor is the stored conversion's whatever its orientation, and its call sites multiply a quantity by the factor from the base to its unit and a price by the factor from its unit to the base, so one of the two is inverted: a payer vanilla swap on 1,000 gallons a month at a 0.5 spread prices 882,000 there, 42² times its value, 500, here; on the base unit every factor is 1 and the prices are QuantLib's (Divergences/EnergyUnitConversionTests.cs).
