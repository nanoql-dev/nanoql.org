The discounting engine for FxForward: each leg discounted on its currency's curve from the settlement date to maturity, the target leg converted at the spot rate, and the result discounted to each curve's reference date.

Remarks

In the source currency, the NPV is (±N_source·D_source(τ,T) ∓ N_target·D_target(τ,T) / S)·D_source(τ), the signs by the side paid, where D(τ,T) is a curve's discount factor from the settlement date τ to the maturity T and S the spot rate, units of the target per unit of the source; the fair forward rate is S·D_source(τ,T) / D_target(τ,T). The results' NPV is the source currency's, and each NPV is as of its own curve's reference date.

The additional results are QuantLib's: "spotFx", "sourceCurrencyDiscountFactor", "targetCurrencyDiscountFactor", "sourceCurrencySettlementDiscountFactor", "targetCurrencySettlementDiscountFactor", "sourceCurrencyPV" and "targetCurrencyPV". QuantLib's inspectors of the engine's curves and quote aren't ported: they are the arguments.
