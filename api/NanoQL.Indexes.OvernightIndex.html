GrowthFactor(double, double)

The factor a fixing grows a unit by over a span: 1 + fixing × span, a simple rate's.

public virtual double GrowthFactor(double fixing, double span)

Parameters

Returns

double

The growth factor.

Remarks

An extension point: an index whose fixings are quoted on another convention states its growth, as Cdi's annually compounded rate does. QuantLib's overnight coupon pricers grow every fixing as a simple rate; nanoql's compounding pricers read this factor, which by default is their expression, and the averaging pricers read SimpleAccrual(double, double), which an index overriding this factor overrides too.

SimpleAccrual(double, double)

The interest a fixing accrues per unit over a span: fixing × span, a simple rate's.

public virtual double SimpleAccrual(double fixing, double span)

Parameters

fixing double

The fixing, as the index quotes it.

span double

The span, as a year fraction on the index's day counter.

Returns

double

The accrual.

Remarks

The averaging pricers' term, the extension point beside GrowthFactor(double, double): an index whose fixings grow on another convention overrides both, its accrual its growth factor less one, as Cdi does. QuantLib's averaging pricers sum fixing × span; nanoql's read this accrual, which by default is their expression, so an index keeps QuantLib's bits. The Black pricers' forward parts read it too, to compare a daily cap's or floor's strike, as its accrual per unit of span, with the simple rate they estimate from the curve, reading the volatility at the strike as quoted; an index accruing simply keeps its strike as quoted.
