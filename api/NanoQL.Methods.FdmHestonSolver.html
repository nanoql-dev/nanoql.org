Remarks

Computed at construction, where QuantLib's lazy object computes at the first read, and immutable after (Fdm2DimSolver's form). The problem's times are Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)'s.

The delta and gamma are the first and second derivatives with respect to the spot, not the model-implied ones (Fabio Mercurio, Massimo Morini, "A Note on Hedging with Local and Stochastic Volatility Models"); the mean-variance delta and gamma add the variance's moves along its correlation with the spot.
