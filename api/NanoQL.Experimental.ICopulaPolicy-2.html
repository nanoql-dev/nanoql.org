The concept of a latent model's copula policy: the distributions of the systemic factors, the idiosyncratic factors and the latent variables they combine into, which LatentModel<TCopula, TInitTraits> takes as its template parameter.

[Experimental("NQL0001")]
public interface ICopulaPolicy<TSelf, TInitTraits> where TSelf : struct, ICopulaPolicy<TSelf, TInitTraits> where TInitTraits : struct

Type Parameters

TSelf

The policy itself.

TInitTraits

The policy's initialization parameters, QuantLib's nested initTraits.

Remarks

QuantLib's policies are classes a latent model holds and builds from its factor weights and the policy's initTraits; nanoql's are readonly structs over immutable state, built through Create(ImmutableArray<ImmutableArray<double>>, TInitTraits). The factors are expected of zero mean and unit variance; nothing checks it, as in QuantLib.
