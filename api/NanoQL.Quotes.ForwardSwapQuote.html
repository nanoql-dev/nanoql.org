Remarks

The swap is the index's underlying swap fixing on the forward start's fixing date: its value date the evaluation date advanced by the index's fixing days on its fixing calendar, its start that date advanced by the forward start, both following; it is priced on the engine the index's underlying swap carries, discounting on the index's exogenous discounting curve or else its forwarding curve. The value is −(floating NPV + spread × floating BPS / 1bp) / (fixed BPS / 1bp).

Immutable: the dates and value are computed once, at construction, under the context (§2.2's quote row). QuantLib's quote rebuilds its swap when the evaluation date moves; here a new date is a new quote. QuantLib's isValid isn't ported: a swap that can't be priced is refused at construction.
