Remarks

For particle i the window is [i − K, i + K], wrapping around, as the class documents, and a particle that is its window's best is its own social best: QuantLib's window runs from i − K − 1 to i + K − 1, and its search starts from particle i's value paired with particle 0's position, so a particle that is its window's best takes particle 0's position (Divergences/ParticleSwarmTopologyTests.cs).
