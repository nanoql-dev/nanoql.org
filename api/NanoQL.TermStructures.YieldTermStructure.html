Parameters

referenceDate Date

The reference date.

cal Calendar

The calendar used for reference and option date calculation, if any.

dc DayCounter

The day counter used for date/time conversion.

settlementDays int?

The settlement days the reference date was calculated with, if any.

jumps IEnumerable<double>

The discount-factor jumps, each applied to the discount factors past its date; none when null.

jumpDates IEnumerable<Date>

The jumps' dates, one per jump; when null or empty, the jumps fall on 31 December of the reference date's year and of the years after it, one per jump.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

Jump dates are given and their number differs from the jumps', or one of them is default(Date).
