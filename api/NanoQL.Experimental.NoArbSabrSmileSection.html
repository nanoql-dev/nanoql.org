A smile section on Doust's no-arbitrage SABR model: its prices, digitals and density are the model's, its volatilities those prices' implied Black volatilities, the SABR expansion's where none is found.

Remarks

Immutable, the model built at construction. QuantLib's section dated by a date floats with the evaluation date; here the date's constructor takes the reference date, and Create(Date, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, EvaluationContext, DayCounter?, double, VolatilityType) the context.
