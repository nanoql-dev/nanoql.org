Remarks

An unsealed, immutable object holding the process and the integration order, its public members QuantLib's (Propagator(double, double), ForwardChF(double, double)), with a static Calculate in the engines' form. QuantLib's nested integrations: 128 Gauss-Legendre points over the variance at the reset, 128 over the characteristic function's argument; within 1e-3 of the reset, the vanilla's integral alone. The value, with QuantLib's additional results ("dcf", "qf", "expiryRatio", "resetRatio", "moneyness", "s0", "fwd", "resetTime", "expiryTime", "P1Hat", "P2Hat", "phiRightLimit", "nuRightLimit").

Documented divergences (Divergences/HestonForwardEngineTests.cs), each leaving QuantLib's bits where it doesn't apply. The propagator's product e^(−(Bν + Λ)/2) · I_(R/2−1)(√(ΛBν)) underflows times overflows to NaN for resets just above the 1e-3 cut, where it is taken as e^(−(Bν + Λ)/2 + x) · Iₑ(x), x = √(ΛBν), QuantLib's own exponentially weighted Bessel function, wherever QuantLib's product isn't finite and nonzero. At κ̂ = κ − ρσ = 0 QuantLib's B is 0/0 and R 0·∞: below |κ̂t| of 1e-2, B = 4 r(κ̂t)/(σ²t) with r(x) = x/(−expm1(−x)), and R = 4κθ/σ² where κ̂ = 0. QuantLib's outer integral, 128 nodes over [0, max(2, ...)] in the reset variance, can't resolve the propagator at short resets, where it is narrow, nor where 2κθ < σ², where it is singular at 0: at a vol of vol of 0.2 a one-day reset prices 10.376 against Monte Carlo's 8.662 ± 0.019, at 0.5 a 182-day reset 5.100 against 5.177 ± 0.014. Where QuantLib's nodes miss the propagator's unit mass by more than 1e-6 the expectation is taken in closed form: QuantLib's characteristic function from the reset is affine in the reset variance, so its expectation over the propagator is the CIR transition's moment-generating function, and each probability is one Fourier integral on the half-line, adaptively, where QuantLib's inner integral stops at a fixed limit that small reset variances outrun. That limit also fails short tenors, where the return's distribution is narrow: at a reset today a one-day call at the money prices 0.297 against the vanilla's 0.420 and calls out of the money below 0. QuantLib's probabilities stand on its reset-today route and on its outer integral only where each is within 1e-7 of the resolved route's, the same half-line integral at v₀ or the closed form, the resolved route elsewhere; the resolved integral runs on Gauss-Legendre pieces whose width doubles from 1 to at most min(8, π/|log K|), half the strike factor's period, until three pieces in a row contribute at most 1e-17. Times run on the model's clock, HestonProcess's model time from the earliest reference date of its curves, each discount read at its instants, none accrued before its curve's own date.
