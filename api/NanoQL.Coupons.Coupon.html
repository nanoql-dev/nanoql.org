Remarks

QuantLib's coupon_cast is C#'s cashFlow as Coupon.

The members that may reach a fixing or the evaluation date take the context (Rate(EvaluationContext), Nominal(EvaluationContext), AccruedAmount(Date, EvaluationContext), Amount(EvaluationContext)); the accrual members, which no coupon computes from either, take none. ContractNominal is the nominal the coupon was built with, which a constructor copying another coupon's nominal reads.
