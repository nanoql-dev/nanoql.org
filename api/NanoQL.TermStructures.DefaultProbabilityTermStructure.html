Remarks

An extension point. Every built structure has a fixed reference date: QuantLib's constructor that takes neither a reference date nor settlement days is the reference-date form, and the settlement-days form takes the reference date its concrete class's Create computed from the evaluation context.

QuantLib's jumps are quotes; here they are values, each multiplying the survival probabilities past its date, and each checked to lie in (0, 1] when a probability reads it, with QuantLib's "invalid … jump value".

The survival-probability jumps, each applied to the probabilities past its date; none when null.

The jumps' dates, one per jump; when null or empty, the jumps fall on 31 December of the reference date's year and of the years after it, one per jump.
