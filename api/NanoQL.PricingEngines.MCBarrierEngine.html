Remarks

A static class over a private simulation deriving McSimulation<TPath, TStats> directly, as QuantLib's engine derives McSimulation: Calculate builds the simulation with QuantLib's checks and returns the NPV and, where the random-number traits allow one, the error estimate. The seed is explicit. QuantLib's references: Beaglehole, Dybvig and Zhou (1997), El Babsiri and Noel (1998).

Documented divergences. The paths and the discounts run on the engine's clock, Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), from the earliest reference date of the structures, where QuantLib's run on the risk-free curve's (Divergences/McBarrierMismatchedTimesTests.cs). And the bridge's uniform numbers come from QuantLib's constant seed 5, but 6 where the pricing seed is 5 on pseudo-random traits, whose paths then draw from the same Mersenne twister stream as the bridge, so that each step's increment and its extreme share their uniform numbers, +31.9 standard errors at ten steps (Divergences/McBarrierSeed5Tests.cs); low-discrepancy paths share nothing with it and keep QuantLib's 5. And a non-European exercise is refused, as the family's European engines refuse it, where QuantLib prices it to its last date: an American down-and-out put at 80 prices 1.6705, its European value, where the binomial engine prices 6.3644 (Divergences/McBarrierExerciseTests.cs).

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The option, the context or the process is null.

ArgumentException

The time steps are given neither or both ways, or aren't positive.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A number of samples is negative, or the seed is outside its range.

NanoQLException

The exercise isn't European, the spot isn't positive or lies beyond the barrier ("barrier touched"), the payoff isn't plain vanilla, neither a tolerance nor a number of samples is given, or the samples can't reach the tolerance.
