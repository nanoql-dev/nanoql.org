Remarks

For a tranche [A, D] with A above 0 the expected loss is ∫_A^D P(L > y) dy, by 24-node Gauss-Legendre over the tranche. For the equity tranche it is E[L] − ∫_D^N P(L > y) dy: E[L] the live portfolio's conditional expected loss integrated over the factor, and the upper tail over [D, D + 5%], [D + 5%, D + 20%] and [D + 20%, 100%] of the live notional N. So the equity tranche never reads the tail near zero loss, where the pool's loss is a lattice of single-name losses the expansion follows coarsely and its tail exceeds 1 at high correlation; a tranche attaching close above zero does, its integral starting below the pool's smallest single-name loss. The attachment that counts is the live one, which a seasoned tranche's realized losses write down (Basket's restated tranche). Every other member is the base's.

The check is the recursive model on the same latent model. On 100 names (notional 100, hazard 1%, recovery 40%), at one and five years, the variant is within 2% of it on the tranches from 3% to 22% at correlations from 0.3 to 0.9, and on the equity tranche within 0.6% to 0.75. Attaching closer to zero it errs more: [2%, 5%] by up to +2.5% at a year, [1%, 3%] by up to +7.5%, [0.5%, 3%] by +13% to +21%, and [0.1%, 3%] is refused from correlation 0.6 (the base's refusal of a tail outside [0, 1]); after four defaults a 3–6% tranche of that pool attaches at 0.625% of the live notional, one default's loss. At 0.9 the equity tranche reads +9.1% at five years; and a tranche whose integral reaches the pool's largest loss meets the expansion's other weak region there, where its tail can read below 0: senior tranches err by −2.6% to −4.4% at five years, and [22%, 60%] is refused at both horizons, its nodes crowding the largest loss (the base's refusal of a tail outside [0, 1]); the equity tranche's upper pieces cross it too. At low correlation the pool's loss is a binomial lattice, which a continuous expansion follows coarsely: at 0.1 a small tranche reads +9% at a year. Both models read the latent model's quadrature, its default QuantLib's 25-node Gauss-Hermite, which itself errs by up to 30% on 3% tranches at correlation 0.9 against its trapezoid integration.
