The G2 model's backward operator on two directions of a mesher: each factor's drift −ax and diffusion ½σ², discounting at half the short rate x + y + φ in each direction, and the factors' correlation ρση through the mixed derivative.

Remarks

A working type: mutable, owned by one computation and not thread-safe. Its directions' operators and the mixed one are built at construction; SetTime(double, double) rewrites the directions' operators in place with the step's φ, at the step's two ends' mean, and allocates nothing.

The model's dynamics are built once, at construction, where QuantLib's setTime calls model->dynamics() each step: the model is immutable, so they are the same each time.
