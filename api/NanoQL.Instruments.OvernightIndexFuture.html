A future on an overnight index's rate over a reference period, compounded or averaged: SOFR and SONIA futures on CME and ICE. Its NPV is 100 × (1 − R), R the period's rate plus the convexity adjustment.

Remarks

The rate's known part reads the stored fixings, each compounded through the index's GrowthFactor(double, double) or accrued through its SimpleAccrual(double, double), QuantLib's 1 + r·τ and r·τ on a simple index; the rest is forecast on the index's forwarding curve, which must reach the dates it forecasts from, with QuantLib master's texts (#2897).

A compounded future on its value date uses today's fixing once it is published, as the averaged one does and as the compounded one does on later dates; QuantLib's skips it there (overnightindexfuture.cpp:89-114, its today > valueDate_), so the same future's price moves when its first fixing becomes a past one.
