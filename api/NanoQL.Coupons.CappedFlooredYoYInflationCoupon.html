Remarks

The payoff of a capped coupon is N T min(a L + b, C), of a floored one N T max(a L + b, F), and of a collared one N T min(max(a L + b, F), C), where N is the nominal, T the accrual time, L the inflation rate, a its gearing, b the spread, and C and F the strikes; at a gearing that isn't positive the cap becomes a floor on the fixing, and the floor a cap. The rate is the swaplet's plus the floorlet's minus the caplet's, through the pricer of the underlying when there is one, as QuantLib's.

A copy with another pricer (SetPricer<T>(T, InflationCouponPricer?)) carries the pricer into a copy of the underlying too, as QuantLib's own setPricer sets both; QuantLib's setCouponPricer calls the non-virtual InflationCoupon::setPricer, so a coupon built on an underlying keeps the underlying's old pricer there, which the copy fixes by construction.
