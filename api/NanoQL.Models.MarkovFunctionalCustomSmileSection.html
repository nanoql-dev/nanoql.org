Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: a custom section implements InverseDigitalCall(double, double) and the smile section's members, and is arbitrage free by assumption. The model's digital gap, market rate accuracy and rate bounds don't apply to it, and its moneyness checkpoints only to the model's outputs. Its base is the smile section's default construction, as QuantLib's (an exercise time of 0, no exercise date, reference date or day counter), whose members a section overrides.
