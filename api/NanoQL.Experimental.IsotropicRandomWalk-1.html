Remarks

The radius is drawn on the given engine, the direction on the walk's own Mersenne Twister, as QuantLib's. In one dimension the sign is a fair coin and in two the angle is uniform on the circle, QuantLib's forms and bits. From three dimensions QuantLib's hyperspherical angles are each uniform in [0, π], where a uniform direction needs densities ∝ sin^(d−k−1)φ, so its draws crowd the poles; nanoql's direction is Muller's (1959), d standard normals from the walk's generator through the inverse cumulative normal, scaled to unit length, the radius and weights then QuantLib's (Divergences/IsotropicRandomWalkTests.cs).

A working type, as the generators are: each draw and each SetDimension changes it, and it is not thread-safe. The seed is required: QuantLib's default, 0, seeds the twister from the clock.
