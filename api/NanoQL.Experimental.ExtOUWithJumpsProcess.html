Kluge's spot model, an extended Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process plus exponential jumps (an extension of Lucia and Schwartz's): S = e^(X + Y), dX = α(μ(t) − X)dt + σdW, dY = −βY dt + J dN, the jumps exponential with rate η.

Remarks

Immutable; two state variables, X and Y, and three factors: X's Brownian draw, the draw that times a jump and the draw that sizes it, each an inverse normal mapped to a uniform within [ε, 1 − ε]. The in-place Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) allocates nothing, for QuantLib's swing cases' 200,000 paths.

A step draws at most one jump, QuantLib's first-order scheme: the probability of a jump in a step of length dt is 1 − e^(−λdt), and two or more are taken as one, a bias of order (λdt)² that shrinks with the step.

References: T. Kluge, Pricing Swing Options and other Electricity Derivatives, 2008; B. Hambly, S. Howison and T. Kluge, Modelling spikes and pricing swing options in electricity markets.
