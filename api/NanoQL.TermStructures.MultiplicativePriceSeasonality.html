Remarks

Stationary multiplicative seasonality in the price implies that zero-coupon inflation swap rates are affected and year-on-year rates aren't; a non-stationary one affects both. The factors are a multiple of those one year needs at the frequency (12 for monthly), reused for as long as needed: 24 factors repeat every two years.

A multi-year seasonality is fragile: its corrections at whole years before and after the term structure's base date must be the same, which IsConsistent(InflationTermStructure) enforces for frequencies lower than daily. The factors are normalized to the term structure's base date for zero inflation and to one year earlier for year-on-year inflation, and the seasonality is piecewise constant.

Built whole: QuantLib's default constructor, which leaves the frequency uninitialized until set, and set itself, whose only caller is the constructor, aren't ported. The constructor's checks are QuantLib's validate, which isn't virtual here; nor are the three inspectors, two of which it reads (the playbook's constructor-read row), and no QuantLib class overrides them. An extension point: KerkhofSeasonality derives from it.
