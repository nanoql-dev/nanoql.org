Remarks

The strip runs in the constructor, in QuantLib's order: for each of the curve's option tenors, the at-the-money cap on the index's forwarding curve and its price on the curve's volatility; then the spread over the first stripper's volatilities that reprices each cap, solved by Brent's method; and the spreaded at-the-money volatility inserted among each optionlet's strikes.

The stripper computes under its own context: built from a first stripper built under another context, it reads its own for its caps and the first stripper's adapter, and takes the first stripper's dates and rates as they were stripped.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

An argument is null.

ArgumentException

The curve's day counter isn't the surface's, an overnight cap of the curve is longer than the first stripper's optionlets, or the first stripper has no settlement days for its adapter.

NanoQLException

The index has no forwarding curve, a cap can't be priced, or no spread reprices an at-the-money cap.
