The smile model of an XABR swaption volatility cube, QuantLib's XabrModelTraits<Model> with its model's Interpolation and SmileSection types: the model's parameter count, the fit of a smile, the fitted fifth parameter of a five-parameter model, and the smile section at given parameters.

public interface IXabrCubeModel

Remarks

A stateless trait: a model is a readonly struct implementing it, its members static, as SwaptionVolCubeSabrModel is. NParams and ExtractGamma(IXabrInterpolation) carry QuantLib's primary template's defaults, four parameters and no fifth, which a five-parameter model overrides; the two factories are the model's own.

An extension point: a model of one's own implements it, to be fitted by an XABR cube on it.
