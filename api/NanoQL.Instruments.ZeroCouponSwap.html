A zero-coupon interest-rate swap: at maturity a fixed amount N[(1 + R)^α(T₀, T_K) − 1], or a given one, is swapped for the floating amount N[∏(1 + α(T_k, T_{k+1}) L(T_k, T_{k+1})) − 1], the index's fixings compounded over its own periods.

Remarks

The legs carry one cash flow each, on unadjusted dates; only the payment date is adjusted, the payment delay's business days after maturity. The fixed leg is the first, the floating leg the second. An engine's results are a ZeroCouponSwapResults, whose fair payment DiscountingSwapEngine's overload gives at the fixed flow's payment date, where QuantLib's reads the discount at the fixed leg's maturity date, the accrual end for the fixed-rate constructor's coupon, so with a payment delay its fair payment fed back leaves a non-zero NPV.
