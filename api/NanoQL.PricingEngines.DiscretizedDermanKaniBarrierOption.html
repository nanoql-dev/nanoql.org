A barrier option on a lattice with Derman and Kani's correction: at the nodes straddling the barrier, the values interpolate between the rolled-back value and the knocked-in vanilla or knocked-out rebate by the nodes' distances from the barrier.

Remarks

A working type, as every discretized asset is: it rolls an unenhanced DiscretizedBarrierOption back beside it, kept and initialized again at each reset, and reads the lattice's grid into a buffer it reserves at its widest step, so a rollback allocates nothing.
