Remarks

Filon's coefficients α, β and γ of θ = t·h cancel as θ falls: QuantLib's closed forms lose 5.6e-11 relative at θ 0.1 and everything near 1e-4, and are NaN at θ 0 (t 0). Below |θ| 1.7 nanoql takes their Taylor series to 14 terms (α = 2θ³/45 − 2θ⁵/315 + …, β = 2/3 + 2θ²/15 − …, γ = 4/3 − 2θ²/15 + …), within 3.3e-16 of a 50-digit evaluation there, and Simpson's rule at θ 0; from 1.7, where they are within 1e-15 of it, QuantLib's closed forms, bit for bit. A documented divergence.

Zero intervals are refused: QuantLib accepts them and divides by 0. QuantLib's integrator counts none of its evaluations and sets no error estimate, so a result's number of evaluations is 0 and its error null.
