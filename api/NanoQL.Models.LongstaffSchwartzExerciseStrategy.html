Remarks

A working type: it steps its own clones of the basis system, the exercise value and the control, and sizes every buffer at construction, so a decision allocates nothing.

Refused, QuantLib reading past them: a basis system with fewer exercises than the exercise value's, fewer coefficient vectors than exercises, or an exercise's coefficients more than its basis functions.
