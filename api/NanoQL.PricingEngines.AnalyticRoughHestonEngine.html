The analytic engine for European plain-vanilla options under RoughHestonModel: the characteristic function's fractional Riccati equation solved by the Adams scheme, the Padé approximation or the lifted approximation, and the price by Andersen and Piterbarg's Fourier integral with a Black-Scholes control variate.

Remarks

An immutable object over its model and settings; the static Calculate builds one. It keeps QuantLib's caches inside itself: the characteristic function by (z, t) and the lifted grid by t, keyed by their arguments' bits, and the two Riemann-Liouville weight sets, built once; each entry is computed from the engine's immutable model and settings, by QuantLib's arithmetic, and filled thread-safely, so every answer is what a fresh engine returns, whatever the engine priced before and on whichever thread. A model with other parameters is a new model and so a new engine, where QuantLib's update() drops the caches. Each price returns its integration's evaluations as the additional result "numberOfEvaluations", where QuantLib's engine keeps the count in a mutable member.

The model's time runs from the earliest curve date (HestonProcess's model time), the date form reading the curves by date and the time form at the instants its time denotes on that clock, where QuantLib's time form reads both curves at the risk-free curve's times; a market whose curves share the risk-free curve's dates and day counter keeps QuantLib's times. At the model's time 0 the engine refuses, as QuantLib's does ("maturity must be positive").

The Adams scheme is the reference route, O(N²) in the time steps; the Padé approximation is a closed form matching the Riccati solution's short- and long-time asymptotics, most precise at the strongly negative correlation of equity indices; the lifted approximation replaces the power-law kernel by a sum of n exponentials, an n-dimensional classical Riccati equation solved in O(nN).

References: O. El Euch and M. Rosenbaum, The characteristic function of rough Heston models, Mathematical Finance 29(1), 3-38 (2019); K. Diethelm, N. J. Ford, A. D. Freed, A Predictor-Corrector Approach for the Numerical Solution of Fractional Differential Equations, Nonlinear Dynamics 29, 3-22 (2002); J. Gatheral and R. Radoičić, Rational approximation of the rough Heston solution, International Journal of Theoretical and Applied Finance 22(3), 1950010 (2019); E. Abi Jaber, Lifting the Heston model, Quantitative Finance 19(12), 1995-2013 (2019).
