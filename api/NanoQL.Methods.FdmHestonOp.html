The Heston backward operator on a mesher of the log spot in direction 0 and the variance in direction 1: the equity's drift r − q − ½vL² and diffusion ½vL², the variance's ½σ_m²v and κ(θ − v), their covariance ρσ_mvL, and discounting at the rate, L a leverage function's value at each point (1 without one) and σ_m the mixing factor times σ.

Remarks

A working type: its parts are built at construction; SetTime(double, double) rewrites them in place, and the apply, splitting solves and preconditioner write into the caller's spans through buffers it holds, so a step allocates nothing.

QuantLib's engines run from the risk-free curve's reference date (process->time), and its operator reads the dividend curve, the quanto helper's structures and the leverage function at the risk-free curve's times (fdmhestonop.cpp:55-89), each of which measures time from its own reference date in its own day counter: FD prices +0.28% on a dividend curve on Act/365 beside a risk-free curve on Act/360, and −0.38% beside a risk-free curve settling T+2. Here the operator's times are Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)'s, from the earliest reference date of the structures it reads, on the risk-free curve's day counter, and each structure is read at the instants they denote, on its own clock, accruing nothing before its own reference date: the rates between them per unit of the clock's time, the leverage, a multiplier of the Heston volatility, at its instant. Where every structure shares the risk-free curve's day counter and reference date, QuantLib's reads and bits (Divergences/FdHestonMismatchedTimesTests.cs).

Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)

The finite-difference Heston engine's clock: the times its meshers, this operator, the solver's maturity and the step conditions run on. Its day counter is the risk-free curve's and its reference date the earliest of the structures the engine reads, the process's curves, the quanto helper's and the leverage function, so the days before a later-dated structure's date are time on the grid, each structure accruing nothing before its own date (Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?)'s form). Where no structure is dated before the risk-free curve, the clock is the risk-free curve itself, whose times are the process's.
