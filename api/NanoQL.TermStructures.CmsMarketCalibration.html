Remarks

Each cost evaluation recalibrates the cube's sections at the candidate βs, one swap tenor after another, reprices the market on it at the candidate reversion, and reads the market's weighted errors on the calibration's type. The βs enter the optimizer as y with β = exp(−y²), clamped to [1e-6, 0.999999], and the reversion as y with reversion = √|y|. The βs reach the cube as its sections' β guesses: they are the cube's βs on a cube that fixes β, and the refit's starting point on one that doesn't, whose results' cube holds the βs fitted.

QuantLib's calibration recalibrates its cube and reprices its market in place; here each Compute(EndCriteria, OptimizationMethod, Vector, bool, EvaluationContext) starts from the cube and market it was given, each cost evaluation building a new cube from the last one and a new market, as QuantLib's evaluations change the one cube in turn, and it returns the recalibrated cube and repriced market in its results, the calibration's own left unchanged. Three of QuantLib's defects are fixed: the fixed-reversion path's β guess enters the optimizer transformed, as its other paths' do; the reversion is √|y|, where QuantLib's √y returns NaN for an optimizer step to y below 0; and a fixed reversion is passed to the pricers as given, where QuantLib passes it through the transform's round trip, √(m²) = |m|.
