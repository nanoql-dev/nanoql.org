Remarks

A fixing on or before the evaluation date gives the caplet and floorlet their intrinsic values; a later one is priced with Black's formula on a shifted lognormal volatility, or Bachelier's on a normal one. On a shifted lognormal volatility a strike at or below −shift, on an adjusted fixing above −shift, which QuantLib's Black formula refuses, prices a caplet at the adjusted fixing less the strike and a floorlet at 0, the formula's limits there (QuantLib's check, its Black formula at −shift + 1e-12), so a capped coupon struck there pays its cap and a digital coupon struck at 0 on an unshifted volatility replicates to probability 1. The timing adjustment corrects the fixing of an in-arrears coupon (Black76), or of any coupon not paid at the index's maturity (BivariateLognormal, with the correlation given).

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, whose subclasses override AdjustedFixing(IborCoupon, EvaluationContext, double?). Every member computes the coupon's values inside the call (see IborCouponPricer).
