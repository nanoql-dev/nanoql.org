Remarks

A working type: Add(ReadOnlySpan<double>, double) and Reset(int) change it, and it isn't thread-safe. Its dimension is set by the constructor, by Reset(int), or by the first sample added to a statistics of dimension 0, QuantLib's lazy dimension.

Each member of IStatistics and of the Gaussian layer (GaussianStatisticsExtensions) is lifted over the components, returning one value per component; the empirical risk measures, which need a statistics that keeps its samples, are extension members on statistics over an IEmpiricalStatistics (GenericSequenceStatisticsExtensions), as QuantLib's template compiles a lifted member only where its type parameter has it.

An extension point: QuantLib's protected members are protected here, Dimension, ComponentStatistics and QuadraticSum, and Add(ReadOnlySpan<double>, double) and Reset(int) are virtual. The constructor, and Add(ReadOnlySpan<double>, double) where the first sample sets the dimension, reset this class's own state, not a derived class's, as QuantLib's call its own reset: a derived override isn't run before its class's constructor.
