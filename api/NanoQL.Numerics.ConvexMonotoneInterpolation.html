Remarks

The y values are the sections' averages, the first ignored: the section from xi−1 to xi averages yi, and the primitive at xi is the sum of the sections' averages times their lengths. A monotonicity of 1 and a quadraticity of 0 is Hagan and West's basic method; a monotonicity below 1 or a quadraticity above 0 gives smoother curves. Forcing positivity keeps the forwards non-negative where the averages are. Past the last node the interpolation is flat at its last value; with a flat final period, the last section is flat at its average, as the local bootstrap builds the curve a section at a time.

Immutable: the section helpers are computed at construction. The derivatives aren't implemented, as in QuantLib.

Three fixes of QuantLib's sections (documented divergences in Divergences/ConvexMonotoneTests.cs): a section's quadratic is split to stay non-negative only where it dips below 0 inside the section, and a discriminant that rounds a hair below 0 is taken as 0, so every section keeps its average and the forwards their continuity; and positivity isn't forced on a section whose average is negative, nor is an end forward beside one floored at 0, so a negative-rate curve keeps its averages. Everywhere else QuantLib's values, bit for bit.
