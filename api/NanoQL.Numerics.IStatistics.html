A statistics accumulator: the members QuantLib's Monte Carlo framework and Gaussian risk measures read of their statistics type parameter, which both of QuantLib's accumulators have. GeneralStatistics keeps its samples; IncrementalStatistics keeps running sums.

Remarks

An accumulator is a working type: Add(double, double), Reset() and Reserve(int) change it, and it isn't thread-safe. The Gaussian risk measures of GaussianStatisticsExtensions apply to it; the empirical ones of RiskStatisticsExtensions to a sample-keeping one, IEmpiricalStatistics.
