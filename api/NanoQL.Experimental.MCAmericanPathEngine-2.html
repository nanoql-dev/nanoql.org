Remarks

The regression's basis is QuantLib's: monomials of order 2 in the payoff's state. The paths run on the clock spanning every Black-Scholes process's structures, as MCPathBasketEngine's, each payment discounted on the first process's risk-free curve at its instant and each fixing's curve the risk-free curve implied from its date; a market on one clock keeps QuantLib's draws and arithmetic.
