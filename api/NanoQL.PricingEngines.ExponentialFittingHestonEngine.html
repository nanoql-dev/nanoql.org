Remarks

An immutable object over its HestonModel, the control variate, the scaling and the damping exponent, holding QuantLib's order-1 AnalyticHestonEngine for the control variate; the static Calculate builds one. The node table is QuantLib's, generated. The model's time runs from the earliest curve date and the forward and discount are read by date; at the model's time 0 the price is the forward's discounted intrinsic value, where QuantLib's average variance is 0/0 and it throws; and below κt = 1e-2 the average variance's factor (1 − e^{−κt})/(κt) is −expm1(−κt)/(κt), where QuantLib's loses the (v₀ − θ) term as 1 − e^{−κt} rounds to 0.

References: D. Conte, L. Ixaru, B. Paternoster, G. Santomauro, Exponentially-fitted Gauss–Laguerre quadrature rule for integrals over an unbounded interval, 2014; for the adaptation, https://hpcquantlib.wordpress.com/2020/05/17/ optimized-heston-model-integration-exponentially-fitted-gauss-laguerre-quadrature-rule/.
