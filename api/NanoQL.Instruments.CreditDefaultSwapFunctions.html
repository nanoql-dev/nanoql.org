CdsMaturity(Date, Period, DateGenerationRule)

The maturity of a standard credit default swap, from its trade date, its tenor and its date-generation rule.

public static Date? CdsMaturity(Date tradeDate, Period tenor, DateGenerationRule rule)

Parameters

tradeDate Date

The trade date.

tenor Period

The tenor: years, or a multiple of 3 months, zero included.

rule DateGenerationRule

The rule: CDS2015, CDS or OldCDS.

Returns

Date?

The maturity, or null, QuantLib's Date(), when a zero tenor under the 2015 rule gives no valid maturity: a trade date whose previous roll date is 20 June or 20 December.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

tradeDate is default(Date); the rule isn't a CDS rule; the tenor isn't years or a multiple of 3 months, or is 0 months under the old rule.

NanoQLException

The maturity isn't after the trade date.
