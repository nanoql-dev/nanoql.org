Remarks

isFxBaseCurrencyCollateralCurrency says whether the base currency of the pair is the collateral currency. The calendar is usually the joint calendar of the pair's two currencies. A trading calendar, usually the business center's (UnitedStates), when given, adjusts the earliest date and moves the latest one together with the calendar; a joint calendar holding it given as the calendar wouldn't do, since the spot convention uses the calendar alone for the earliest date. QuantLib warns that overnight swaps (no fixing days, a tenor of 1 day) and tomorrow-next ones (1 fixing day) on crosses without the business center's currency are not trivial: a holiday there today or tomorrow moves or removes the trade, and a trading calendar is then safer left out.

A helper built from dates has no fixing days, convention or end-of-month flag, which QuantLib leaves uninitialized and returns from its inspectors: here those three are methods, which refuse for such a helper. Its tenor is zero days and its calendars are QuantLib's empty one, null. The collateral curve is read directly when the helper is priced.
