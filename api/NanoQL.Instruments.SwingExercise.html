Remarks

Immutable. The date times are sorted together, where QuantLib's Bermudan base sorts the dates and leaves the seconds in the order given, so that date times given out of order are paired wrongly; and a fixed step of a day or more moves as many days as it holds, where QuantLib's moves one, so that a two-day step exercises daily. In order, and at steps under a day, they are QuantLib's. Two equal date times are refused, with QuantLib's message.
