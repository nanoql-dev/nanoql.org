Class FdmExtOUJumpOp

Namespace
NanoQL.Experimental
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The finite-difference operator of Kluge's ExtOUWithJumpsProcess on a two-dimensional mesher, X in direction 0 and the jump component Y in direction 1: the extended Ornstein-Uhlenbeck operator in X, Y's decay, and the jumps' integral by Gauss-Laguerre quadrature over the exponential jump sizes, linearly interpolated in Y.

Remarks

A finite-difference operator is a solver's working state: SetTime(double, double) sets the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck part in place, as QuantLib's does; the integral's sparse matrix is built at construction.

Reference: T. Kluge, Pricing Swing Options and other Electricity Derivatives, 2008.
