The perturbative engine for up-and-out put options on time-dependent Black-Scholes markets (Fatone, Recchioni and Zirilli): the barrier price on the market's averages, corrected to first or second order in the drift and variance's departures from them.

Remarks

QuantLib's algorithm, its midpoint rules in the time variables (npoint outer and npoint2 inner points) and its local distribution helpers: Hart's normal (algorithm 5666 by Recchioni), and Genz's bivariate normal (Drezner and Wesolowsky's method) and trivariate normal (Plackett's formula by adaptive Kronrod integration), as QuantLib's port of Genz's TVPACK has them. QuantLib's warning: the engine was reported to fail tests on Mac OS X 10.8.4.

Documented divergences (Divergences/PerturbativeBarrierEngineTests.cs), each leaving QuantLib's bits where it doesn't apply, order 0's among them. Three lines of QuantLib's TVPACK port are Genz's: Plackett's integrand squares BA − R·BB where QuantLib 1.44 takes its square root (upstream's own fix after v1.44, #2935); its Student branch belongs to the degrees-of-freedom test, where QuantLib's attaches it to the normal integrand's cut, so a normal integrand past the cut returns 0, not QuantLib's 0.5 or NaN; and the bivariate normal's |ρ| ≥ 0.925 branch takes √(1 − x²), where QuantLib's takes (1 − x²)². QuantLib's first 12-point weight, short of Genz's in the last digits, stands. The second-order term's derivative helpers are their functions' derivatives in a, term by term from the identity ∂Φ₂(h, k; ρ)/∂h = φ(h) Φ((k − ρh)/√(1 − ρ²)) their own terms apply: the F helper's factor at tt − s, its function's, where QuantLib's reads tt − p; and the E helper's limits √(τ(p − s)/(2ps)) and √(τ(p − s)/(2(τ − p)(τ − s))) with the sign of its derivative through the first limit, where QuantLib's are mistranscribed. The rate, carry and variance integrals run on the engine's clock, Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), each structure read at the instants its times denote on its own clock; where every structure shares the risk-free curve's times, QuantLib's.

The approximation's order: 0, 1 or 2.

zeroGamma bool

Whether the approximation's γ is 0, where it is the market's average carry over half its average variance by default.

The number of points of the outer time integrals, positive.

The number of points of the second-order term's inner time integral, positive.
