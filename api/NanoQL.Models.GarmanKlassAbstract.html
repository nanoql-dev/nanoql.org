The Garman-Klass volatility models, based on high-low formulas using the method of Garman and Klass in their paper "On the Estimation of the Security Price from Historical Data": at each date, √(|f(p)|/y) for a point estimator f of the interval's prices p and a year fraction y. Volatilities are assumed to be expressed on an annual basis.

Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: each estimator implements its point function. QuantLib's GarmanKlassOpenClose<T>, a template deriving its parameter, isn't a type here: the estimators that read the previous close and today's open (GarmanKlassSigma1, GarmanKlassSigma3 and GarmanKlassSigma6) run its calculation through OpenClose(TimeSeries<IntervalPrice>, double, double, double, Func<IntervalPrice, double>) on their base estimator's point function, QuantLib's arithmetic.

A documented divergence: the open-close calculation refuses an empty series, where QuantLib increments its begin past its end.
