Remarks

The moneyness is absolute (the strike less the at-the-money level) for a normal section and relative (the shifted strike over the shifted level) for a shifted lognormal one. A shifted lognormal grid starts at a moneyness of 0, the strike at minus the shift, whose call price is the shifted level. Where a strike falls outside the section's strike range, the range's end takes its place, once.

Immutable: the grids and the region are computed at construction. The relative moneyness of a range's end is its shifted strike over the shifted level, as at the grid's other points. QuantLib divides the shifted strike by the unshifted level (smilesectionutils.cpp:100-101, :107-108), so with a shift the endpoints' moneyness is off: the grid can come out unsorted, and the at-the-money point is then located in the wrong place.
