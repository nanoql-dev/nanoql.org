Remarks

The method is intrinsically weak for out-of-the-money options, as QuantLib warns. The pricing and the calibration draw from the same traits; the value, its error estimate where the traits allow one, and the exercise probability among the additional results as "exerciseProbability", as QuantLib's. The calibration is one call's working state.

The paths run on the clock spanning every Black-Scholes process's structures, each process read on it, and the payoffs are discounted on the first process's risk-free curve at that clock's times, where QuantLib evolves every process on the first's times (Divergences/TwoAssetMismatchedTimesTests.cs); a market on one clock keeps QuantLib's arithmetic.
