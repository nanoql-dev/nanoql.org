The finite-difference pricing engine for vanilla options, European, American or Bermudan, on a Black-Scholes equity under a Cox-Ingersoll-Ross short rate, with cash dividends and optionally quanto: FdmCIRSolver on FdmBlackScholesMesher's grid of the log spot by a uniform grid of the short rate.

Remarks

The engine reports the value, delta, gamma and theta; the theta is null when the problem's first stopping time is time 0, as QuantLib's is. Its finite-difference working types are built inside each calculation and dropped after it. Its times run on Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), from the earliest reference date of the equity's structures, each accruing nothing before its own date; where none is dated before the risk-free curve, they are QuantLib's.

QuantLib's engine prices another model: its operator's rate diffusion is σ²r where the CIR generator's is ½σ²r, and its covariance 2ρσ_Sσ where it is ρσ_Sσ√r (FdmCIROp), so that its rate has twice the CIR variance and it blows up at ρ ≠ 0; and its rate grid, FdmSimpleProcess1dMesher's time-averaged quantiles, stops above zero and, where the rate drifts or σ is small, leaves the rate's path (3.58 to 15.7 where the deterministic-rate limit is 4.21 and 3.28). Here the CIR generator's coefficients, and the rate grid uniform on [0, max(upper, r₀, θ, E[r_T])], upper the last point of QuantLib's grid, where at r = 0 the equation is its own boundary condition. The default scheme is Hundsdorfer's, where QuantLib's modified Hundsdorfer blows up on fine grids when the rate drifts up fast; a scheme the caller names is used as given. With a quanto helper the operator applies its adjustment (FdmCIROp's remarks), where QuantLib passes the helper to the mesher alone. Documented divergences: Divergences/FdCirModelTests.cs.
