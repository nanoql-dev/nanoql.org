Remarks

QuantLib's: the product flushed into the average, as its root, where the next factor would overflow it.

Documented divergences. The product is flushed as well where the next factor would take it below double's normal range, where QuantLib's underflows (Divergences/GeometricAsianProductRangeTests.cs); the engine's route takes the past fixings' log where their product isn't finite and normal, and gives an index once per fixing on its time (Divergences/AsianDuplicateFixingsTests.cs). QuantLib's arithmetic and bits wherever the product stays in range.
