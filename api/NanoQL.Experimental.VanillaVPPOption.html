A virtual power plant: the right to run a gas-fired plant at each exercise date, earning power less gas at its heat rate, within its output range, minimum up and down times, start costs and, optionally, a limit on its starts or running hours.

Remarks

The option is its engines' arguments, as QuantLib's arguments carries its members; its payoff is QuantLib's, the average basket payoff of power and gas weighted 1 and −heatRate on the identity.
