Remarks

Immutable, a Gaussian1dModel implementing ICalibratedModel over its two arguments, the reversions and the volatilities, in QuantLib's order (the playbook's calibration row): a calibration returns a new model. QuantLib's quotes are values (the playbook's quote row), so its four constructors are two. Its memo of the process's integrals lives for one call, so QuantLib's flushCache and its observers have no counterpart.

WithNumeraireTime(double) returns a copy, its process moved to the new forward measure time with its core, and refuses a time below the last step time, as QuantLib's master does after the pin (#2842); QuantLib 1.44's moves the process and leaves its core at the constructor's time. A curve given to the numeraire is read with extrapolation, as the model's own is, where QuantLib's reads a given one at the numeraire time without it (Divergences/Gaussian1dNumeraireExtrapolationTests.cs).

Remarks

QuantLib's passes the whole weight vector to each one-helper step, which its calibration refuses for every non-empty vector of more than one weight: a documented divergence (Divergences/GsrIterativeWeightsTests.cs). With no helper, QuantLib's loop does nothing and leaves the model's last record; here there is no record to return, so it is refused with its calibration's text.

Remarks

QuantLib's setParams with its generateArguments: the process takes the new volatilities and reversions, its step times and forward measure time kept. As QuantLib's, the parameters aren't checked against the arguments' constraints, and the refusals of a wrong size come before any write. The copy's core takes its zero-reversion flags from the new reversions, the model built at them; QuantLib's recomputes them from the old ones before it sets the new, so its model keeps the previous parameters' flags (Divergences/GsrWithParamsTests.cs).
