Remarks

The engine reports the value, and at the hit the delta, gamma and rho (QuantLib's to-do: more Greeks). An exercise window opening after the volatility's reference date is refused, as QuantLib refuses it. AnalyticDigitalAmericanKOEngine prices the knock-out at expiry: QuantLib's sibling class, whose knock_in() override is a flag here.

Each structure is read at the exercise date by date, nothing accrued before its own reference date (the mismatched-dates class), where QuantLib's reads throw; the rho's time is the risk-free curve's, as QuantLib's.
