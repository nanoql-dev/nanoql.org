Remarks

QuantLib's curve derives from RelativeDerivedYieldTermStructure<ZeroYieldStructure>, a class template over its own base, which C# can't express; it implements the template's members itself, as ZeroSpreadedTermStructure does. Its reference date and day counter are the original's, read when it is built, and so is its extrapolation flag; its calendar and settlement days are the original's, read when they are asked for, and its maximum date is the earlier of the original's and the last spread's date. The spreads' times are counted from the reference date when the curve is built. Spreads or an original that change make a new curve: QuantLib's curve follows its quotes and its handle.

The spread is added in the given compounding and frequency: the original's zero rate at a time in them, plus the spread, converted to continuous compounding at that time. QuantLib's deprecated constructor taking a day counter, which it ignores, isn't ported. Unsealed for its named class, which adds constructors only.
