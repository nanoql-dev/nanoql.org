Remarks

The implementation derives from G. West, "Better Approximations To Cumulative Normal Distributions", Wilmott Magazine (May 2005), 70-76, a port of its C++ code, on the near double-precision algorithm of A. Genz, "Numerical Computation of Rectangular Bivariate and Trivariate Normal and t Probabilities", Statistics and Computing 14 (2004), 151-160; the univariate normal is CumulativeNormalDistribution and the Gauss-Legendre rules are TabulatedGaussLegendre's. Immutable. QuantLib's typedef BivariateCumulativeNormalDistribution names this class.

At an infinite bound the probability is the other bound's Φ (+∞) or 0 (−∞), where QuantLib's integrand meets ∞ − ∞ and returns NaN (at +∞ for ρ > 0, at −∞ for ρ < 0, and wherever the other bound is 0; Divergences/BivariateInfiniteArgumentTests.cs).
