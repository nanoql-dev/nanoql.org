The Rendistato calculator: the yield and modified duration of a basket of BTPs, weighted by their outstanding amounts, and the Euribor swap of one to fifteen years whose fixed leg, as a bond at par, has the duration closest below the basket's.

Remarks

It computes in its constructor, under the context it is built with, what QuantLib's first calculation computes; QuantLib starts a recalculation's yield solves from the last calculation's yields, so its warm start is a documented divergence. The swaps start one day after spot, at a fixed rate of 5%, and are priced on the discount curve without the settlement date's flows. The search for the equivalent swap stops at the first swap whose duration exceeds the basket's; the later swaps' rates, yields and durations aren't computed and are null, where QuantLib's rates and durations are its null value and its yields its starting guess, 5% (a defect found while porting it).
