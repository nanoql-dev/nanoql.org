Remarks

This class does not perform any date adjustment, i.e., the start and end date passed upon construction should be already rolled to a business day.

Its rate needs a CmsSpreadCouponPricer, which CouponPricerFunctions sets, refusing any other pricer as QuantLib's visitor does; without one its rate refuses with QuantLib's "pricer not set". Experimental, as in QuantLib.
