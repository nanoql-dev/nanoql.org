Remarks

Most members inherited from Bond, such as the yield and the yield-based prices, refer to the underlying plain-vanilla bond and don't take convertibility and callability into account, as QuantLib warns.

Remarks

The notional is 100 whatever the redemption, as QuantLib forces it.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The exercise, the callabilities or the schedule is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays is negative.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date); a callability is null; or the last callability is after the maturity date.

NanoQLException

The schedule is empty.
