Remarks

QuantLib's four constructors collapse into two: its quote overloads are the double ones, and the settlement-days form, whose reference date moves with the evaluation date, is Create(int, Calendar, BusinessDayConvention, double, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, VolatilityType, double). Its smile sections are flat, with the structure's volatility type and shift and no at-the-money level: by date, with the structure's reference date, for an option date and a swap tenor, and by time otherwise.
