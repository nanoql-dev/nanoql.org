Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the processes derive from it. A process is a value: its members take vectors and return new vectors and matrices, and never keep one they are given. QuantLib's observer interface is removed.

Expectation(double, Vector, double), StdDeviation(double, Vector, double) and Covariance(double, Vector, double) default to the process's discretization, as QuantLib's do; a process built without one overrides the three, or they refuse.

InitialValues(Span<double>) and Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) are the in-place forms the multi-dimensional Monte Carlo loop steps through: each defaults to its allocating form and a copy, so a process that doesn't override them keeps its bits; a process overrides them to step without allocating, by QuantLib's arithmetic.
