Remarks

A working type, as every discretized asset is: its values are views of its buffers, its exercise reads the lattice's grid into a buffer it reserves at its widest step, and the vanilla sub-asset a knock-in rolls back beside it is kept and initialized again at each reset, so a rollback allocates nothing.

The American window is bounded by its two times compared with IsOnTime as well, upstream's fix after v1.44 (#2878): the grid's last time can be an ulp off the maturity the engine starts the asset at, where QuantLib 1.44's plain comparison skips the exercise at expiry (Divergences/BarrierUpstreamFixesTests.cs).
