Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's chained setters (withYGridPoints and the others, addAdjustment, removeAdjustment) return modified copies. By default 64 grid points on each side over 7 standard deviations, 32 Gauss-Hermite points, a digital gap of 1e-5, a market rate accuracy of 1e-7, rates between 0 and 2, and Kahale's smile with exponential extrapolation.

Validate() refuses any two of Kahale's, SABR's and a custom smile, as its text says. QuantLib's refuses only all three (markovfunctional.hpp:162-167), so Kahale's and SABR's together are accepted and SABR's is ignored.

Exceptions

NanoQLException

More than one of Kahale's, SABR's and a custom smile is set; there are fewer than two grid points; the integration count, the grid's width, the digital gap or the accuracy isn't positive; Kahale's smile is set with a lower rate bound other than 0; the lower rate bound isn't below the upper; or a custom smile has no factory.
