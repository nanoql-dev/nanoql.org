Remarks

Unsealed, as QuantLib's FixedVsFloatingSwap::results is a base its swaps' results extend. A result QuantLib leaves null is null, where QuantLib throws "result not available" on reading it: the fair rate when the fixed leg's BPS is zero or unknown, the fair spread likewise for the floating leg, and both for an expired swap.

The leg accessors are QuantLib's instrument accessors, read from the per-leg arrays: the fixed leg is the first leg and the floating leg the second. Equality compares the per-leg arrays element by element, and the fair rate and spread by value.
