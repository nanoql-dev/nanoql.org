Remarks

The constructors read no evaluation date: the overnight coupons' value dates are always the full daily list, so telescopicValueDates keeps only its effect on averaged coupons, whose pricer it switches to the averaging approximation, and a telescopic swap gives QuantLib's full-schedule results.

The payment lag, adjustment and calendar apply to both legs; the overnight coupons accrue on the index's day counter.
