Remarks

An instrument QuantLib prices without an engine: Calculate(EvaluationContext) computes it. Its NPV is the package's value, the risky bond's price less the fixed leg plus the floating leg and the spread, so at the fair spread it is the par nominal, QuantLib's. Without a coupon the par coupon is used on every calculation, where QuantLib's first calculation stores it in place of the coupon for later ones.

The annuities: the fixed annuity runs over the fixed schedule, where QuantLib's runs over the float schedule's dates with the fixed day counter, and the fair spread's denominator is the float annuity, the leg the spread is paid on, where QuantLib's is that fixed annuity: an annual-fixed, quarterly-float swap's fair spread reads 0.0118084 in QuantLib for 0.0115616 on equal schedules, and its package 99.58 on 100 at the fair spread. Where the schedules and the day counters coincide the values are QuantLib's. The recovery value: the daily Euler sum runs to each period's end, where QuantLib's stops a day short, −2.74e-3 relative on annual periods. Reads between the evaluation date and a curve's later reference date are a discount and a survival of 1, where QuantLib's refuses them; before the evaluation date, a seasoned swap's past periods, they are QuantLib's, which refuses a date before the curve's (Divergences/RiskyAssetSwapTests.cs).
