The Hull-White stochastic process: the short rate r = x + α(t), x an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process of speed a and volatility σ from the curve's instantaneous forward at 0, and α(t) = f(t) + ½(σ(1 − e^(−at))/a)², f the curve's instantaneous forward.

Remarks

The drift's σ²(1 − e^(−2at))/(2a) and α's σ(1 − e^(−at))/a are written as σ²t·(1 − e^(−2at))/(2at) and σt·(1 − e^(−at))/(at), the cancellation-free forms of QuantLib's master's Vasicek: a divergence, QuantLib's drift NaN at a = 0, which the constructor accepts, and cancelling below, its α cut at QL_EPSILON (Divergences/HullWhiteProcessSmallReversionTests.cs). The drift's derivative of the forward is QuantLib's forward difference over 0.0001.
