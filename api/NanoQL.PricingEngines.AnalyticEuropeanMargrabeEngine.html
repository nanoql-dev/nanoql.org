Remarks

The value, each asset's delta and gamma, and the theta; the rho is 0 and the other Greeks are left null, as QuantLib's. Each gamma takes its asset's quantity once and the theta's carry terms each once, where QuantLib's gammas drop the quantities and its theta takes them squared (Divergences/MargrabeGreeksQuantityTests.cs).

Each forward reads its own process's curves and the payoff is discounted on the first process's risk-free curve, where QuantLib builds the second forward on the first process's discount; the theta reads the second asset's carry on the first process's curve (its yield plus the first rate less the second), as QuantLib's engine on that market restated (Divergences/TwoAssetTwoRiskFreeCurvesTests.cs). The structures are read by date, accruing nothing before their dates, and the theta runs on the clock across both processes (NanoQL.Methods.FdmBlackScholesOp.ClockAcross(System.Collections.Generic.IReadOnlyList{NanoQL.Processes.GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess})), the first process's risk-free curve where every structure shares its times, so a market on one clock keeps QuantLib's arithmetic (Divergences/TwoAssetMismatchedTimesTests.cs).
