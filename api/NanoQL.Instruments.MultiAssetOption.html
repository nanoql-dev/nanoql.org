Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the basket, Margrabe and two-asset correlation options derive from it, and so do the experimental Everest, Himalaya and Pagoda options. The option holds its payoff and exercise and is immutable; its engines are static, each returning a MultiAssetOptionResults.
