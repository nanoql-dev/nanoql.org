Remarks

An immutable object over its model, its formulation and its integration; the static Calculate builds one. Each price returns its integrations' evaluations as the additional result "numberOfEvaluations", where QuantLib's engine keeps the count in a mutable member. The model's time runs from the earlier of its curves' dates and the discounts are read by date, the forward rates between its grid's times at the instants they denote on that clock; at the model's time 0 the price is the forward's discounted intrinsic value, where QuantLib's Gauss-Laguerre integrates sin(φ(x − ln K))/φ, which doesn't decay.

References: S. Heston, A Closed-Form Solution for Options with Stochastic Volatility with Applications to Bond and Currency Options, The Review of Financial Studies 6(2), 1993, 327-343; J. Gatheral, The Volatility Surface: A Practitioner's Guide, Wiley Finance; A. Elices, Models with time-dependent parameters using transform methods: application to Heston's model, http://arxiv.org/pdf/0708.2020.
