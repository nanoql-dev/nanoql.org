Remarks

A helper is immutable. QuantLib's setTermStructure, which links the curve under construction into the helper, becomes SetTermStructure<T>(T, YieldTermStructure): it returns a copy bound to the term structure, which has rebuilt on itself whatever prices against it (an index cloned on the curve, a swap's coupons copied with that index). The helper it was called on stays unbound, and an unbound helper's ImpliedQuote(EvaluationContext) throws "term structure not set". The bootstraps bind copies of a curve's helpers to the curve under construction, and discard them when the curve is built.

A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date (QuantLib's RelativeDateBootstrapHelper) computes them in its constructor from the context's evaluation date, which it records. QuantLib's helper re-runs initializeDates() whenever the global evaluation date moves; here, a curve built for another evaluation date binds a copy of the helper re-dated for it, through InitializeDates(EvaluationContext), so a curve's helpers always have its evaluation date's dates. A helper built with explicit dates (updateDates false) is used as built.

The five dates are QuantLib's, with its fallbacks: the maturity date falls back on the latest relevant date, which falls back on the latest date, as the pillar date does; the latest date falls back on the stored pillar date. A null date is QuantLib's Date(), which the bootstraps read as earlier than every date. An extension point: the protected date fields are written by a derived constructor and by InitializeDates(EvaluationContext), on a helper under construction or on a fresh copy only.

The base does nothing.
