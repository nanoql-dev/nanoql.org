Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's map is changed in place by its operator[], += and -=; here the indexer reads, a date the basket doesn't hold reading 0 as QuantLib's inserted entry does, and operator +(TimeBasket, TimeBasket), operator -(TimeBasket, TimeBasket) and Rebin(IEnumerable<Date>) return new baskets.
