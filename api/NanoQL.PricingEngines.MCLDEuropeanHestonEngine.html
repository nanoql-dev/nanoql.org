Prices a European vanilla option by Monte Carlo under a Heston-like process.

public static OneAssetOptionResults Calculate(VanillaOption option, EvaluationContext context, HestonProcess process, int? timeSteps, int? timeStepsPerYear, bool antitheticVariate, int? requiredSamples, double? requiredTolerance, int? maxSamples, long seed)

Parameters

option VanillaOption

The option.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context.

process HestonProcess

The Heston process, or a Bates process.

timeSteps int?

The number of time steps, or null for steps per year.

timeStepsPerYear int?

The number of time steps per year, or null for a number of steps.

antitheticVariate bool

Whether antithetic variates are used.

requiredSamples int?

The number of samples, or null.

requiredTolerance double?

The absolute tolerance, or null; it takes precedence.

maxSamples int?

The most samples for a tolerance, or null for no limit.

seed long

The seed, in [1, 4294967295].

Returns

OneAssetOptionResults

The option's results, the NPV and the error estimate; the expired results when it has expired.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The option, the context or the process is null.

ArgumentException

The time steps are given neither or both ways, or aren't positive; or the strike is negative.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A number of samples is negative, or the seed is outside its range.

NanoQLException

The payoff isn't plain vanilla, neither a tolerance nor a number of samples is given, the samples can't reach the tolerance, or a step refuses.
