Remarks

A CPI cap or floor has one flow, on cumulative inflation to its maturity, so no stripping is needed, and the index's zero-inflation curve gives the at-the-money level. Strikes are quoted as yearly average inflation and become actual strikes as (1 + quote)^T, as zero-coupon inflation swaps quote; the observation lag is the quoted instruments'.

An extension point: InterpolatedCPICapFloorTermPriceSurface<TInterpolator2D> derives from it. QuantLib's surface follows the evaluation date (TermStructure(0, …)), so a derived class's factory computes the reference date and passes it in; AtmRate(Date, EvaluationContext) and Price(Date, double, EvaluationContext) read the index's fixings, from the context. The protected range checks, which QuantLib never calls, are kept; a price past the last maturity or strike extrapolates.
