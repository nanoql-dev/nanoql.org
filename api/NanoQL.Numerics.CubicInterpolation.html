Remarks

Different first-derivative approximations are implemented, both local and non-local (CubicInterpolationDerivativeApprox). Local schemes (fourth-order, parabolic, modified parabolic, Fritsch–Butland, Akima, Kruger) use only the values near xi to calculate each derivative there; the non-local spline schemes, with different boundary conditions, use all the values and solve a linear system. Local schemes produce C¹ interpolants, the spline schemes C² ones.

Hyman's monotonicity-constrained filter is also implemented: it can be applied to every scheme to ensure that, in the regions of local monotonicity of the input (three successive increasing or decreasing values), the interpolating cubic remains monotonic. A cubic already monotonic is left unchanged. For the C² interpolants the filter ensures local monotonicity at the expense of the second derivative, which is no longer continuous where it applies. Some non-linear schemes (modified parabolic, Fritsch–Butland, Kruger) are locally monotonic as they stand; the others must be filtered, at the expense of their linearity. See R. L. Dougherty, A. Edelman and J. M. Hyman, "Nonnegativity-, Monotonicity-, or Convexity-Preserving Cubic Spline and Quintic Hermite Interpolation", Mathematics of Computation, v. 52, n. 186, April 1989, pp. 471-494.

Not yet implemented in QuantLib: the fourth-order and modified parabolic schemes, and the periodic boundary condition. Immutable: the coefficients are computed at construction, and UpdateLeftConditionValue(double) and UpdateRightConditionValue(double) return a new interpolation, where QuantLib's change the boundary value for a later update(). An extension point, as in QuantLib: the named interpolations derive from it.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The sizes differ, or there are too few points: 4 for Akima's approximation or a Lagrange condition, and under the spline 3 for a NotAKnot end and 4 for two (QuantLib's master's count, #2888, where 1.44 reads past its arrays on 2 points and finds 3 singular).

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

An enum argument isn't a member of its type.

NanoQLException

The scheme or a condition isn't implemented (FourthOrder with more than 2 points, Periodic under the spline), or the spline's system has a zero pivot.
