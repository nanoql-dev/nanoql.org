Namespace
NanoQL.PricingEngines

The finite-difference Heston pricing engine for a barrier option's rebate: the rebate paid at the barrier, its Dirichlet value, or at expiry where the barrier is never hit, the knock-in leg of FdHestonBarrierEngine.

Remarks

European exercise only, as QuantLib's. The engine reports the value, delta, gamma and theta, on FdHestonBarrierEngine's grids and Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)'s times. A spot beyond the barrier is refused before the solve ("barrier touched"), where QuantLib's grid interpolation fails outside its range.
