A mortgage pool's prepayment model: the conditional prepayment rate (CPR), an annual rate, and the single monthly mortality (SMM), its monthly equivalent, at a date.

public interface IPrepayModel

Remarks

The extension point for a user's own model (a vector of CPRs, a refinancing model). An implementation is immutable and thread-safe, like every nanoql object: its rates depend on the date given alone. PSACurve and ConstantCPR are QLNet's.
