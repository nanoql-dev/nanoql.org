The analytic formula for American exercise with the payoff at expiry: a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing payoff paid at expiry if the underlying has reached the strike (knock-in: an up-and-in call or a down-and-in put), or if it hasn't (knock-out: an up-and-out call or a down-and-out put).

Remarks

An immutable calculator over the spot, the discount and dividend discount to expiry and the variance to expiry, as QuantLib's; its value. QuantLib's master after the pin keeps the price at low volatility (#2855, taken as a fix): where N(D₂) underflows or the power it multiplies overflows, their finite product is taken in logs, through log N's asymptotic tail. Only where N(D₂) is the formula's: with the strike on the spot, a knock-out is already knocked out and QuantLib sets N(D₂) to 0, which #2855's test reads as an underflow, pricing −D·K·N(D₂) where QuantLib 1.44 prices 0; here 1.44's 0 stands, a documented divergence (Divergences/AmericanPayoffAtExpiryKnockedOutTests.cs).

Below a variance of ε, QuantLib takes the spot's side of the strike for both probabilities (americanpayoffatexpiry.cpp:112-121), so a call whose forward passes the strike is worth nothing; here the underlying follows its forward, which reaches the strike by expiry where the forward is past it, and the payoff is paid at expiry if it did (knock-in) or didn't (knock-out): a documented divergence (Divergences/AmericanPayoffZeroVarianceTests.cs).

A payoff other than a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing one is refused, where QuantLib leaves the paid amount unset.
