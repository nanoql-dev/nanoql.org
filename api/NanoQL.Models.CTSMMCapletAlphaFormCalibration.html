Remarks

Immutable ((b)): it holds a copy of the alpha form given, and each pass solves on a copy of that, so a Calibrate(int, int, double, int, double) leaves the next one's form as it was. CapletAlphaFormCalibration(EvolutionDescription, PiecewiseConstantCorrelation, ReadOnlySpan<PiecewiseConstantVariance>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, CurveState, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, bool, AlphaForm, int, int, double) returns its pass's pseudo-roots and α, a and b in place of QuantLib's out-parameters, and solves on a copy of the form given.

As in QuantLib, a caplet no α fits throws ("alpha form failure"), where the other calibrations count a failure; and, as in QuantLib's master after 1.44 (#2910), so does a step whose final part fails, which QuantLib 1.44's solve returns as solved with the swap rate's last volatility left as it was, its swaptions missed (Divergences/CTSMMCapletAlphaFormCalibrationTests.cs). Two departures from master: the calibration refuses such a step in the pass it returns, and counts it in an earlier pass, whose successor re-targets the caplets and can fit, where master throws in any pass, so a calibration whose returned pass fits keeps QuantLib 1.44's numbers; and at maximum homogeneity, where the fixed minimization leaves a caplet no α, the pass runs again on QuantLib 1.44's test of the objective's value before it throws, where master throws. CapletAlphaFormCalibration(EvolutionDescription, PiecewiseConstantCorrelation, ReadOnlySpan<PiecewiseConstantVariance>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, CurveState, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, bool, AlphaForm, int, int, double), one pass alone, refuses a failed final part as master does. α bounds shorter than the rates, which QuantLib's function reads unchecked, are refused.
