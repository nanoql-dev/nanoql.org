Remarks

Immutable: the jump intensity, the mean log jump and the log jump volatility are numbers, as every quote is. As QuantLib's, the process carries its parameters for the jump-diffusion engine and implements no dynamics: its drift, diffusion and step refuse with QuantLib's texts, and so do its expectation, standard deviation and variance, which the discretization reads from them.
