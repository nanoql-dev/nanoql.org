Remarks

The methods that are implemented here to price capped / floored compounded ON coupons are highly experimental and ad-hoc. As soon as a market best practice has evolved, the pricer should be revised.

A cap or floor on the period rate is priced with the Black or Bachelier formula on the effective index fixing, its volatility dampened over the fixing period (Lyashenko and Mercurio, Looking forward to backward looking rates, section 6.3) unless the volatility given is the effective one; a daily cap or floor caps each past fixing and prices the forward part as one option in the middle of its period. Each member computes, inside the call, what QuantLib's initialize keeps: the coupon's swaplet rate, effective spread and effective index fixing.
