Remarks

Payoffs: a cash-or-nothing call pays rate + ξ·payoff·H(rate − strike), a put rate + ξ·payoff·H(strike − rate); an asset-or-nothing call pays rate + ξ·rate·H(rate − strike), a put rate + ξ·rate·H(strike − rate), with ξ = +1 for a long option and −1 for a short one. A naked option's rate in the payoff is 0.

Each digital is the spread of two capped (call) or floored (put) coupons on the underlying, at the strike plus and minus the replication's gap, so its rate reads the underlying's pricer through those coupons, each building the pricer's state once, as QuantLib's coupons each initialize it. A fixed rate (a fixing before the evaluation date, or on it when the context enforces today's historic fixings or the index holds it) pays the digital's payoff directly. The coupon keeps its underlying's ex-coupon date and fixing convention, which QuantLib's drops to their defaults, no ex-coupon date and Preceding, so its fixing date is its underlying's.

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where the digital CMS, Ibor and CMS spread coupons derive from it. A copy with another pricer (SetPricer<T>(T, FloatingRateCouponPricer?)) carries it into a copy of the underlying, as QuantLib's setPricer sets both.
