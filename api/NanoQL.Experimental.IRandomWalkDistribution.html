A distribution an isotropic random walk draws its step's radius from, over a 32-bit Mersenne Twister: QuantLib's Distribution template parameter, "compatible with std::mt19937".

[Experimental("NQL0001")]
public interface IRandomWalkDistribution

Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib. A distribution may keep state between draws, as std::normal_distribution keeps its second value; Clone() copies it.
