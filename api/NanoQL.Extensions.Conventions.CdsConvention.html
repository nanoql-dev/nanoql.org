Remarks

An option the record leaves out is the set's MakeCreditDefaultSwap record's, or QuantLib's. The builder takes no settlement days or pricing model, and its calendar only from its default record; the helpers take no cash settlement days but the swap's three (the upfront helper's are its upfront date's): a record whose value for one of them differs is refused.
