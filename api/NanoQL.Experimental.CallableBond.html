Remarks

Its engines read what QuantLib's setupArguments computes for the settlement date of the context they price under: the coupons still to be paid, and the callabilities still to be exercised, their clean prices made dirty with the accrued amount at the call date. A plain bond's engine (DiscountingBondEngine) refuses a callable bond, as QuantLib's refuses it when it sets up its arguments, with "no arguments given".

The option-adjusted spread. QuantLib's OAS, cleanPriceOAS, effectiveDuration and effectiveConvexity re-run the bond's attached engine with a spread written into its arguments. Here they take the engine as a delegate that takes the spread, such as (bond, context, spread) => TreeCallableFixedRateBondEngine.Calculate(bond, context, model, 240, null, spread), and compute as QuantLib does: on the engine's NPV, at its curve's reference date, not its settlement value, so the spread of a bond's own clean price isn't 0; with Brent's solver bracketing from QuantLib's step of 0.001; and with the spread converted between continuous compounding and the given conventions on the given curve, which serves only that conversion. An engine that ignores the spread, such as the Black engine, gives a flat price, which the solver can't bracket, as QuantLib's can't.

Unsealed, an extension point, as QuantLib's base is. Its protected constructor follows Bond's construction contract: a derived constructor writes the cash flows and builds the redemptions.

Remarks

The target is the dirty price times the face amount over 100, matched by the Black engine's settlement value, with Brent's solver from the middle of the range. Each trial builds the engine's constant volatility at the trial's value, where QuantLib's NPVSpreadHelper sets a quote its engine observes.

Remarks

The clean price is made dirty with the accrued amount at the settlement date and turned into an amount on the notional there, which the engine's NPV, at its curve's reference date, must match: QuantLib's basis, so the spread of the engine's own clean price, whose value is at the settlement date, isn't 0. An expired bond is refused, as ImpliedVolatility(BondPrice, YieldTermStructure, EvaluationContext, double, int, double, double) refuses it, where QuantLib's solve prices the expired bond's arguments.
