A calendar preset's holiday edits, which ResolveCalendarEdits(string, CalendarEdits) returns: the dates added to its holidays and the dates removed from them, applied after its rules as AddHoliday(Date) and RemoveHoliday(Date) apply them.

Remarks

Two edits are equal when they add the same dates and remove the same dates. A date is either added or removed, never both.
