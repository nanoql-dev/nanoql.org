The analytic engine for discrete geometric average-price Asian options: the geometric average of log-normal fixings is log-normal, priced by Black's formula on its forward and variance (Clewlow and Strickland, "Implementing Derivatives Models", 1998, pp. 118-123).

Remarks

The value and the Greeks QuantLib sets. It also prices an arithmetic option, as the geometric control of the arithmetic Monte Carlo engine, reading no past fixing, as QuantLib's does.

Documented divergences. The average is priced exactly under deterministic term structures: ln G is normal with mean (1/N)(the past fixings' log-sum + Σ[ln F(tᵢ) − V(tᵢ)/2]) and variance (1/N²)ΣΣV(min(tᵢ, tⱼ)) over the future fixings, F the forward and V the Black variance at the strike, each read by date, where QuantLib reads one volatility at the exercise date and the zero rates to it for every fixing, +37.4% and −18.9% off on shaped markets (Divergences/GeometricAsianTermStructureTests.cs). The Greeks are the form's: vega under a parallel shift of the volatilities, rho and dividend rho under parallel shifts of the curves, each on its own structure's clock; on a flat market they are QuantLib's. A running product that has left double's range is read through the past fixings' log-sum, where QuantLib prices ∞ (Divergences/GeometricAsianProductRangeTests.cs).

A fixing dated from the evaluation date to the clock's origin, where the structures settle after the evaluation date, is taken at the origin, the spot with no variance and no discount accrued, so as a past fixing at the spot (Divergences/AsianLateSettlementTests.cs), where QuantLib drops it from the average.
