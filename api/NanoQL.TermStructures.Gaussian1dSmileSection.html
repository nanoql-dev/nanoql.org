A smile section generated by a Gaussian1d model: on a swap index, the model's swaption prices on the swap rate fixing on the section's date, per unit of annuity; on an Ibor index, its caplet prices per unit of the caplet's accrued discount. Its volatility is the lognormal one implied from them, 0 where it can't be implied.

Remarks

If curves are attached to the index, the engine's default discounting uses them; if not, the model's curve. The at-the-money level and the annuity are computed at construction under the context given, which the section keeps for its prices. An engine is a function, nanoql's engines being static: by default QuantLib's, the Gaussian1d swaption engine on the swap index's discounting curve or the cap/floor engine on the model curve, 64 points a side and 7 standard deviations.

Two of QuantLib's defects are fixed. QuantLib's caplet starts on the fixing date, so it fixes two business days before the date of the forward it is quoted against; here it starts on the fixing's value date. And QuantLib 1.44 refuses every section on an Ibor index ("cannot calculate ATM without a BlackCapFloorEngine or BachelierCapFloorEngine"): the caplet its annuity reads is built without a strike or an engine, which its builder needs for the at-the-money strike; here it is struck at the forward, the annuity reading only its dates. On an index without a forwarding curve the level is the model curve's, and the prices are refused as QuantLib's caplet refuses them.
