The engine that values pathwise market-model products, their deltas and their vegas along given bumps of the pseudo-roots, along a log-normal Euler evolver's paths, by Giles and Glasserman's smoking adjoints: the delta engine's recursion, with each step's rates differentiated along the bumps as the path goes and combined with the derivatives as early as possible.

Remarks

The bumps: one list per step, each of the same number of bumps, each bump a matrix of the pseudo-root's size; a vega is the change along a bump given at every step. Each product's results are its value, its R deltas and its vegas, in units of the initial numeraire. It reads the model's pseudo-roots and displacements, and works in the discretely compounding money-market account.

A working type: it holds the caller's evolver and steps it, so an evolver serves one engine at a time; it holds its own clone of the product and copies of the pseudo-roots and bumps. Its buffers are sized at construction, so its valuation allocates nothing after a warm-up, and it writes its means and errors into the caller's spans, QuantLib's caller vectors.

Departures from QuantLib (documented divergences; Divergences/PathwiseVegaTests.cs): the delta engine's recursion (PathwiseAccountingEngine); RatePseudoRootJacobian's derivative; and the Jacobian takes the one-step discount factors at the step's start, where QuantLib's hands it the step's end (pathwiseaccountingengine.cpp:494) and its own test the start (marketmodel.cpp:2899-2900). The prices are QuantLib's. Refused: an evolver not in the money-market measure; and, where QuantLib reads past its buffers, a product whose rates or evolution aren't the model's, a product not finished at the evolver's last step.
