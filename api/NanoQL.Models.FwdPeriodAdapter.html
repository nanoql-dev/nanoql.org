A forward-rate market model on short forwards as one on long forwards, each spanning a period of the short ones from an offset: its curve the short model's restricted to the long rate times, its evolution the short model's to the last long reset, each step's pseudo-root the Y matrix times the short model's, the expired rates' rows zero.

Remarks

Immutable: its pseudo-roots are computed at construction, and a read returns a copy.

Two departures from QuantLib (Divergences/FwdPeriodAdapterTests.cs): with no displacements given, each long rate's is the mean of its own period's short displacements, where QuantLib's running sum is never reset and starts at the first rate, not the offset (fwdperiodadapter.cpp:54-64); and the Y matrix is YMatrix(CurveState, ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, int, int)'s, the long forwards' true elasticities. A short model whose evolution holds no time to the last long reset, which leaves QuantLib's adapter with steps it never computes, no long forward fitting the short ones, and a pseudo-root read past the steps, which QuantLib reads unchecked, are refused.
