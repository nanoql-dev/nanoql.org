Remarks

A working type (the playbook's finite-difference row): mutable, owned by one rollback and not thread-safe. Its time step rewrites its operators in place, so a step on Hundsdorfer's scheme, the engine's default, allocates nothing.

Its times run on the clock spanning both processes' structures (the internal clock across processes beside Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?)), each structure read at the instants those times denote and accruing nothing before its own date, where QuantLib runs on the first process's risk-free times and reads the second's structures at them; on one clock its arithmetic is QuantLib's (Divergences/Fd2dMismatchedTimesTests.cs).

Each asset's operator discounts at its own risk-free rate, and the mixed term adds back the second's, so the price discounts on the first process's curve, as every analytic two-asset engine does; QuantLib's mixed term adds back the first's, so its price discounts at the second's rate (Divergences/Fd2dTwoRiskFreeCurvesTests.cs). On one risk-free curve the two are the same.
