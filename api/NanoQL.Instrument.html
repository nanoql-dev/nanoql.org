Remarks

An engine prices an instrument through the four steps of QuantLib's Instrument::calculate, which the instrument owns: IsExpired(EvaluationContext), SetupExpired(), Validate() and FetchResults(InstrumentResults). QuantLib's setPricingEngine, pricingEngine and NPV() become the engine's static Calculate, which returns the results; QuantLib's result<T>(tag) and additionalResults() are Result<T>(string) and AdditionalResults on them.
