The default-event latent model: a latent model of a portfolio's joint default events, a name defaulting by a date when its latent variable falls below the inverse of its default probability. It models the events alone, no severity or exposure; the basket's names and the model's variables correspond by index.

Remarks

Unsealed and immutable. ResetBasket(Basket) returns a copy bound to a basket, where QuantLib's model holds a mutable pointer to one; bound to a basket with realized defaults, the copy keeps the live names' rows. A curve read before its reference date is a default probability of 0, where QuantLib's refuses it ("negative time").

ProbAtLeastNEvents(int, Date) sums over every default configuration, 2ⁿ per integration node, counting them in QuantLib's BigNatural, 32 bits under MSVC: from 32 names its count is 0 and its probability of one or more defaults 0, and an order above 30 shifts a 32-bit 1 past its sign (defaultprobabilitylatentmodel.hpp:295-309), both refused. DefaultCorrelation(Date, int, int) divides by the product of the probabilities and their complements, 0/0 at a probability of 0 or 1, refused.
