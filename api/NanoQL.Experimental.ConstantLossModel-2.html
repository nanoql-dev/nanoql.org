Remarks

Unsealed and immutable. QuantLib's derives the latent model and the loss model both; here it derives the latent model and implements IDefaultLossModel over it: its correlations, event counts and recoveries are the latent model's, its other statistics refuse with QuantLib's texts. SetBasket(Basket) is QuantLib's resetModel, the latent model bound to the basket's live names.
