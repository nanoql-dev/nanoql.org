A constant local volatility, with no time or asset dependence. Local and Black volatility are the same when the volatility depends at most on time, so this is a proxy for a constant Black volatility.

Remarks

QuantLib's constructors taking the volatility as a quote and as a number coincide once a quote is a number. The settlement-days form is Create(int, Calendar, double, DayCounter, EvaluationContext), whose reference date is fixed at the context's evaluation date.
