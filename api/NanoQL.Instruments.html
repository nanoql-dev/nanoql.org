AssetOrNothingPayoff

The binary asset-or-nothing payoff.

AssetSwap

A bond's asset swap: the bond's coupons and final payment against a floating leg, at par (the bond bought at par, an upfront paying its premium) or at market (the floating notional scaled by the bond's dirty price); D. O'Kane, "Introduction to Asset Swaps", Lehman Brothers European Fixed Income Research, January 2000.

AssetSwapResults

The results of an AssetSwap's calculation: the swap's results, and the fair spread, clean price and non-par repayment derived from them.

AverageBasketPayoff

A basket payoff on the weighted sum of the assets' prices.

BMASwap

A swap paying Libor against BMA coupons.

BMASwapResults

The results an engine returns for a BMASwap: the swap's, with QuantLib's inspectors derived from them, each leg's NPV and BPS, the fair Libor fraction and the fair Libor spread.

BTP

An Italian BTP (Buono del Tesoro Poliennale): a fixed-rate bond with two settlement days, a face of 100, a semiannual schedule ending on its maturity date, coupons on Actual/Actual (ISMA) paid on TARGET business days.

BarrierOption

A barrier option on a single asset: a striked payoff and an exercise, knocked in or out at a barrier.

BarrierTypeFunctions

The free functions of QuantLib's barriertype.hpp.

BasketOption

A basket option on several assets: a basket payoff and an exercise.

BasketPayoff

The payoff of a basket option: a one-dimensional payoff applied to a value accumulated from the assets' prices.

Bond

The base bond class: a leg of cash flows, its redemptions among them, with a settlement rule.

BondConventions

A fixed-rate bond preset's conventions, which ResolveBond(string, Func<BondConventions>) returns: QuantLib's values for the preset (BTP), which its class builds itself on. The trade's terms (the maturity, the coupons, the dates and the redemption) are its constructor's arguments.

BondForward

A forward contract on a bond: the bond's dirty price at spot, less its coupons paid before delivery, carried to the delivery date; a repo, for instance, at its repurchase price.

BondPrice

A bond price, clean or dirty, per 100 of notional: QuantLib's Bond::Price.

BondResults

The results an engine returns for a bond: the instrument's, and the settlement value.

CCTEU

An Italian CCTEU (Certificato di Credito del Tesoro): a floating-rate bond on six-month Euribor plus a spread, with two settlement days, a face of 100 and a semiannual schedule ending on its maturity date.

CPIBond

A zero-inflation-indexed bond: fixed coupon rates inflated by the ratio of a zero-inflation index to its base, and the inflated notional redeemed at maturity.

CPICapFloor

A CPI cap or floor: an option on the cumulative inflation from a base date to maturity, quoted as a fixed strike rate K, paying P(0,T) max(y (N [(1 + K)^T − 1] − N [I(T)/I(0) − 1]), 0), T the maturity time, N the notional, I the index and y the option's sign.

CPISwap

A zero-inflation-indexed swap: a CPI leg, fixed rates inflated by a zero-inflation index with an inflated notional at the end, against a floating leg paying an Ibor rate plus a spread, with a notional flow where it is needed.

CPISwapResults

The results an engine returns for a CPISwap: the swap's, with the fair fixed rate of the CPI leg and the fair spread over the floating leg.

Callability

An instrument's callability: a call or a put at a price, on a date.

Cap

A cap: a strip of caplets on a floating leg.

CapFloor

The base class of cap-like instruments: caps, floors and collars on a floating leg.

CapFloorDefaults

The defaults MakeCapFloor takes for an option it isn't given, which ResolveCapFloorDefaults(Func<CapFloorDefaults>) returns: QuantLib's. The leg's schedule is its index's, through MakeVanillaSwap's floating leg and that builder's defaults, so it isn't a default here.

CashOrNothingPayoff

The binary cash-or-nothing payoff.

CdsDefaults

The defaults MakeCreditDefaultSwap takes for an option it isn't given, which ResolveCdsDefaults(Func<CdsDefaults>) returns: QuantLib's, the calendar among them.

Claim

The claim associated with a default event: the amount the protection pays.

CliquetOption

A cliquet option, or ratchet option: a series of forward-starting options, each struck at a fixed percentage of the spot at the start of its period.

CmsDefaults

The defaults MakeCms takes for an option it isn't given, which ResolveCmsDefaults(Func<CmsDefaults>) returns: QuantLib's. The CMS leg's calendar is the swap index's, and the floating leg's calendar, tenor, conventions and day counter its Ibor index's, so they aren't defaults.

CmsRateBond

A CMS-rate bond, possibly capped or floored: CMS coupons on a schedule, and a single redemption.

Collar

A collar: long a cap and short a floor on the same floating leg.

ComplexChooserOption

A complex chooser option: its holder chooses at a date before exercise whether it is a call or a put, each with its own strike and exercise.

CompositeInstrument

An aggregate of other instruments: its value is the sum of its components' values, each times its multiplier, QuantLib's CompositeInstrument.

CompositeInstrumentComponent

A component of a CompositeInstrument: an instrument, the engine that prices it and its multiplier, QuantLib's std::pair<ext::shared_ptr<Instrument>, Real> with the engine the instrument carries.

CompoundOption

A compound option: the mother option, an option on its underlying daughter option.

ConstNotionalCrossCurrencyBasisSwap

A cross-currency basis swap with constant notionals, exchanged at inception and maturity.

ConstNotionalCrossCurrencyBasisSwapResults

The results an engine returns for a constant-notional cross-currency basis swap: the cross-currency swap's, with each leg's fair spread.

ConstNotionalCrossCurrencyFixedVsFloatingSwap

A cross-currency swap exchanging a fixed-rate leg in one currency against a floating-rate leg in another, with constant notionals exchanged at inception and maturity.

ConstNotionalCrossCurrencyFixedVsFloatingSwapResults

The results an engine returns for a constant-notional cross-currency fixed-vs-floating swap: the cross-currency swap's, with the fair fixed rate and the fair floating spread.

ConstNotionalCrossCurrencySwap

A generic constant-notional cross-currency swap: the notional amounts are constant and exchanged at inception and maturity.

ContinuousAveragingAsianOption

A continuous-averaging Asian option: its payoff reads the arithmetic or geometric average of the underlying over the averaging period.

ContinuousFixedLookbackOption

A continuous fixed-strike lookback option: a call pays the maximum the underlying reaches over the option's life less the strike, a put the strike less the minimum.

ContinuousFloatingLookbackOption

A continuous floating-strike lookback option: a call pays the terminal spot less the minimum the underlying reaches over the option's life, a put the maximum less the terminal spot.

ContinuousPartialFixedLookbackOption

A continuous partial-time fixed-strike lookback option (Heynen and Kat, 1994): the extremum is observed from a date after the start to expiry, and a call pays the maximum less the strike, a put the strike less the minimum.

ContinuousPartialFloatingLookbackOption

A continuous partial-time floating-strike lookback option (Heynen and Kat, 1994): the extremum is observed from the start up to a date before expiry, and a call pays the terminal spot less λ times the minimum, a put λ times the maximum less the terminal spot.

ConvertibleBond

The convertible bond base class: a bond its holder may convert into shares at the conversion ratio, under an exercise, with a schedule of calls, soft calls and puts.

ConvertibleFixedCouponBond

A convertible fixed-coupon bond: fixed-rate coupons on a notional of 100 over the schedule, paid on the schedule's convention, then its redemption.

ConvertibleFloatingRateBond

A convertible floating-rate bond: Ibor coupons on a notional of 100 over the schedule, paid on the schedule's convention, then its redemption.

ConvertibleZeroCouponBond

A convertible zero-coupon bond: its redemption alone, on a notional of 100 at the schedule's end date.

CreditDefaultSwap

A credit default swap: a premium leg of running-spread coupons against protection on a reference entity's default, with an optional upfront and an accrual rebate.

CreditDefaultSwapFunctions

QuantLib's free functions on credit default swaps.

CreditDefaultSwapResults

The results an engine returns for a CreditDefaultSwap: the instrument's, with the fair running spread and upfront, and the legs' values and sensitivities.

CrossCurrencySwap

DiscreteAveragingAsianOption

DoubleBarrierOption

DoubleStickyRatchetPayoff

The payoff of a single or double sticky or ratchet option: on a forward F, the accrual factor times the swaplet a₃F + b₃ less, by the first type t₁, t₁ max(t₁(swaplet − K₁), t₁t₂ max(t₂(swaplet − K₂), 0)), with the effective strikes K₁ = a₁I₁ + b₁ and K₂ = a₂I₂ + b₂ on the initial values I₁ and I₂, a forward rate or a coupon over its accrual factor.

EquityTotalReturnSwap
