Remarks

Its arguments are θ, κ, σ, ρ and v₀, in that order: the first four as given, v₀ a positive constant parameter. Immutable: WithParams(Vector) returns a new model. The model's time runs from the earlier of its curves' reference dates on the risk-free curve's day counter, and its parameters' grid is on that clock, where QuantLib's runs from the risk-free curve's reference date and reads the dividend curve at the risk-free curve's times; a market whose dividend curve isn't dated before the risk-free curve keeps QuantLib's times.

References: S. Heston, A Closed-Form Solution for Options with Stochastic Volatility with Applications to Bond and Currency Options, The Review of Financial Studies 6(2), 1993, 327-343; A. Elices, Models with time-dependent parameters using transform methods: application to Heston's model, http://arxiv.org/pdf/0708.2020.
