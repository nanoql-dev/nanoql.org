Remarks

Immutable. The state is the underlying divided by the strike where the payoff is striked, for the regression's numerical stability. The Legendre and first-kind Chebyshev bases are refused, as QuantLib refuses them; the second-kind Chebyshev basis is NaN above 1, so a call's regression, on its in-the-money paths, is refused by the singular value decomposition.
