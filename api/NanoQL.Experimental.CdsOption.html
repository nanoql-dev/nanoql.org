An option on a credit default swap. The side of the option is set by choosing the side of the swap: a receiver option is a right to buy an underlying swap selling protection and receiving a coupon, a payer option a right to buy one buying protection and paying a coupon.

Remarks

QuantLib's engine and its at-the-money rate read the swap's attached engine; here each takes the swap's engine as a delegate. The implied volatility is an instance method running the Black engine's body on each trial.
