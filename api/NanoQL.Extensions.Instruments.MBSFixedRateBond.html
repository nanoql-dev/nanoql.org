Remarks

Each period pays a FixedRateCoupon at the pass-through rate on the pool's balance at the period's start, its scheduled principal (AmortizingPayment), the level-payment schedule's at the WAC scaled to the balance, and its prepayment (VoluntaryPrepay), the balance after the scheduled principal times the period's single monthly mortality (SMM); the bond's leg holds them in that order on each date, as a bond's redemptions follow its coupons. The model reads the pool's age: a date of the bond's schedule plus the original length less the remaining one, so a seasoned pool starts its PSA ramp part way.

Ported from QLNet, which derives it from its AmortizingFixedRateBond; here it builds on Bond, its schedule and the level-payment notionals from AmortizingFixedRateBondFunctions. Its departures from QLNet: the notional schedule and the redemptions are the pool's expected ones, so the yields price the pool's balance at the settlement, where QLNet's read the level-payment schedule's; the flows pay on the period ends adjusted by the payment convention on the calendar, which QLNet ignores; a period of a frequency other than monthly survives the product of its months' prepayments (or, for a period counted in weeks or days, its share of a month's), where QLNet applies one month's SMM a period; the monthly yield's bracket widens past QLNet's [0, 1]% a month when that doesn't hold the root; and the yields refuse a pool fully paid off at the settlement, where QLNet's throw on its stale notional, and price the settlement date's flows as paid whatever the context's settings, where QLNet's read them. MonthlyYield(EvaluationContext) keeps QLNet's whole periods with no delay; Yield(double, int, EvaluationContext, Date?) and Price(double, int, EvaluationContext, Date?) are SIFMA's standard yield and price.

MonthlyYield(EvaluationContext)

The monthly yield at par, QLNet's: the rate per month at which the expected flows after the settlement date, discounted by whole periods per payment date with no delay, are worth the pool's balance at the settlement date.

public double MonthlyYield(EvaluationContext context)

Parameters

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context, whose evaluation date sets the settlement date.

Returns

double

The yield per month, a fraction.

Remarks

The solve is QLNet's: Brent from a guess of 0.02% a month over [0, 1]% a month, to 1e-10, in 100 evaluations; where that range doesn't bracket the root, Brent's step search from the same guess, above −100% a month. On a pool of another frequency the root is a yield per period, and the result its monthly equivalent. The settlement date's flows count as paid, as the balance takes them, whatever the context's IncludeReferenceDateEvents and IncludeTodaysCashFlows.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

context is null.

ArgumentException

The pool is fully paid off at the settlement date.

NanoQLException

The solver finds no root.

SMM(Date)

The prepayment model's single monthly mortality at a date plus the pool's age, QLNet's function. It isn't always a period's SMM: the flows read the model at the start's anniversaries, the start plus the age plus the months before the period, which a schedule date plus the age misses by a month from a start after the 28th, and a period of another frequency survives the product of its months' prepayments.

Yield(double, int, EvaluationContext, Date?)

SIFMA's standard yield (Uniform Practices, Chapter SF, SF-48): the bond-equivalent yield Y at which the expected flows due after the settlement, each discounted by (1 + Y/2)^(−2T), are worth the dollar price.

public double Yield(double cleanPrice, int delayDays, EvaluationContext context, Date? settlementDate = null)

Parameters

cleanPrice double

The price per 100 of the current balance, without accrued interest.

delayDays int

The actual delay, in days from each period's end to its payment (SF-44).

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context, whose evaluation date sets the settlement date.

settlementDate Date?

The settlement date; the bond's when null.

Returns

double

The yield, a fraction a year, compounded semi-annually.

Remarks

The dollar price is the clean price per 100 of the balance at the start of the period holding the settlement, times that balance, plus the interest accrued at the pass-through rate from that period's start (SF-46). T is in years on SIFMA's 30/360 (SF-44), from the settlement to each period's unadjusted end, plus the delay days, so the payment convention doesn't enter; SIFMA's yield is semi-annual whatever the pool's frequency.
