Remarks

QuantLib sets the pricer on the cash flows a leg holds; nanoql's cash flows are immutable, so these return a new leg, or a copy of the swap holding it, as CouponPricerFunctions does for coupon pricers. Cash flows that aren't FX-reset coupons or exchanges are kept as they are.
