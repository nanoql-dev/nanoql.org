Remarks

Immutable: the calibration runs at construction (the playbook's LazyObject row), where QuantLib's model calibrates at its first read. With logging, LogEntries holds each step's density and mesher, filled once, where QuantLib's log read recomputes the calibration for the same values. The leverage function is FixedLocalVolSurface on the calibrated values; the calibration itself reads its working surface as QuantLib's does (NanoQL.TermStructures.FixedLocalVolWorkingSurface).

QuantLib appends the mandatory dates' times to its exponential grid unsorted and builds the leverage surface on that list, which refuses a mandatory date inside the period ("dates must be sorted unique!") and one at its end ("need strikes for every time step") (hestonslvfdmmodel.cpp:325-330, :412). Here the surface and the loop run on the time grid's times, sorted and made unique; a mandatory date on the grid's own time is QuantLib's calibration without it.

The model's times are a clock's, from the earliest reference date of the curves and the local volatility on the risk-free curve's day counter, and each structure is read at the instants they denote: QuantLib's grid runs from the risk-free curve's reference date and reads the dividend curve and the local volatility at its times. Where every structure shares the risk-free curve's day counter and reference date, QuantLib's reads and bits.

The first step's density, the Green's function, is the density of the process the forward operator evolves, its variance's volatility the mixing factor times σ, as the variance mesher's: QuantLib's takes the unmixed σ, a density 76% to 113% off the mixed process's own in L1 on the Gaussian algorithm at mixing factors 0.5 and 0.2, where the mixed σ's is within 2% to 10%. Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

A structure, the model or the settings are null.

ArgumentException

The end date is the default date, the clock's reference date isn't before it ("reference date must be smaller than final calibration date"), or it lies beyond the local volatility ("final calibration maturity exceeds local volatility surface"), with QuantLib's messages.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

The settings' scheme isn't one the calibration runs ("Unknown scheme type").

NanoQLException

A structure can't be read, or a step can't be solved.
