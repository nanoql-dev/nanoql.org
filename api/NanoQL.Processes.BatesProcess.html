The Bates process: the Heston square-root stochastic-volatility process with log-normal jumps, dS = (r − q − λm)S dt + √v S dW₁ + (e^J − 1)S dN, dv = κ(θ − v) dt + σ√v dW₂, dW₁dW₂ = ρ dt, N a Poisson process of intensity λ and J normal with mean ν and standard deviation δ, m = e^{ν + δ²/2} − 1.

Remarks

Immutable, a HestonProcess as QuantLib's derives from it: its drift less λm, two more factors, the first drawing the step's number of jumps through the inverse Poisson distribution and the second their sizes, and QuantLib's default discretization, full truncation, where the Heston process's is the quadratic-exponential martingale scheme. The in-place Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) allocates nothing where the Heston step doesn't.

The truncation, reflection and quadratic-exponential schemes step without allocating, by QuantLib's arithmetic, so the values are Evolve(double, Vector, double, Vector)'s bit for bit; the exact schemes step through it.
