Remarks

A static class. The volatility is the quoted forward yield volatility, not a price volatility: it is turned into a forward price volatility through the bond's forward yield and modified duration at the exercise date. QuantLib's quote constructor builds a constant volatility on the null calendar with no settlement days, whose reference date follows the evaluation date: its overload builds that volatility for the context of each call. QuantLib notes the engine as yet untested.

The engine reads no spread, so the OAS family can't bracket a spread through it, as QuantLib's can't.
