PVDifference(double, ImmutableArray<CashFlow>, double, Date, EvaluationContext)

The present value of the cash flows still to come at a yield per payment date, less a face amount: each date's flows discounted by (1 + yield / 100)^k, k counting the distinct payment dates after the settlement from 1. It counts whole periods per payment date, with no day count and no delay, as QLNet does (MonthlyYield(EvaluationContext)'s yield); SIFMA's yield is Yield(double, int, EvaluationContext, Date?).

public static double PVDifference(double faceAmount, ImmutableArray<CashFlow> cashflows, double yield, Date settlement, EvaluationContext context)
