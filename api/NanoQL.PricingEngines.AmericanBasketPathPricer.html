The early-exercise path pricer of a basket option for the Longstaff-Schwartz engine: the assets' prices at a step, scaled by the strike, as the regression's state, and its basis, the polynomials of several variables with the payoff appended.

Remarks

Immutable. The state is a new vector per call, the Longstaff-Schwartz pricer keeping each as one call's working state.

The state is scaled by 1/|K|, and by 1 at a zero strike, where QuantLib scales by 1/K: at a zero strike (an exchange option) its scale is infinite and the price NaN, and at a negative one the scale's sign swaps the minimum and the maximum (Divergences/AmericanBasketStrikeScalingTests.cs); a positive scale leaves the regression's polynomial span unchanged, so a positive strike keeps QuantLib's arithmetic.
