The forward G2 stochastic process: G2Process's in the forward measure of a time T, its state again shifted so that z₁ + z₂ = r(t), with the T-forward corrections to the drift and the conditional expectation.

Remarks

The drift's corrections, QuantLib's −(σ²/a)(1 − e^(−a(T−t))) − (ρση/b)(1 − e^(−b(T−t))) and its mirror, are written as −σ²τf(aτ) − ρσητf(bτ), τ = T − t, f(x) = (1 − e^(−x))/x; the expectation's, QuantLib's Mx_T and My_T, as σ²τ₁f(aτ₁)[τ₂f(aτ₂) + ½e^(−aτ₂)τ₁f(aτ₁)] + ρσητ₁[τ₁D(aτ₁, bτ₁) + τ₂f(bτ₂)f((a+b)τ₁)], τ₁ = t − s, τ₂ = T − t, D(u, v) = (f(u) − f(u+v))/v, and their mirrors, every term positive; the correlation and φ as G2Process's: a divergence, QuantLib's terms cancelling at small reversion.

QuantLib's constructor leaves the forward measure time unset; WithForwardMeasureTime(double) gives a copy at one, and the drift and the expectation refuse to read an unset one.
