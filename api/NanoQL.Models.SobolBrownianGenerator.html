The Sobol Brownian generator for market-model simulations: each path a Sobol sequence of the factors times the steps, turned into Gaussian variates by the inverse cumulative normal and Brownian-bridged by the base's ordering.

Remarks

A working type. Its seed is explicit, in [1, 4294967295], as SobolRsg takes it: QuantLib's default 0 draws one from the clock.
