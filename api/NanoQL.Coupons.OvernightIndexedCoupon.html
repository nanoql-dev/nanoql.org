Remarks

The constructor attaches QuantLib's default pricer: a CompoundingOvernightIndexedCouponPricer for a compounded coupon, an ArithmeticAveragedOvernightIndexedCouponPricer for an averaged one; SetPricer<T>(T, FloatingRateCouponPricer?) returns a copy holding another.

Telescopic value dates. The flag is taken, with QuantLib's check (a lookback without the observation shift can't use it), and on an averaged coupon it selects the averaging pricer's approximation, as QuantLib's does. Its date optimisation isn't ported: the value dates are always the full daily list, so the coupon reads no evaluation date and gives QuantLib's full-schedule result on any date. The compounding pricer still telescopes the forward part wherever CanApplyTelescopicFormula holds. QuantLib's shortened dates, which can give wrong results once the coupon ages, are a documented divergence.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

overnightIndex is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

The lookback or the lockout is negative, the rounding precision is outside [0, 16], or the averaging method isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The dates are out of order; the gearing is zero; the lockout isn't shorter than the period; the telescopic flag is given with a lookback that can't use it; or an averaged coupon has a lookback, a lockout or the observation shift.

NanoQLException

The rate computation spans fewer than two value dates.
