The one-factor copula models' base: John Hull and Alan White, "The Perfect Copula", June 2006. The variables Yᵢ = a·M + √(1 − a²)·Zᵢ, M and Zᵢ independent of zero mean and unit variance, their correlation a² the copula's; a name's default by t is Yᵢ below F_Y⁻¹(Qᵢ(t)), so that, given M = m, it defaults with probability F_Z((F_Y⁻¹(p) − a·m)/√(1 − a²)). The densities of M and Z are the derived models'; F_Y is tabulated by them unless it is analytic, and the integrals over M are Euler sums on a midpoint grid.

Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's lazy copula observes its correlation quote; here a correlation is a double and a derived model tabulates F_Y at construction, in QuantLib's loop order. The correlation lies in [0, 1]: QuantLib's accepts [−1, 1] and takes its square root, a negative one reading NaN (onefactorcopula.hpp:110-112, onefactorcopula.cpp:35). At 1 the conditional probability divides by √(1 − c) = 0 and reads the comonotonic step, QuantLib's continuous limit, kept: the Gaussian's integral at p = 0.05 reads 0.0545030778829 at 1 and 0.0545030775379 at 0.9999, the Euler grid's 0.0545 for 0.05 at such correlations QuantLib's own \todo (onefactorcopula.hpp:100), and CheckMoments(double) refuses both, as QuantLib's does (Conventions/AnalyticLossModelConventionTests.cs).
