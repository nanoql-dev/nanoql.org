Remarks

While deriving from the hazard rate structure, its hazard rate is the deterministic part of the model alone, which derived classes give where there is one (none here); the probabilities depend on it and the stochastic part. The density is that deterministic hazard rate times the survival over the model's bond price, QuantLib's, kept: 0 here where −dS/dt is 0.0187. The model's initial intensity is its dynamics' short rate at the process's x0. QuantLib's reference-date constructor drops its calendar argument; here it is kept. The structure whose reference date follows the evaluation date is built by Create(OneFactorAffineModel, int, Calendar, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, IEnumerable<double>?, IEnumerable<Date>?).

Curve extrapolation means something else on different curves: on curves matched to the market the credit curves would be asked to extrapolate; on affine models over a static term structure it is this one. To be done, as in QuantLib: the jumps' treatment in the conditional probabilities, and the remaining constructors.
