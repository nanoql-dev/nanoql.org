Remarks

The coupon fixes on a StubIborCouponWeightedIndex of the indices the selection picks for its accrual dates, which is its IborIndex. Its fixing reads no at-par choice, so it needs no pricer; its rate does, as any Ibor coupon's.

Its fixing days are a plain count: QuantLib's stub index reads Null<Natural>() as a count of days, and its only caller, IborLeg, passes the leg's coupon's resolved count.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The selection is empty; the coupon is in arrears; a candidate's value date isn't the accrual start; two candidates share a maturity; or no two maturities bracket the end date.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

fixingDays is negative, or fixingConvention isn't a member of its type.
