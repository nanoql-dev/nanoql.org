Remarks

The futures price and strike are quoted as 100 less a rate; the solve is on the rate, so a call on the futures is a put on the rate. Where the rate strike, 100 less the strike, is above the rate forward, 100 less the futures price, QuantLib solves a call on the rate at the put's price, and otherwise a put on the rate at the call's price; it reads only that price.

Immutable: its value is solved once, at construction, from the guess given (§2.2's quote row). QuantLib's quote starts each solve after the first from the last one's result; here a new price is a new quote, solved from its caller's guess, a documented divergence. A solve that fails is refused, as QuantLib's is (eurodollarfuturesquote.cpp:58-75). QuantLib's isValid isn't ported.
