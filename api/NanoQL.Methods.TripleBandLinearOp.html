A general triple-band operator in one direction of a multi-dimensional layout: each point's row reads the point and its two neighbours in that direction, reflected at the grid's edges.

Remarks

A working type: mutable, owned by one computation and not thread-safe. Apply(ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) and SolveSplitting(ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, double, Span<double>) write into the caller's span, which mustn't overlap the input, through a workspace the operator holds; Axpyb(Vector, TripleBandLinearOp, TripleBandLinearOp, Vector) rewrites the operator in place. The composition methods Mult(Vector), MultR(Vector) and the two Add(TripleBandLinearOp) forms return new operators, as QuantLib's do, since they run while an operator is built.

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the derivative operators derive from it and fill the protected bands in their constructors. QuantLib's swap and move members are C++ value semantics: Clone() and references replace them.
