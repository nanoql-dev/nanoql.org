Namespace NanoQL.Coupons

Classes

AmortizingPayment

An amortizing payment: a simple cash flow that more detailed cash-flow analysis can tell apart.

AnalyticHaganPricer

The pricing of a CMS coupon by static replication with Hagan's analytic forms (his 3.4c, 3.5b and 3.5c): a vanilla swaption's price plus a convexity term in the g-function's slope at the forward.

ArithmeticAveragedOvernightIndexedCouponPricer

The pricer for arithmetically averaged overnight-indexed coupons.

AverageBMACoupon

A coupon paying a BMA index, whose rate is a weighted average of the relevant fixings: each fixing weighs the actual calendar days for which it is valid within the interest period. Before the weights are computed, the fixing schedule is adjusted for the index's fixing-day gap.

AverageBMALeg

A builder of a sequence of average-BMA coupons.

AveragingMultipleResetsPricer

The averaging pricer of multiple-resets coupons: each reset's fixing weighted by its accrual time, over the accrual period, geared and spread.

BachelierYoYInflationCouponPricer

The Bachelier-formula pricer of capped and floored year-on-year inflation coupons.

BlackAveragingOvernightIndexedCouponPricer

The Black pricer of capped and floored averaged overnight-indexed coupons.

BlackCompoundingOvernightIndexedCouponPricer

The Black pricer of capped and floored compounded overnight-indexed coupons.

BlackIborCouponPricer

The Black-formula pricer for Ibor coupons, capped, floored or not.

BlackYoYInflationCouponPricer

The Black-formula pricer of capped and floored year-on-year inflation coupons.

CPICashFlow

A cash flow paying the performance of a zero-inflation (CPI) index: the notional times the ratio of the index observed on the observation date, with the lag, to its base. It isn't a coupon: it has no accruals.

CPICoupon

A coupon paying the performance of a zero-inflation (CPI) index: a fixed rate times the ratio of the index observed at the accrual end, with the lag, to its base value.

CPICouponPricer

The base pricer of CPI coupons: the swaplet is the fixed rate times the index ratio; a volatility-dependent optionlet needs a derived pricer.

CPILeg

A helper class building a sequence of CPI coupons, with the inflated notional at the end: QuantLib's CPILeg.

CappedFlooredCmsCoupon

A CMS coupon with a cap, a floor or both on its rate: a CappedFlooredCoupon over a CmsCoupon it builds.

CappedFlooredCoupon

A floating-rate coupon with a cap, a floor or both on its rate.

CappedFlooredIborCoupon

An Ibor coupon with a cap, a floor or both on its rate: a CappedFlooredCoupon over an IborCoupon it builds.

CappedFlooredOvernightIndexedCoupon

A capped or floored overnight-indexed coupon: the cap and the floor apply to the coupon's effective period rate (the default) or to its daily rates.

CappedFlooredYoYInflationCoupon

A year-on-year inflation coupon with a cap, a floor or both on its rate.

CashFlowVectorsFunctions

Cash-flow vector builders: QuantLib's free functions of cashflowvectors.hpp.

CashFlows

Cash-flow analysis functions.

CmsCoupon

A CMS coupon: a floating-rate coupon fixing on a swap index.

CmsCouponPricer

The base class of the pricers for CMS coupons, on a swaption volatility.

CmsLeg

A builder of a sequence of CMS coupons, capped, floored or not.

CompoundingMultipleResetsPricer

The compounding pricer of multiple-resets coupons: the product of each reset's growth, less one, over the accrual period, geared and spread.

CompoundingOvernightIndexedCouponPricer

The pricer for compounded overnight-indexed coupons.

Coupon

The base class of coupons accruing over a fixed period: still abstract, it gives derived classes the accrual-period calculations.

CouponPricerFunctions

Setting pricers on a leg's coupons: QuantLib's setCouponPricer and setCouponPricers, which return a new leg here, where QuantLib's change the coupons.

DigitalCmsCoupon

A CMS coupon with a digital call, a digital put or both: QuantLib's DigitalCmsCoupon.

DigitalCmsLeg

A builder of a sequence of digital CMS coupons: QuantLib's DigitalCmsLeg.

DigitalCoupon

A floating-rate coupon with a digital call, a digital put or both: QuantLib's DigitalCoupon, priced by call and put spread replication through capped and floored coupons on its underlying.

DigitalIborCoupon

A Ibor coupon with a digital call, a digital put or both: QuantLib's DigitalIborCoupon.

DigitalIborLeg

A builder of a sequence of digital Ibor coupons: QuantLib's DigitalIborLeg.

DigitalReplication

The replication strategy used to price the digital option embedded in a digital coupon, and the gap of the call spread that replicates it.

Dividend

A predetermined cash flow paid on a stock: a dividend.

DividendFunctions

The free functions of QuantLib's dividend.hpp.

EquityCashFlow

A cash flow paying an equity index's ratio, I(T)/I(0), or its growth, I(T)/I(0) - 1, the default, times the notional; with a pricer, the ratio the pricer gives, such as a quanto-adjusted one.

EquityCashFlowFunctions

QuantLib's free function setCouponPricer for equity cash flow pricers, on immutable legs.

EquityCashFlowPricer

The base of the equity cash flow pricers: the index ratio a flow pays, per unit of notional.

EquityQuantoCashFlowPricer

The quanto pricer of an equity cash flow paid in another currency: the index's ratio forecast on its dividend curve quanto-adjusted by the equity and exchange-rate volatilities and their correlation, struck at the index's fixing, and discounted on the payment currency's curve.

FixedDividend

A predetermined cash amount paid on a stock.

FixedRateCoupon

A coupon paying a fixed interest rate.

FixedRateLeg

A helper class building a sequence of fixed-rate coupons.

FloatingRateCoupon

The base class of coupons paying a floating rate: an index fixing, geared and spread.

FloatingRateCouponExtensions

FloatingRateCoupon's modifier, which returns a modified copy of the receiver, keeping its static type, where QuantLib's setPricer changes the coupon.

FloatingRateCouponPricer

The base class of pricers for floating-rate coupons.

FractionalDividend

A predetermined fraction of the underlying's value, paid on a stock.

GFunction

Hagan's G(R): the payment-date discount over the annuity as a function of the swap rate, under a model of the yield curve, which the CMS replication integrates against; GFunctionFactory builds them.

GFunctionFactory

The factory of Hagan's g-functions, one per yield-curve model (GFunctionFactoryYieldCurveModel).

HaganPricer

The base class of the pricing of a CMS coupon by static replication, as in Hagan's "Conundrums..." article: a caplet's, a floorlet's and the swaplet's price from vanilla swaptions, through a g-function of the yield-curve model.

IborCoupon

A coupon paying an Ibor index's fixing, geared and spread.

IborCouponPricer

The base class of pricers for Ibor coupons, capped, floored or not.

IborLeg

A builder of a sequence of Ibor coupons, capped, floored or not.

IndexedCashFlow

A cash flow paying an index ratio: the notional times I(T)/I(0), or times I(T)/I(0) - 1 when only the growth is paid. It isn't a coupon: it has no accruals.

InflationCoupon

The base class of coupons paying a rate from an inflation index, whose pricer computes it: QuantLib's InflationCoupon.

InflationCouponExtensions

QuantLib's InflationCoupon::setPricer, which changes a coupon, as a copy of the receiver's type.

InflationCouponPricer

The base class of inflation-coupon pricers: QuantLib's InflationCouponPricer, which differs from the floating-rate pricers because it deals with inflation indexes and their lags (the option may look three months back and the index two).

InflationCouponPricerFunctions

QuantLib's free function setCouponPricer for inflation-coupon pricers, on immutable legs, with the swap and bond forms beside it, as CouponPricerFunctions has for floating-rate pricers.

LinearTsrPricer

The pricing of a CMS coupon on a linear terminal swap rate model, whose slope is linked to a Gaussian short-rate model (Andersen and Piterbarg, Interest Rate Modeling, 16.3.2).

LinearTsrPricerSettings

The linear TSR pricer's integration bounds: QuantLib's LinearTsrPricer::Settings, whose with… members return a copy here.

MarketQuotedOptionPricer

A vanilla option on a swap rate priced on a swaption volatility's smile section: Black's formula on a zero-shift lognormal volatility, Bachelier's on a normal one.

MultipleResetsCoupon

A coupon paying a rate compounded or averaged from several fixings over its accrual period, one per period of its reset schedule, each fixing its fixing days before its period's start.

MultipleResetsLeg

A helper class building a sequence of multiple-resets coupons: QuantLib's MultipleResetsLeg.

MultipleResetsPricer

The base of the multiple-resets coupon pricers: each reset's fixing plus the rate spread, which the compounding and averaging pricers combine into the coupon's rate. Its prices and options are QuantLib's refusals.

NumericHaganPricer

The pricing of a CMS coupon by static replication, as in Hagan's "Conundrums..." article, by numerical integration over the prices of vanilla swaptions.

NumericHaganPricerIntegrationLimits

The numeric Hagan pricer's integration limits after an optionlet's price: QuantLib's lowerLimit(), upperLimit() and stdDeviations(), which that price sets in the pricer's mutable members.

OvernightIndexedCoupon

A coupon paying the interest, depending on the averaging convention, due to daily overnight fixings: compounded or averaged, with QuantLib's lookback, lockout and observation-shift conventions.

OvernightIndexedCouponPricer

The base pricer for coupons indexed to an overnight rate, compounded or averaged, capped or floored.

OvernightIndexedCouponPricerExtensions

OvernightIndexedCouponPricer's modifiers, which return a modified copy of the receiver, keeping its static type, where QuantLib's change the pricer.

OvernightLeg

A helper class building a sequence of overnight-indexed coupons.

RangeAccrualFloatersCoupon

A range-accrual floating-rate coupon: the geared index rate plus the spread, accrued over the share of its observation dates on which the index fixes between a lower and an upper trigger.

RangeAccrualLeg

A helper class building a sequence of range-accrual floating-rate coupons: QuantLib's RangeAccrualLeg.

RangeAccrualPricer

The base of the range-accrual coupon pricers: the coupon's rate is its price over its accrual and the discount at its payment date; its options are QuantLib's refusals.

RangeAccrualPricer.State

A range-accrual coupon's state for one call: QuantLib's pricer members after initialize.

RangeAccrualPricerByBgm

The range-accrual pricer in the BGM model: each observation's range is a pair of digitals on the index's forward rate, the forwards from the coupon's start S and its end T lognormal with the expiry and payment smiles' volatilities and a correlation, priced without the smile, with the smile through a call spread, or with a smile correction.

Redemption

A bond redemption: a simple cash flow that more detailed cash-flow analysis can tell apart.

SimpleCashFlow

A predetermined cash flow: an amount paid on a date.

StubIborCoupon

An Ibor coupon for a broken period, fixing on indices chosen by a StubIndexSelection.

StubIborCouponWeightedIndex

An index fixing as a fixed-weight linear combination of Ibor indices: QuantLib's StubIborCoupon::WeightedIndex.

StubIndexSelection

The index selection for irregular Ibor coupons.

TimeBasket

A distribution of values over dates: a map from each date to its value, in date order.

UnitDisplacedBlackYoYInflationCouponPricer

The unit-displaced Black-formula pricer of capped and floored year-on-year inflation coupons.

VanillaOptionPricer

The price of a vanilla option on a swap rate, deflated: the replication's building block in Hagan's pricers.

YoYInflationCoupon

A coupon paying a year-on-year inflation rate, with a gearing and a spread.

YoYInflationCouponPricer

The base pricer of year-on-year inflation coupons, capped and floored ones included: the swaplet is the gearing times the coupon's fixing plus the spread; a volatility-dependent optionlet needs a derived pricer.

YoYInflationLeg

A helper class building a sequence of year-on-year inflation coupons: QuantLib's yoyInflationLeg.

ZeroInflationCashFlow

A cash flow paying a zero-inflation index ratio between the start and end dates less the observation lag: with the dates in June and a three-month lag, the ratio is taken between March fixings. It isn't a coupon: it has no accruals.
