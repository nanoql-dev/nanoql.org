RiemannLiouvilleIntegral<T>(ReadOnlySpan<T>, double, double)

The product-trapezoidal Riemann-Liouville fractional integral I^α y(t_N) = (1/Γ(α)) ∫₀^{t_N} (t_N − s)^{α−1} y(s) ds, α ≥ 0, from the values of y on a uniform grid, by integrating y's piecewise linear interpolant against the kernel: at α = 1 the trapezoidal rule, and the identity as α falls to 0.

public static T RiemannLiouvilleIntegral<T>(ReadOnlySpan<T> y, double alpha, double dt) where T : INumberBase<T>

Parameters

y ReadOnlySpan<T>

The values on the grid, at least two.

alpha double

The integration order, not negative.

dt double

The grid's spacing.

Returns

T

The integral.

Type Parameters

T

The values' type: double, or Complex.

Remarks

It builds the weights at every call; integrations on one (α, N) grid can share a RiemannLiouvilleWeights.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

alpha is negative, there are fewer than two values, or dt isn't positive.
