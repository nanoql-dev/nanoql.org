Remarks

The helper's dates are those of the index's period at the maturity less the swap's observation lag: with an interpolated observation, from that period's start to the next one's, the pillar chosen as QuantLib chooses it; otherwise the period's start alone. The swap's two legs share one annual schedule, generated backwards from the maturity, unadjusted. Without a start date the swap starts on the evaluation date, which the helper's dates follow; with one, they don't.

The helper bound to a curve (SetTermStructure<T>(T, YoYInflationTermStructure)) holds the index cloned on the curve, and the swap rebuilt on it. Its implied quote is the swap's fair rate, priced by the discounting swap engine on the nominal curve; with no nominal curve, it refuses with QuantLib's engine's text unless the swap has expired, and then with QuantLib's "result not available".

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
