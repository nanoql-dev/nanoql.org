Class OrthogonalProjections

Namespace
NanoQL.Numerics
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

Given vectors w_i, the vectors x_i with x_i orthogonal to every w_j but w_i and ⟨x_i, w_i⟩ = ⟨w_i, w_i⟩: Gram-Schmidt on the others, then the projection onto their orthogonal space, each vector valid where its multiplier is below the cut-off and the others independent to the tolerance.

Remarks

Immutable: its vectors are computed at construction, and its reads are immutable. As in QuantLib, an invalid vector's projection is the last valid one's before it, zeros before any. A vector read past the vectors, which QuantLib reads unchecked, is refused.
