Remarks

The coupon holds its pricer, set at construction by a leg builder or by SetPricer<T>(T, InflationCouponPricer?), which returns a copy; each kind of coupon accepts only its kind of pricer. Pricers are stateless functions of the coupon and the context. The rate is computed on each call, where QuantLib caches it.

The coupon has no gearing or spread: those belong to year-on-year coupons, not zero-inflation ones. Inflation indexes have no day counters or calendars. An extension point.
