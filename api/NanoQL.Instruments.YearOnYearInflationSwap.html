A year-on-year inflation-indexed swap, quoted as a fixed rate K: each period exchanges N K against the index's year-on-year growth, N [I(t_i)/I(t_i - 1) - 1], plus a spread.

Remarks

The type, payer or receiver, refers to the fixed leg. The fixed leg is the first leg, fixed-rate coupons on the fixed schedule; the year-on-year leg is the second, YoYInflationLeg's coupons with its default pricer, paying on the payment calendar, since an inflation index has none. Both legs pay on the payment convention.

The constructor reads no evaluation date. An engine's results are a YearOnYearInflationSwapResults, whose fair rate and spread FetchResults(InstrumentResults) derives from the legs' BPS, as QuantLib's swap derives them from a swap engine's results; QuantLib's own arguments and engine classes aren't ported, since no engine uses them. A type that isn't a member of SwapType is refused, where QuantLib takes it as a receiver.
