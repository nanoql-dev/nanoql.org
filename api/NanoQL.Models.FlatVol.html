The flat-volatility market model: each rate with a constant volatility until its fixing, correlated by a piecewise-constant correlation, each step's pseudo-root the rank-reduced square root of its covariance.

Remarks

Immutable: its pseudo-roots are computed at construction, and a read returns a copy.

Two inputs whose reads QuantLib leaves undefined are refused (Divergences/MarketModelUndefinedReadsTests.cs): correlation times ending before the last evolution time, which QuantLib reads past (an abort, 0xC0000409), and a correlation with fewer rates than the model, whose matrices QuantLib reads past.
