Remarks

For each swap length and index the market's spot CMS leg NPV is implied from the spot swap's Ibor leg (its NPV and BPS on the discounting curve at its reference date, and the mid spread), and a forward CMS leg is the difference of two spot ones; the model prices each forward-starting CMS swap's CMS leg with the index's pricer and sums them into spot legs, whose equilibrium spread over the Ibor leg is the model's spread.

QuantLib's market is a lazy object that its calibration reprices in place, giving its pricers a new volatility and mean reversion. This one computes at construction, under the context it is given, and Reprice(SwaptionVolatilityStructure?, double?, EvaluationContext) returns a new market whose pricers are copies with the new volatility and reversion, its legs re-priced through CouponPricerFunctions; QuantLib's pricers, shared with other legs, change with its reprice, which an immutable pricer can't. QuantLib's quotes are their values. Immutable.
