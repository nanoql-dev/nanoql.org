Remarks

A working type (DiscretizedAsset), built for one calculation from the bond's arguments at the context's settlement date. Times are the risk-free curve's year fractions from the bond's settlement date, moved to the closest times of the given grid. The dividends not yet paid at settlement are added back to the underlying at each node, each discounted from its date on the risk-free curve. A node's discount rate blends the risk-free rate and the risk-free rate plus the credit spread by its conversion probability.

The American window is bounded by its two times compared with IsOnTime as well, upstream's fix after v1.44 (#2878): the grid's last time can be an ulp off the maturity the engine starts the asset at, where QuantLib 1.44's plain comparison skips the conversion at expiry or rejects a window ending there (Divergences/AmericanBoundsOnTimeTests.cs).

Its conversion probabilities and blended rates are double-buffered, as its values are, so a rollback allocates none; the underlying's grid is read into a buffer reserved at its widest step.
