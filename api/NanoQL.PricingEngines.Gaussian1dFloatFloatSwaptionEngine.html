The Gaussian1d float-float swaption engine: the swaption's value rolled back on the model's state grid from its last event, each coupon fixing and exercise date, to today, each leg's coupons estimated at their fixing (an Ibor index's forward rate, a swap index's rate, a swap spread index's two rates geared), capped and floored.

Remarks

All float coupons with a fixing date on or after the respective option expiry are part of the exercise into right; a redemption flow belongs to it when the start date of the regular coupon period paid with it is on or after the exercise. Cash-settled (par yield curve) swaptions are not supported. The additional result underlyingValue is the underlying's value from today, with every fixing after today, or on it too with includeTodaysExercise; the probabilities, another, are QuantLib's.

Immutable, a BasketGeneratingEngine: Calculate(FloatFloatSwaption, EvaluationContext) prices with the engine's model and parameters, and the static Calculate builds the engine for one call. Four of QuantLib's defects are fixed. A rebate is valued in the state, on the discount curve, as the nonstandard engine values it, where QuantLib's reads the model curve's bond at the mean state; the model reads a discount curve on another clock by date at every instant QuantLib derives from a date (an event's numeraire, the Digital probabilities' discount); flat payoff extrapolation's right tail reads the last node; and the calibration basket's initial guess refuses an expiry with no leg 1 coupon left or with leg 1's remaining nominals summing to zero, as QuantLib's master does (#2845).
