The caplets and coterminal swaptions on the long forwards made of a period of rates each, from an offset: at each long forward's reset, a caplet on the long forward and a swaption on the long coterminal swap from it, the first half of the products the caplets and the second the swaptions.

Remarks

A working type; the payoffs are shared, immutable.

QuantLib's design, kept and documented: each value is paid at its long forward's start, its end's discount inside the value (the caplet's τ times the period's discount ratio), and a value of 0 pays nothing.
