Class EquityFXVolSurface

Namespace
NanoQL.Experimental
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The base of equity and FX volatility (smile) surfaces, with the at-the-money forward volatility and variance between two times. Volatilities are on an annual basis; only without a smile does the at-the-money forward volatility make sense.
