The two-dimensional Black-Scholes finite-difference solver: the Black-Scholes operator in direction 0 of a mesher in the logarithm of the underlying by a second direction the operator leaves alone, such as the swing option's rights used, rolled back by the two-dimensional solver, read at a spot and a second coordinate.

Remarks

A working type built per call: computed at construction, where QuantLib's lazy object computes at the first read, and immutable after. The second coordinate is read at its logarithm, as QuantLib reads it: a value at 1 is the value at the second direction's location 0.
