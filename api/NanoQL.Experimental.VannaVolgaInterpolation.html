The Vanna-Volga interpolation of a smile between three volatilities in strike space: the at-the-money call's Black price corrected by the three quoted calls' market premia over their at-the-money ones, weighted by each strike's vega, and turned back into a volatility.

Remarks

The second point is the at-the-money volatility. QuantLib's update(), which computes the premia and vegas and appends them to its vectors on every call, runs once at construction: nanoql's interpolations have no update. Immutable once built; it copies its inputs. It has no primitive, derivative or second derivative, as QuantLib's.
