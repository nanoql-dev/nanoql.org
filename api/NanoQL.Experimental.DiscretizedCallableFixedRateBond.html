Remarks

A working type (DiscretizedAsset), built for one calculation from the bond's arguments at the context's settlement date. Times are measured on the curve's day counter from its reference date. A callability within a week before a coupon date is moved to that coupon's time, its price carried there by the discount factor between the two dates, spread included, and that coupon is then added before the callabilities at its time; a callability on the date of a coupon so moved ahead gets that coupon added to its price. Prices are scaled from per 100 to the face amount.

Each live callability is applied by its own type, as QuantLib's fix after v1.44 does (PR #2875), where QuantLib 1.44 read the i-th live callability's type from the i-th entry of the whole schedule, so that after a callability on or before the settlement date a call was applied as a put, or a put as a call. A call caps the values at its price, a put floors them.
