The base class of the pricing of a CMS coupon by static replication, as in Hagan's "Conundrums..." article: a caplet's, a floorlet's and the swaplet's price from vanilla swaptions, through a g-function of the yield-curve model.

Remarks

Immutable: each public member computes, inside the call, what QuantLib's initialize(coupon) stores in the pricer before its queries, the g-function with its memo among it, and a capped or collared coupon's rate reads its three rates from one such state. WithMeanReversion(double) and WithSwaptionVolatility(SwaptionVolatilityStructure?) return copies of the pricer's own type, where QuantLib's setters replace its quote and handle in place.

The standard model reads the swap index's tenor as its length in years, where QuantLib reads its length in the tenor's own units, so an 18-month index was 18 years; a tenor that isn't a whole number of fixed-leg periods (18 months on an annual leg) starts with a stub the model can't price, and is refused.

On a lognormal volatility a caplet struck at or below 0, on a forward above 0, where QuantLib prices the caplet at a strike of 1e-10 and so drops the strike, is the coupon's expected rate less the strike, the limit of the replication's own formula there; the floorlet there is 0, as QuantLib's. A forward at or below 0 has no lognormal rate distribution, and its caplet refuses, as QuantLib's.

Derived inside the library only, by NumericHaganPricer and AnalyticHaganPricer, since its per-coupon state is internal.
