Remarks

An extension point: EnergyVanillaSwap and EnergyBasisSwap derive from it. A swap prices each pricing day of each period on its calendar, from the adjusted start, reading its index's stored fixing up to the index's last quote date and its forward price after it. The fixing is read as the index reads it, so a day the index doesn't fix is refused ("… is not a valid fixing date"): a swap's calendar must be its index's on its fixed days, as QuantLib's.

Each payment is made in the currency of the leg the net amount goes through, the receive leg's when the discounted amount is positive, at that currency's exchange rate, and discounted on that leg's curve. QuantLib pairs the payment's currency with the other leg's rate in the basis swap and a payer vanilla swap, and a receiver vanilla swap's payment never changes currency, its pay-receive flag 0 where −1 would switch it (Divergences/EnergySwapPaymentTests.cs), so only a receiver's payments to the holder are QuantLib's; the NPV, in the base currency, is the same either way.

A swap whose index has no fixings prices every day from its forward curve, with QuantLib's warning, where QuantLib's throws "empty timeseries". Quantity sums the periods' amounts under the first period's unit, as QuantLib's, right for periods in one unit.
