Remarks

Immutable. Its argument is the strike, S itself, and its times are the process's, the risk-free curve's.

The dividend curve and the volatility are read at the instant a time denotes, each on its own clock, the variance accrued from the risk-free curve's reference date and the volatility its root per unit of that curve's time, where QuantLib reads them at the risk-free curve's time (the mismatched-dates class); structures that share the curve's times keep QuantLib's bits. A documented divergence.
