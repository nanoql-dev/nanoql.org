Remarks

Immutable: WithSwaptionVolatility(SwaptionVolatilityStructure?) returns a copy of the pricer's own type, where QuantLib's setSwaptionVolatility replaces the pricer's handle in place. Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where Hagan's pricers and the linear TSR pricer derive from it; a pricer of another assembly derives from it and implements FloatingRateCouponPricer's members.
