Remarks

A working type, with Clone().

Three departures from QuantLib (Divergences/SquareRootAndersenFirstStepTests.cs, …StepsTests.cs, …SmallReversionTests.cs): the first evolution step's sub-steps revert by e^{−κΔt}, where QuantLib leaves that factor 0, reverting the variance to its mean in full over the first step (squarerootandersen.cpp:51-52); NumberSteps is the evolution steps, where QuantLib's multiplies its sub-steps by the sub-steps again (:80); and below |κΔt| = 1e-2 the conditional variance's (1 − e^{−κΔt})/κ is −expm1(−κΔt)/κ, Δt at κ = 0, where QuantLib's cancels to 0 or NaN (:96-97), QuantLib's form and bits at and above the cut. A process with no evolution times or a first one not positive, a step before a path starts or past the path's steps, and too few variates, which QuantLib reads before or past its vectors, are refused.

Two more fixes (Divergences/SquareRootAndersenDegenerateStepTests.cs): where the conditional variance is 0 (no vol of variance), the variance and its mean both 0, or ψ so small that QuantLib's quadratic form overflows (below about 1.5e-154), the sub-step's variance is its conditional mean, the form's limit, where QuantLib's is NaN (:102-103); and the cut point picks each ψ's form inside its domain, clamped to [1, 2], where QuantLib's cut below 1 sends ψ below 1 to the exponential form, whose p is then no probability, and a cut above 2 sends ψ above 2 to the quadratic form's square root of a negative (:100). QuantLib's bits elsewhere and at every cut in [1, 2].
