NanoQL.Experimental

The ZABR pricing operator on a mesher whose first direction is the forward F and second the volatility v: ½v²F2β·∂²/∂F² + ½ν²v2γ·∂²/∂v² + νρ|v|γ+1Fβ·∂²/∂F∂v; γ = 1 is SABR.

Remarks

A working type: its bands are built at construction and never change, and the sum Apply(ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) forms is written through buffers it holds, so a step allocates nothing; not thread-safe. Experimental, as in QuantLib.
