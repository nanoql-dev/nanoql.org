The finite-difference pricing engine for vanilla options, European, American or Bermudan, on the constant elasticity of variance (CEV) process df = α·f^β·dW, absorbed at f = 0: on FdmCEV1dMesher's grid, concentrating at the strike, with the analytic CEV value at the upper end and, where the process can reach zero (δ < 2), the payoff at the lower end discounted from maturity.

Remarks

The engine reports the value, delta, gamma and theta; the theta is null when the problem's first stopping time is time 0, as QuantLib's is. Its finite-difference working types are built inside each calculation and dropped after it. Its times are the discount curve's, from its reference date in its day counter, as QuantLib's; an exercise on or before that date leaves the engine no time to roll back over, and it is refused, as QuantLib's engine throws there (Boost's refusal of an infinite non-centrality, or a negative time in the incomplete gamma function), and as nanoql's other finite-difference engines refuse an exercise their clocks have no time for.

Near β = 1 the mesher's bounds and the upper end's value are the lognormal limit's and Black's formula at Hagan's equivalent volatility (FdmCEV1dMesher, CEVCalculator), where QuantLib's engine throws.
