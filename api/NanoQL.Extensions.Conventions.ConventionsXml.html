Remarks

A document's root may be the Conventions element or any element holding one. A schema violation is the System.Xml.Schema.XmlSchemaValidationException .NET raises, with its line and position. DTDs are refused and nothing outside the document is resolved.

The writer writes the canonical form: the records in order, attributes name and base first, two-space indents, LF line ends, UTF-8 without a byte-order mark and a final newline; each record's and the root's foreign attributes and elements are written back where they were read.
