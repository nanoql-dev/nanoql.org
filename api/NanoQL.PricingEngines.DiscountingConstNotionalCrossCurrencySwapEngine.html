The discounting engine of constant-notional swaps whose legs involve two currencies: each leg is discounted on its currency's curve, and the NPV is expressed in the domestic currency.

Remarks

The given domestic and foreign currencies are matched to the swap's legs. The evaluation date is the reference date of either discount curve, which must be equal. The spot rate is quoted as units of the domestic currency for one unit of the foreign currency, for settlement on the NPV date.

Static: QuantLib's engine object becomes the arguments of Calculate, one overload per swap type, each returning its swap's results. An explicit FX settlement date converts the quote from that date to the NPV date by the discount factors' parity, as QuantLib's mark-to-market engine does, where QuantLib converts it to the curves' reference date; without one, the quote is the NPV date's, as QuantLib reads it (tests/NanoQL.Tests/Divergences/CrossCurrencySwapNpvDateFxTests.cs).
