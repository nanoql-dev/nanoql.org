Remarks

A date has no time of day here, as in QuantLib's default build, whose roundToDayStart returns the date unchanged; its high-resolution build rounds a date at or past noon to the next day.

Remarks

QuantLib casts a NaN time's day count to an integer unchecked, which is undefined (yearfractiontodate.cpp:40-41): here a NaN is refused on entry, a documented divergence. An infinite or overflowing day count is refused where QuantLib's boost::numeric_cast throws.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

dayCounter is null.

ArgumentException

t is NaN, or its day count overflows an integer (an infinite time among them); or referenceDate is default(Date).
