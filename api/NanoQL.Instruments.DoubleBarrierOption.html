Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the quanto double-barrier option derives from it, and the experimental double-barrier engines price it. Immutable. A knock-out's rebate is paid at the first touch of either barrier, a knock-in's at expiry if neither was touched (as the analytic and binary engines price it; the binary engine refuses a KIKO's or KOKI's); QuantLib's analytic and binary engines read no rebate.

Remarks

The solve prices through the analytic engine as nanoql fixes it (its rebate, its terms beyond five, each rate on the volatility's clock), so it moves where those fixes move the price. QuantLib's warning: the result is consistent only with prices from the same engine.
