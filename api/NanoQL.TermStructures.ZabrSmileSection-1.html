Remarks

The section builds its ZabrModel from α, β, ν, ρ and γ at its exercise time and forward. With the short-maturity lognormal kernel its volatility is the model's lognormal volatility at the strike, floored at 1e-6, and its option prices are SmileSection's. With the normal kernel its option prices are Bachelier's with the model's normal volatility, and its volatility is the lognormal one implied from them, 0 where that can't be implied, as QuantLib's catches every failure. The finite-difference kernels price calls at a strike grid, the moneyness grid times the forward (by default 0.01 to 20, its non-positive strikes dropped) with fdRefinement points between two of its strikes: by FdPrice(ReadOnlySpan<double>), or by FullFdPrice(double) at each strike. A cubic spline through those prices and the forward at strike 0 prices up to the grid's last strike, an exponential fitted to its end beyond; puts by parity; and the volatility is the lognormal one implied from those prices, as the normal kernel's. The short-maturity kernels don't read the grid.

QuantLib's date constructor, whose section floats with the evaluation date, is Create(Date, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, EvaluationContext, DayCounter?, ReadOnlySpan<double>, int), which takes the evaluation context and fixes the date. QuantLib's short-maturity kernels read the five parameters unchecked; fewer are refused here, for every kernel, with the text of QuantLib's local-volatility kernel.

Unsealed for its named classes, QuantLib-Python's (ZabrShortMaturityLognormalSmileSection, ZabrShortMaturityNormalSmileSection, ZabrLocalVolatilitySmileSection and ZabrFullFdSmileSection). Its constructor reads the exercise time, so ExerciseTime is sealed here.
