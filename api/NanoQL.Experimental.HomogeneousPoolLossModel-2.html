Remarks

The losses are split into buckets from 0 to the tranche's detachment, the atoms at their buckets' left edges, and the expected tranche loss is a left Riemann sum of the excess probability: QuantLib's design, kept, 3% above the recursive model's exact value on a [3%, 6%] tranche at 200 buckets and converging with the bucket count. Bound to a basket with realized defaults, the model prices its live names over the restated tranche.
