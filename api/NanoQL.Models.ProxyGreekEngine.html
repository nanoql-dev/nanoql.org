The engine that values market-model products and their Greeks by Fries and Joshi's partial proxy simulation: each path is valued on the original evolver, which records each step's constrained rate, then on each bumped constrained evolver pinned to those rates, its weights the likelihood ratio; finite-difference weights combine the original and the bumped values into each Greek's path value.

Remarks

The Greeks: groups of constrained evolvers, each group with its lists of weights, one per Greek, the first weight the original value's and the next ones the group's evolvers'. Each path adds the original values to the caller's statistics and each Greek's values to its own, in units of the initial numeraire.

A working type: it holds the caller's evolvers, as QuantLib's shared_ptr, and steps them, so an evolver serves one engine at a time; it holds its own clone of the product. Its buffers are sized at construction, so a path, the original's and the constrained evolvers', allocates nothing after a warm-up path.

The constrained evolvers' weights are the constrained Euler evolver's, which carry the reference model's transition density: a volatility-bumped evolver given the original model as its reference yields vegas within their standard errors of Black's, where QuantLib's are of the wrong sign (Divergences/ProxyGreekVegaTests.cs).

Refused, where QuantLib reads past its buffers: a group of weights without its evolvers, a list of weights longer than its group's evolvers and the original, constraints fewer than the product's steps, statistics fewer than the weights, and a product not finished at the evolver's last step.
