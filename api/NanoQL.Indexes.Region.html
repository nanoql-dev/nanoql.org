Remarks

Immutable, and an extension point: QuantLib's regions derive from it, sealed, and CustomRegion builds one without a class of its own.

Two regions are equal when their names are, compared ordinally, as QuantLib's operator== compares them, whatever their codes. The equality is sealed, so every subclass compares the same way.
