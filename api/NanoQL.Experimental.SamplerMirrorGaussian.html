Remarks

Each pass of QuantLib's reflection moves the point by up to the box's width w, so a draw takes about |step|/w passes and never returns on a zero width, nor once w is below the point's last bit. nanoql's holds a zero-width dimension at its bound, its draw still taken, so the other dimensions draw as QuantLib's; and on a narrow one runs QuantLib's passes up to 10,000, its bits wherever they end, then folds in one step from the drawn point x, the reflection into [lower, upper] of (x − lower) mod 2w, the passes' own end (Conventions/AnnealingSamplerFoldTests.cs).

Its draws are std::normal_distribution<Real>(0.0, 1.0) on a std::mt19937, as MSVC draws them, on the seed, which is required: QuantLib's default seeds from the clock. A working type.

A lower bound above its upper is refused, where QuantLib's reflection never ends, and so are bounds of different sizes and a point whose size isn't theirs, which QuantLib's reads past (Conventions/ExperimentalMathRefusalTests.cs).
