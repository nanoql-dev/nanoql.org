Remarks

The first remaining coupon is the first that starts on or after the discount curve's reference date; with none, the flows are empty. Each spread is the coupon's forward against the discount curve's forward over its accrual, continuously compounded and paid at the coupon's start, or simply compounded and paid at its payment date. The times are from the reference date, Actual/365 (Fixed), and the weights the flows' amounts, read in the context.

An extension point, as QuantLib's: SwapCashFlows derives from it. QuantLib reads its first cash flow as a coupon before it checks the leg, so an empty leg, or a first flow that isn't a coupon, is undefined there; here both are refused, the latter with QuantLib's own "FloatingLeg CashFlow is no Coupon.".
