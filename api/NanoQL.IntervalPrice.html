Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's setValue and setValues change the object, where SetValue(double, IntervalPriceType) and SetValues(double, double, double, double) return a copy. Two prices are equal when their four values are, by Equals(double); QuantLib has no equality on the type. QuantLib's parameterless constructor isn't ported: its nulls serve QuantLib's default insertion into a mutable time series, and no QuantLib test builds one.
