A soft barrier option: knocked in or out in proportion over a range of barriers, [L, U], instead of in full at one hard barrier; the average of the single-barrier options with barriers in the range.

Remarks

Immutable and sealed: QuantLib derives nothing from it. European exercise only, as in QuantLib; it has no checks of its own beyond the option's, its engine checking the barriers.

Remarks

The solve prices through the analytic engine as nanoql fixes it (the band average where Haug's form doesn't hold, each rate on the volatility's clock). QuantLib's warning: the result is consistent only with prices from the same engine.
