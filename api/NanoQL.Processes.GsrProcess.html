Remarks

Immutable. QuantLib's process caches its integrals for performance, so that a parameter change needs flushCache(); here each public member computes its terms afresh, within one call, and a model's engine call shares one memo across its reads through the internal overloads. QuantLib's flushCache and its parameter setters, which only the model calls, have no counterpart: a parameter change builds a new process.

WithForwardMeasureTime(double) moves the core's forward measure time with the process's and refuses one below the last step time, as QuantLib's master does after the pin (#2842): QuantLib 1.44's leaves its core at the constructor's time.

The volatilities: one more than the step times.

The reversions: one, constant, or one more than the step times.

T double

The forward measure time; 60 by default, QuantLib's. It may lie below the last step time, as QuantLib accepts it: a step past it moves nothing, and nothing past it is read.

The reference date of Time(Date); null, QuantLib's empty date, by default.
