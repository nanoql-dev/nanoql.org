Remarks

QuantLib's convenience class: a MixedLinearCubicInterpolation with Parabolic, monotonic false, and second-derivative boundary conditions of 0, which only the spline schemes read.

Whether the two interpolations share the points' range or split it.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

n is negative, or behavior isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

The sizes differ, there are no points, n isn't the index of a point, or a part has too few points.
