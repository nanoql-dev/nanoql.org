Remarks

The value, with QuantLib's additional results ("dcf", "s0", "strike", "expiryTime", "term1", "term2", "xiRightLimit", "fixingTimes", "tauK", "adjustedStrike", "prefactor", "kStar"); "term2" is the integral's term on the route below, so the value is D × prefactor × (±term1 + term2), as QuantLib's. An arithmetic option is priced as the geometric one on its fixings to come, unseasoned, QuantLib's use of the engine as the arithmetic Monte Carlo's control variate.

Documented divergences. QuantLib cuts its integral at ξ 100 on 128 Gauss-Legendre points, before the integrand decays at short maturities and small variance (−51% on a ten-day Asian at v₀ 0.0025; Divergences/HestonGeometricAsianCutoffTests.cs), and leaves out [0, 1e-8 ξ_max]. Here:

  • Phi in a stable form, ω̃ through tanh and the logs of the Riccati factors through log1p, where QuantLib's terms of order 1/σ² cancel at small vol of vol and its cosh and sinh overflow at long maturities (Divergences/HestonGeometricAsianSmallParameterTests.cs); below a vol of vol of 1e-4, a cubic in σ through the Black-Scholes limit at σ = 0; at a vanishing square root, γ = 0, the forms' limits (κ = 0).
  • Before the integral, a switch: a point mass at v₀ = θ = 0, priced at its intrinsic; and the discounted intrinsic where a Chernoff bound on the extrinsic value, from Phi at real s up to the moment explosion, is at or below 1e-12 × max(the discounted intrinsic, D F_G/100) (Divergences/HestonGeometricAsianIntrinsicTests.cs).
  • The integral on Carr and Madan's damped contour: the out-of-the-money option on its own strip where that strip is at least 0.5 wide, else on Lewis's middle strip, the other by parity; Lord and Kahl's α inside the half of the strip next to its pole; the explosions by the closed-form first-zero test of the Riccati factors (Divergences/HestonGeometricAsianExplosionTests.cs); cut where Phi's modulus falls below 1e-14 of its value on the contour's axis, or at the engine's ξ_max where that is later; split at the decades of ξ and at the peak's width below 1; on NanoQL's adaptive Kronrod rule at the price's tolerance scale, max(the price, the lower bound, D F_G/100), in two passes, accepted where two breakpoint sets agree within it, a third on disagreement, each set capped at 1e6 evaluations (Divergences/HestonGeometricAsianStrikeScaleTests.cs).
  • Refused, with "variance too small for the Heston geometric engine near the forward", where the switch doesn't fire and the cut lies past ξ 1e7, which no checkable route reaches.
  • F_G exact, S₀ D_q(t)/D_r(t), where every fixing time is one t and none is past, where Phi(1, 0) is 0/0; the call floored at D max(F_G − K, 0), the put at its mirror.

Times run on the engine's clock, Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?), from the earliest reference date of the process's curves, each discount read at its instants. A fixing before the evaluation date is left out, as the Monte Carlo engines leave it, where QuantLib reads its negative time into the intervals; one from the evaluation date to the clock's origin is taken at the origin, as a past fixing at the spot (Divergences/AsianLateSettlementTests.cs).
