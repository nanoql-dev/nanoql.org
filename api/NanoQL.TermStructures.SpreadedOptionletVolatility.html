Remarks

QuantLib's spread quote is its value: a new spread is a new structure. The base's dates, calendar, conventions, strikes, type and displacement are the structure's, and so is its extrapolation flag, read when the structure is built, as QuantLib reads it. The base's volatilities and smile sections are read with extrapolation.
