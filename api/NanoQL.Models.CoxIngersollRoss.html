Remarks

Its arguments are θ, k, σ and r₀, in that order, each a constant parameter, positive. QuantLib's header warns that the class "was not tested enough to guarantee its functionality". Its dynamics simulate the short rate directly, on CoxIngersollRossProcess's quadratic-exponential scheme.

The Feller constraint (K1): with it, the model's parameters satisfy σ² < 2kθ at each vector's own k and θ, through ParametersConstraint(), and σ's own constraint is positivity: a divergence, QuantLib's testing σ against the constructor's k and θ (coxingersollross.cpp:24-37), so a calibration can leave the Feller region and σ stops at a bound the constructor set (Divergences/CirFellerConstraintTests.cs). The constructor refuses a σ outside it with QuantLib's text.

The tree (K2): on QuantLib's dynamics before its 1.23, the square root y = √r of the short rate, of constant volatility σ/2, Euler-discretized, the short rate y², which the trinomial tree's state-independent variance fits: a divergence, QuantLib's tree reading CoxIngersollRossProcess, whose variance depends on the state, at its initial value, so that its prices stall away from its own analytic ones (Divergences/CirTreeConvergenceTests.cs).

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the extended Cox-Ingersoll-Ross model derives from it.
