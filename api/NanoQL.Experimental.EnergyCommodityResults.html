Remarks

QuantLib's EnergyCommodity::results also declares a currency and a unit of measure, which nothing sets, and isn't ported with them. A swap's daily positions are also its additional result dailyPositions, as QuantLib writes it. Equality compares every member by value, the collections element by element, but the payment cash flows, which compare by reference, as every CashFlow does.
