Bond adapters of the CashFlows functions: each passes the bond's cash flows, its settlement date and includeSettlementDateFlows = false, and gives prices per 100 of the notional at the settlement date.

Remarks

A null settlement date is the bond's settlement date. Every function takes the context: the settlement date reads the evaluation date, and whether a cash flow on the settlement date has occurred reads the context's IncludeTodaysCashFlows when that date is the evaluation date, despite the false passed. The bond's Notional(EvaluationContext, Date?) and AccruedAmount(EvaluationContext, Date?) are called virtually, as QuantLib's are, so a derived bond's own accrued amount reaches these functions.

A price or yield function refuses a settlement date at which the bond isn't tradable, its notional being 0, with QuantLib's text ("non tradable at …", with " settlement date" in the curve-based dirty price's alone); the accrued amount is 0 there instead, and the cash-flow and coupon-rate inspectors don't check. The yield is solved for by NewtonSafe, QuantLib's default, or by the NewtonSafe or Brent solver given, the two solvers nanoql has of those QuantLib's template takes. QuantLib's three overloads taking a day counter they ignore are deprecated and not ported.
