Remarks

The instrument exchanges a principal of 100 at its start and end and pays the overnight rate compounded over each payment period plus the margin; every payment, the principal exchanges included, takes the payment lag. The overnight rate is projected from the index's own forwarding curve, which it must have; the curve being bootstrapped discounts every flow, flows on its reference date included. The implied margin is the swap's NPV over its overnight leg's BPS.

A helper built on a tenor follows the evaluation date: re-dated for another context, it rebuilds its swap. QuantLib's margin quote is a double.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
