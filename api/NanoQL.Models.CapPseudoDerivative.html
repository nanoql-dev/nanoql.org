A cap's implied volatility in a market model, the flat undisplaced Black volatility that reprices the model's displaced caplets, and the derivatives of its price and its implied volatility with respect to the pseudo-roots' elements, step by step, for market vegas.

Remarks

Immutable: computed at construction, as QuantLib's constructor computes; its matrix reads return copies.

The implied volatility's solve (a documented divergence; Divergences/CapPseudoDerivativeBracketTests.cs): QuantLib's Brent bracket, [0.99, 1.01] times the least displaced and the greatest scaled caplet volatility (swaptionpseudojacobian.cpp:331), first; where it doesn't hold the root, as away from the money on a displaced model, Brent's bracketing solve from QuantLib's guess, where QuantLib refuses "root not bracketed". Wherever QuantLib's bracket holds the root, QuantLib's bits. A negative displacement is refused by Black's formula's own check, as in QuantLib.
