Remarks

Immutable. The curves are read on a clock, each at the instants its times denote: QuantLib reads the rates at the risk-free curve's times. The public constructor's clock is Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)'s, the risk-free curve itself where the dividend curve isn't dated before it, QuantLib's reads.
