Remarks

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where the Ibor, overnight and capped coupons derive from it. The coupon holds its pricer, given at construction or by SetPricer<T>(T, FloatingRateCouponPricer?), which returns a copy of the coupon's own type; the coupon is never changed. The rate and the amount are the pricer's, computed on each call from the coupon and the context, in place of QuantLib's cached lazy calculation.

A derived coupon that adds state overrides WithPricer(FloatingRateCouponPricer?) only when a copy must change more than the pricer (a capped coupon copies its underlying too); the base's copy is memberwise, so the derived state comes along.
