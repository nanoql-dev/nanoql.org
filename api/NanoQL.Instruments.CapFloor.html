Remarks

The correctness of the returned value is tested by checking that the price of a cap (resp. floor) decreases (resp. increases) with the strike rate; the relationship between the values of caps, floors and the resulting collars is checked, and so is the put-call parity between the values of caps, floors and swaps; the returned implied volatility is tested by reproducing the target value with it, and the returned value against a known good value.

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where Cap, Floor and Collar derive from it and engines take it. It holds its leg and its rates; an engine computes QuantLib's arguments from them with the context (NanoQL.Instruments.CapFloor.SetupArguments(NanoQL.EvaluationContext)) and returns the instrument's results, QuantLib's plain Instrument::results with the engine's additional results. A cash flow of the leg that isn't a floating-rate coupon is refused when the instrument is priced, as QuantLib refuses it, not when it is built. Equality is by reference.

Remarks

As QuantLib's helper does, the arguments are computed once and the engine's body runs alone at each evaluation, without the instrument's checks. Each evaluation prices afresh, where QuantLib's helper reuses its last result while the volatility is unchanged; the volatility's reference date is the context's evaluation date.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

context or discountCurve is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

maxEvaluations is negative, or type isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

NanoQLException

The instrument has expired, a cash flow isn't a floating-rate coupon, the price can't be bracketed, the solver exceeds its evaluations, or the engine fails.
