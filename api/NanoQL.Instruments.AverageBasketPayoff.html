Remarks

A price count other than the weights' is refused, with the text of QuantLib's Choi, Deng-Li-Zhou and single-factor engines, where QuantLib's inner_product reads past a shorter array or sums a partial basket, so that its Monte Carlo and finite-difference engines price a run-dependent value or a partial basket there (Divergences/AverageBasketPayoffWeightCountTests.cs).
