Remarks

The effective rate is solved on the trade date, at initial recognition, as IFRS 9 and ASC 310-20's interest method require: the rate, simple within each coupon period and compounded at each coupon date, at which the bond's remaining coupons and redemption are worth the price paid, the cost plus the accrued interest purchased (Yield(ImmutableArray<CashFlow>, double, DayCounter, Compounding, Frequency, EvaluationContext, bool?, Date?, Date?, double, int, double), settled on the trade date). Each coupon date after the trade books an amortisation (Amortizations): the interest at the effective rate on the carrying amount, over the yield's own time, less the coupon (less the accrued interest purchased, in the first period), rounded to the cent, positive when the carrying amount accretes toward a face above it; the last closes the carrying amount to the face amount. AmortizationValue(Date) is the carrying amount, without accrued interest, at any date of the bond's life: the cost on the trade date, continuous, and the face amount at maturity.

Ported from QLNet. Its departures from QLNet: the rate is solved at the trade date with the accrued interest purchased, where QLNet solves at the coupon date on or before the trade (or at its global evaluation date, with no coupon before it) and its carrying amount jumps at the first coupon date; a partial period accrues on the carrying amount at its start, where QLNet's accrues on the cost; the coupon is the leg's own, day-counted, where QLNet's amortisation pays the face amount times the rate over the frequency; an amortisation's sign is its interest less its coupon, where QLNet's is the bond's being at a premium; the last amortisation closes to the face amount, where QLNet's cents leave it off; a given yield of 0 is taken, where QLNet solves instead; the bond's calendar is the one given, where QLNet's base uses TARGET; and the bond's cash flows are its coupons and redemption, where QLNet's hold the accounting entries and coupons whose amounts it overrides. On a purchase on a coupon date the method is QLNet's.
