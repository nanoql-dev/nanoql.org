Remarks

QuantLib's BMASwap derives these inspectors from its swap results on demand; here they are members of the results the swap's FetchResults(InstrumentResults) returns. The Libor leg is the first leg and the BMA leg the second.

A result QuantLib refuses to read is null: a leg's NPV or BPS it leaves null ("result not available"), and the fair Libor fraction when the Libor leg's NPV without its spread is zero ("result not available (null libor NPV)"), as for an expired swap, or when an input to it is null; and the fair Libor spread when the NPV or the Libor leg's BPS is null. A defined but infinite or NaN value is kept, as QuantLib computes it: an expired swap's fair Libor spread divides zero by zero. Equality compares the per-leg arrays element by element, and the two fair values by value.
