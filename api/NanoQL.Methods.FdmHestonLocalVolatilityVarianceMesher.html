Remarks

Immutable. Without a leverage function it is the variance mesher. The skew factor is the variance mesher's, bounded at σ_m/κ = 2, and its fallbacks master's.

QuantLib reads the curves and the leverage function at the risk-free curve's times (fdmhestonvariancemesher.cpp:176-201). Here the times are the finite-difference Heston engine's clock's (Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)), from the earliest reference date of the structures it reads, and each structure is read at the instants they denote, on its own clock, accruing nothing before its own reference date; the leverage, a multiplier of the Heston volatility, at its instant. Where every structure shares the risk-free curve's day counter and reference date, QuantLib's reads.
