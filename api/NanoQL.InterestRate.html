An interest rate with its day-counting and compounding conventions: discount and compound factors, and implied and equivalent rates.

Remarks

A value: two rates are equal when their rate, day counter (by name), compounding and frequency are. QuantLib's null interest rate is a InterestRate? null, and its conversion to Rate is Rate.
