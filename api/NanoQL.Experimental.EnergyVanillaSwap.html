Remarks

A payer pays the fixed price and receives the index's; a receiver the reverse. Each period's quantity is spread evenly over its pricing days and valued in the base currency and unit, discounted on each leg's curve from two days after the evaluation date, a payment on or before then at 1; the secondary costs are charged on the total quantity in the base unit. The unit conversions are EnergyCommodity's, direction-aware, the payments EnergySwap's. As QuantLib files the legs, the fixed leg is in the pay currency and the floating leg in the receive currency whichever way the swap faces, so a receiver receives the fixed price in the pay currency.
