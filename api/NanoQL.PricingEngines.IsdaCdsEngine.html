Remarks

References: [1] The Pricing and Risk Management of Credit Default Swaps, with a Focus on the ISDA Model, OpenGamma Quantitative Research, version as of 15 October 2013; [2] ISDA CDS Standard Model Proposed Numerical Fix, Markit, 15 November 2012; [3] Markit Interest Rate Curve XML Specifications, version 1.16, 15 October 2013. According to [1] the options matching the standard model's C code are HalfDayBias and Flat before its version 1.8.2, and NoBias and Flat from it; the theoretically correct setting is NoBias and Piecewise.

The curves must be ISDA compliant: Actual/365 (Fixed), dated on the evaluation date, a discount curve log-linear in its discounts or flat in its forwards, and a credit curve log-linear in its survival probabilities or flat in its hazard rates. QuantLib casts the curves to its interpolated curve types; here a curve qualifies by the IInterpolatedCurve<TTraits, TInterpolator> it implements, which the interpolated and the piecewise curves of those kinds both do, or by being a FlatForward or a FlatHazardRate. For precise consistency with the specification, Ibor coupons should be at par and the rate helpers' Ibor index should not provide the evaluation date's fixing; neither is checked.

The protection leg runs to the maturity: the maturity joins the curves' nodes when their last one is before it. QuantLib adds the maturity only when the curves have no nodes, so on curves that end before the maturity it drops the protection from their last node to the maturity, a documented divergence. A static class: an expired swap's results are SetupExpired()'s.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

cds or context is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

An option isn't a member of its type, with QuantLib's text.

ArgumentException

A curve isn't ISDA compliant; the swap doesn't settle its accrual, pays at the period's end or has another claim than a face-value one; or a coupon's day counter isn't Actual/365 (Fixed) or Actual/360.

NanoQLException

A curve is missing ("no discount term structure set", "no probability term structure set"), or the swap fails its validation.
