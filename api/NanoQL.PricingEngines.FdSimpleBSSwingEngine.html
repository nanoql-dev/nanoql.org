Remarks

The engine reports the value, delta, gamma and theta, the delta and gamma by central differences at 1% of the spot; the theta is null when the problem's first stopping time is time 0, as QuantLib's is. Its finite-difference working types are built inside each calculation and dropped after it.

The exercises are timed by the swing exercise's date times (ExerciseTimes(DayCounter, Date)), seconds included, on Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), the maturity the last of them, where QuantLib times each by its date alone and the maturity by the last date, so that 60 half-daily exercises price as 30 daily ones. On whole dates and where no structure is dated before the risk-free curve, QuantLib's times.
