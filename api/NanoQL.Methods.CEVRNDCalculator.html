Remarks

QuantLib refuses β = 1, where δ = (1 − 2β)/(1 − β) divides by zero, and near it Boost's non-central chi-squared refuses or runs for seconds. Here β = 1 is the lognormal density of the driftless forward, the CEV density's limit, with no mass at zero; for 0 < |β − 1| < 1e-3 the constructor refuses. A documented divergence (Divergences/CevNearOneTests.cs).

The inverse first solves Sankaran's approximation by Brent's method for its guess; where that fails, QuantLib's catch (...) starts from the forward instead, here on NanoQLException, the type of every failure QuantLib's solver and Boost's distribution raise there. That second Brent can step below zero, where QuantLib's distribution reads X(f) = f^(2(1 − β)) as NaN and Boost refuses it, so QuantLib throws at a quantile that exists (β 0.95, σ 200%, five years, the median); here the density and distribution are 0 below zero, the absorbing boundary, and QuantLib's values at and above it stand. A documented divergence (a run find, Divergences/CevDensityBelowZeroTests.cs), checked by the quantile's own distribution.
