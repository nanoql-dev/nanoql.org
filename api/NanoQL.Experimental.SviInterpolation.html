Remarks

The fit is SABRInterpolation's, XABR's, over SVI's parameters: it runs when the interpolation is built, from the guesses and then, while the error stays above errorAccept, from up to maxGuesses − 1 Halton guesses seeded with QuantLib's 42, keeping the best; its values are QuantLib's at every step but where NanoQL.Experimental.SviSpecs' transforms depart from QuantLib's (a guess at an edge of the transforms' ranges, and a trial the parameters' check would refuse with a or b fixed).

As an IXabrInterpolation, its first four parameters, a, b, σ and ρ, are the slots QuantLib's coefficient holder keeps in α's, β's, ν's and ρ's places.

Immutable and thread-safe once built: it copies its inputs, and nothing writes the fit after it.

Whether m is fixed at its guess.

vegaWeighted bool

Whether the errors are weighted by the strikes' Black vegas.

endCriteria EndCriteria

The end criteria, or null for QuantLib's EndCriteria(60000, 100, 1e-8, 1e-8, 1e-8).

optMethod OptimizationMethod

The optimizer, or null for QuantLib's LevenbergMarquardt(1e-8, 1e-8, 1e-8).

errorAccept double

The error at or below which a fit is accepted and the restarts stop.

useMaxError bool

Whether a fit is scored by its maximum error, not its root-mean-square error.

maxGuesses int

The most minimizations; 0 runs one, as 1 does.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

maxGuesses is negative.

ArgumentException

The sizes differ, there is no strike, the time isn't positive, the guesses fail CheckSviParameters(double, double, double, double, double, double), the volatilities are all zero with vega weights, or the optimizer refuses the problem.

NanoQLException

The optimizer fails.
