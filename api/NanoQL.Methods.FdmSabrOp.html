The backward operator of the SABR model dF = α·F^β·dW₁, dα = ν·α·dW₂, d⟨W₁, W₂⟩ = ρ·dt, on a mesher of the forward in direction 0 and the log volatility x = ln α in direction 1: the forward's diffusion ½e^{2x}F^{2β}, the log volatility's drift −½ν² and diffusion ½ν², the correlation term ρνe^xF^β, each direction discounting at half the curve's forward rate.

Remarks

A working type: the parts are built at construction; SetTime(double, double) rewrites the two directions' operators in place through buffers it holds, and the apply, splitting solves and preconditioner write into the caller's spans through buffers it holds, so a step allocates nothing. The forward rate is the curve's over its own times, read through YieldTermStructure's internal ContinuousForwardRate, its bits ForwardRate's.
