Remarks

Immutable. The rate r is given with the convention that a unit of the source is worth r units of the target. QuantLib's default constructor, an empty rate whose value is Null<Real>(), isn't ported: no member builds one.

Equality is nanoql's, where QuantLib has none: two rates are equal when they have the same source, target, rate (by Equals(double)) and type, and, for derived rates, equal chains. The exchange-rate registry's equality reads it.

Chain(ExchangeRate, ExchangeRate) refuses two rates over one pair of currencies, which QuantLib chains into a rate from a currency to itself (EUR→USD at 1.1 with EUR→USD at 1.2 gives USD→USD at 1.0909, which exchanges 100 USD into 109.09 USD): a documented divergence (Divergences/ExchangeRateChainRefusalTests.cs). The exchange-rate registry's chained lookups chain as QuantLib's, since their steps are never over one pair in its key.
