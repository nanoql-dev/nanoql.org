A zero-inflation-indexed swap: a CPI leg, fixed rates inflated by a zero-inflation index with an inflated notional at the end, against a floating leg paying an Ibor rate plus a spread, with a notional flow where it is needed.

Remarks

The type, payer or receiver, refers to the floating leg. The CPI leg is the first leg, CPILeg's; the floating leg is the second, Ibor coupons on the floating schedule when it has more than one date, then a notional flow when the schedule has a single date, when the inflation nominal isn't subtracted, or when it differs from the nominal: the nominal less the inflation nominal where it is subtracted, else the nominal, paid with the last coupon or on the single date. Accrual adjustments and calendars are in the schedules.

The constructor builds the Ibor leg with the index's default pricer, which reads the context's at-par switch, so it takes the context; a null floating index is accepted where the schedule has a single date, since QuantLib never reads it then. A type that isn't a member of SwapType is refused, where QuantLib takes it as a receiver. An engine's results are a CPISwapResults: its fair rate is the swap engine's (see its remarks), and its fair spread is derived from the floating leg's BPS. QuantLib's own arguments, results and engine classes aren't ported.

Remarks

The fair rate is the engine's, and null when the engine gives none: QuantLib's derivation from the CPI leg's BPS isn't ported. The fair spread is the engine's when it gives one; otherwise it is derived from the NPV and the floating leg's BPS, and is null when that BPS is zero or unknown.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The results aren't a swap's, or a per-leg result has the wrong number of legs.
