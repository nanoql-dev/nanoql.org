Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's. The basket's processes are QuantLib's: each fixing's forward as the spot, zero rates, and a constant volatility scaled to the last fixing's time; the basket priced by ChoiBasketEngine with QuantLib's λ and integration steps.

Documented divergences. A seasoned option reads its correlations and volatilities at the future average's own strike, K_f = (K − A/m)·m/f for f of m fixings to come, so it prices as (f/m) times the unseasoned option at K_f, where QuantLib reads its correlations at K − A/m and its volatilities at K, −2.6% to +306% off on a smile varying with maturity (Divergences/SeasonedAsianSmileTests.cs). An effective strike below zero is the certain exercise, priced as Turnbull and Wakeman's engine prices it, its value alone, where QuantLib refuses it (Divergences/ChoiCertainExerciseTests.cs). Its times run on the engine's clock, Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), where QuantLib takes them from the process.

A fixing dated from the evaluation date to the latest structure's date, where the structures settle after the evaluation date, is taken there, the spot with no variance and no discount accrued, so as a past fixing at the spot (Divergences/AsianLateSettlementTests.cs), where QuantLib refuses it as past.
