Remarks

With ShareRanges both interpolations are defined over all the points; with SplitRanges the linear one over the points up to the switch point and the cubic one over the points from it on. Under SplitRanges, a FirstDerivative left condition whose value is null (QuantLib's Null<Real>()) matches the cubic's first derivative at the switch point to the linear part's. Under any other left condition, a null value is QuantLib's Null<Real>() as a number, 3.4028234663852886e38, which QuantLib's cubic reads as the condition's value.

It copies its inputs, as every Interpolation does. An extension point, as in QuantLib: the named mixed interpolations derive from it.

behavior MixedInterpolationBehavior

Whether the two interpolations share the points' range or split it.

The cubic part's first-derivative approximation.

monotonic bool

Whether to apply Hyman's monotonicity filter to the cubic part.

leftC CubicInterpolationBoundaryCondition

The cubic part's left boundary condition, for the spline approximation.

leftConditionValue double?

The left condition's value, where it takes one; null with a first-derivative condition to match the linear part's derivative at the switch point.

rightC CubicInterpolationBoundaryCondition

The cubic part's right boundary condition, for the spline approximation.

rightConditionValue double

The right condition's value, where it takes one.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

n is negative, or an enum argument isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The derivatives are matched with ShareRanges, the sizes differ, there are no points, n isn't the index of a point, or a part has too few points.

NanoQLException

The cubic part's scheme or a condition isn't implemented, or its spline's system has a zero pivot.
