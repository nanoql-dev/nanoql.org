Remarks

K is completely monotone, K(t) = ∫₀^∞ e^{−xt} μ(dx) with μ(dx) = x^{−α}/(Γ(α)Γ(1 − α)) dx (Abi Jaber), and a partition 0 = η₀ < η₁ < … < ηₙ gives non-negative cᵢ and xᵢ, so a completely monotone Kⁿ, by collapsing each interval's mass onto its barycentre: cᵢ = ∫ μ(dx) and xᵢ = (1/cᵢ) ∫ x μ(dx) over [ηᵢ₋₁, ηᵢ]. With η₀ = 0 the first interval absorbs all the mass as α rises to 1, so c₁ → 1, x₁ → 0 and Kⁿ → K ≡ 1. The partition's ends widen with n, η₁ = 1/(√n t_max) and ηₙ = 4n^{5/2}/t_max, their exponents QuantLib's empirical ones.

Immutable and thread-safe.

References: E. Abi Jaber, Lifting the Heston model, Quantitative Finance 19(12), 1995-2013 (2019); E. Abi Jaber and O. El Euch, Multifactor approximation of rough volatility models, SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics 10(2), 309-349 (2019).
