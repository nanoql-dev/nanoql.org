The mappings between swap rates and forward rates: annuities, the swap rates' derivatives and Jacobians in the forwards, the Z matrices that switch a model's base from forwards to swap rates, and a swaption's implied volatility by frozen coefficients.

Remarks

Three departures from QuantLib: the coinitial Z matrix scales every entry of its rows, where QuantLib's scales from the diagonal rightward, as the coterminal one does, though a coinitial row is nonzero left of it (Divergences/CoinitialZedMatrixTests.cs); and the swaption's implied volatility refuses an evolution whose times don't hold the swaption's expiry, and unequal displacements, as QuantLib's frozen-coefficient adapters refuse them, since it sums whole steps up to the expiry and reads the first displacement alone (Divergences/SwaptionImpliedVolatilityGridTests.cs).

Remarks

Each row is scaled whole: QuantLib's scales from the diagonal rightward, leaving the entries left of it, nonzero for a coinitial swap, raw derivatives blind to the displacement. Each row then equals QuantLib's own CmSwapZedMatrix(CurveState, int, double)'s first row at a spanning of the row's swap, bit for bit.
