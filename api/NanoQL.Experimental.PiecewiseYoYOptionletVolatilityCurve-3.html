Remarks

The curve bootstraps in its factory (Create(int, Calendar, BusinessDayConvention, DayCounter, Period, Frequency, bool, double, double, double, IEnumerable<BootstrapHelper<YoYOptionletVolatilitySurface>>, EvaluationContext, double, TInterpolator, TBootstrap)), over a working state its helpers' bound copies price against, as the piecewise year-on-year inflation curve does; its first node is the base date at the base volatility, the assumption on the unquoted early optionlets. It implements IInterpolatedCurve<TTraits, TInterpolator>, where QuantLib's derives from the interpolated curve, and its nodes come from its dates and data, QuantLib's master's (#2941), where 1.44's nodes are empty. The last date is the reference date plus the last time rounded up to whole years, adjusted, QuantLib's approximation.
