Remarks

Superfund is sometimes also called "supershare", which can lead to ambiguity; QuantLib uses the terms supershare and superfund consistently with the definitions in the help pages of Bloomberg's OVX function.

It is equivalent to being (1/lower strike) long an AssetOrNothingPayoff call at the lower strike and short one at the higher strike. Its type is always Call, and its description is the striked payoff's, without the second strike, as QuantLib's is.
