Remarks

The volatility spreads are a row per option and swap tenor pair, the pair (j, k) of the j-th option tenor and the k-th swap tenor in row j·(swap tenors) + k, with a column per strike spread. A cube's grid, reference date and conventions are its at-the-money structure's: the constructor passes that structure's reference date, calendar, business-day convention, day counter and settlement days to SwaptionVolatilityDiscrete, whose constructor computes the grid from them. Its maximum date and time, maximum swap tenor, volatility type and shift are the at-the-money structure's, read when asked; its strikes are unbounded.

The at-the-money strike of an option date and a swap tenor is the fixing of a swap index of that tenor, with the conventions of the long swap index for a tenor past the short index's, else of the short one: it reads the context's evaluation date, its fixings and its at-par switch. The at-the-money volatility is read from a copy of the structure with extrapolation enabled, which the cube holds: QuantLib enables extrapolation on the caller's structure itself, for every holder of it, where the caller's structure here is left unchanged.

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the interpolated cube and the XABR cubes derive from it. QuantLib's requiredNumberOfStrikes() virtual, which its calculation reads, is a constructor argument here, checked when the cube is built; QuantLib's unused localStrikes_ and localSmile_ aren't ported.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The structure, a collection or an index is null.

ArgumentException

The tenors are empty, not positive or not increasing, a swap tenor isn't in months or years, the strike spreads aren't increasing, the volatility spreads' rows aren't one per option and swap tenor pair or their columns one per strike spread, the short index's tenor is past the long index's, or there are fewer strike spreads than required.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

requiredNumberOfStrikes is negative.

NanoQLException

Two tenors can't be compared, or the structure has no calendar.
