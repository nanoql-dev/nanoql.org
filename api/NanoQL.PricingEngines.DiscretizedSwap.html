Remarks

A working type (DiscretizedAsset). Each coupon is added at its reset time, in the pre-adjustment or, as its adjustment says, the post-adjustment: a fixed coupon as its amount times a discount bond from its payment time, a floating one as the nominal times one less that bond, plus the nominal times its accrual time and spread times the bond. A coupon whose reset time is before the reference date and whose payment isn't (or is on it, when the context's IncludeTodaysCashFlows is true) is added in the post-adjustment at its payment time instead, as its known amount; a floating one whose amount can't be computed is refused there. The coupons' times are the given day counter's year fractions from the reference date.

A floating coupon known by its fixing date: the public constructor, a swap's own value as the tree swap engine prices it, knows a floating coupon whose fixing date is on or before the reference date, adding its amount at its payment time, where QuantLib knows it by its accrual start (its reset date, vanillaswap.cpp:67; discretizedswap.cpp:84-87), so between its fixing and its start the tree forecasts a coupon whose fixing the discounting engine reads (Divergences/TreeKnownCouponTests.cs). The internal constructor, which the discretized swaption calls, keeps QuantLib's rule, so a swaption exercised in that window takes its floating leg at par, QuantLib's convention.

A floating coupon paid after its accrual end: QuantLib values a floating coupon as a par floater to its payment date, the nominal times one less the bond from its payment time (discretizedswap.cpp:199-218), which overprices one paid on a lag or an adjusted date after its accrual end. Here such a coupon takes its forward over its own accrual period and is paid on its payment date: the nominal times the bond from its payment time over the bond from its accrual end, less the bond from its payment time, plus its spread; its accrual end is a mandatory time only there. With QuantLib's check, the finite-difference engine on a lagged swap (Divergences/TreeSwaptionSnappingTests.cs); a coupon paid on its accrual end keeps QuantLib's value.

Its discount bonds. QuantLib's swap builds a discount bond for each coupon it adds, initializes it at the payment time and rolls it back to the swap's time. This swap keeps one bond, re-initialized for each coupon (a discount bond has no adjustments, so the adjustment times its memo keeps skip nothing), and a second for the accrual ends, whose buffers its Reset(int) reserves at the widest step of the swap's own rollback, from its initial step down to the grid's start: every coupon's bond starts at or before the swap's initial time and rolls back to the swap's time, so no coupon's bond allocates.
