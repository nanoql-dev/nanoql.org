The adaptive Runge-Kutta integrator of an ordinary differential equation f'(x) = F(x, f(x)), F from ℝ × Kⁿ to Kⁿ, K the real or the complex numbers: Cash and Karp's embedded fifth-order step, whose fourth-order error estimate sets the next step's size (Numerical Recipes in C, chapter 16.2).

Remarks

QuantLib's real coefficients multiply the values as values of T, and a value's magnitude is T.Abs's, as a real. For finite values that is C++'s arithmetic on std::complex too.

An equation whose function returns a vector of another length than its argument's is refused, where QuantLib reads past the shorter one.

Immutable and thread-safe: it holds only its settings, and each integration works on its own state. Unsealed for its named class, QuantLib-Python's RungeKutta.
