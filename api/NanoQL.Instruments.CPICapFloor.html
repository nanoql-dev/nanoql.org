A CPI cap or floor: an option on the cumulative inflation from a base date to maturity, quoted as a fixed strike rate K, paying P(0,T) max(y (N [(1 + K)^T − 1] − N [I(T)/I(0) − 1]), 0), T the maturity time, N the notional, I the index and y the option's sign.

Remarks

A CPI cap or floor is an option, not a cap or floor on a coupon: like a zero-coupon inflation swap it has a single flow, cumulative from the base date to the option's maturity. Inflation is published on every day, holidays and weekends included, so whether the fixing date is adjusted is the fixing convention's choice. Its engine, InterpolatingCPICapFloorEngine, reads QuantLib's arguments through an internal builder.

It expires after its unadjusted maturity, an option's expiry, as QuantLib's.
