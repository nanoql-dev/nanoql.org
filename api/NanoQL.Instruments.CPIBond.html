Remarks

The cash flows are CPILeg's, its notional flow the redemption, paying the whole inflated notional. It is built through Bond's construction contract, so its issue date isn't checked against the first payment, as in QuantLib. GrowthOnly is QuantLib's inspector, false for every bond the constructor builds; QuantLib's deprecated constructor setting it isn't ported.

The bond holds QuantLib's default CPI-coupon pricer on its coupons; InflationCouponPricerFunctions sets another on a copy.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

schedule, cpiIndex, coupons or accrualDayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

settlementDays is negative, or an enum argument isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The issue date is default(Date), or a coupon refuses its base.

NanoQLException

The schedule has no tenor or is empty; no fixed rate is given; a coupon with a cap or floor is asked for; or a date is computed on QuantLib's empty calendar where it can't give one.
