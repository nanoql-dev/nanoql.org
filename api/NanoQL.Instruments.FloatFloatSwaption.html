Remarks

A swaption has no payoff, and its results are an engine's InstrumentResults. The settlement type and method are checked against each other when the swaption is priced, as QuantLib's arguments check them. CalibrationBasket(BasketGeneratingEngine, SwapIndex, SwaptionVolatilityStructure, EvaluationContext, BasketGeneratingEngineCalibrationBasketType) takes the basket-generating engine, which QuantLib's instrument holds and nanoql's doesn't.
