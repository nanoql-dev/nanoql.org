Remarks

A static class (the playbook's engine row): Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1). Only the fixed coupons whose start is on or after the exercise date are part of the bond, which pays the nominal at the floating leg's accrual end. A floating coupon paid after its accrual end (an overnight swap with a payment lag) has the difference its delay makes frozen at time zero and folded into the nominal at the value date, as QuantLib's does. One call shares one memo of the model's terms.

Two documented divergences (Divergences/JamshidianBracketTests.cs and Divergences/JamshidianNoFixedCouponTests.cs). QuantLib solves for y* on the fixed bracket [−8, 8] and refuses short-dated swaptions away from the money; the bracket here is QuantLib's first and is doubled only where it doesn't bracket the root, so QuantLib's solver path and bits stand wherever it prices. And with no fixed coupon starting on or after the exercise, QuantLib reads the fixed reset dates past their end as the bond's value date; here the value date is the first floating reset on or after the exercise, the start of the coupons QuantLib's Gaussian1d swaption engine exercises into, and with none the swaption is worth 0, as that engine values it.
