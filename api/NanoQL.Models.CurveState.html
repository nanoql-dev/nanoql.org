The curve state of a market-model simulation: the yield curve at the simulation's fixed rate times, the workhorse discounting object a product reads, so that a product runs on any kind of model (LIBOR, coterminal swap or constant-maturity swap).

Remarks

There are n + 1 rate times, the payment and reset times of n forward rates: t₀ … tₙ, the forwards f₀ … fₙ₋₁, the discount bonds d₀ … dₙ, the discount ratios dᵢ/d₀, and the coterminal swap rates sr₀ … srₙ₋₁.

A working type: a derived state changes in place when it is set, and computes some of its reads lazily into buffers of its own, so it is owned by one computation and isn't thread-safe. Its vector reads (ForwardRates(), CoterminalSwapRates(), CmSwapRates(int)) are views of its own buffers, valid until it is next set or the same read is made with another argument; a caller that keeps one copies it, and Clone() keeps the whole state. An extension point, as in QuantLib; the setters live on the derived states only.
