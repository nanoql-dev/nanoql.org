Remarks

A working type, copying each step's pseudo-root at construction; one step allocates nothing after a path's warm-up.

SetInitialState(CurveState) starts each path from the state's forwards, where QuantLib's sets only the initial drifts and leaves the paths starting from the model's forwards: its loop's body is the drifts' computation, the starting forwards never written (normalfwdratepc.cpp:80-81; Divergences/NormalFwdRatePcInitialStateTests.cs).
