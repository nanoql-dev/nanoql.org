Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's. At zero carry, where the formula's ratio is 0/0, its limit, upstream's fix after v1.44 (#2856).

Documented divergences. Each rate is read on the volatility's clock and each time is the volatility's, as AnalyticBarrierEngine reads them, where QuantLib reads every structure at the risk-free curve's times (Divergences/LookbackMismatchedTimesTests.cs). The extremum observed so far is read as the spot where it lies beyond it, the spot being observed, where QuantLib prices the impossible extremum (Divergences/LookbackExtremeBeyondSpotTests.cs). Near zero carry the formula is evaluated in double-double, where QuantLib's double cancels and #2856 switches to its limit below |λ| of 1e-7 (NanoQL.PricingEngines.LookbackSmallCarry, Divergences/LookbackSmallCarryTests.cs). At small volatilities, where λ = 2b/σ² is large and QuantLib's power of s times its normal factor is +∞ times 0, NaN, the product is taken in logs (NanoQL.PricingEngines.LookbackTails, Divergences/LookbackSmallVolatilityTests.cs). And below machine-epsilon variance the underlying follows its forward, where QuantLib divides by the volatility (Divergences/LookbackZeroVolatilityTests.cs).

The formula is a first-passage closed form in equivalent-constant rates and a volatility, the variance and discounts to expiry: no exact closed form exists for a first passage under general deterministic term structures. It is exact where (r − q)/σ² is constant over the option's life and the volatility depends on time alone, the price then a time change of a constant market's (Conventions/ClosedFormExactnessTests.cs); the term-structure-exact route is MCLookbackEngine, which reads the extremum at its grid's dates.
