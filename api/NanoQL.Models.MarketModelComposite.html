Remarks

A working type: Add(MarketModelMultiProduct, double) and Subtract(MarketModelMultiProduct, double) store a clone of the product given, QuantLib's Clone<>, so the caller's product is never stepped by the composite; FinalizeComposite() fixes the parts, after which the composite steps; Item(int) is the composite's own part.

A part's end is recorded (a documented divergence): a part that reports its path finished isn't stepped again, and the composite's path is finished when every part's is, or at its last step. QuantLib records no part's end (compositeproduct.cpp:43 resets the flag its nextTimeStep tests and never sets), so a finished part steps again at each of its later times, and it finishes the composite's path when the parts it stepped at a step are finished, whatever the others' later times: a swaption beside forwards was worth 4.2 to 7.7 times its value alone, and forwards beside a rebate lost their later flows (Divergences/MarketModelCompositeEndTests.cs). A derived composite steps its parts through StepPart(int, CurveState) and ends its step with EndStep(), which keep these rules.

An extension point.
