Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The option, the context or the process is null.

ArgumentException

The time steps are given neither or both ways, or aren't positive; the basis family is insufficient; a regression's matrix isn't finite (the second-kind Chebyshev basis on a call).

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A number of samples is negative, a seed is outside its range, or the default calibration seed is 0.

NanoQLException

The exercise isn't early or pays at expiry, the control variate's payoff isn't striked, neither a tolerance nor a number of samples is given, or the samples can't reach the tolerance.
