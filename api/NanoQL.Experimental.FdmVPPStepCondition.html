The step condition of a virtual power plant valued by dynamic programming: each step adds the running states' spark-spread earnings, at the minimum or the maximum output, then lets each point's states change as the plant's rules allow, at the gas price there.

Remarks

The plant's states cycle in blocks of 2·tMinUp + tMinDown: tMinUp states running at the minimum output, tMinUp at the maximum, and tMinDown off. A derived condition gives the state changes and the value of a point's states.

A step condition is a solver's working state: it holds the states it changes at a point.
