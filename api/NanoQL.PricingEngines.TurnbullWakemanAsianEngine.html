Remarks

The value, delta and gamma, and QuantLib's additional results ("accrued", "discount", "strike", "effective_strike", "forward", "exp_A_2", "tte", "sigma", "times", "spotVols", "forwards"). An effective strike at or below zero is the certain exercise, priced in closed form (Haug, "Option Pricing Formulas", 2nd ed., p. 193).

Documented divergences. With every fixing past, the known payoff discounted, as Choi's engine prices it, where QuantLib's reads the last of no fixing times and crashes the process (Divergences/TurnbullWakemanPastFixingsTests.cs); QuantLib's master prices it so after 1.44 (#2954), its branch ahead of the certain exercise's, as here. A seasoned option reads its variances and spot volatilities at the future average's own strike, K_f = (K − A/m)·m/f for f of m fixings to come, so it prices as (f/m) times the unseasoned option at K_f, where QuantLib reads them at K − A/m, +42% and +1,302% off on a smile varying with maturity (Divergences/SeasonedAsianSmileTests.cs).

The last fixing on the evaluation date, at time 0, is priced with a zero standard deviation and σ, its delta discount · α · E[A] / S, as QuantLib's master prices it after 1.44 (#2954, Divergences/AsianUpstreamFixesTests.cs), where QuantLib 1.44 divides by the last time and refuses the NaN standard deviation; the standard deviation is the master's, √log(E[A²]/E[A]²), and each spot volatility the structure's volatility, so the price's last bits differ from 1.44's.

A fixing dated from the evaluation date to the latest structure's date, where the structures settle after the evaluation date, is taken there, the spot with no variance and no discount accrued, so as a past fixing at the spot (Divergences/AsianLateSettlementTests.cs), where QuantLib reads its negative time and refuses it.
