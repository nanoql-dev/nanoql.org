Remarks

A field the record leaves out is the set's FixedRateBond record's, which mirrors QuantLib's constructors' defaults, or theirs: payment Following, redemption 100, ex-coupon dates unadjusted without end of month, a clean price. The schedule is MakeSchedule's from the issue date to the maturity, its options the record's. The bond takes no price type, so a record giving one is refused there.

A record whose chain reaches BTP's preset record configures that class, which rounds its accrued amount: build BTP in the set for it, which the routes here refuse.
