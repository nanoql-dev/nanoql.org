Remarks

The legs are signed by the protection side, as QuantLib's engines sign them; PremiumLegNPV and ProtectionLegNPV are QuantLib's instrument's, which sign them again (the premium leg's value is always the negative of the premium's size, the protection leg's the protection's size).
