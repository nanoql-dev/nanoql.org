The G2 stochastic process: the two-factor G2++ process with its state shifted so that the two components sum to the short rate, (z₁, z₂) = (x + φ(t), y), x and y the zero-mean Ornstein-Uhlenbeck factors and φ the deterministic offset that fits the initial curve; without a curve, the two zero-mean processes (φ ≡ 0).

Remarks

A path generated on it satisfies r(tᵢ) = z₁ᵢ + z₂ᵢ, its expectation φ(tᵢ). The step's conditional correlation, QuantLib's ρση(1 − e^(−(a+b)Δt))/(a + b) over ½ση√((1 − e^(−2aΔt))(1 − e^(−2bΔt))/(ab)), is written as ρ·f((a+b)Δt)/√(f(2aΔt)·f(2bΔt)), f(x) = (1 − e^(−x))/x, and held in [−1, 1], ρ itself where a = b; φ's terms as G2's: a divergence, QuantLib's √(1 − ρ²) of a correlation an ulp past ±1 being NaN at ρ = ±1 with a = b (Divergences/G2ProcessCorrelationBoundTests.cs), and its terms cancelling at small reversion.
