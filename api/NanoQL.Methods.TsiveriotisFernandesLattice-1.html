The binomial lattice of the Tsiveriotis-Fernandes model: a Black-Scholes lattice whose step back carries each node's conversion probability and discounts each branch at its node's blended rate, the risk-free rate and the risk-free rate plus the credit spread weighted by that probability.

Remarks

It rolls back a DiscretizedConvertible only, as QuantLib's does; another asset is refused. Its step back is QuantLib's, the tree's first-node probabilities applied to every node: pd·v[j]/(1 + r[j]·dt) + pu·v[j + 1]/(1 + r[j + 1]·dt), the probabilities and rates of the step stepped back likewise, into the asset's spare buffers.

The volatility and the dividend yield, which QuantLib's constructor takes and doesn't read, are kept in the signature.
