Remarks

The approximation replaces √v_t in the equity-rate covariance by a fit E[√v_t] ≈ a + b·e^{−ct}, its term I₄ added to AnalyticHestonHullWhiteEngine's. QuantLib's values are corrected where they're wrong:

  • Where the fit has no decaying exponential, the ratio (Λ − a)/b that gives c at or below 0, or b = 0, c is the limit c = +∞, the b terms dropped, and at or above 1 the limit c = 0, its b terms b·T and −b(1 − e^{−λT})/λ; QuantLib's c is NaN there, and so is its price, at ρ_Sr = 0 too, or the fit grows exponentially. In between, QuantLib's fit and bits.
  • I₄'s last term is b·e^{−cT}·(−expm1((c − λ)T))/(c − λ), with its limit −b·T·e^{−cT} at c = λ, where QuantLib's b/(c − λ) is 0/0, and below λT = 2e-2, where I₄'s terms cancel to order λ and QuantLib's double departs from the expression's exact value by more than 1e-12 relative, I₄ is evaluated in double-double, below 1e-10 in quad-double and below 1e-12 by the cubic through four quad-double values, the cut and floors measured against the expression in 120 digits.

References: Lech A. Grzelak, Cornelis W. Oosterlee, On The Heston Model with Stochastic; Lech A. Grzelak, Equity and Foreign Exchange Hybrid Models for Pricing Long-Maturity Financial Derivatives.
