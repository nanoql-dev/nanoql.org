The clubs topology, after H. M. Emara, "Adaptive Clubs-based Particle Swarm Optimization": each particle belongs to a default number of clubs, and its social best is the best of its clubs' members; a club's best leaves a random club (down to a minimum) and its worst joins one (up to a maximum), and every reset iterations the others return to the default: QuantLib's ClubsTopology.

Remarks

Fixes (Divergences/ParticleSwarmTopologyTests.cs): the initial clubs are drawn in [0, totalClubs − 1], where QuantLib's draws in [1, totalClubs] and reads one past each particle's clubs; a particle's social best is its best club's best particle, where QuantLib's takes the club's index for a particle; each club's worst is weighed over every member, where QuantLib's tests it only on a member that isn't the new best, so a club whose values fall in order names particle 0 its worst, a member or not; and a best particle leaves a club only above the minimum and a worst one joins only below the maximum, as the class documents, where QuantLib's never tests either, so a particle that stays best leaves every club and its social best is then the largest double. A club none of whose members passes QuantLib's comparisons, one with no members or whose personal bests are all NaN, has no best, and a particle reads only its clubs with a best, or its own personal best where none has one, where QuantLib's reads particle 0, a member or not, for such a club; a minimum below 1 is refused, as a particle in no club has no club-mates and so no social best, where QuantLib's best particle leaves every club and keeps particle 0's position at the largest double.

Its integers are std::uniform_int_distribution's on a std::mt19937 on the seed, drawn as MSVC draws them; the seed is required, QuantLib's default seeding from the clock. Each minimization starts the generator from the seed and the iterations from 0.
