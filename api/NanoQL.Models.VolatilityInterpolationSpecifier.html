Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's setScalingFactors and setLastCapletVol are WithScalingFactors(ReadOnlySpan<double>) and WithLastCapletVol(double), each returning a new specifier and leaving this one unchanged. An extension point, as in QuantLib.
