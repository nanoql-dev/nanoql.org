Remarks

The fit is computed by Create<TX>(IReadOnlyList<TX>, IReadOnlyList<double>, IReadOnlyList<Func<TX, double>>), QuantLib's templated constructor, and the object is immutable: its members return copies. Singular values below n·ε times the largest are dropped, as QuantLib drops them.

The standard errors are the modelling uncertainty times the residuals' standard deviation over n − p degrees of freedom, p the coefficients the fit estimates (the singular values it keeps: the basis' size at full rank), as R's lm computes them. QuantLib divides by n − 2 whatever the basis (generallinearleastsquares.hpp:144), which is R's only for a straight line, infinite at n = 2 and undefined at n = 1, where n − 2 wraps: a documented divergence (Divergences/LeastSquaresStandardErrorTests.cs); at p = 2 the errors are QuantLib's.

Samples and values of different sizes are refused, with QuantLib's text, where QuantLib checks the values' size against itself and writes the samples past the matrix's column, or leaves it partly unset.
