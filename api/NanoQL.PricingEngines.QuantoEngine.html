Remarks

QuantLib's QuantoEngine<Instr, Engine> as a static class over a delegate in the engines' form: each overload runs the instrument's steps, builds QuantLib's quanto process (QuantoTermStructure as its dividend curve, at the drift's strike and the exchange rate's at-the-money level 1) and calls the engine on the instrument's terms as the inner engine's own instrument, QuantLib's arguments copy; the generic core takes any one-asset instrument and its terms, so later engines and a user's own quanto instruments plug in.

Qvega and Qlambda read the underlying's volatility at the drift's strike, where QuantLib reads it at the spot, so on a smile they are the derivatives of the price the drift gives (Divergences/QuantoSmileTests.cs); on a flat volatility, QuantLib's. A quanto forward-start option's drift is read at the moneyness times the spot, the strike its forward engine prices at, where QuantLib reads it at the payoff's strike, which the forward engine ignores.

The rho, vega, Qrho, Qvega and Qlambda come from the inner dividend rho through each input's change to the quanto curve's log discount, by date, at the dates the inner engine reads: at expiry, and for a forward-start option at the reset too. QuantLib's assume the input moves the quanto zero rate by one factor at expiry, which misses where a structure counts or dates its time apart from the dividend curve, whose clock the quanto curve keeps, and for a forward-start option where an input's contribution varies between the reset and expiry, as on a volatility term structure (Divergences/QuantoGreeksTermStructureTests.cs). Where every structure shares the dividend curve's times and the factor is one, QuantLib's formula.

Remarks

The derived Greeks (rho, vega, qrho, qvega, qlambda) take each input's change to the quanto curve at the underlying's expiry alone: they assume the inner engine reads the quanto curve's dividend discount there and nowhere earlier, as a European, barrier or double-barrier engine does. An inner engine reading it at an earlier date too, as the forward-start engine does at its reset, needs that date's terms, which the forward-start overload adds.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

An argument is null.

NanoQLException

The underlying's payoff isn't striked, the spot isn't positive, or the inner engine refuses.
