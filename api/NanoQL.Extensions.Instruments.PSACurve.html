Remarks

The rates follow SIFMA's standard formula (Uniform Practices, Chapter SF, SF-6: CPR = min{100, PSA/100 · 0.2 · max{1, min{MONTH, 30}}}), MONTH "the accrual period during which the age of the mortgage loans increases from MONTH – 1 to MONTH": the whole calendar months from the curve's start, plus one, so that a date's MONTH is k + 1 from the start's k-month anniversary until the next. Three departures from QLNet follow it. QLNet counts the month as the 30/360 Bond Basis days from the start over 30, plus one, which from a start on the 29th to the 31st repeats a month at February's anniversary and skips the next, and counts a 31st a month ahead from a start on the 1st; the CPR is capped at 100%, where QLNet's passes it above 1,666⅔% PSA and its SMM is NaN; and every month before the first counts as the first ("MONTH equals 1 for all prior months"), where QLNet's falls to 0 and below. Elsewhere the rates are QLNet's, bit for bit.

The SMM is the CPR's monthly equivalent, 1 − (1 − CPR)^(1/12) (SF-5).
