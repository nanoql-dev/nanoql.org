Remarks

The returned FX rate is expressed in units of resettable-leg currency per unit of constant-leg currency. Historical observations are obtained from the context's ExchangeRates on the reset fixing date; future observations are projected to the reset value date.

Immutable, its spot a double. A reset fixed today is read from the registry unless the context enforces today's historic fixings, and forecast when the registry holds no rate for it, the only refusal QuantLib's comment means ("A today's fixing may not have been published yet"); QuantLib forecasts on any refusal, a published non-positive fixing among them, which a fixing on a past date refuses (tests/NanoQL.Tests/Divergences/FxResetTodaysFixingTests.cs).
