Parameters

to Date

The date.

Returns

double

The factor of the date's period, counted from the base date and wrapped around the factors.

Remarks

The product of the factors indexed by the months from the base date's month up to the date's month, excluded; its inverse when the date's month is earlier in the year.

Exceptions

NanoQLException

The seasonality doesn't hold 12 factors.
