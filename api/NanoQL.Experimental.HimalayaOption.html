A Himalaya option on a basket: at each fixing date the best performer since inception among the assets left joins the average and leaves the basket; at the last fixing the option pays the average's excess over the strike.

Remarks

Immutable; its payoff is a plain-vanilla call at the strike and its exercise European at the last fixing date, as QuantLib builds them. Its engines return a MultiAssetOptionResults, QuantLib's results class adding nothing to the multi-asset option's. QuantLib doesn't manage seasoned options: a fixing before the evaluation date is refused by the engine. The paths start at the evaluation date, the performances' inception, so a fixing on it is that inception rather than a selection (every asset's performance is 1 there), as QuantLib's grid reads it and its own case lists one.
