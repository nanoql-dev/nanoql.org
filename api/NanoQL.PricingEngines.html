Namespace NanoQL.PricingEngines

Classes

AmericanBasketPathPricer

The early-exercise path pricer of a basket option for the Longstaff-Schwartz engine: the assets' prices at a step, scaled by the strike, as the regression's state, and its basis, the polynomials of several variables with the payoff appended.

AmericanPathPricer

The American early-exercise path pricer: the payoff of the underlying at a time step, the state the underlying scaled by the strike, and a polynomial basis of the state with the payoff as one more function.

AmericanPayoffAtExpiry

The analytic formula for American exercise with the payoff at expiry: a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing payoff paid at expiry if the underlying has reached the strike (knock-in: an up-and-in call or a down-and-in put), or if it hasn't (knock-out: an up-and-out call or a down-and-out put).

AmericanPayoffAtHit

The analytic formula for American exercise with the payoff at hit: a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing payoff paid when the underlying first reaches the strike, an up-and-in call or a down-and-in put.

AnalyticAmericanMargrabeEngine

The analytic engine for an American Margrabe option: the exchange priced as an American call on Q₁S₁ struck at Q₂S₂, on the first asset's yield as dividend, the second's as rate and their spread's volatility, by BjerksundStenslandApproximationEngine; E. G. Haug, "The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas", 2nd edition (2007).

AnalyticBSMHullWhiteEngine

The analytic European option pricer including stochastic interest rates: the Black-Scholes engine on the equity variance shifted by the Hull-White rate's variance and its covariance with the equity (Brigo and Mercurio, "Interest Rate Models").

AnalyticBarrierEngine

The analytic engine for European barrier options: Haug's closed forms ("Option pricing formulas", 1998, pp. 69-72) on a Black-Scholes process.

AnalyticBinaryBarrierEngine

The analytic engine for binary barrier options: a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing payoff at expiry, knocked in or out at a barrier, by Haug's formulas ("The complete guide to option pricing formulas", 2nd ed., p. 176 and following).

AnalyticBlackVasicekEngine

The pricing of vanilla European options under a stochastic Vasicek interest rate: Black's formula on the Vasicek bond with the total variance of the equity, the rate and their covariance (the Black-Scholes-Vasicek model, http://hsrm-mathematik.de/WS201516/master/option-pricing/Black-Scholes-Vasicek-Model.pdf).

AnalyticCEVEngine

The pricing engine for European vanilla options on the constant elasticity of variance (CEV) process df = α·f^β·dW, with an absorbing boundary at f = 0: CEVCalculator's value, discounted.

AnalyticCapFloorEngine

The analytic cap/floor engine: each optionlet an option on a discount bond of an affine model.

AnalyticCliquetEngine

The analytic engine for a European cliquet option: each period a forward-start option struck at the moneyness times the spot, by Black's formula on the period's forward variance, weighted by the dividend discount to its start.

AnalyticComplexChooserEngine

The analytic engine for a complex chooser option: Rubinstein's formula, the critical spot at the choosing date where the call and the put are worth the same, and bivariate terms for each.

AnalyticCompoundOptionEngine

The analytic engine for a compound option: Geske's formula, the critical spot at the mother's maturity where the daughter is worth the mother's strike, and a bivariate term for the daughter.

AnalyticContinuousFixedLookbackEngine

The analytic engine for continuous fixed-strike lookback options: Conze and Viswanathan's closed form, as in Haug, "Option Pricing Formulas" (1998), pp. 63-64.

AnalyticContinuousFloatingLookbackEngine

The analytic engine for continuous floating-strike lookback options: Goldman, Sosin and Gatto's closed form, as in Haug, "Option Pricing Formulas" (1998), pp. 61-62.

AnalyticContinuousGeometricAveragePriceAsianEngine

The analytic engine for continuous geometric average-price Asian options, averaging from the evaluation date: the continuous geometric average of a log-normal process is log-normal (Haug, "Option Pricing Formulas", 1998, pp. 96-97).

AnalyticContinuousPartialFixedLookbackEngine

The analytic engine for continuous partial-time fixed-strike lookback options: Heynen and Kat's closed form, as in Haug, "The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas" (2007), pp. 148-149.

AnalyticContinuousPartialFloatingLookbackEngine

The analytic engine for continuous partial-time floating-strike lookback options: Heynen and Kat's closed form, as in Haug, "The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas" (2007), pp. 146-147.

AnalyticDigitalAmericanEngine

The analytic pricing engine for American vanilla options with a digital payoff: cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing, paid at the hit (AmericanPayoffAtHit) or at expiry (AmericanPayoffAtExpiry, knock-in).

AnalyticDigitalAmericanKOEngine

The analytic pricing engine for American knock-out options with a digital payoff at expiry: a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing payoff paid at expiry if the underlying never reached the strike (an up-and-out call or a down-and-out put).

AnalyticDiscreteGeometricAveragePriceAsianEngine

The analytic engine for discrete geometric average-price Asian options: the geometric average of log-normal fixings is log-normal, priced by Black's formula on its forward and variance (Clewlow and Strickland, "Implementing Derivatives Models", 1998, pp. 118-123).

AnalyticDiscreteGeometricAverageStrikeAsianEngine

The analytic engine for discrete geometric average-strike Asian options: the option to exchange the geometric average of log-normal fixings for the terminal spot, both log-normal, priced in closed form.

AnalyticDividendEuropeanEngine

The analytic pricing engine for European options with discrete dividends: the Black-Scholes formulas on the spot less the present value of the dividends paid from the settlement date to the exercise date.

AnalyticDoubleBarrierBinaryEngine

The analytic engine for double barrier binary options: cash-or-nothing knock-outs and knock-ins paid at expiry by Hui's eigenfunction series ("One-touch double barrier binary option values", Applied Financial Economics 6/1996), and KIKO and KOKI options paid at the hit, by the same series' sums.

AnalyticDoubleBarrierEngine

The analytic engine for European double barrier options: Ikeda and Kunitomo's image series ("Pricing Options with Curved Boundaries", Mathematical Finance 2/1992) as Haug gives it ("The complete guide to option pricing formulas", 2nd ed., p. 156 and following), for flat barriers.

AnalyticEuropeanEngine

The pricing engine for European vanilla options by the analytic Black-Scholes formulas, through BlackCalculator, for plain vanilla, cash-or-nothing, asset-or-nothing and gap payoffs.

AnalyticEuropeanMargrabeEngine

The analytic engine for a European Margrabe option: W. Margrabe, "The Value of an Option to Exchange One Asset for Another", Journal of Finance 33 (1978), 177-186; with E. G. Haug, "The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas", 2nd edition (2007), for the Greeks.

AnalyticGJRGARCHEngine

The analytic GJR-GARCH(1,1) engine for European options: Duan, Gauthier, Simonato and Sasseville's Edgeworth approximation, from the first four moments of the log return over the option's days.

AnalyticH1HWEngine

The analytic Heston-Hull-White engine on Grzelak and Oosterlee's H1-HW approximation: the equity correlated with the short rate, ρ_Sr ≥ 0, the variance independent of it.

AnalyticHestonEngine

The analytic Heston engine: European plain-vanilla options under the Heston model, by the Fourier transform of the model's characteristic function, integrated by a FourierIntegration.

AnalyticHestonEngineAPHelper

The integrand of AnalyticHestonEngine's control-variate forms and the control variate's value: QuantLib's AnalyticHestonEngine::AP_Helper.

AnalyticHestonEngineOptimalAlpha

The damping exponent α that minimizes the Fourier integrand's size at the origin, for the engine's control variates: Andersen and Lake's choice, QuantLib's AnalyticHestonEngine::OptimalAlpha.

AnalyticHestonHullWhiteEngine

The analytic Heston engine with a Hull-White short rate independent of the equity and its variance: dS = (r − d)S dt

  • √v S dW₁, dv = κ(θ − v) dt + σ√v dW₂, dr = (θ(t) − ar) dt + η dW₃, dW₁dW₂ = ρ dt, dW₁dW₃ = dW₂dW₃ = 0.
AnalyticHolderExtensibleOptionEngine

The analytic engine for a holder-extensible option: Longstaff's formula, the vanilla to the first maturity and bivariate terms over the region where the holder extends, between two critical prices.

AnalyticPDFHestonEngine

The Heston engine for European options on any payoff: the payoff integrated against HestonRNDCalculator's density of the log spot at expiry.

AnalyticPTDHestonEngine

The analytic engine for European plain-vanilla options under PiecewiseTimeDependentHestonModel: the characteristic function's recursion across the model's time grid, integrated by Gatheral's form or Andersen and Piterbarg's.

AnalyticPartialTimeBarrierOptionEngine

The analytic engine for partial-time barrier options: Haug's formulas ("Option Pricing Formulas"), a put priced by the put-call symmetry from the call.

AnalyticPerformanceEngine

The analytic engine for a European performance option: a cliquet whose periods pay their returns, each by Black's formula on the period's forward variance, discounted to its start.

AnalyticRoughHestonEngine

The analytic engine for European plain-vanilla options under RoughHestonModel: the characteristic function's fractional Riccati equation solved by the Adams scheme, the Padé approximation or the lifted approximation, and the price by Andersen and Piterbarg's Fourier integral with a Black-Scholes control variate.

AnalyticSimpleChooserEngine

The analytic engine for a simple chooser option: Rubinstein's decomposition at the choosing date, the call to maturity and the put to the choosing date at the strike carried there.

AnalyticSoftBarrierEngine

The analytic engine for European soft barrier options: Haug's closed form ("The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas", 2nd ed., after Hart and Ross, 1994), and the soft barrier's definition, the average of the single-barrier options with barriers in [L, U], where that form doesn't hold.

AnalyticTwoAssetBarrierEngine

The analytic engine for a barrier option on two assets: the first asset's payoff, knocked in or out by the second asset at the barrier (Haug, "The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas", 2nd ed.).

AnalyticTwoAssetCorrelationEngine

The analytic engine for a European two-asset correlation option: M. Zhang, "Exotic Options", World Scientific (1998); E. G. Haug, "The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas", 2nd edition (2007).

AnalyticWriterExtensibleOptionEngine

The analytic engine for a writer-extensible option: Longstaff's formula, the vanilla to the first exercise and a bivariate term for the extension, on West's bivariate normal.

ArithmeticAPOHestonPathPricer

ArithmeticAPOPathPricer

The path pricer of a discrete arithmetic average-price Asian option: the discounted payoff at the average of the path's fixings and the past ones.

ArithmeticASOPathPricer

The path pricer of a discrete arithmetic average-strike Asian option: the discounted payoff of the path's last value struck at the average of its fixings and the past ones.

BachelierCalculator

The path pricer of a barrier option with the Brownian bridge correction for the barrier's crossings between the path's points: each step's extreme is drawn from its bridge, given a uniform number of its own (Beaglehole, Dybvig and Zhou, "Going to Extremes: Correcting Simulation Bias in Exotic Option Valuation", Financial Analysts Journal, Jan/Feb 1997; El Babsiri and Noel, "Simulating path-dependent options: A new approach", Journal of Derivatives, Winter 1998).

BatesEngine

DigitalPathPricer

DiscountingSwapEngine
