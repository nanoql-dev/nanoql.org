Remarks

The curve holds continuously compounded zero rates: rates given with another compounding are converted to their continuous equivalents at the nodes' times when it is built, and its data are those equivalents. Past its last node it extrapolates with a flat instantaneous forward rate. Its formula and maximum date are the ZeroYield traits', which a PiecewiseYieldCurve<TTraits, TInterpolator, TBootstrap> on those traits shares. Unsealed for its named classes, which add constructors only. QuantLib's protected constructors, through which its piecewise curve derives, aren't ported: no class derives through them here.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

dates, yields or dayCounter is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

compounding isn't a member of its enum, or the compounding isn't continuous and frequency isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

There are fewer dates than the interpolation needs, the counts of dates and rates differ, the dates aren't sorted or two of them give the same time, the jumps and their dates differ in number, a compounded rate's frequency is Once or NoFrequency, a rate's compound factor isn't positive, or the interpolation refuses the nodes.

NanoQLException

The compounding is one that QuantLib can't convert directly, or the interpolation refuses the nodes.
