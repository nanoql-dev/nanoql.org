Remarks

The helper's dates are those of the index's period at the maturity less the swap's observation lag: with an interpolated observation, from that period's start to the next one's, the pillar chosen as QuantLib chooses it; otherwise the period's start alone. Without a start date the swap starts on the evaluation date, which the helper's dates follow; with one, they don't.

The helper bound to a curve (SetTermStructure<T>(T, ZeroInflationTermStructure)) holds the index cloned on the curve, and the swap rebuilt on it. Its implied quote is the swap's fair rate, which reads the index through the curve and no discount: QuantLib's swap is priced on a flat zero nominal curve, whose equal discounts on the two legs' one payment date cancel, and its fair rate reads none of them. QuantLib's deprecated nominal-curve member isn't ported.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
