Remarks

The instrument assumes that the issuer hasn't defaulted by the evaluation date. With IncludeReferenceDateEvents set, payments at the swap's settlement date may count in its NPV and its fair spread. The implied quantities price with the mid-point engine by default, which isn't ISDA conformant.

QuantLib's constructors read no evaluation date: the trade date, when not given, is deduced from the protection start and the schedule's rule, and the cash settlement date from the trade date. An empty schedule is refused before its first date is read, where QuantLib, given no protection start, reads it first for one, an undefined read (a documented divergence). The accrual rebate reads its first live coupon's amount or accrued amount through the coupon's context-free forms. The engines are static (MidPointCdsEngine, IsdaCdsEngine), and the results are a CreditDefaultSwapResults. An expired swap's results are all 0, its accrual rebate's NPV included, where QuantLib leaves that one unset and reads an uninitialized value, a documented divergence. Equality is by reference.

ImpliedHazardRate(double, YieldTermStructure, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, double, double, CreditDefaultSwapPricingModel) and ConventionalSpread(double, YieldTermStructure, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, CreditDefaultSwapPricingModel) refuse an expired swap ("instrument expired"), where QuantLib returns its solver's guess or a sentinel; the conventional spread also refuses where the engine leaves the fair spread null, with QuantLib's "fair spread not available". Under the ISDA model their flat curve is dated on the evaluation date, which the ISDA engine requires, where QuantLib's rolls a weekend to Monday and its engine then refuses it: documented divergences.

Remarks

The flat curve is ImpliedHazardRate(double, YieldTermStructure, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, double, double, CreditDefaultSwapPricingModel)'s. QuantLib returns its engine's fair spread from the solver's last evaluation, at the root; here the engine prices once more at the root the solver returns. A flat hazard rate doesn't recover the market quotes.

Remarks

Under Midpoint the curve is QuantLib's, settled no business days after the evaluation date on the weekends-only calendar; under ISDA it is dated on the evaluation date, which the ISDA engine requires. The solver starts from QuantLib's guess, the running spread over the loss rate times 365/360, with a tenth of it as its step.
