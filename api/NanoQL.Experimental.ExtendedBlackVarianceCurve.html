A Black variance curve on volatilities at dates: the variance interpolated in time (linearly by default) up to the last date, the last date's volatility flat beyond it. QuantLib's extension of BlackVarianceCurve to quoted volatilities.

Remarks

Quotes are values (the playbook's Quote row), so the curve is computed at construction; QuantLib's update() isn't ported. SetInterpolation<TInterpolator>(TInterpolator) returns a copy on the new interpolation, where QuantLib changes the curve. Unlike the landed curve, QuantLib's experimental one doesn't floor the interpolated variance at 0 nor choose its extrapolation. QuantLib's acyclic visitor isn't ported.
