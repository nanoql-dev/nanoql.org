Remarks

Its three coefficients are QuantLib's expressions, evaluated where κt calls for it in another arithmetic: QuantLib's double, and so its bits, for κt from the coefficient's cut to its upper cut; double-double below the cut, where the double cancels; quad-double below its floor, where double-double cancels too, and below quad-double's floor the coefficient's cubic in κ through four quad-double values; and past the upper cut, where e^{κt}'s powers overflow, an extended-range double, each cut measured at the run against QuantLib's expressions in 50 to 250 digits. At a term of 0 each coefficient is its limit, the first three FordeHestonExpansion's leading order, where QuantLib's divide 0 by 0.

Reference: M. Lorig, S. Pagliarani, A. Pascucci, Explicit implied vols for multifactor local-stochastic vol models, arXiv:1306.5447v3, 2014; the formula's Mathematica notebook at http://explicitsolutions.wordpress.com/.
