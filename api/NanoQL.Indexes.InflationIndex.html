Remarks

A fixing stored through AddFixing(FinancialIndex, Date, double, bool) is written on every day of its date's inflation period, as QuantLib's addFixing writes it, through FixingDates(Date); the reads key on the period's first day. The store's bulk writers store the dates they are given, as QuantLib's non-virtual addFixings does.

Inflation indexes aren't associated with a day but with a period, so they have no fixing calendar: QuantLib's NullCalendar stands in, and every date is a valid fixing date. An extension point.
