Remarks

The volatilities are the standard deviations over the square root of the exercise time, and the interpolation over them is built when the section is built. QuantLib's quote forms are the value forms here, a quote being its value: a new value is a new section. Outside the strikes the interpolation extrapolates, floored at 0; with flat strike extrapolation it takes its value at the nearest end strike instead. A standard deviation beyond the strikes' count is ignored, as QuantLib ignores it, and fewer standard deviations than strikes are refused, where QuantLib reads past them. QuantLib's date forms without a reference date, which float with the evaluation date, are the Create factories, which take the evaluation context and fix the date.

Unsealed for its named classes, QuantLib-Python's (LinearInterpolatedSmileSection and the three cubic ones). Its constructor reads the exercise time, so ExerciseTime is sealed here.
