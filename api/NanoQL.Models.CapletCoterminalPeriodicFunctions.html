CapletSwaptionPeriodicCalibration(EvolutionDescription, PiecewiseConstantCorrelation, VolatilityInterpolationSpecifier, ReadOnlySpan<double>, CurveState, double, double, int, int, int, double, int, double, int, double)

Calibrates to the short caplets and the long swaptions: each pass runs the maximally homogeneous calibration on the specifier's short variances at the current scales, then rescales each long swaption's by the market's over the model's volatility, until the long swaptions' root-mean-square error is within the tolerance, stops improving by a tenth of it, or the passes run out.
