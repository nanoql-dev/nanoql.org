Remarks

An immutable builder: each method returns a modified copy, so a builder kept and reused never changes under its holder. ToYoYInflationCapFloor(EvaluationContext) builds the instrument, in place of QuantLib's conversion operators. The leg is YoYInflationLeg's on an annual schedule over the given number of years, from the effective date or, without one, from the spot date (the evaluation date advanced by the fixing days) plus the forward start; the fixing days move the start only. An optionlet keeps the last coupon alone. Its defaults are QuantLib's: a nominal of 1,000,000, 30/360 (Bond Basis), ModifiedFollowing, no fixing days.

With no strike, the instrument is struck at the money: at the leg's at-the-money rate on the nominal curve given to WithAtmStrike(YieldTermStructure?), at its reference date and without the flows paid on it; with neither, the conversion refuses with QuantLib's empty-handle text. QuantLib's withFirstCapletExcluded is declared and never defined, so it isn't ported. WithPricingEngine(Func<YoYInflationCapFloor, EvaluationContext, InstrumentResults>?) is kept and changes nothing: the instrument holds no engine. A Collar is refused by the instrument's constructor after the strike, as QuantLib refuses it. A builder has no value equality.

An option the builder isn't given that QuantLib's builder fixes takes the builder's provider's default (Defaults(ConventionProvider)), read when the instrument is built: QuantLib's values with nothing configured.
