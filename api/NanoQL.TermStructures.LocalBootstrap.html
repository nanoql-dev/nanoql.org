Remarks

A configuration struct (IBootstrap, D12): its zero value is QuantLib's default construction, a localisation of 2, positivity forced and the curve's accuracy. The curve's nodes are its first date and every helper's pillar, sorted; two helpers on one pillar are refused. From the localisation-th helper on, each step builds the interpolation over the nodes so far through LocalInterpolate(ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<double>, int, Interpolation?, int), keeping the earlier sections, and solves the step's last nodes by Levenberg-Marquardt (at most 100 iterations, the accuracy as the function's tolerance, positive values where positivity is forced) so that its last localisation helpers reprice their quotes.

The interpolator must be an ILocalInterpolator (of QuantLib's, ConvexMonotone); another is refused when the curve is built, where QuantLib's template fails to compile. As QuantLib's, each calculation is a cold start, and a step's guess for its new node is the traits' guess at the step's index, QuantLib's Traits::guess(iInst, ts_, false, 0) (localbootstrap.hpp:229), kept as written.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The interpolator isn't an ILocalInterpolator ("LocalBootstrap needs a local interpolation"), or the localisation is below 1 or below the interpolator's data size adjustment, where QuantLib's steps read out of their arrays.

NanoQLException

There are fewer helpers than the interpolation requires or than the localisation and one, two helpers have the same pillar, or a step's solve doesn't reach the accuracy.
