A targeted redemption note: at each rate's reset it receives the floating coupon (f + s) τᶠˡᵒᵃᵗ and pays the inverse floating coupon max(K − m f, 0) τ, until the coupons paid reach the total coupon or the last rate, when it pays what is left of the total and ends.

Remarks

A working type.

The floating coupons' payment times are the floating schedule's (a documented divergence): QuantLib appends the fixed payment times again (multisteptarn.cpp:50, paymentTimes[i]), so the floating coupons are discounted at the fixed leg's times (Divergences/MultiStepTarnPaymentTimesTests.cs). Where the two schedules coincide, QuantLib's value.
