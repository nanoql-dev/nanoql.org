A Black variance surface on volatilities at dates and strikes: the variance interpolated in time and strike (bilinearly by default) up to the last date, the last date's volatility flat beyond it. QuantLib's extension of BlackVarianceSurface to quoted volatilities.

Remarks

Quotes are values (the playbook's Quote row), given strike by strike, each strike's at every date, as QuantLib lays out its quotes; the surface is computed at construction and QuantLib's update() isn't ported. SetInterpolation<TInterpolator>(TInterpolator) returns a copy on the new interpolation, where QuantLib changes the surface. Unlike the landed surface, QuantLib's experimental one doesn't floor the interpolated variance at 0, and its first date must be after the reference date. QuantLib holds a reference to its caller's quotes, which the values replace. QuantLib's acyclic visitor isn't ported.
