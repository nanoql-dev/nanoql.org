Remarks

A working type: its scratch is QuantLib's members, so an instance serves one caller at a time. It copies the pseudo-root and the bumps.

The derivative (a documented divergence; Divergences/RatePseudoRootJacobianTests.cs): the drift's sum carries each rate's own accrual period, e_j = Σ_{k<j} τ_k (f_k + d_k) P_k a_k, and the derivative of rate j along rate k's root carries f_j + d_j. QuantLib's sums the terms without τ_k and multiplies by τ_j, right on equal periods only, and carries f_j (ratepseudorootjacobian.cpp:183, :191, :197). On equal periods of a power of two and no displacement, QuantLib's bits.
