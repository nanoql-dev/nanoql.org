Remarks

Immutable. Its accuracy is QuantLib's Null<Real>(): the rule runs its evaluations and reports no error. Fewer than two evaluations are refused, where QuantLib's count wraps below zero (discreteintegrals.cpp:92); at two, one interval, the trapezoid rule, where QuantLib's end correction counts f(a) a second time (2.52581 where its DiscreteSimpsonIntegral on the same two points gives the trapezoid's 1.85914). From three evaluations, QuantLib's arithmetic.
