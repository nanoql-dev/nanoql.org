Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's, its bivariate normal QuantLib's We04DP.

Documented divergences. Each rate is read on the volatility's clock and each time is the volatility's, as AnalyticBarrierEngine reads them, where QuantLib reads every structure at the risk-free curve's times (Divergences/LookbackMismatchedTimesTests.cs). Near zero carry, where QuantLib's terms over 2b/σ² cancel and it is NaN at r = q, the value is the cubic in the carry through QuantLib's values either side of a cut (NanoQL.PricingEngines.LookbackSmallCarry, Divergences/LookbackSmallCarryTests.cs). At small volatilities, where λ = 2b/σ² is large and QuantLib's power of s times its normal or bivariate normal factor is +∞ times 0, NaN, the product is taken in logs (NanoQL.PricingEngines.LookbackTails, Divergences/LookbackSmallVolatilityTests.cs). A window opening from the evaluation date to the volatility clock's origin, inclusive, is the full fixed lookback, the spot its maximum, nothing accruing before the origin, where QuantLib's f₁ is 0/0 at the money, and one opened before the evaluation date is refused, the option holding no maximum observed since its start, where QuantLib throws its bivariate normal's check of ρ (Divergences/LookbackPartialWindowTodayTests.cs). And below machine-epsilon variance the underlying follows its forward, where QuantLib divides by the volatility (Divergences/LookbackZeroVolatilityTests.cs).

The formula is a first-passage closed form in equivalent-constant rates and a volatility, the variance and discounts to expiry: no exact closed form exists for a first passage under general deterministic term structures. It is exact only where σ and r − q are flat, r and q themselves free: the engine reads the volatility and the rates at expiry and splits the variance at the window's date in calendar time, so a time change doesn't carry it, and where the volatility steps at the window's date under a constant (r − q)/σ² it misses by −4.9% (Conventions/ClosedFormExactnessTests.cs). The term-structure-exact route is MCLookbackEngine, which reads the extremum at its grid's dates.
