Remarks

The standard YoY inflation cap or floor differs from a nominal one: a nominal cap has no first optionlet, since it sets in advance, while a YoY one sets (effectively) in arrears, against a lag of a few months, so its first optionlet counts; the parity test needs no special definition of the instrument. The relationship between the values of caps, floors and collars is checked, and so are the put-call parity with swaps and a known good value.

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where YoYInflationCap, YoYInflationFloor and YoYInflationCollar derive from it and engines take it. It holds its leg and its rates; an engine computes QuantLib's arguments from them with the context (NanoQL.Instruments.YoYInflationCapFloor.SetupArguments(NanoQL.EvaluationContext)) and returns the instrument's results, QuantLib's plain Instrument::results with the engine's additional results. A cash flow of the leg that isn't a year-on-year inflation coupon is refused when the instrument is priced, as QuantLib refuses it, not when it is built. Equality is by reference.
