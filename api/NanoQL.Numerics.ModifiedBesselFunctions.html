Class ModifiedBesselFunctions

Namespace
NanoQL.Numerics
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The modified Bessel functions of the first and second kind, I_ν(x) and K_ν(x), real and complex, by the series expansions in e.g. http://www.mhtlab.uwaterloo.ca/courses/me755/web_chap4.pdf; the exponentially weighted versions return the function's value times e^(−x) (I) and e^(x) (K).

Remarks

QuantLib's templates over Real and std::complex<Real> are written twice here, each complex operation in QuantLib's order: .NET's complex products and quotients agree with MSVC's bit for bit, its complex Exp, Log, Sqrt, Abs and Pow within a few units in the last place.
