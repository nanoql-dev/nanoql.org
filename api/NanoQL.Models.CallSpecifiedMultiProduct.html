Remarks

A working type, unsealed: QuantLib's upper-bound engine derives its decorated hedge from it. It steps its own clones of the underlying, the strategy and the rebate.

Its end (a documented divergence): QuantLib's end, with its underlying, is kept, but on an underlying with no possible cash-flow times (QuantLib's MultiStepNothing), which can't end it while an exercise time remains; a finished underlying isn't stepped again; and once ended with its underlying it stays ended, with no call and no cash flow. QuantLib's standalone Bermudan on MultiStepNothing, done at its first step, ends at once (Divergences/CallSpecifiedEndTests.cs).

Every count is zeroed at each step's start (a documented divergence): QuantLib writes none at a step where neither the underlying nor the rebate steps, so the engine adds the previous step's flows again (callspecifiedmultiproduct.cpp:144-167). A rebate of more products than the underlying, which writes past the buffers QuantLib sizes by the underlying's, is refused; a rebate of fewer pays its own products, the others nothing (the upper-bound engine's one-product rebate of a hedge of several, upperboundengine.cpp:124-125).
