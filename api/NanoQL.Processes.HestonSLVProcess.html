The Heston stochastic-local volatility process: the Heston process's spot with its volatility √v scaled by a leverage function L(t, S), and its variance's volatility scaled by a mixing factor.

Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's process re-reads its Heston process's parameters when notified; here they are read at construction. Its step, the quadratic-exponential scheme for the variance and the leverage at the step's start, runs in place without allocating, the allocating step its values bit for bit.

Its times are the Heston process's, the curves read as that process reads them, and the leverage function at the instants those times denote, on its own clock; QuantLib's reads where the structures share the risk-free curve's times.
