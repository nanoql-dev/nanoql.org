Remarks

The fit runs when the interpolation is built: QuantLib's construction followed by its update(), which every QuantLib user calls at once. A new fit is a new interpolation, so update() isn't ported. The fit minimizes the (vega-weighted, by default) squared errors from the given guesses, then, while the error stays above errorAccept, from up to maxGuesses − 1 more guesses drawn from a Halton sequence seeded with QuantLib's 42, keeping the best; its results are QuantLib's at every step.

As QuantLib's, a restart is kept by the error the cost function's last evaluation gives: the result's under LevenbergMarquardt, the default, which evaluates its result last, but a later trial's under Simplex, which returns its best vertex without evaluating it again. Fixed parameters go through the parameter transforms at every evaluation, as QuantLib's do, so a fixed β of 0.5 is reported as 0.5000000000000001. A fixed or guessed ρ beyond ±0.9999, or α or ν below 1e-7, is taken as the nearest value the transform reaches, where QuantLib's transform flips ρ's sign or fails; and a smile of zero volatilities with vega weights is refused, where QuantLib fits on NaN weights.

Immutable and thread-safe once built: it copies its inputs, and nothing writes the fit after it.

errorAccept double

The error at or below which a fit is accepted and the restarts stop.

useMaxError bool

Whether a fit is scored by its maximum error, not its root-mean-square error.

maxGuesses int

The most minimizations; 0 runs one, as 1 does.

shift double

The shift of a shifted SABR model.

volatilityType VolatilityType

The volatilities' type: shifted lognormal or normal.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

maxGuesses is negative, or volatilityType isn't a member of its type.

ArgumentException

The sizes differ, there is no strike, the time isn't positive, the forward plus the shift isn't positive, a guess is out of its range, a strike plus the shift isn't positive, the volatilities are all zero with vega weights, or the optimizer refuses the problem.

NanoQLException

The optimizer fails.
