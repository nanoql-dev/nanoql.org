Remarks

A working type (the playbook's finite-difference row): mutable, owned by one rollback and not thread-safe. Its time step rewrites only its rate, so a step on Douglas's scheme, the engine's default, allocates nothing.

A direction past the operator's count is refused, where QuantLib reads its operators' vector past its end (Divergences/FdmWienerOpDirectionTests.cs).
