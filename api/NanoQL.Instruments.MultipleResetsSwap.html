Remarks

The spread is added to each fixing, the multiple-resets leg's rate spread. The floating leg is built by FixedVsFloatingSwap's protected constructor's builder, where QuantLib replaces the base's leg after construction; the base's floating schedule is the coupons' dates.

QuantLib's fair spread is linear in the floating leg's BPS, exact on an averaged leg but not on a compounded one, whose spread compounds, so at it the swap misses par. On a compounded leg DiscountingSwapEngine's MultipleResetsSwap overload solves for the spread that zeroes the NPV, and FetchResults(InstrumentResults) leaves the fair spread null, QuantLib's "result not available", for another engine that gives none; an averaged leg keeps QuantLib's formula.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

A schedule or the index is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

resetsPerCoupon isn't positive, where QuantLib divides by it first; or an enum argument isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

The reset periods aren't a multiple of resetsPerCoupon, with QuantLib's text.

NanoQLException

The fixed leg can't be built from the arguments.
