Remarks

The fixing is the index's year-on-year rate observed on the accrual end with the lag, flat or interpolated (LaggedYoYRate(YoYInflationIndex, Date, Period, CPIInterpolationType, EvaluationContext)). It accepts a YoYInflationCouponPricer. An extension point: CappedFlooredYoYInflationCoupon derives from it.
