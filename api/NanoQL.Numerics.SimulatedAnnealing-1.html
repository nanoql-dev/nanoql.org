Remarks

Immutable: it holds a copy of the generator it is given, and each minimization draws from a copy of that, so a second minimization repeats the first's draws, where QuantLib's continues its generator's stream (Divergences/SimulatedAnnealingTests.cs). The generator is required: QuantLib's default, a Mersenne Twister seeded from the clock, is refused.

A vertex whose value is NaN enters the simplex at the largest double, where QuantLib's guard on the initial vertices tests the previous trial's value instead (left from an earlier minimization, or unset) and so lets the NaN in; the vertices a contraction moves are guarded alike, which QuantLib's leaves out.

SimulatedAnnealing(double, double, int, double, TRng)

Initializes a new instance of the SimulatedAnnealing<TRng> class that budgets a total of K moves and sets the temperature T to the initial temperature times (1 − k/K)^α, with k the total number of moves so far. After K moves the temperature is guaranteed to be zero, after that the optimization runs like a deterministic simplex algorithm.
