Remarks

A working type: it steps its own clones of the underlying, the strategy and the rebate.

Its end and counts are CallSpecifiedMultiProduct's (documented divergences): QuantLib's end with its underlying, but on an underlying with no possible cash-flow times while an exercise time remains; a finished underlying not stepped again; ended for good once ended with its underlying; and every count zeroed at each step's start (pathwiseproductcallspecified.cpp:136-153). A rebate of more products than the underlying, which writes past the buffers QuantLib sizes by the underlying's, is refused.
