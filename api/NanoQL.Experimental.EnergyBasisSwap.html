Remarks

The holder pays the pay index's price and receives the receive index's, the basis added to the pay leg or the receive leg. The basis, a price per its unit in its currency, converts into the base currency from its own currency, as every other price here; QuantLib converts it from the base currency into the basis currency, the reverse (energybasisswap.cpp:126-129; Divergences/EnergyBasisSwapTests.cs); a basis in the base currency is QuantLib's.

The last quote date is the earlier of the two indexes'; an index with no fixings has none, and every day is priced from the forward curves, the other index's fixings unread. The spread index is held and read by nothing, as QuantLib's.
