Remarks

Immutable. The expectation over a step integrates the level by the discretization chosen; the standard deviation and variance are the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process's at level 0.

Two of QuantLib's defects are fixed (Divergences/ExtendedOrnsteinUhlenbeckExpectationTests.cs): the trapezoidal rule's (1 − e^(−a·dt))/(a·dt), 0/0 at a speed of 0 and cancelling below, is a series below a·dt of 1e-2 and QuantLib's quotient from it; and the Gauss-Lobatto rule, whose integrand b(x)e^(ax) QuantLib integrates to an absolute accuracy e^(a(t₀+dt)) puts out of reach (it throws from a(t₀+dt) of 35), integrates the bounded b(x)e^(−a(t₀+dt−x)) where a(t₀+dt) passes 30, with a relative accuracy of 1e-10 beside the absolute one, and QuantLib's integrand at and below 30.
