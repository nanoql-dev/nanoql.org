Remarks

A static class: Calculate takes the engine's inputs and returns the results, in place of QuantLib's engine object, whose discountCurve() accessor has no instance to belong to. Its overloads for FixedVsFloatingSwap, OvernightIndexedSwap, BMASwap, FloatFloatSwap, YearOnYearInflationSwap and CPISwap run the same steps and body and return those swaps' results records, so a caller needs no cast; the CPISwap overload also gives the fair rate, which QuantLib's swap derives from the CPI leg's BPS (see CPISwapResults), and the FloatFloatSwap overload solves a capped or floored leg's fair spread.

Remarks

The fair rate is fixedRate - NPV / (p S), where p is the CPI leg's sign and S its sensitivity to its rate: the NPV, on this curve and under these dates, of the swap's CPI coupons rebuilt at a unit rate, so the swap rebuilt at the fair rate is worth zero. It is null when S is zero, the CPI leg having no live coupon. QuantLib's fixedRate - NPV / (legBPS[0] / 1bp) leaves out the index ratio a CPI coupon's sensitivity carries, a documented divergence.

The swap's results: the expired results when the swap has expired.

Remarks

A leg's fair spread is QuantLib's, derived from its BPS, where the leg has no cap or floor: there its amount moves one for one with its spread, and the BPS route is exact. On a capped or floored leg QuantLib's BPS route doesn't zero the NPV, once a cap or floor binds; there the fair spread is the leg's first spread plus the uniform shift of its coupons' spreads that zeroes the NPV, solved on the swap's own pricing, each coupon keeping its pricer, cap and floor. The shift is bracketed from the BPS route's estimate within the spreads the pricers price, and is null where no shift in that domain, within a shift of 100%, changes the NPV's sign (tests/NanoQL.Tests/Divergences/FloatFloatSwapOptionalLegFairSpreadTests.cs).

Remarks

On a compounded leg the spread compounds with each fixing, so QuantLib's fair spread, linear in the floating leg's BPS, misses par; here it is the spread at which the swap, rebuilt at it and priced on this curve and under these dates, is worth zero, solved by Brent's method to 1e-12 from QuantLib's estimate. It is null when the floating leg's BPS is zero or unknown, as QuantLib's. On an averaged leg the spread is linear, and the swap's results derive QuantLib's.

ZeroCouponSwapResults

The swap's results: the expired results when the swap has expired.

Remarks

The fair payment is QuantLib's fairFixedPayment, the floating leg's NPV over the discount at the fixed amount's payment date, signed, with the discount read at the swap's payment date, where QuantLib reads its fixed leg's end discount, at the leg's maturity date: the accrual end for the fixed-rate constructor's coupon, so with a payment delay, or an adjusted payment date, its fair payment fed back leaves a non-zero NPV. For the fixed-payment constructor the two dates are one, and the payment QuantLib's. The floating leg's NPV, given at the NPV date, is taken back to the curve's reference date by the NPV date's discount, as QuantLib's asset swap scales its fair values (assetswap.cpp:273, :294), where QuantLib's divides it by a discount from the reference date, so with an NPV date after the reference date its fair payment is high by that discount's reciprocal; at the default NPV date the discount is 1.
