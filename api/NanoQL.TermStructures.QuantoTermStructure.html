Remarks

QuantLib's curve answers its reference date, calendar, settlement days and day counter from the dividend curve, which can move; here they are the dividend curve's, passed at construction, since that curve's are fixed. Its maximum date is the earliest of the five structures'.

QuantLib reads every structure at the time the dividend curve's day counter measures, with its own comment that this assumes one day count and "should be QL_REQUIREd" (quantotermstructure.hpp:124-131), so curves equal at every date but on different day counters give different quanto rates. Here a structure on another day counter or reference date is read at the instants the curve's times denote, on its own clock: the curves' discounts and the volatilities' variances accrued from the curve's reference date to the time, the correlation term the root of the two variances' product. Where every structure shares the dividend curve's day counter and reference date, the zero rate is QuantLib's. A documented divergence, as the finite-difference engine's mismatched times are.
