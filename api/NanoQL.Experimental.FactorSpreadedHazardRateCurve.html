Remarks

Immutable: QuantLib's stays linked to its original and its spread quote, which change through it; here both are values, a new curve for a new original or spread. The survival probability is the exact integral of the hazard rate the curve reports, the original's times 1 + s: QuantLib's integrates it numerically with its base's 48-point rule, which misses the exact survival by 5.4e-6 to 5.4e-5 relative on flat originals at 1 to 10 years and by 1.05e-4 on a stepped one at four years, and which QuantLib warns derived classes to override (Divergences/SpreadedHazardRateCurveTests.cs). The original's rate is its own kind's: on the hazard kinds its survival's, jumps apart; on the survival and density kinds the density over the survival with its jumps; on the affine structures the deterministic part alone, the model's left out, as QuantLib's integral leaves it; so each kind integrates its rate in closed form, and an original that gives none, a user's, is integrated numerically, as QuantLib does.
