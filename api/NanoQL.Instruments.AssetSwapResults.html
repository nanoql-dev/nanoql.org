Remarks

QuantLib's fairSpread(), fairCleanPrice() and fairNonParRepayment() compute these from the swap's results on reading and cache them; here FetchResults(InstrumentResults) derives them in the engine's results step. A value that can't be derived is null, and AssetSwap's members that read it refuse with QuantLib's texts. Equality compares the swap's results and the three values.
