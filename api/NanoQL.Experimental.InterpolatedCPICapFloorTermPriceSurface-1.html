Remarks

At each maturity the quoted prices fill every strike by put-call parity, a floor's price giving the cap's at its strike and the reverse: C − F = (A − K)·D, with A and K the at-the-money rate and the strike compounded over the maturity in years, (1 + r)^T, and D the nominal discount factor to the option date, the reference date plus the maturity adjusted.

QuantLib compounds over the maturity's length in its own unit, so an 18-month maturity compounds over 18 years; here over 1.5, QuantLib's own years of the period, its bits on maturities in years, and a maturity in weeks or days, which has no length in years, is refused (Divergences/CPICapFloorPriceSurfaceTests.cs).

The at-the-money rates read the index's fixings, so the surface is built from a context (Create(double, double, Period, Calendar, BusinessDayConvention, DayCounter, ZeroInflationIndex, CPIInterpolationType, YieldTermStructure, IReadOnlyList<double>, IReadOnlyList<double>, IReadOnlyList<Period>, Matrix, Matrix, EvaluationContext, TInterpolator2D)), as QuantLib's is built from the global settings' at its first read; QuantLib recomputes on a change, and here a new market builds a new surface. A price past the last maturity or strike extrapolates, as QuantLib's interpolations, which enable it.
