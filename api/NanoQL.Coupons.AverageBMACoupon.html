A coupon paying a BMA index, whose rate is a weighted average of the relevant fixings: each fixing weighs the actual calendar days for which it is valid within the interest period. Before the weights are computed, the fixing schedule is adjusted for the index's fixing-day gap.

Remarks

The coupon has no single fixing: FixingDate(), IndexFixing(EvaluationContext) and ConvexityAdjustment(EvaluationContext) throw QuantLib's texts, and FixingDates() and IndexFixings(EvaluationContext) give the averaged dates and fixings. Its pricer is set at construction, as QuantLib sets it; it gives the swaplet rate only, and refuses the prices and the caplet and floorlet rates.
