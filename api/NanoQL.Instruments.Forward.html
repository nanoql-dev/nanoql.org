A forward contract on an underlying, whose derived classes give its spot value and income: the forward value is the spot value less the income, over the discount at the maturity date.

Remarks

QuantLib's derived classes set the mutable spot value and income in their performCalculations before the base's runs; here a derived class's calculation passes them to Calculate(double, double, EvaluationContext, Date?), which gives the results. The discount curve discounts the contract's cash flows and gives the forward value; the income curve, which a derived class sets, discounts the underlying's income.

QuantLib's class still needs to be rigorously tested, its header says.

ImpliedYield(double, double, Date, Compounding, DayCounter, EvaluationContext)

The simple yield implied by a spot and a forward value, net of the underlying's income on the income curve: the rate that compounds the spot value less the income to the forward value from a settlement date to the maturity date. For a repo at time 0 it reproduces the spot repo rate; for an FRA, the zero rate at its maturity.
