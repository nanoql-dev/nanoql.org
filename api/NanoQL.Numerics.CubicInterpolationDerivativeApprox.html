How a CubicInterpolation approximates the first derivatives at its nodes: QuantLib's CubicInterpolation::DerivativeApprox, with its values.

public enum CubicInterpolationDerivativeApprox

Fields

Akima = 6

Akima approximation (local, non-monotonic, non-linear); at least 4 points.

FourthOrder = 3

Fourth-order approximation (local, non-monotonic, linear); not implemented in QuantLib.

FritschButland = 5

Fritsch–Butland approximation (local, monotonic, non-linear).

Harmonic = 8

Weighted harmonic mean approximation (local, monotonic, non-linear).

Kruger = 7

Kruger approximation (local, monotonic, non-linear).

Parabolic = 4

Parabolic approximation (local, non-monotonic, linear).

Spline = 0

Spline approximation (non-local, non-monotonic, linear), with the boundary conditions of CubicInterpolationBoundaryCondition on each side.

SplineOM1 = 1

Overshooting minimization, 1st derivative.

SplineOM2 = 2

Overshooting minimization, 2nd derivative.

Remarks

Local schemes (fourth-order, parabolic, Fritsch–Butland, Akima, Kruger, harmonic) use only the values near a node; the non-local spline schemes solve a linear system over all of them. Local schemes give C¹ interpolants, the spline schemes C² ones.
