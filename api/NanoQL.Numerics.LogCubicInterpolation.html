Remarks

It copies its inputs, as every Interpolation does. It has no primitive. An extension point, as in QuantLib: the named log-cubic interpolations derive from it.

Parameters

x ReadOnlySpan<double>

The x values, which must be sorted; at least 2, and at least 4 for Akima's approximation or a Lagrange condition.

y ReadOnlySpan<double>

The y values, one per x value, each positive.

da CubicInterpolationDerivativeApprox

The first-derivative approximation of the logarithms.

monotonic bool

Whether to apply Hyman's monotonicity filter to the logarithms' cubic.

leftC CubicInterpolationBoundaryCondition

The left boundary condition, for the spline approximation.

leftConditionValue double

The left condition's value, where it takes one.

rightC CubicInterpolationBoundaryCondition

The right boundary condition, for the spline approximation.

rightConditionValue double

The right condition's value, where it takes one.

Exceptions

ArgumentException

The sizes differ, there are too few points, or a y value isn't positive.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

An enum argument isn't a member of its type.

NanoQLException

The scheme or a condition isn't implemented, or the spline's system has a zero pivot.
