Remarks

QuantLib's four constructors collapse into two: its quote overloads are the double ones, and the settlement-days form, whose reference date moves with the evaluation date, is Create(int, Calendar, double, DayCounter, EvaluationContext). The date form has QuantLib's empty calendar, so its option dates can be computed only from tenors in weeks, months or years, and QuantLib's base convention, Following, which that calendar refuses for any date it would adjust. The bond tenors reach 100 years, the bond lengths and the strikes have no limit, and the smile sections are flat, by time, with no at-the-money level.
