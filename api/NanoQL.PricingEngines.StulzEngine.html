Remarks

The bivariate normal is QuantLib's default, BivariateCumulativeNormalDistributionWe04DP. Each forward reads its own process's curves and the payoff is discounted on the first process's risk-free curve, where QuantLib builds the second forward on the first process's discount (Divergences/TwoAssetTwoRiskFreeCurvesTests.cs); each structure is read by date and accrues nothing before its reference date (Divergences/TwoAssetMismatchedTimesTests.cs).

Where the assets move together, a combined deviation of zero (a correlation of 1 with equal volatilities, or two zero volatilities), the minimum is the asset of the smaller forward and the maximum that of the larger, each priced by Black's formula, where QuantLib's derived correlations are NaN or infinite and refused; an asset of zero volatility reads the bivariate normal at an infinite bound, its limit (Divergences/StulzDegenerateTests.cs). At a negative strike the payoffs are linear: a call is the call struck at zero less the discounted strike, a put is worth nothing, where QuantLib takes the log of a negative ratio (Divergences/StulzNegativeStrikeTests.cs).
