Remarks

An extension point and an immutable object (over McSimulation<TPath, TStats>): a concrete engine's static Calculate builds one per call from the option, the context, the process and QuantLib's settings, implements the path pricer, and reads Run()'s model and results. QuantLib's arguments are the option's own, split by the context's date where it holds every past fixing, read once the engine prices: AverageType, RunningAccumulator, PastFixings, FixingDates and the past fixings' log-sum, RunningLog. The control variate's value comes from ControlPricingEngine(), an engine delegate of the engines' form, on the option; a controlled value is clamped at 0, and the grid is the additional result "TimeGrid", as QuantLib's. Every fixing in the past, or the one left today, is refused with QuantLib's "all fixings are in the past".

The process is the engine's: the Black-Scholes and Heston engines pass the process on their clock, whose times run from the earliest structure's reference date, so TimeGrid()'s times are that clock's.

A fixing dated from the evaluation date to the clock's origin, where the structures settle after the evaluation date, is taken at the origin, the spot with no variance and no discount accrued, so as a past fixing at the spot (Divergences/AsianLateSettlementTests.cs), where QuantLib's grid drops it from the average.
