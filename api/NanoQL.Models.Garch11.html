The GARCH(1,1) volatility model: σ²ₜ = ω + α u²ₜ₋₁ + β σ²ₜ₋₁, ω = γ V_L with γ = 1 − α − β and V_L the long-term variance. Volatilities are assumed to be expressed on an annual basis.

Remarks

Immutable: a model built from a series is calibrated at construction, and each Calibrate returns a new model with the receiver's mode, where QuantLib's change the object. The statics are QuantLib's: Calculate(TimeSeries<double>, double, double, double), ToR2(ReadOnlySpan<double>), CostFunction(ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, double, double) and CalibrateR2's six overloads, each returning the parameters and the minimization's results that QuantLib writes through its references and returns.

Documented divergences:

  • A calibration whose minimizer doesn't converge is refused, "GARCH(1,1) calibration did not converge: optimizer stopped with end criterion …", which the mode-based calibrations catch and fall back to their initial guess: QuantLib's master (#2844), which 1.44 doesn't check (Divergences/GarchConvergenceTests.cs).
  • The cost's gradient is the gradient of its value, the recursive ∂σ²ₜ/∂θ = (1, u²ₜ₋₁, σ²ₜ₋₁) + β ∂σ²ₜ₋₁/∂θ, where QuantLib's takes the direct term alone, 0.13 to 0.80 times the true gradient; QuantLib's own paths, the Simplex and the ACF fit, never read it (Divergences/GarchGradientTests.cs).
  • The cost refuses a conditional variance that isn't positive, which only the overloads that subtract the mean from the squared returns can produce, where QuantLib takes its log, NaN (Divergences/GarchDemeanedVarianceTests.cs).
  • A series of fewer than two points is refused by Calculate(TimeSeries<double>, double, double, double), where QuantLib reads before its start.
  • The mode-based initial guesses fit the autocorrelations by NonLinearLeastSquare on the conjugate gradient, whose returned point is the last one its line search accepted (Divergences/LineSearchReturnTests.cs), so the Simplex starts a hair from QuantLib's guess and the calibrated parameters end within its tolerance, 1e-8, of QuantLib's, the log-likelihood the same.
  • The second initial guess (the ACF fit's, which GammaGuess and BestOfTwo take) sets α = γ − β, as the first does, where the fit fails or its guess misses the constraint; QuantLib leaves α there as its caller's, an uninitialized element of its guess array (garch.cpp:318-368, :435-439), so its value is undefined.
