Remarks

Computed at construction, where QuantLib's lazy object computes at the first read, and immutable after. The problem's times are Clock(HestonProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?, LocalVolTermStructure?)'s. The delta and gamma are the derivatives with respect to the spot, not the model-implied ones.
