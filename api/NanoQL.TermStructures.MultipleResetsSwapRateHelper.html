Remarks

On the rate helpers' contract: the helper's index is cloned without a curve, and the copy bound to a curve under construction rebuilds the swap on the index cloned on it, where QuantLib relinks its handle; a re-dated helper rebuilds the swap under the context. The implied quote is the swap's fair rate, priced on the discounting curve given or else on the curve under construction, without the settlement date's flows. The latest date is the last payment date of either leg or the end of the last fixing's period, whichever is later.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
