Remarks

QuantLib's spread quote is its value: a new spread is a new structure. The base's dates, calendar, conventions, strikes, maximum swap tenor, type and shifts are the structure's, and so is its extrapolation flag, read when the structure is built, as QuantLib reads it. The base's volatilities, shifts and smile sections are read with extrapolation, and a smile section is a SpreadedSmileSection over the base's.
