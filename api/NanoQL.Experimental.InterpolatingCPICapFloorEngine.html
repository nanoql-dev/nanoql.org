Remarks

The surface's time axis is its quoted instruments' maturities, so the instrument's payment date moves back by the difference between its observation lag and the surface's, which must be non-negative. A flat observation reads the surface at the start of the effective maturity's inflation period, where the fixing can't change; a linear one interpolates between that period's start and the next period's by days. The value is the surface's price, on the surface's nominal, as QuantLib's. The lag difference of periods in units QuantLib's period arithmetic can't subtract, years from weeks for one, is refused, QuantLib's own refusal (its \TODO).
