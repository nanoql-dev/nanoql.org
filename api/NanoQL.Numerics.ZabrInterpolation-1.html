Remarks

The fit is SABRInterpolation's, XABR's, over ZABR's parameters: it runs when the interpolation is built, from the guesses and then, while the error stays above errorAccept, from up to maxGuesses − 1 Halton guesses seeded with QuantLib's 42, keeping the best; its values are QuantLib's at every step. ν is fitted in [0, 5] and γ in [0, 1.9], the ranges QuantLib's transform maps them into.

The model the interpolation prices with is a section built from a copy of the parameters, as QuantLib's is. QuantLib doesn't rebuild it after writing the best restart's parameters back, so where the restarts run out without an accepted fit, or the last trial wasn't the best one, its values and errors are its last trial's while its parameters are the best restart's. Here the section is rebuilt from the best parameters, so the values, the errors and the parameters agree. A fixed or guessed ν above 5, or γ above 1.9, is taken as 5 or 1.9, where QuantLib's transform wraps it to another value inside the range; ρ and α as SABRInterpolation takes them.

Immutable and thread-safe once built: it copies its inputs, and nothing writes the fit after it.

Exceptions

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

maxGuesses is negative.

ArgumentException

The sizes differ, there is no strike, the time isn't positive, the model refuses a guess or the forward, the volatilities are all zero with vega weights, or the optimizer refuses the problem.

NanoQLException

The optimizer or the model's integration fails.
