The backward operator of the constant elasticity of variance (CEV) process df = α·f^β·dW, absorbed at f = 0, in one direction of a mesher: the diffusion ½α²f^{2β}, discounting at the curve's forward rate.

Remarks

A working type: SetTime(double, double) rewrites the step's operator in place through a buffer it holds, and the apply and splitting solves write into the caller's spans, so a step allocates nothing. The forward rate is the curve's over its own times, read through YieldTermStructure's internal ContinuousForwardRate, its bits ForwardRate's.

The second derivative is taken in the direction given, where QuantLib's is built on direction 0 whatever the direction (fdmcevop.cpp:39-40), so that in another direction of a mesher of more than one it differentiates along the wrong axis: QuantLib's operator in direction 1 of a two-dimensional mesher differs from its operator in direction 0 of the transposed mesher. In direction 0, QuantLib's bits.
