Remarks

Immutable. Its argument x is ln(S) and its times are the process's, the risk-free curve's. The volatility is the model's constant one, read at the strike e^x; the inverse reads it at e^p, the probability as QuantLib passes it, which a constant volatility doesn't see.

The dividend curve and the volatility are read at the instant a time denotes, each on its own clock, the variance accrued from the risk-free curve's reference date, where QuantLib reads them at the risk-free curve's time (the mismatched-dates class); structures that share the curve's times keep QuantLib's bits. A documented divergence.
