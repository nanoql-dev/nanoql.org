Remarks

Unlike the forward contract conventions on carryable financial assets (stocks, bonds, commodities), the value date for a FRA is taken to be the day when the forward loan or deposit begins and when full settlement takes place (based on the NPV of the contract on that date). The maturity date is the date when the forward loan or deposit ends. In fact, the FRA settles and expires on the value date, not on the (later) maturity date. It follows that the time from the value date to the maturity date is the tenor or term of the underlying loan or deposit.

Choose Long for an "FRA purchase" (future long loan, short deposit, the borrower), and Short for an "FRA sale" (future short loan, long deposit, the lender).

Immutable. QuantLib's FRA computes in the instrument, with no engine: Calculate(EvaluationContext) does, running Instrument::calculate's steps and returning the NPV, the payoff and the forward rate (ForwardRateAgreementResults). Nothing is cached: each call computes again.

QuantLib's to-do list: preconditions and tests, and the BBA (British), AFB (French, assumed here) and ABA (Australian) banker conventions told apart in the calculations. QuantLib warns that the class still needs to be rigorously tested.
