Remarks

Variance(double, double, double) is the conditional variance the scheme's own step reads, x₀σ²e^(−kΔt)(1 − e^(−kΔt))/k + θσ²(1 − e^(−kΔt))²/(2k), at the state given, and Diffusion(double, double) is σ√x, the header's SDE (K2): a divergence, QuantLib's variance reading the process's initial value for the state and θσ²/k for its level term, and its diffusion σ (coxingersollrossprocess.cpp:32-38, .hpp:90-92; Divergences/CirProcessMomentsTests.cs).

The step's (1 − e^(−kΔt))/k is written as Δt·(1 − e^(−x))/x, x = kΔt, the cancellation-free form of QuantLib's master's mean-reversion kernels, exact in the limit k → 0, so the step's last bits differ from QuantLib's at every speed (a fix of QuantLib's cancellation at small speed).
