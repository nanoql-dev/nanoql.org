Class MakeFdCIRVanillaEngine

Namespace
NanoQL.PricingEngines
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

A builder of FdCIRVanillaEngine: QuantLib's defaults, 10 time steps, 100 points of the log spot and 100 of the short rate, no damping steps, no dividends and no quanto helper, but the scheme Hundsdorfer's, where QuantLib's is modified Hundsdorfer.
