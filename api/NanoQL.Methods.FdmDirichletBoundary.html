A Dirichlet boundary condition: a fixed value on one side of one direction of the grid, written into the solution after each application of the operator and each solve.

Remarks

Immutable, its indices fixed at construction; the schemes call it in QuantLib's order, and it writes into the caller's span, allocating nothing.
