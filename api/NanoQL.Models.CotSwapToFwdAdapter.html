A coterminal swap-rate market model as a forward-rate one, in the frozen-coefficient approximation: its initial rates the forwards of the coterminal model's curve, each step's pseudo-root the inverse of the coterminal Z matrix times the coterminal model's, the expired rates' rows zero.

Remarks

Immutable: its pseudo-roots are computed at construction, and a read returns a copy. Its displacements, evolution and counts are the coterminal model's. A pseudo-root read past the steps, which QuantLib reads unchecked, is refused.
