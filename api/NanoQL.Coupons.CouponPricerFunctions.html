Remarks

QuantLib's visitor is a switch over the coupon types, most derived first, as accept dispatches; each later coupon type adds its case in its own port. A leg is set all or nothing: a pricer one coupon refuses leaves no new leg, where QuantLib has already changed the earlier coupons (a documented divergence). Cash flows that aren't floating-rate coupons are kept as they are. QuantLib's overload for inflation-coupon pricers is InflationCouponPricerFunctions, with its swap and bond forms.
