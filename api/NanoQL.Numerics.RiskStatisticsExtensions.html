Remarks

QuantLib's RiskStatistics, GenericRiskStatistics<GaussianStatistics>, and its Statistics typedef are GeneralStatistics with these members and those of GaussianStatisticsExtensions.

ExpectedShortfall<TStat>(TStat, double) where no sample lies below the value-at-risk's level reads the level itself, 0 with no loss and the value-at-risk where the tail is an atom at that level, where QuantLib refuses "no data below the target": a documented divergence.

ExpectedShortfall<TStat>(TStat, double)

The expected shortfall at a percentile: the expected loss where the loss exceeds the value-at-risk, E[x | x < VaR(p)], the average of observations below the percentile, also known as conditional value-at-risk (Artzner, Delbaen, Eber and Heath, "Coherent measures of risk", Mathematical Finance 9, 1999); capped at 0 and negated.

public static double ExpectedShortfall<TStat>(this TStat stats, double percentile) where TStat : notnull, IEmpiricalStatistics
