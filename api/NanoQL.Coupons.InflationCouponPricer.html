The base class of inflation-coupon pricers: QuantLib's InflationCouponPricer, which differs from the floating-rate pricers because it deals with inflation indexes and their lags (the option may look three months back and the index two).

Remarks

Pricers price the inverse cap and floorlets too, the usual ones paying when the underlying is above or below a strike, so that conventional caps price simply; the non-inverse ones are from the coupon's side, a capped coupon having a maximum at the strike.

Stateless: each member takes the coupon and the context, and computes inside the call what QuantLib's initialize stores. An extension point.
