Remarks

The engine reports the value, delta, gamma and theta; the theta is null when the problem's first stopping time is time 0, as QuantLib's is; the other Greeks are null. Its finite-difference working types are built inside each calculation and dropped after it.

Its times run on Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), from the earliest reference date of the structures it reads, each structure accruing nothing before its own date; where no structure is dated before the risk-free curve, they are QuantLib's (Divergences/FdmMismatchedTimesTests.cs). The theta is the change over the clock's first calendar day, QuantLib's own engine's theta on the market restated from that date: where a structure is dated after the evaluation date, that day accrues none of its dividends or variance, so the theta differs from the formula theta of the analytic and binomial engines, even in sign (+2.47 against the analytic engine's −27.57 on a 14-day put whose dividend curve and volatility start four days later).

QuantLib's four constructors are one method here: the dividends and the quanto helper are optional arguments.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

The option, the context or the process is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

A grid size or the damping steps are negative, or the model or the scheme isn't a member of its enum.

ArgumentException

A dividend is null; the escrowed model is asked for a quanto option; the grid has fewer than two points; or the rollback's arguments are refused.

NanoQLException

The payoff isn't striked, the spot less the escrowed dividends isn't positive, or a curve, the volatility, a step or the spline fails.
