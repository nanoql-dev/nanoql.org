Remarks

An immutable builder: each method returns a modified copy, so a builder kept and reused never changes under its holder. ToCreditDefaultSwap(EvaluationContext) builds the swap, in place of QuantLib's conversion operators. Its defaults are QuantLib's: protection bought on a notional of 1, no upfront, quarterly coupons under the CDS rule, Following, Actual/360 with Actual/360 (including the last day) for the last period, accrual settled and paid at the default time, the accrual rebated, and three days of cash settlement on WeekendsOnly.

The trade date is the evaluation date unless given, and the upfront date the trade date advanced by the cash settlement days on the defaults' calendar. Unless a schedule is given, the protection starts on the trade date under the 2009 rules and the day after it under the others, and the schedule runs from it on the defaults' calendar to the term date, or, from a tenor, to the trade date plus the tenor or, under a CDS rule, to CdsMaturity(Date, Period, DateGenerationRule)'s maturity, which the schedule refuses when there is none ("null termination date"). WithPricingEngine(Func<CreditDefaultSwap, EvaluationContext, InstrumentResults>?) is kept and changes nothing: the swap holds no engine. A builder has no value equality.

An option the builder isn't given, its calendar among them, takes the default of the provider current when the builder was created (Defaults(ConventionProvider)), read when the swap is built: QuantLib's values with nothing configured.
