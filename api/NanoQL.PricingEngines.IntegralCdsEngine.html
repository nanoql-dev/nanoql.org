Remarks

A static class: Calculate(CreditDefaultSwap, EvaluationContext, Period, DefaultProbabilityTermStructure?, double, YieldTermStructure?, bool?) takes the engine's inputs and returns the results, in place of QuantLib's engine object. An expired swap's results are SetupExpired()'s, as QuantLib's instrument gives them before it calls the engine; otherwise the swap is validated and priced.

A curve accrues nothing before its reference date: the discount at a step's end before the discount curve's reference date is 1, and the survival at a payment date and the default probability at a step's ends before the probability curve's reference date are 1 and 0, where QuantLib's reads refuse them ("negative time"): on a curve that settles after the evaluation date, as for QuantLib's own CDS on a T+2 curve, and on curves dated the evaluation date for a live coupon whose period ended before it (one paid after the weekend that ended it). No default before the reference date is QuantLib's own premise for its two-date default probability (defaulttermstructure.cpp:112-120). A documented divergence; wherever QuantLib's reads succeed, its values, bit for bit.

A negative step is refused, where QuantLib's loop walks back from the period's start until a curve refuses its date.
