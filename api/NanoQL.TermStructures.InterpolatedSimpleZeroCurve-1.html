Remarks

The discount factor at a time t is 1 / (1 + R·t), R the interpolated simple rate; past the last node the curve extrapolates R with a flat instantaneous forward rate of the rates. Its formula and maximum date, its last node's, are the SimpleZeroYield traits', which a PiecewiseYieldCurve<TTraits, TInterpolator, TBootstrap> on those traits shares. Unsealed for its named class, which adds constructors only. QuantLib's protected constructors, through which its piecewise curve derives, aren't ported: no class derives through them here.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

dates, yields or dayCounter is null.

ArgumentException

There are fewer dates than the interpolation needs, the counts of dates and rates differ, the dates aren't sorted or two of them give the same time, the jumps and their dates differ in number, or the interpolation refuses the nodes.

NanoQLException

The interpolation refuses the nodes.
