Remarks

The helper builds its swap when it is constructed, a payer of BMA on a nominal of 100 with an arbitrary Libor fraction of 0.75, from the settlement date for the tenor, so it reads the context's evaluation date and its at-par switch then. Its dates are the earliest date and the latest date, the BMA value date of the first Wednesday after the swap's maturity; the maturity, latest relevant and pillar dates fall back on the latest date.

Only the BMA leg prices against the curve the helper is bound to: its coupons are copied on a BMA index forecasting on that curve. The Libor leg forecasts on the Libor index's own curve, and the swap is discounted on it, as in QuantLib; without that curve the implied quote refuses "discounting term structure handle is empty".

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
