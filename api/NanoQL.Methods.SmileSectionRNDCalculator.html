The risk-neutral density of the logarithm of the underlying at a smile section's expiry, by the Breeden-Litzenberger identity: the density and distribution from the section's prices, the inverse from a table of log-moneyness against the normal quantile z = Φ⁻¹(P(S ≤ K)), linear for a lognormal law and well conditioned in the wings.

Remarks

Immutable. The quantile table is built at construction (the playbook's LazyObject row), where QuantLib builds it at the first inverse; where it can't be built, the refusal is recorded and raised from InvCdf(double, double), QuantLib's point, since the density and distribution never need the table. Its argument x is ln(S); its only time is the section's exercise time.
