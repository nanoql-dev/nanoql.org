Remarks

The surface's reference date, calendar, day counter, settlement days and maximum date are the at-the-money curve's, as QuantLib's overrides give them. It keeps the context it was built with, which the index's fixings and forecasts read and on which each smile section is dated; each section is fitted per query, uncached. Quotes are values. QuantLib's updateSabrGuesses, private and never called, isn't ported (it writes past its guesses); its guess search is kept, every guess equal. QuantLib's acyclic visitor isn't ported.

Documented divergence (Divergences/SabrVolSurfaceFixingTests.cs): the section at time t reads the index's fixing on the date ⌊365t⌋ days after the reference date adjusted on the index's fixing calendar by Following, the adjustment QuantLib's interest-rate surface makes to its reference date, where QuantLib reads the unadjusted date, refused ("Fixing date … is not valid") two days in seven; the section keeps the unadjusted date. Rows of volatility spreads fewer than the option tenors are refused, where QuantLib reads past them.
