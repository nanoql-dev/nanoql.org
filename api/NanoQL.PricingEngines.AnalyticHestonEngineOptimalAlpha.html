Remarks

It searches α below −1 and above 0, each within the range where the characteristic function's moments exist (from M(double)'s explosion time), by Brent's minimization of the logarithm of the integrand at the origin, and takes the smaller minimum. Immutable: each search returns its number of evaluations with its value, where QuantLib's object counts them across its calls.

The date form reads the forward by the maturity date and the model's time from the earliest curve date, as the engine's price does; QuantLib's form takes a time and reads each curve at it, the same only where the curves share the risk-free curve's day counter and reference date (Divergences/HestonForwardByDateTests.cs, Divergences/HestonMismatchedTimesTests.cs).
