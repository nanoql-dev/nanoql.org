The abstract correlation term structure, pretty much like the volatility term structure: the correlation's range is known in advance and there is no strike. Derived structures might hold correlations of any dimension.

Remarks

In principle there might be several extrapolation dimensions; at this level neither their number nor their nature (time, strike, …) is known, and neither is whether the correlation is a number or a matrix, which the hierarchy defers to its derived structures.
