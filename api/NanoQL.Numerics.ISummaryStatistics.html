Interface ISummaryStatistics

Namespace
NanoQL.Numerics

NanoQL.dll

A distribution's mean and standard deviation: what QuantLib's Gaussian risk measures read of the statistics tool they wrap, GenericGaussianStatistics<Stat>'s Stat. Every accumulator has them (IStatistics), and so does StatsHolder, which holds two precomputed values.
