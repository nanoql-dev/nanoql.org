The iterative predictor-corrector evolver of a displaced log-normal LIBOR market model under the terminal measure: the forwards stepped from the last to the first, each corrected with the drift its later forwards' new values give.

Remarks

A working type. It copies each step's pseudo-root and covariance from the model at construction, so a step reads nothing shared; one step allocates nothing after a path's warm-up.
