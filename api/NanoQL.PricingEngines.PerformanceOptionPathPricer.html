Remarks

Immutable. QuantLib reads the periods as the path's consecutive points after its start, refusing a path of another length ("discounts/options mismatch"), so a reset at the grid's start, which the grid merges into it, is refused (Divergences/McPerformanceResetTodayTests.cs); given the resets' indices on the grid, it reads each period between its own points, as the Monte Carlo forward engines read their reset, and on a grid of distinct reset times the same points as QuantLib's.
