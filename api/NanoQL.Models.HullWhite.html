Remarks

Its short rate is r = φ(t) + x, with x an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process and φ the deterministic fitting parameter: analytical in Dynamics(), φ(t) = f(t) + ½[σ(1 − e^(−αt))/α]², and numerical in Tree(TimeGrid), whose tree fits φ to the curve at each step as it is built. Its parameters are α and σ; b and λ are null parameters.

Its constructor computes the curve's instantaneous forward rate at 0 and then its zero rate at 0, as QuantLib's does, so the curve's refusals come in QuantLib's order; the second is the initial short rate.

B(t, T) is Vasicek's, in QuantLib's master's form after the pin (#2882); the members QuantLib 1.44 cuts at small α, the bond options, the analytical fitting parameter and the convexity bias, are written in the same cancellation-free forms, exact in the limit α → 0, so their last bits differ from 1.44's at every α (Divergences/SmallReversionTests.cs).
