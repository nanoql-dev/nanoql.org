Remarks

Reference: Katsumi Takada 2011, Valuation of Arithmetically Average of Fed Funds Rates and Construction of the US Dollar Swap Yield Curve. The forward part is forecast fixing by fixing, each with a Hull-White convexity adjustment for its payment delay, or, with the approximation, through one discount ratio less two convexity adjustments.

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where the Black averaging pricer derives from it. A mean reversion of zero gives NaN wherever a fixing is forecast, as QuantLib's formulas do, the volatility being divided by its cube.
