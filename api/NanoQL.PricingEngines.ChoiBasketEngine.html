The engine for European basket and spread options on several assets by Choi's method: the basket's projection on one factor priced in closed form, integrated over the others by Gauss-Hermite quadrature.

Remarks

J. Choi, "Sum of all Black-Scholes-Merton Models: An efficient Pricing Method for Spread, Basket and Asian Options", 2018, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2913048; the author's Python implementation is at https://github.com/PyFE/PyFENG. With calcFwdDelta (or the control variate) the forward deltas are returned among the additional results as "forwardDelta k", as QuantLib's.

Each node is priced by the single-factor engine's formula on the node's forwards and signed deviations, where QuantLib builds quotes, processes and an option priced by that engine; the arithmetic is the same. Where every volatility is 0 a node's value is the intrinsic one and its root's d the limit, ±∞ by the side of the strike the forward basket is on, where QuantLib's node sets no d and its read fails (Divergences/BasketZeroVolatilitiesTests.cs).

The structures are read by date, accruing nothing before their dates, where QuantLib's reads throw for an option expiring before a later structure's date (Divergences/BasketExpiryBeforeReferenceDateTests.cs).

The processes must discount alike: QuantLib's check that every risk-free discount is close enough to the first's. The correctness of the returned value is tested by reproducing results available in literature.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context.

processes ImmutableArray<GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess>

The assets' processes.

rho Matrix

The assets' correlation matrix.

lambda double

The integration's scale, from which each direction's order follows.

maxNrIntegrationSteps int?

The largest number of integration nodes, the scale reduced until the orders fit; null (QuantLib's largest Size) for no limit.

calcFwdDelta bool

Whether to compute the forward deltas.

controlVariate bool

Whether to correct the value by the forward deltas' control variate.

Returns

MultiAssetOptionResults

The value, and the forward deltas among the additional results where asked for.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

An argument is null.

ArgumentException

No process is given, the correlation's size isn't the processes', or lambda isn't positive.

NanoQLException

The exercise isn't European, the payoff isn't an average or spread basket, the weights aren't one per process or there is only one, the processes discount differently, the covariance isn't positive definite, or the orders can't be fitted.
