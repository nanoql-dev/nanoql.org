Remarks

The coupon wraps an underlying floating-rate coupon: its nominal is the constant leg's notional converted at the period's FX-reset observation, by its FX-reset pricer; its rate is the underlying's, and its amounts the underlying's rescaled to that nominal, which keeps the underlying's accrual logic, e.g. the partial-period compounding of an overnight-indexed coupon. ContractNominal is the underlying's.

Immutable, as every coupon is: SetFxResetPricer(FxResetPricer?) returns a copy holding the pricer, and a coupon pricer set through SetPricer<T>(T, FloatingRateCouponPricer?) is forwarded to a copy of the underlying, whose pricer drives the paid rate.
