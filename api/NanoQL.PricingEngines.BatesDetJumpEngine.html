Remarks

A BatesEngine, as QuantLib's derives from it, over its BatesDetJumpModel.

The intensity's average weights the constant-intensity term by (x − 1 + e^{−x})/x and (1 − e^{−x})/x, x = κ_λt, which QuantLib writes as differences that cancel as x falls: 1.2e-6 off the constant-intensity price at κ_λ = 1e-12 (batesengine.cpp:80, :140). Here, below x = 1e-2, the first is x·φ₂(−x), φ₂(u) = (eᵘ − 1 − u)/u² by its series, and the second −expm1(−x)/x; QuantLib's expressions and bits at and above it (Divergences/BatesDetJumpSmallReversionTests.cs).
