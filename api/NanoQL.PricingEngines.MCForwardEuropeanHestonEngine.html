Remarks

A static class over MCForwardVanillaEngine<TMc, TPath, TStats>, pinned to HestonProcess, QuantLib's default template argument. The paths run on the process's clock, as the Monte Carlo Heston engine runs them, the reset read at its index on the grid and the payoff discounted at expiry there; on structures dated alike, QuantLib's bits. The control variate is the vanilla struck at the moneyness times the spot, priced by AnalyticHestonEngine and simulated with its reset at the start, QuantLib's. The payoff must be plain vanilla; the seed is explicit.
