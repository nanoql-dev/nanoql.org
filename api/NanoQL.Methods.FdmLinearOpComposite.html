Remarks

A working type: SetTime(double, double) rewrites the operator in buffers it holds, where QuantLib builds new bands per call, and the applies and solves write into the caller's span, which mustn't overlap the input. The value-returning forms are QuantLib's. An extension point, as in QuantLib: later units derive their operators from it.
