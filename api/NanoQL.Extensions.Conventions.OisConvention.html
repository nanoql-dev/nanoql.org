Remarks

An option the record leaves out is the builder's: the set's MakeOIS. record for the index's class, or its MakeOIS record, read through the index's provider when the swap is built, or the index's own conventions. The rate helper takes one convention for both legs and their termination dates, one rule and one end-of-month flag, and counts the settlement days on the overnight leg's calendar: a record whose values for them differ is refused.
