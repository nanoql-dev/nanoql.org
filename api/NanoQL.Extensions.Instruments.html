MBSFixedRateBond

A fixed-rate mortgage pass-through with prepayment: a pool of level-payment mortgages at a weighted-average coupon (WAC), whose expected principal and pass-through interest reach the holder each period, the prepayments as a model gives them.

MBSFixedRateBondFunctions

PSACurve

The Public Securities Association's prepayment benchmark at a multiple of its speed: a CPR rising by 0.2% a month from 0.2% in the first month to 6% in the thirtieth, and 6% from then on, times the multiplier.
