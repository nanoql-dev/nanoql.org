Remarks

A working type, mutable and not thread-safe, as the generators are: the one-dimensional Evolve(double, double, double, double) draws its two jump uniforms from a Mersenne Twister it builds on its first call, seeded from that call's Gaussian draw as QuantLib's (1234·dw + 56789, as an unsigned 32-bit integer), and keeps for every later call; so a path's jumps depend on how often the process was evolved before, as QuantLib's. Clone() returns a copy with its own, unseeded generator, for a second simulation or a second thread. The five-argument Evolve(double, double, double, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>), taking the jump uniforms, is stateless. A process array holding it is as thread-safe as its least safe member.

The standard deviation over a step, √(σ²(t)(1 − e^(−2θ₁Δt))/(2θ₁)), cancels in 1 − exp(−2θ₁Δt) at small θ₁ and is NaN at θ₁ = 0 in QuantLib's: here below |2θ₁Δt| 1e-2 it is √(σ²(t)·(−expm1(−2θ₁Δt))/(2θ₁)), and σ(t)√Δt at θ₁ = 0, QuantLib's formula at and above (Divergences/GemanRoncoroniDeviationTests.cs).
