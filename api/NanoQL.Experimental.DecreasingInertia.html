Remarks

The factor is threshold + (1 − threshold)(K − k)/K at the k-th call of K iterations, k counted from 1, so the last iteration's is the threshold. QuantLib's never sets its swarm size, which it loops over (undefined), and never advances its iteration, so its factor stays 1 and the inertia never decreases (Divergences/ParticleSwarmInertiaTests.cs).
