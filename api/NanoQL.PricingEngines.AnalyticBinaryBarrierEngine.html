Remarks

The option's exercise is American with its payoff at expiry, its window open from the start, as in QuantLib. The engine reads its discounts and its variance by date, so curves on other day counters or dates price as on one clock. The formulas are first-passage closed forms in one equivalent-constant carry and volatility, the variance and discounts to expiry: no exact closed form exists for a first passage under general deterministic term structures, and on shaped markets they miss QuantLib's finite-difference barrier engine or a Monte Carlo by -3.6% to +1.7%. They are exact where (r − q)/σ² is constant over the option's life and the volatility depends on time alone, the price then a time change of a constant market's; a knock-out's rebate, paid at the hit, where r/σ² and q/σ² are each constant (Conventions/ClosedFormExactnessTests.cs). The term-structure-exact route is FdBlackScholesBarrierEngine; the Monte Carlo engines monitor the barrier at their grid's dates.

Two documented divergences. At a variance below machine epsilon the underlying follows its forward, and hits the barrier, if at all, when the forward reaches it, as QuantLib's master prices the analytic barrier engine (e29c64126), where QuantLib's indicators read the spot and its power of the barrier overflows to 0 (Divergences/BinaryBarrierZeroVolatilityTests.cs). And the option's rebate is priced, a knock-out's at the hit, now where the barrier is already touched, and a knock-in's at expiry, by the analytic barrier engine's rebate terms, where QuantLib's engine drops it (Divergences/BinaryBarrierRebateTests.cs).
