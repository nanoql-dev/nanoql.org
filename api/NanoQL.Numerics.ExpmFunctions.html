The matrix exponential by the ordinary differential equations method (Moler and Van Loan 1978, "Nineteen Dubious Ways to Compute the Exponential of a Matrix"): each column of exp(t·M) solves x' = M·x from a unit vector, by adaptive Runge-Kutta.
