Remarks

φ is analytical in Dynamics(), φ(t) = f(t) − 2kθ(e^(th) − 1)/(2h + (k + h)(e^(th) − 1)) − 4x₀h²e^(th)/(2h + (k + h)(e^(th) − 1))², f the curve's instantaneous forward and h = √(k² + 2σ²), and numerical in Tree(TimeGrid), whose tree fits it to the curve at each step as it is built. QuantLib's header warns that the class "was not tested enough to guarantee its functionality".

As CoxIngersollRoss's, its Feller constraint reads each parameter vector's own k and θ, and its tree is on the square root of the process, y = √(r − φ(t)), the short rate y² + φ(t).
