The one-dimensional mesher of a Black-Scholes process in the logarithm of the underlying for several strikes at once: from below the lowest to above the highest of the forwards S²/K·d the strikes give, by a multiple of the standard deviation at each, concentrating around a critical point when one is given inside.

Remarks

Immutable. QuantLib reads the curves and the volatility at the risk-free curve's times (fdmblackscholesmultistrikemesher.cpp:45-62); here each at the instants its maturity denotes on Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), on its own clock, as FdmBlackScholesMesher reads them, and QuantLib's reads where they share the risk-free curve's day counter and reference date.
