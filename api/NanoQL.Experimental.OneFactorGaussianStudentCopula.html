Class OneFactorGaussianStudentCopula

Namespace
NanoQL.Experimental
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The one-factor Gaussian-Student t copula: Yᵢ = a·M + √(1 − a²)·Zᵢ, M Gaussian and Zᵢ Student t of N_z degrees of freedom, each Student variable scaled by √((ν − 2)/ν) to unit variance. Y's distribution is tabulated at construction on 201 points of [−10, 10], each a double Euler sum of the convolution on [−10, 10] in 400 steps, QuantLib's loop order.

Remarks

The factor is integrated over [−maximum, maximum]: QuantLib's Student classes pass no minimum, so its base's −5 stands and the integrals lose the factor's left tail, where defaults are nearly certain (the copula's integral of p = 0.001 reads 0.000898 on Student 5/5 at correlation 0.3 and 0.000102 on Student-Gaussian ν 3 at 0.6; Divergences/OneFactorStudentCopulaRangeTests.cs). The Student densities and distributions are nanoql's fixed ones, so a tabulated value reads the last bits of their fixes where QuantLib's formula loses its digits.
