Remarks

Immutable: a matrix read returns a copy.

Three departures from QuantLib (Divergences/ExponentialCorrelationTests.cs): one rate is accepted, which QuantLib refuses as "Rate times must contain at least two values" when given two; at γ ≠ 1, correlation times that skip a rate time but the last are refused, as QuantLib's own FIXME asks, since a period's midpoint then falls after a rate that is still alive in the period has fixed and the models give that rate no variance there; and the refusal of a last correlation time past the last rate time names that time, where QuantLib's text calls it the next-to-last.
