Remarks

Immutable. On the unit square's edges it returns the boundary values, C(u, 0) = C(0, v) = 0, C(u, 1) = u and C(1, v) = v, before its formula, as every copula here does. QuantLib's formula cancels wherever the root nears s: near the edges at every θ (1e-7 relative at θ 0.5 and 2 on points 1e-8 from them) and everywhere as θ nears 1 (9.5e-5). nanoql takes the stable root, 2xyθ/(s + √…) where s is positive, QuantLib's form elsewhere, and above θ 1 the radicand as 1 + 2(θ − 1)(x(1 − y) + y(1 − x)) + ((θ − 1)(x − y))², whose terms are all non-negative, where QuantLib's cancels as θ grows (1e-8 at θ 1e8): within 4e-16 relative of a high-precision evaluation of QuantLib's formula on points within 1e-12 of the edges, from θ 0.1 to 1e12; toward θ 0 the copula nears max(x + y − 1, 0), whose cancellation near x + y = 1 no form avoids.
