Remarks

QuantLib's four constructors collapse into two: its quote overloads are the double ones, and the settlement-days form, whose reference date moves with the evaluation date, is Create(int, Calendar, BusinessDayConvention, double, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, VolatilityType, double).
