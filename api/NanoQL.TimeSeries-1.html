Remarks

Immutable. QuantLib's inserting operator[] and its inserting find aren't ported: a missing date is read with TryGetValue(Date, out T), which leaves the series unchanged, where QuantLib's operator[] const returns Null<T>(). QuantLib's container template parameter isn't ported: the series always keeps its data in date order.

Two series are equal when they hold the same dates with equal values, compared by Default; the hash is computed when the series is built.
