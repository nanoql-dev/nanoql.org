A synthetic collateralized debt obligation: protection on a basket's tranche between its attachment D₁ and detachment D₂, against a running premium on the tranche's notional, reduced by the expected protection payments, and an upfront.

Remarks

For purchased protection the value is the protection leg's V₁ = Σ (Eᵢ − Eᵢ₋₁)·dᵢ less the premium leg's V₂ = m·Σ (D₂ − D₁ − Eᵢ)·Δᵢ₋₁,ᵢ·dᵢ, Eᵢ the expected tranche loss at tᵢ. The CDO holds its basket, bound to a loss model, and is priced by MidPointCDOEngine or IntegralCDOEngine. A notional above the basket's tranche notional levers the CDO by their ratio.

ImplicitCorrelation(IReadOnlyList<double>, YieldTermStructure, EvaluationContext, double, double) binds a Gaussian LHP model to a copy of the basket at each trial correlation and leaves the CDO's basket untouched, where QuantLib's sets the model on the user's basket and leaves it there.
