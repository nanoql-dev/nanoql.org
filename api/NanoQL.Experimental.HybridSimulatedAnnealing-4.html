The hybrid simulated annealing: a sampler draws each new point, a probability accepts it, a temperature schedule cools the dimensions and a reannealing rescales their steps, with a local optimizer at each new or best point: QuantLib's HybridSimulatedAnnealing.

Remarks

The sampler provides a density, on the current point, for the parameters; the probability decides whether the new point is accepted; the temperature is a schedule T(k) over the iteration k, which the sampler and the probability read; the reannealing, a departure from Boltzmann annealing, rescales k for each dimension to improve convergence. The hybrid is the local optimizer, run at every accepted point or at every new best point. A trial whose evaluation throws is skipped, as QuantLib's catch(...) skips it. It stops at its iterations, after its stationary iterations, or when every temperature is below the end temperature, then returning None, as QuantLib's does.

Immutable: it copies its functors at construction, and each minimization copies them again, so each starts from their state at construction, where QuantLib's continues from the last minimization's.
