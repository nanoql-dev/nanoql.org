A one-factor affine survival structure whose deterministic hazard rate is interpolated on nodes: the survival is that rate's, flat past the last node, times the model's bond price at its initial intensity.

Remarks

The formulas are AffineHazardRate's, the model's initial intensity its dynamics' short rate at x0, where QuantLib squares x0. FromNodes<TBootstrap>(PiecewiseDefaultCurve<AffineHazardRate, TInterpolator, TBootstrap>) builds the curve on a piecewise affine curve's nodes, for its conditional survival: QuantLib's piecewise affine curve is this curve, and nanoql's piecewise curve can't change its base by its traits.
