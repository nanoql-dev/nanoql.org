A credit basket: credit names, each with a notional, drawn from a pool of issuers, and a tranche of the pool's loss, with the default loss model that gives its loss statistics.

Remarks

Immutable, of its terms and its evaluation context: it computes QuantLib's computeBasket() at the context's evaluation date at construction, its live names, notionals and keys, its settled loss and its remaining tranche, and keeps the context, since QuantLib's statistics take no date of evaluation and its cached state is that date's. QuantLib's constructor computes that state before it sets the tranche's attachment and detachment amounts, so its remaining amounts read 0 until the first statistic (basket.cpp:57-68); here they are computed after them (Divergences/BasketConstructionOrderTests.cs).

SetLossModel(IDefaultLossModel?) returns a basket of the same terms and context whose LossModel is the model bound to it, its precomputation run once, there: QuantLib's flows set a model on a basket and change it through observers, which become rebuilds. A basket with no model refuses its statistics with QuantLib's "Basket has no default loss model assigned."

The live reads take the live names' positions: QuantLib's remainingNotionals(d) and remainingProbabilities(d) index the live list's positions, not its names (basket.cpp:196-220), so after the first of three names defaults they read the first two names' values (Divergences/BasketLiveReadsTests.cs). Every curve read before a curve's reference date is a default probability of 0, where QuantLib's refuses it ("negative time").

Realized defaults. QuantLib's basket misprices once a name has defaulted: its expected tranche loss adds the whole settled loss, its models tranche the live names' loss against min(D, A + max(0, L − A)) where its own header means max(0, A − L), its n-th-to-default probabilities ignore the defaults, and an unsettled default's recovery is a null; its header warns "Baskets with realized defaults not tested/WIP." Here the names defaulted by the evaluation date leave the pricing basket: the lost notional L writes the tranche down from its attachment, the recovered notional amortizes it from its detachment's side (min(D, N − recovered)), and the model is bound to a basket of the live names over that restated tranche, each latent model restricted to their rows (PricingBasket). Its statistics add the realized tranche loss: the expected tranche loss, the percentile and the expected shortfall add it to the live model's; ProbOverLoss(Date, double) maps its inception fraction onto the restated tranche, 1 at or below the realized loss; the loss distribution is the live one shifted by the realized loss; the event counts take the order less the realized defaults, 1 (and QuantLib's zeros) once they reach it; the vectors run over the live names; the name-indexed reads take pool positions, a defaulted name's recovery its settled one and its default correlation refused. A default not settled for the name's key's seniority is refused, QuantLib's own \todo (basket.cpp:128-131). An event dated after the evaluation date isn't realized there, so it stays out of the restatement, and the reads with a date argument keep QuantLib's use of such "programmed" events for jump-to-default analysis (basket.hpp:138-141). On a basket with no realized default, QuantLib's values, bit for bit. Divergences/BasketRealizedDefaultsTests.cs.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

An argument but claim is null.

ArgumentException

refDate is default(Date); the notionals are empty ("notionals empty"); the ratios aren't 0 ≤ attachment ≤ detachment ≤ 1 ("invalid attachment/detachment ratio"); or the notionals don't number the pool's names ("unmatched data entry sizes in basket").

NanoQLException

The evaluation date precedes the inception ("Target date lies before basket inception"), or a name has defaulted by it without a settlement for its key's seniority.
