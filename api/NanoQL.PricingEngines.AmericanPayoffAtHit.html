Remarks

An immutable calculator over the spot, the discount and dividend discount to expiry and the variance to expiry, as QuantLib's; its value and its delta, gamma and rho.

Two documented divergences, each leaving QuantLib's arithmetic where it doesn't apply. Where the closed form has no real λ (λ² = μ² − 2 ln D / σ²T < 0, at negative rates), QuantLib's square root is NaN (americanpayoffathit.cpp:75); here the value and its Greeks are the payoff's present value at the hit integrated over the first-passage density of the log-price to the strike, which is the closed form where λ is real (as the barrier engines' rebate; Divergences/AmericanPayoffAtHitNegativeRateTests.cs). Below a variance of ε, QuantLib divides by the variance (:87-102), NaN out of the money; here the underlying follows its forward, ln S(τ) = ln S + τ ln(D_q/D) over the fraction τ of the time to expiry, and the payoff is paid at the τ* where that path reaches the strike, if it does by expiry, with the Greeks of that value (Divergences/AmericanPayoffZeroVarianceTests.cs).

A payoff other than a cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing one is refused, where QuantLib leaves the paid amount unset.
