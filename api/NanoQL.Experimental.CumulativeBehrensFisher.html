Remarks

Immutable. The characteristic function of a t of order ν = 2n + 1 is a polynomial of degree n times exp(−√ν|t|); the combination's is their product, and the Gil-Pelaez theorem inverts it term by term in closed form. The orders must be odd.

A term of degree k divides (k − 1)!, or k! for the density, by the power k of a radius, and QuantLib's arithmetic overflows where either leaves the doubles: from 172 on, on the cumulative of two t's of order 173, it reads NaN, and its inverse refuses with "root not bracketed" (convolvedstudentt.cpp:124-127), where the series stays accurate. There nanoql scales the factorial and the power together, in logarithms; QuantLib's bits wherever its arithmetic doesn't overflow, through order 171, where the series is accurate to 2e-14 relative against 50 digits. A documented divergence (Divergences/BehrensFisherHighOrderTests.cs).
