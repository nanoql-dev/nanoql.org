Remarks

Amount(EvaluationContext) is the undiscounted amount, as Amount(EvaluationContext) is documented ("the actual amount paid at the cash flow date", cashflow.hpp:62-66); QuantLib's amount() returns the discounted one (commoditycashflow.hpp:56), so a consumer reading these as cash flows discounts them twice. The discounted amount is DiscountedAmount (Divergences/CommodityCashFlowAmountTests.cs).
