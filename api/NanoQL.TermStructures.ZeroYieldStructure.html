A zero-yield term structure: an adapter to YieldTermStructure that lets a derived class implement only ZeroYieldImpl(double); discount factors and forward rates are calculated from the zero yields, which are continuously compounded with an annual time unit.

Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: the interpolated zero and forward curves derive from it. QuantLib's constructor taking settlement days is YieldTermStructure's reference-date constructor with the settlement days given, the reference date computed by the derived class's Create(…, context) factory.
