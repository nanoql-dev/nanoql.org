Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: an engine for another model derives it and returns, for each expiry, the model's state there: its characteristic function, its discount factors and the strip of exponents with a finite moment. Immutable: QuantLib's cache of precalculated prices is the list form's result, so Calculate(IReadOnlyList<VanillaOption>, EvaluationContext) returns each option's results in the list's order, QuantLib's precalculate followed by each option's NPV(), one transform per expiry on the grid QuantLib's map would hold; a single option is a list of one, QuantLib's uncached route. An expired option gets its expired results without joining a transform. QuantLib's update() and clone() aren't ported: there is no state to clear or separate.

Documented divergences (Divergences/VarianceGammaFftTests.cs). The grid is sized from the largest |log K| of the expiry's strikes and at least 2^14 points, QuantLib's size wherever its own is at least 2^14 (every QuantLib case), where QuantLib's size is undefined when every strike is below e^(−λ), and below 2^14 points, as for any strike between 1 and about 60, its frequency spacing 2π/(λn) is too coarse for the transform to price. The damping is QuantLib's 1.25, and its grid, wherever E[S^2.25] is finite (every Black-Scholes market, every QuantLib case), the grid doubled at the same spacing until two successive grids agree within 5e-5 of the spot at every strike, QuantLib's prices where its own grid agrees with the first doubling and the finer grid's where a later one agrees, up to 2^22, past which a refusal: QuantLib's period λn aliases the damped call's tail once σ√T passes about 1.2, an at-the-money call pricing −298.9 against 66.80 at σ√T 2; the same refusal where the damped tail's growth, ln E[S^2.25] − 2.25 ln E[S] (α(α + 1)σ²T/2 under Black-Scholes), passes 18, σ√T 3.58, where the recovered prices carry a rounding error no grid changes, so two grids agree while both are off; otherwise half the strip's upper bound with the grid doubled until α·nλ/2 ≥ 20, while the grid stays within 2^22 points; past that the put side, α + 1 = max(u_lo/2, −0.25) inside the strip's lower part, the grid doubled from QuantLib's until two successive grids agree within 1e-8 of each strike's larger of call and put, up to 2^22, the calls by QuantLib's parity; past that a refusal.
