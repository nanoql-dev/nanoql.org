Remarks

Prices a MtMCrossCurrencyBasisSwap by discounting each leg on its own currency curve and converting to the domestic currency through the FX spot. For the resettable leg, the engine prices copies of its FX-linked cash flows holding a DiscountingFxResetPricer built from the engine's market data, so pricing leaves the swap's own cash flows unchanged. A future reset is projected to its FX value date from the spot quote and the two discount curves; an already-fixed reset is read from the context's ExchangeRates on its fixing date.

Static: QuantLib's engine object becomes the arguments of Calculate(MtMCrossCurrencyBasisSwap, EvaluationContext, Currency?, YieldTermStructure, Currency?, YieldTermStructure, double, bool?, Date?, Date?, Date?).

Exceptions

NanoQLException

A discount factor the FX conversion divides by is zero; a leg fails to price, or its currency is neither of the two, the message naming the leg; or a fair spread is derived from a zero leg BPS.
