Remarks

Its times run on the clock spanning both processes' structures, from their earliest reference date on the first process's risk-free day counter, each structure read at the instants those times denote and accruing nothing before its own date, where QuantLib runs on the first process's risk-free times and reads the second's structures at them (Divergences/Fd2dMismatchedTimesTests.cs); a market on one clock keeps QuantLib's arithmetic.

The price discounts on the first process's risk-free curve, as the analytic two-asset engines do, where QuantLib's discounts at the second's (Fdm2dBlackScholesOp; Divergences/Fd2dTwoRiskFreeCurvesTests.cs).

The correctness of the returned value is tested by reproducing results available in literature and comparison with Kirk's approximation.
