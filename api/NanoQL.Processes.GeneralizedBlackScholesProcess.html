Class GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess

Namespace
NanoQL.Processes
Assembly
NanoQL.dll

The generalized Black-Scholes stochastic process, d ln S(t) = (r(t) − q(t) − σ(t, S)²/2) dt + σ dW_t, on a risk-free curve, a dividend curve and a Black volatility, with its local volatility σ(t, S) derived from the Black one. The interface is expressed in S; the internal calculations work on ln S.

Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib: its four named processes derive from it. The process is immutable; the curves and volatilities it holds have reference dates fixed when they were built, so Time(Date) needs no context.

The local volatility is chosen in the constructor, by QuantLib's type tests in QuantLib's order: a BlackConstantVol gives a LocalConstantVol at its volatility at the spot, a BlackVarianceCurve a LocalVolCurve, and any other structure a LocalVolSurface at the spot. The first two are strike-independent, so the expectation, the variance and the evolution are exact; on a surface they are the discretization's. QuantLib chooses and caches it at the first use. A process given its local volatility uses it, and is never strike-independent.
