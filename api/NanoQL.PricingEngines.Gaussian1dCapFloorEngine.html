Remarks

A static class: Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1) on the model, an argument where QuantLib's engine holds it. The coupons must carry no spread. The results add each optionlet's price and its at-the-money forward, the coupon's (optionletsPrice, optionletsAtmForward).

Seven of QuantLib's defects are fixed. The strike's leg is discounted on the discount curve, as the floating leg and the swaption engine's are, where QuantLib's reads the model curve (:86-89, :158-161); the at-the-money forwards are the coupons', where QuantLib's are left 0; a caplet fixed before today is the known payoff discounted to its payment, where QuantLib's is undiscounted; a curve on another clock is read at a caplet's fixing date; a CMS coupon's rate is the model's swap rate at its fixing, and a CMS spread coupon's the geared spread of its two indexes' swap rates, the float-float engine's estimates, where QuantLib's prices the coupon period's simple forward; flat payoff extrapolation's right tail reads the last node. And a compounded overnight coupon, which QuantLib prices on its last day's rate times the whole accrual, is a Black call on its window's compounding factor on GSR: each daily factor is a zero bond affine in the state, so the factor is lognormal given the state at the window's start, exactly for plain compounding and an observation shift and to second order for a lookback without the shift and a lockout, its log-variance the discrete sum's over the fixing dates; fixings already made come from the index's history, and a payment after the window's end is handled statically, the window end's forward deflated by the payment date's bond. On a Markov-functional model, which defines no short rate, such a coupon is refused.
