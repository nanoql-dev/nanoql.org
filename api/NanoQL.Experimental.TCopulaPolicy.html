The Student t latent model's copula policy: independent t factors, each normalized to unit variance by √((ν − 2)/ν), so that a latent variable is a combination of t's (CumulativeBehrensFisher).

Remarks

A readonly struct built by Create(ImmutableArray<ImmutableArray<double>>, TCopulaPolicyInitTraits), over immutable state; its zero value holds no distribution, and every member of default(TCopulaPolicy) throws InvalidOperationException. The orders must exceed 2 (finite variance) and, for a model with variables, be odd, as the convolution requires.

The distributions are Boost.Math's students_t, ported: its cdf, pdf and quantile. At 4 and 6 degrees of freedom Boost's quantile takes Shaw's forms, which cancel near ½ (InverseCumulativeStudent's remarks); there, within 0.05 of ½, the policy takes one Newton step on Boost's cumulative function, as InverseCumulativeStudent does. Those orders are even, which the convolution refuses, so only a policy built without variables reads them.
