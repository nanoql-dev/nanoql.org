The constrained Euler evolver of a displaced log-normal LIBOR market model, for the Fries-Joshi proxy simulation of Greeks: at each step whose constraint is active, the log forwards are shifted along the constrained rate's covariances so that it lands on its constraint, and the step's weight takes the likelihood ratio of the shift.

Remarks

A working type, copying each step's pseudo-root at construction.

Two departures from QuantLib (Divergences/ConstrainedEulerCovarianceTests.cs, …DisplacementTests.cs): each step's covariances with its constrained rate are its own pseudo-root's, where QuantLib builds every step's from the pseudo-root of the step SetConstraintType(ReadOnlySpan<int>, ReadOnlySpan<int>) is called at, so a later step's constraint misses (lognormalfwdrateeulerconstrained.cpp:117); and the log constraint is displaced by the constrained rate's displacement, where QuantLib's reads the step's index into the displacements (:146). A step before SetThisConstraint(ReadOnlySpan<double>, ReadOnlySpan<bool>), an active constraint before SetConstraintType(ReadOnlySpan<int>, ReadOnlySpan<int>) and a constrained rate past the rates, which QuantLib reads before or past its vectors, are refused.

The likelihood ratio (a documented divergence; Divergences/ProxyGreekVegaTests.cs): the evolver takes the reference model, the unbumped model whose distribution the proxy simulation keeps, and a constrained step's weight carries the ratio of the two models' transition densities of the constrained rate, which beside QuantLib's ratio of shifted to unshifted Gaussian densities (lognormalfwdrateeulerconstrained.cpp:200-212) holds √(var_reference / var_own) of the rate's step variance. QuantLib's weight misses that factor, right for a forward bump, whose variances are equal, and wrong for a volatility bump: its proxy vegas take the wrong sign. Where the reference is the evolver's own model, QuantLib's weight bit for bit.
