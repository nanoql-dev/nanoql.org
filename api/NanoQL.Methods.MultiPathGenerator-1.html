Remarks

A working type, not thread-safe: Next() and Antithetic() draw from its generator and write its own paths, so the sample's value is a view valid until the next draw. It copies the generator it is given. Each step evolves the process over the step's increments through the process's in-place InitialValues(Span<double>) and Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>), into buffers the generator holds, where QuantLib's process returns a new array a step: a process that overrides the two steps without allocating, and the loop allocates nothing per sample; one that doesn't keeps its allocating forms' bits.

QuantLib refuses a Brownian bridge at the first draw ("Brownian bridge not supported"); this generator refuses it at construction, with the same text.
