Remarks

An unsealed, immutable object holding the process, the summation cutoff and the integral's limit, its public member QuantLib's Phi(Complex, Complex, double, double, int), with a static Calculate in the engines' form. Phi computes its coefficients from its times on each call, where QuantLib's read the last pricing's (0 before any); the recursion of its series, QuantLib's memo, is one call's working state. The value, with QuantLib's additional results ("dcf", "qf", "s0", "strike", "expiryTime", "exercise", "term1", "term2", "xiRightLimit", "summationCutoff", "a1" to "a5").

Documented divergences (Divergences/HestonContinuousGeometricAsianTests.cs). Phi's coefficient of w is log S₀ + log(D_q/D_r) − ρv₀/σ − ρκθτ/σ, log S_T's constant, where QuantLib's multiplies the log spot by the carry factor and adds ρκθτ/σ, the sign its a3 doesn't take, so its Phi(0, 1), the expected terminal spot, is 81.92 against the forward 103.05; the prices read w = 0 only and are QuantLib's. An option whose averaging starts before or after the model's time 0 is refused, as QuantLib's Black-Scholes continuous engine refuses a start date, where QuantLib's Heston engine prices it as unseasoned from today; one starting at time 0 is QuantLib's own case. Times run on the model's clock, HestonProcess's model time from the earliest reference date of its curves, each discount read at its instants, none accrued before its curve's own date.
