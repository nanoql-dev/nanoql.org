Remarks

Immutable over its Heston process and its forward Hull-White process. The Hull-White process the hybrid evolves, its model and its numeraire's end discount are on the Heston process's risk-free curve, from the given process's reversion, volatility and forward-measure time, which HullWhiteProcess returns; the given process's own curve isn't read. QuantLib builds its model on the Heston curve but takes the short rate from the given process's curve (hybridhestonhullwhiteprocess.cpp:37-46, :64, :137, :179), so the numeraire prices a bond on one curve at a rate taken from the other; here all of it runs on the one curve, QuantLib's bits where the two curves are one.

The process's times run on the Heston process's model clock, from the earliest reference date of its curves on the risk-free curve's day counter, each curve read at the instant a time denotes, the Hull-White parts on the risk-free curve restated on that clock; QuantLib's process reads the dividend curve at the risk-free curve's times.

The step's terms m₂, m₄, m₅, v₁ and v₁₂, QuantLib's expressions, cancel as a·dt falls: below a·dt of NanoQL.Processes.HybridHestonHullWhiteProcess.StepCut they are evaluated in double-double, below its floor in quad-double and below that by the cubic in a through four quad-double values, QuantLib's double and bits from the cut up, the cut where QuantLib's double first departs from the expressions' exact values by more than 1e-12 relative, measured at the run. The in-place Evolve(double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, double, ReadOnlySpan<double>, Span<double>) steps by the same arithmetic without allocating.
