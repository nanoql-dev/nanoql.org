Remarks

The index has no forwarding curve of its own: its forecast at a fixing date is the gearing times the proxied index's fixing there, plus the spread. QuantLib's multiplies by the spread, gearing × proxied × spread, 5.96e-5 where a gearing of 2 and a spread of 0.001 on a 2.98% fixing mean 0.0606 (Divergences/ProxyIborTests.cs). A coupon on it forecasts through this forecast; QuantLib's coupons forecast from the index's forwarding curve between their dates, which it lacks ("null term structure set to this instance of …"), so they can't price.

Clone(YieldTermStructure?) is QuantLib's inherited one: a plain Ibor index with the proxy's conventions on the curve given (iborindex.cpp:61-73), which QuantLib's rate helpers rely on when they clone every index onto the curve they build; such a clone forecasts from that curve, not from the proxied index.
