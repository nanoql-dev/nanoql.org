The backward operator of a Black-Scholes equity under a Cox-Ingersoll-Ross short rate, dr = κ(θ − r)dt + σ√r·dW₂, d⟨W₁, W₂⟩ = ρ·dt, on a mesher of the log spot in direction 0 and the short rate in direction 1: the equity's drift r − q − ½v and diffusion ½v, the rate's ½σ²r and κ(θ − r), their covariance ρσ_Sσ√r, discounting at the short rate.

Remarks

A working type: its three parts are built at construction; SetTime(double, double) rewrites them in place, and the apply, splitting solves and preconditioner write into the caller's spans through buffers it holds, so a step allocates nothing. Its times are Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?)'s, the dividend curve and the volatility read on it, each on its own.

QuantLib's rates part is σ²r ∂ᵣᵣ where the CIR generator's is ½σ²r ∂ᵣᵣ, and its mixed term 2ρσ_Sσ where the covariance is ρσ_Sσ√r (fdmcirop.cpp:55-60, :74-82): QuantLib's operator prices a rate with twice the CIR variance, and blows up at ρ ≠ 0. Here the generator's coefficients. With a quanto helper the drift is less its adjustment at the grid's rate (NanoQL.Methods.FdmCIREquityPart's remarks), where QuantLib's operator takes no helper.
