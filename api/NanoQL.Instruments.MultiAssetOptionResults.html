Remarks

An extension point, as in QuantLib's results class: the results of options derived from MultiAssetOption derive from it (Margrabe's option adds its two deltas and gammas). QuantLib's Greeks are its members.

Where QuantLib's option throws "<greek> not provided" on reading a Greek its engine left null, the member is null and the caller checks it, as InstrumentResults documents for every result.
