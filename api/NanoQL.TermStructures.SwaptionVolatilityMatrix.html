Remarks

The volatility matrix has a row per option date and a column per swap tenor: the element (i, j) is the volatility of the i-th option and the j-th tenor; the shifts, when given, are laid out alike. The constructor copies both. Outside the grid the interpolation extends linearly, or, with flat extrapolation, takes the nearest edge's value; a query outside the structure's range is refused unless extrapolation is enabled or requested.

QuantLib's constructors on quotes and on a Matrix coincide once a quote is a double: the Matrix forms are kept, and the floating forms, whose reference date is the evaluation date adjusted on the calendar, are Create(Calendar, BusinessDayConvention, IEnumerable<Period>, IEnumerable<Period>, Matrix, DayCounter, EvaluationContext, bool, VolatilityType, Matrix?). As in QuantLib, the smile sections are by time, flat at the at-the-money volatility (read at a dummy strike of 5%), and the shift is read without the lognormal check that ConstantSwaptionVolatility makes.

A collection, the volatilities or the day counter is null.

ArgumentException

The dates are empty, not after the reference date or not increasing, the swap tenors are empty, not positive, not increasing or not in months or years, a matrix's size isn't the grid's, or there are fewer than two option dates or swap tenors.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

NanoQLException
