Remarks

A static class: Calculate runs QuantLib's four steps (playbook §2.1) and returns the instrument's results, with QuantLib's additional results, read by Result<T>(string): vega (a double), and per optionlet optionletsPrice, optionletsVega, optionletsDelta, optionletsDiscountFactor and optionletsStdDev (each an ImmutableArray<T> of double) and optionletsAtmForward (of Nullable<T> double, null for a coupon paid before the evaluation date, where QuantLib's is Null<Rate>()). A collar's results hold no optionletsStdDev, as QuantLib's don't.

The volatility's reference date is today and the discount curve's the settlement date: a caplet paid on or before the settlement date is skipped, and one fixed on or before today is priced at its intrinsic value, its vega and delta 0. A caplet the engine prices whose coupon is paid before the evaluation date has no forward, where QuantLib prices on its Null<Rate>() sentinel, 3.4028234663852886e+38 (a discount curve dated before the evaluation date reaches it): it is refused, a documented divergence.

QuantLib's constructors on a volatility value and on a quote coincide once a quote is a double: the overload on a double builds, at each call, the constant volatility QuantLib's builds, shifted lognormal on no calendar, with its reference date the context's evaluation date. Its day counter defaults to QuantLib's, Actual365Fixed, for null.
