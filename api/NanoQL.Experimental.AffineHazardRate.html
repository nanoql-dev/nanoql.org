The affine hazard-rate curve traits: a deterministic hazard rate interpolated on the nodes, added to a one-factor affine model of the default intensity, bootstrapped so that the model's curve reprices the market.

Remarks

The curve kind takes its model: the model-aware members carry the formulas, and the model-free ones refuse, as QuantLib's piecewise curve fails to build on these traits without a model. The survival probability is the deterministic part's times the model's bond price at its initial intensity, the dynamics' short rate at the process's x0: QuantLib squares x0 (interpolatedaffinehazardratecurve.hpp:297), right for its 1.22 √r CIR helper and wrong since 1.23 put CIR's dynamics on the CIR process, 0.9089 for 0.8769 at five years (Divergences/AffineHazardRateTests.cs).
