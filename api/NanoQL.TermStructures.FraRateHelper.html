Remarks

The FRA starts a period, or a number of IMM dates on the main cycle, after the spot date, which is counted from the evaluation date by the index's fixing days even when the evaluation date isn't a business day; or between explicit start and end dates, which don't follow the evaluation date. QuantLib checks neither that the end IMM offset is after the start one nor that the end date is after the start date, and nor does this helper.

useIndexedCoupon is the helper's own choice, read nowhere else: with it, the rate is the index fixing on the curve, forecast even on the evaluation date, and the latest relevant date is the index's maturity from the start; without it, the simple rate over the year fraction between the start and maturity dates, which is the latest relevant date. Bound to a curve, its copy forecasts on a clone of the index on that curve.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
