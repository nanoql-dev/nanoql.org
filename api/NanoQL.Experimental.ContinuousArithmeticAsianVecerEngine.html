Remarks

The engine reads every structure on one clock, the earliest reference date of the process's structures on the risk-free day counter, each at the instant a time denotes and nothing accrued before its own date, where QuantLib measures the time from the evaluation date on the risk-free day counter beside each curve's zero rate from its own date: each rate is the curve's own zero rate to the maturity times its own time over the clock's, and the volatility its own Black volatility at the maturity times the square root of its time over the clock's; on structures sharing the evaluation date and the day counter each factor is 1 and QuantLib's numbers stand. Where the initial point lies on the grid's upper end the price is the last node's value, the interpolation's own limit, where QuantLib reads one node past the grid. The operators are built per call.

The averaging starts at the engine's start date, as QuantLib's; an option carrying a start date of its own that differs is refused, where QuantLib ignores it. QuantLib's current average is never read by its engine, nor here.
