Remarks

Immutable. The equity leg is the first, its one cash flow paid the payment delay's days after the schedule's end on the payment calendar, or on the schedule's own; the interest-rate leg is the second. The swap's type refers to the equity leg: a payer pays the equity return and receives the floating leg.
