The non-adaptive Gauss-Kronrod integration: QuantLib's 10-, 21-, 43- and 87-point rules in succession, each reusing its predecessors' points, until an estimate is within the absolute or the relative accuracy; for fast integration of smooth functions.

Remarks

The rules sample the integrand at 87 points at most, on QUADPACK's qng abscissae and weights (L. W. Fullerton, Bell Labs, 1981). Each integration's result carries its error estimate, AbsoluteError, and its number of evaluations, 21, 43 or 87, which QuantLib keeps in the integrator's mutable members (kronrodintegral.cpp:299-350); IntegrationSuccess(IntegrationResult) reads them.

The maximum number of evaluations isn't a bound here: the last rule's result is returned whatever its error, and IntegrationSuccess(IntegrationResult) compares its count with the maximum, as QuantLib's does.
