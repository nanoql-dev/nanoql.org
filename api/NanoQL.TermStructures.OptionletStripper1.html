Remarks

The strip runs in the constructor, in QuantLib's order: the optionlets' dates and at-the-money rates, the switch strike, then per strike the caps' or floors' prices on the term volatilities, the optionlets' prices by difference and their implied volatilities. The strip reads the context it is given, its evaluation date and fixings, and is done once: a new evaluation date or market is a new stripper.

Each shifted lognormal implied standard deviation is solved from QuantLib's first guess, 0.14, where QuantLib's recalculation starts from the previous calculation's result; a normal one takes no guess. A failure it was told not to throw leaves 0. Out-of-the-money options are used: floors below the switch strike and caps from it.

Parameters

termVolSurface CapFloorTermVolSurface

The cap and floor term-volatility surface.

index IborIndex

The index of the caps' and floors' coupons.

context EvaluationContext

The evaluation context: its evaluation date starts the Ibor caps, and its fixings and forecasts give the at-the-money rates and the caps' prices.

switchStrike double?

The strike below which floors are used, or null, QuantLib's Null<Rate>(), for the average of the at-the-money rates.

accuracy double

The implied volatilities' accuracy.

maxIter int

The implied volatilities' maximum number of evaluations.

discount YieldTermStructure

The discount curve, or null, QuantLib's empty handle, for the index's forwarding curve.

type VolatilityType

The type of the stripped volatilities.

displacement double

The displacement of shifted lognormal volatilities.

dontThrow bool

Whether an optionlet whose volatility can't be implied gets 0, instead of the strip throwing.

optionletFrequency Period?

The optionlets' frequency, or null for the index's tenor; required for an overnight index.

paymentLag int

The coupons' payment lag, in business days.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

termVolSurface, index or context is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

type isn't a member of its type, or maxIter or paymentLag is negative.

ArgumentException

The base refuses its arguments (OptionletStripper).

NanoQLException

No discount curve is given and the index has no forwarding curve, a cap or floor can't be priced, or, without dontThrow, an optionlet's volatility can't be implied.
