Remarks

A working type. It copies each step's pseudo-root at construction and owns a copy of its volatility process, so a step reads nothing shared; one step allocates nothing after a path's warm-up.

QuantLib's design, kept: volatilityFactorStep isn't read, the process's variates spread evenly from the first volatility factor by the constructor's own rule (svddfwdratepc.cpp:85-92), any spread a valid estimator. A process of no variates per step, whose spread QuantLib divides by, is refused.
