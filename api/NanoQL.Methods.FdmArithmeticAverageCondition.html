The step condition of a discrete arithmetic average on a two-dimensional mesher of the log spot and the log average: at each fixing time the average moves to its new value, the value function interpolated along the average's direction by a monotonic natural cubic spline.

Remarks

A working type: it holds its buffers, so a step allocates nothing, its spline QuantLib's MonotonicCubicNaturalSpline computed into them with the same arithmetic. QuantLib's unused running-accumulator argument is kept, its signature's.

A documented divergence. With several fixings on one time, the new average weighs them by the count after the duplicates, n/i for n fixings ending the i-th, where QuantLib's weights read the count up to the first of them, +4.4% against an exact Monte Carlo on two fixings on one date (Divergences/AsianDuplicateFixingsTests.cs).
