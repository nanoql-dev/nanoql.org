Remarks

A DigitalCoupon on a CmsSpreadCoupon, replicated through capped and floored coupons on it, each priced by its CmsSpreadCouponPricer (the visitor refuses any other pricer). Built by DigitalCmsSpreadLeg. Experimental, as in QuantLib.

The replication; QuantLib's default, central with a gap of 1e-4, when null.

Whether the coupon pays the options alone, without the underlying's rate.
