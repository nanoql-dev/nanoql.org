Remarks

Immutable. The constructor builds what QuantLib's helper builds at its first calculation, under the context it is given: the floating leg on the index from the curve's reference date (or a period of the index's tenor after it, without the first caplet) to the length after it, with no fixing days; a fixed leg on the fixed-leg frequency, unadjusted, at a dummy 4%; their swap's fair rate on the term structure, without the settlement date's flows; the cap on the floating leg at that rate; and the market value. QuantLib's helper also builds a dummy index it never reads, which this one leaves out.

The model value is the pricing engine's, set by SetPricingEngine<TModel>(Func<CapFloor, EvaluationContext, TModel, InstrumentResults>?), which takes the model being calibrated as its last argument; with no engine, pricing a cap that hasn't expired refuses with QuantLib's "null pricing engine".
