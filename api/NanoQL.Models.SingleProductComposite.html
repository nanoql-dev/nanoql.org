Remarks

A working type; a part's end is recorded and the composite's end is every part's or its last step (MarketModelComposite; a documented divergence), and its count is 0 at a step where no part steps, as MultiProductComposite zeroes a part it doesn't step, where QuantLib's leaves the last step's count (singleproductcomposite.cpp:63).

MaxNumberOfCashFlowsPerProductPerStep counts each part's products (a documented divergence): QuantLib's sums the parts' maxima per product, so a part of several products paying at one step writes past its buffer (Divergences/SingleProductCompositeBufferTests.cs).
