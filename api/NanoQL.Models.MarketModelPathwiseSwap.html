Remarks

A working type. Its cash-flow times are the rate times, the first unused, as QuantLib's.

One strike for every rate (a documented divergence): the strikes' check reads the strikes expanded from one, as its text offers ("does not equal numberOfRates or 1"). QuantLib's reads the parameter (pathwiseproductswap.cpp:59), so it refuses the one strike it has just expanded (Divergences/MarketModelPathwiseSwapStrikeTests.cs). With a strike a rate, QuantLib's swap.
