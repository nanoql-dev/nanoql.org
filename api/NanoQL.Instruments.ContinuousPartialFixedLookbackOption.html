A continuous partial-time fixed-strike lookback option (Heynen and Kat, 1994): the extremum is observed from a date after the start to expiry, and a call pays the maximum less the strike, a put the strike less the minimum.

Remarks

Immutable. Its extremum observed so far is 0, as QuantLib's constructor sets it: the observation starts later. Its terms are checked before they are read, where QuantLib's validation dereferences a European exercise unchecked.
