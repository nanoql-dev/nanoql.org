Class GaussianQuadrature

Namespace

A Gaussian quadrature: the nodes and weights of an orthogonal polynomial's rule of a given order, from the eigen-decomposition of its recurrence's Jacobi matrix (G.H. Golub and J.H. Welsch, "Calculation of Gauss quadrature rules", Math. Comput. 23 (1969), 221-230), and the weighted sum of a function at the nodes.

Remarks

A weight whose quotient overflows or underflows is formed in logarithms; one that can't be recovered, its eigenvector component zero, is dropped as QuantLib drops it. Order zero is refused: QuantLib allocates an array of n − 1 elements, which wraps (gaussianquadratures.cpp:41), and throws C++'s std::bad_array_new_length.
