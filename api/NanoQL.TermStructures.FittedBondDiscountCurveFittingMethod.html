Remarks

A derived method defines DiscountFunction(ReadOnlySpan<double>, double) and Size, the number of parameters fitted. The generic fit is nonlinear, in contrast to the typically faster linear fitting methods. The bonds' price errors are weighted by the given weights, or by their inverse modified durations, normalized; an L2 penalty, if given, applies a Gaussian prior to each parameter around the initial guess. Before the minimum cut-off time and after the maximum one the discount extrapolates with a flat instantaneous forward rate. The parameters of a method may need tuning for a proper, reasonable or fast convergence, depending on the method.

QuantLib's public nested class FittedBondDiscountCurve::FittingMethod. A curve fits a copy of its method, made as QuantLib's clone() makes it, and its FitResults is that copy, holding the fit's solution, iterations, cost, end criterion and the weights it used; the method given stays as it was built. So a derived method needs no clone of its own. An extension point, as in QuantLib.
