The Black-Scholes forward (Fokker-Planck) operator in the logarithm of the underlying, in one direction: the density's drift −(r − q − σ²/2) and half the variance, each on the columns of the first and second derivatives, over a time step, the variance the Black forward variance or, on request, the local variance at each point.

Remarks

A working type: SetTime(double, double) rewrites the operator's bands in place, through buffers it holds, by QuantLib's arithmetic in QuantLib's order, so a time step allocates nothing. Its times and reads are FdmBlackScholesOp's: each structure read at the instants the step's times denote on Clock(GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess, FdmQuantoHelper?), on its own clock, where QuantLib reads them at the risk-free curve's times (the mismatched-dates class); structures that share the risk-free curve's times keep QuantLib's bits. A documented divergence.
