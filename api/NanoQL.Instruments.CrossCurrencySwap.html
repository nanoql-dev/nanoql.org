Remarks

Unsealed, as in QuantLib, where the constant-notional and mark-to-market swaps derive from it; a derived swap builds its legs and passes them, with their currencies, to the multi-leg constructor. A leg's currency may be null, QuantLib's empty currency, as QuantLib's fixed-vs-floating rate helper gives its fixed leg. The in-currency results QuantLib's swap holds (inCcyLegNPV, inCcyLegBPS, npvDateDiscounts) are members of the CrossCurrencySwapResults an engine returns.
