Remarks

A latent model implementing IDefaultLossModel over itself: its correlation is a double, so QuantLib's quote and value constructors merge. Bound to a basket, a copy keeps the live names' rows and recoveries and prices the restated tranche.

The pool's average default probability and recovery are weighted by the notionals of the names alive at the evaluation date. QuantLib's reads the notionals alive at the statistic's date beside the probabilities and the first remainingSize() recoveries of the evaluation date (gaussianlhplossmodel.hpp:153-190), so a default programmed after the evaluation date reads past its vectors, undefined (2.24789, 2.24363, 1.77310 and a throw in four runs); here the model reads QuantLib's model without the event (5.50809; Divergences/LhpProgrammedDefaultTests.cs).
