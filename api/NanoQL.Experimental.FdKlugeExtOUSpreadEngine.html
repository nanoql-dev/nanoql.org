The three-dimensional finite-difference engine of a spread option between Kluge's power e^(f_p(t) + X + Y) and the gas price e^(f_g(t) + U) of a KlugeExtOUProcess: X's and U's meshers from their extended Ornstein-Uhlenbeck processes, Y's an exponential jump mesher, the basket payoff on power and gas, and early exercise by the vanilla step conditions.

Remarks

QuantLib's engine takes the vanilla option's arguments and casts its payoff to a basket payoff; its case prices a basket option, which is the instrument here. The maturity and the step conditions' times are read on the discount curve's day counter from its reference date, as QuantLib's. A time past a seasonal shape's last is refused.
