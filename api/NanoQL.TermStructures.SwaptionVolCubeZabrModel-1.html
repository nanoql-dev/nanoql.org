The ZABR model of a swaption volatility cube: QuantLib's SwaptionVolCubeZabrModel<Kernel> with its XabrModelTraits specialization, its smiles fitted by ZabrInterpolation<TEvaluation> and its sections ZabrSmileSection<TEvaluation>s, five parameters α, β, ν, ρ and γ.

Remarks

A stateless trait: its members are static, and every instance, its zero value included, is the same model. Fewer than five guesses or fixed flags are refused, as QuantLib's traits refuse them; more are ignored. ZABR has no shifted form: a section on a shifted at-the-money structure is refused when it is built, at the cube's construction for an at-the-money-calibrated cube and at its first query otherwise, as QuantLib's is.
