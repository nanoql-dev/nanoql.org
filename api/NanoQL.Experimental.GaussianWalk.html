Remarks

The radius is std::normal_distribution<Real>(0.0, σ) on a std::mt19937, drawn as MSVC draws it (NormalRandomWalkDistribution), and the direction on a Mersenne Twister, both on the seed, which is required: QuantLib's default seeds from the clock.
