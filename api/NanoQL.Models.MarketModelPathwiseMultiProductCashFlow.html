Remarks

A working type: a product writes it in place, into the caller's buffer, whose Amount the caller sizes to 1 + the number of rates. CopyFrom(MarketModelPathwiseMultiProductCashFlow) copies another cash flow's values where QuantLib assigns one cash flow to another.
