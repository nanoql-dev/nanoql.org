Remarks

QuantLib's spotValue() is the bond's dirty price on the bond's own engine; here Calculate(EvaluationContext, Func<Bond, EvaluationContext, BondResults>) takes the bond's engine, and CleanForwardPrice(ForwardResults, EvaluationContext) and ForwardPrice(ForwardResults) read its results.

The income, the coupons paid after the settlement date and up to the delivery date discounted on the income curve, is per 100 of the bond's notional at its settlement date, as the dirty price is, where QuantLib's sums the coupons' amounts on the bond's face (bondforward.cpp:59-83), so on a face other than 100 delivering after a coupon its forward value mixes the two. The forward value and the prices are then per 100.

The spot value, the bond's dirty price, is a value at the bond's settlement date: it is carried back to the discount curve's reference date before the income, discounted to that date, is netted and the rest carried to delivery, where QuantLib's grows the settlement-date price from the reference date (bondforward.cpp:86-87, forward.cpp:61-65), so for a bond that doesn't settle on the curve's reference date its forward is high by 1/P(ref, settlement). On a bond settling on the reference date the two agree. The clean forward price takes the accrued amount at delivery on the forward's basis, per 100 of the notional at settlement, where QuantLib's subtracts it per 100 of the notional at delivery (bondforward.cpp:49-51), another basis once the bond amortizes between the two.
