Remarks

A target swaption's smile is the base smile at a strike mapped through the tenor basis spread, under an affine terminal swap rate model fitted to the base swaption's deterministic flows, scaled by the target swap's annuity against the final swap's. A smile section is built per query, as QuantLib's, from the swaps' fair rates on the discount curve; the swaps are built in the context the structure was built with, which it keeps. The target floating schedule is on the base index's calendar, as QuantLib's.

The swaps run the swap length rounded to whole months (Divergences/TenorSwaptionLengthTests.cs): QuantLib casts the length to whole years before it multiplies by twelve, so an 18-month tenor builds 12-month swaps; on whole years both are QuantLib's. A swap with no coupon on or after the discount curve's reference date leaves its flows empty, which QuantLib then reads the last of, undefined; here it is refused.
