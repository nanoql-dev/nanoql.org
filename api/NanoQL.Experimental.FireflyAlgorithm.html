Remarks

M fireflies explore the N-dimensional parameter space, each moved by its attraction to the brighter ones (the intensity) and a random walk: X_{i,j}^{k+1} = X_{i,j}^k + I(X^k)_{i,j} + RandomWalk_{i,j}^k, the intensity monotonic. The population is sorted each iteration and split: the M − M_de brightest move as fireflies, the M_de others by the differential evolution operator Y^{k+1} = X_best^k + F (X_{r1}^k − X_{r2}^k), each coordinate crossed over with probability C (one always), and accepted where it improves. With M_de = M it is a differential evolution optimizer whose base is a random member. The algorithm stops at its iterations or after its stationary iterations. Reference: A. Abdullah et al., "A New Hybrid Firefly Algorithm for Complex and Nonlinear Problem", Advances in Intelligent and Soft Computing 151, pp. 673-680, 2012.

The population starts on a Sobol sequence within the bounds, its seed the algorithm's: QuantLib's is 0, the clock, read only past the 32 dimensions of Jaeckel's direction integers, so the bits are QuantLib's up to there. A problem's constraint is never empty, so QuantLib's refusal of one doesn't arise. The differential evolution's integers are std::uniform_int_distribution's on a std::mt19937, drawn as MSVC draws them, and its crossover's uniforms a Mersenne Twister's, both on the seed, which is required: QuantLib's default seeds from the clock. Immutable: each minimization copies the intensity and the walk and starts the generators from the seed, where QuantLib's continues them.

Fixes (Divergences/FireflyAlgorithmTests.cs): each moved firefly's new value goes to its own place in the sorted population, where QuantLib's writes it at the place its index names, another firefly's, so the population's values and positions part after the first move; pure differential evolution takes a copy of its random base, where QuantLib's assigns through a reference and overwrites the best firefly with it; and fewer than three differential evolution members are refused, since three distinct members can't be drawn from fewer, where QuantLib's draws loop forever.

The differential evolution's donors, and pure differential evolution's base, are drawn in [Mfa, M − 1] and read as fireflies' labels, the order of the Sobol start, not as places in the sorted population, as QuantLib reads them; its header doesn't say which members it means. So in a hybrid the donors are the fireflies labelled Mfa to M − 1, which may be moving as fireflies, and a target may be its own donor; in pure differential evolution the two readings agree. The three-member refusal follows the label reading.
