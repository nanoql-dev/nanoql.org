Remarks

The value alone, as QuantLib's.

The second asset's yield is read on the first process's risk-free curve, its yield plus the first rate less the second, where QuantLib reads no risk-free rate, so two risk-free curves price as QuantLib's engine on the market restated onto the first (Divergences/TwoAssetTwoRiskFreeCurvesTests.cs). The flat market runs on the clock across both processes (NanoQL.Methods.FdmBlackScholesOp.ClockAcross(System.Collections.Generic.IReadOnlyList{NanoQL.Processes.GeneralizedBlackScholesProcess})), each structure read by date, accruing nothing before its date, where QuantLib builds it from the evaluation date with rates over the risk-free curve's time; on structures dated at the evaluation date alike the market is QuantLib's (Divergences/TwoAssetMismatchedTimesTests.cs).
