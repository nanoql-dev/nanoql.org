Remarks

The helper builds its swap when it is constructed, through MakeOIS, at a zero fixed rate and without the spread, which the implied quote adds. An empty calendar, day counter, end-of-month flag or fixed-leg frequency leaves the builder's default; a pricer given is set on the swap's overnight leg. The overnight coupons build every daily value date, telescopic or not (playbook §2's telescopic row).

The latest relevant date is the latest of the maturity, the last payment and the end of the last fixing's deposit, and the latest date stays that date whatever the pillar, where QuantLib's FRA and swap helpers reset theirs to the pillar date. Bound to a curve, its copy holds a swap whose overnight coupons are copies on a clone of the index on that curve, each keeping its pricer: QuantLib relinks the index's handle under the built swap. The swap is discounted on the curve, or on the discounting curve given. Its dates follow the evaluation date unless explicit start and end dates are given.

InitializeDates(EvaluationContext)

Computes the helper's dates for an evaluation date: QuantLib's initializeDates(). A helper whose dates follow the evaluation date overrides it and calls it from its constructor; the bootstraps call it on a fresh copy to re-date the helper for a curve built under another evaluation date. The base does nothing.
