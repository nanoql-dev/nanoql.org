Remarks

The constructor checks its arguments and computes the optionlet tenors and the cap and floor lengths, as QuantLib's does. A derived class strips in its own constructor, which sets the stripped values; QuantLib's lazy calculation is that constructor, and its inspectors return what it set.

Unsealed: QuantLib leaves the strip to its derived classes (OptionletStripper1, OptionletStripper2), and a stripper of nanoql's users is one more.

Exceptions

ArgumentNullException

termVolSurface or iborIndex is null.

ArgumentOutOfRangeException

type isn't a member of its type, or paymentLag is negative.

ArgumentException

A normal volatility has a displacement, an overnight index has no optionlet frequency, or the surface's last option tenor is shorter than the first cap and floor length.
