Swaptions

A European swaption is the right to enter a swap on its expiry. MakeSwaption builds one from a swap index, whose conventions give the underlying swap, an expiry and a strike, as QuantLib's does; the strike, if not given, is the underlying's fair rate.

Pricing on a volatility

Payer swaptions into a five-year Euribor swap, expiring in one year and in five, on the curve of the curves guide, each priced on a 20% lognormal volatility and on a 0.60% normal one:

var index = new EuriborSwapIsdaFixA(new Period(5, TimeUnit.Years), curve);
Swaption swaption = new MakeSwaption(index, new Period(expiry, TimeUnit.Years), strike)
    .WithNominal(10e6)
    .ToSwaption(context);

double black = BlackSwaptionEngine.Calculate(swaption, context, curve, 0.20).NPV!.Value;
double bachelier = BachelierSwaptionEngine.Calculate(swaption, context, curve, 0.0060).NPV!.Value;
Swaption Strike Black, 20% Bachelier, 0.60%
1Y into 5Y payer 2.50% 111,256.85 127,924.14
1Y into 5Y payer 3.00% 32,834.57 38,957.46
5Y into 5Y payer 2.50% 311,110.95 330,436.01
5Y into 5Y payer 3.00% 212,463.69 214,752.13

BlackSwaptionEngine prices by Black's formula and BachelierSwaptionEngine by Bachelier's, on the annuity of the underlying's fixed leg. Each takes a volatility as a number, as here, or as a swaption volatility structure: a constant, a matrix by expiry and tenor (SwaptionVolatilityMatrix), or a cube with a smile (SabrSwaptionVolatilityCube, InterpolatedSwaptionVolatilityCube).

Bermudan swaptions

A swaption with several exercise dates is priced on a short-rate model: TreeSwaptionEngine on any model with a tree (Hull-White, Black-Karasinski, …), FdHullWhiteSwaptionEngine and FdG2SwaptionEngine by finite differences, and Gaussian1dSwaptionEngine on the Gaussian1d models. JamshidianSwaptionEngine prices a European one on an affine model in closed form.
