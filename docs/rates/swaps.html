Swaps

The builders make swaps as QuantLib's do: MakeVanillaSwap from a tenor and an Ibor index, MakeOIS from a tenor and an overnight index, each option not given taking the index's conventions or QuantLib's default. A builder ends in ToVanillaSwap(context) or ToOvernightIndexedSwap(context), the context giving the evaluation date its start is counted from.

A vanilla swap

Two five-year payer swaps on Euribor 6M at 2.50%, one spot and one starting in five years, on the curve of the curves guide, which forecasts and discounts:

var euribor = new Euribor6M(curve);
VanillaSwap spot = new MakeVanillaSwap(new Period(5, TimeUnit.Years), euribor)
    .WithFixedRate(0.025)
    .WithNominal(10e6)
    .ToVanillaSwap(context);
VanillaSwap forward = new MakeVanillaSwap(new Period(5, TimeUnit.Years), euribor)
    .WithForwardStart(new Period(5, TimeUnit.Years))
    .WithFixedRate(0.025)
    .WithNominal(10e6)
    .ToVanillaSwap(context);

FixedVsFloatingSwapResults spotResult = DiscountingSwapEngine.Calculate(spot, context, curve);
FixedVsFloatingSwapResults forwardResult = DiscountingSwapEngine.Calculate(forward, context, curve);

An overnight swap

OvernightIndexedSwap ois = new MakeOIS(new Period(5, TimeUnit.Years), new Estr(curve))
    .WithFixedRate(0.025)
    .WithNominal(10e6)
    .ToOvernightIndexedSwap(context);
OvernightIndexedSwapResults oisResult = DiscountingSwapEngine.Calculate(ois, context, curve);

Here €STR forecasts from the Euribor curve, to keep the example short; a desk's €STR swap would take an €STR curve, bootstrapped from €STR swaps.

The results

Swap NPV Fair rate Fixed leg BPS
5Y payer at 2.50%, spot -23,323.32 2.4500% -4,664.66
5Y payer at 2.50%, 5Y forward 197,846.96 2.9871% -4,061.51
5Y €STR payer at 2.50% -40,175.14 2.4151% -4,732.07

DiscountingSwapEngine.Calculate returns the swap's NPV, its fair rate and fair spread, and each leg's NPV and basis-point value, the fixed leg first. A results record is a value: comparing two compares every result.

Other swaps

The same engine prices FloatFloatSwap, ZeroCouponSwap and a Swap of any legs, each with its own results record; the cross-currency swaps have engines of their own. A leg is built by its builder (FixedRateLeg, IborLeg, OvernightLeg and the rest), from a schedule.
