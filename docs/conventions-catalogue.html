The shipped conventions

Every record ConventionSet.QuantLib reads, by kind: QuantLib's presets and its builders' defaults, each with QuantLib's values, generated from QuantLib and checked against it (docs/conventions.md). A record named after a coded preset is that preset's; a record named after a builder, MakeVanillaSwap.EUR or MakeOIS, holds the options its builder fixes. Each table lists a record's name, its base where any record of the kind has one, and the fields it gives, in the files' order; a field left out comes from the record's base, or QuantLib's reference.
